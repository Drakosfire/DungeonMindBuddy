- Multi-column grouping must keep statblock actions and biography/exterior distinct; source readback must expose every original unit and the adaptation.
- Existing branch alternatives remain visible; no social route is deleted to force confrontation.

- Render selected statblock, exterior, explanation, consequences and cover against the PDF before/after, checking legibility, audience boundaries and media identity. Operator task review remains separate.
