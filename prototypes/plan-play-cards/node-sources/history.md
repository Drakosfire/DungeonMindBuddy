# Lysandra Ironveil — Mireward history & Ironveil family

**Use:** When Lysandra is **home soil** (Mireward gate, downwind lane, shrine, Last Dry Bed). She does **not** monologue this file — she **deflects**, **corrects**, or **goes quiet**. The party learns it in **scraps**, **arguments**, and **Lysandro being Lysandro**.

**Voice contrast:** In Mirathorn she is **rank, restraint, deferred feeling**. In Mireward she is **someone’s daughter** first — and that reordering **humiliates and steadies her at once**.

---

## One-line soap pitch

**Lysandra Ironveil ran away from the most sincerely embarrassing anti-authoritarian family on the Reach to become a guard captain — and the first person who recognizes her at the gate is her **father**, who taught her that gates are theatre.**

---

## Mireward — what it means to her

She was **raised here**, not “from Mirathorn originally.” The city is **duty**; Mireward is **origin**.

| Place | Personal meaning |
|-------|------------------|
| **Reach Gate / mud apron** | Childhood chore: fetch water for tithe scale; learned head-count math from **Torr** (grandfather) before she learned sword forms. |
| **Wall walk (east segment)** | **Lysandro’s** dawn route; she walked it with **her father** age 8–14 every summer. Knows every patched board by touch. |
| **Downwind lane — Fuller’s Row** | **Merrow’s** vats; smell of honest work; neighbors who **vote against Mirathorn guilds** at dinner, not ballot. |
| **Bell / shrine square** | **Aunt Bib’s** candle queue; patrol widows’ gossip; where **Delwen** kissed her once and she pretended it was wind. |
| **The Last Dry Bed** | Drover-season sleepovers on the floor as a girl; Maera’s mother knew her by sight — **“Lysa, off the counter.”** |
| **Ferry head** | Where she said **“one season in Mirathorn”** and meant it; where **Delwen** stopped waving. |

**Self-image split:** Mireward taught her **accountability without crown worship**. Mirathorn taught her **what happens when institutions rot**. She loves both truths and **cannot say either out loud** without sounding like a speech — which is why **her father’s** speeches mortify her: he says aloud what she **believes** in **private**.

## The Ironveil family — cast

Embarrassingly anti-authoritarian: not criminals, not clever radicals — **sincere**, **loud**, **domestic**, and **wrong at the volume of right**. They treat **salutes as optional**, **tolls as negotiable philosophy**, and **rank as a costume** — while still **showing up** for wall walk, tithe honest weight, and neighbor soup.

**Generation map:** **Torr** (grandfather, quiet) ← **Lysandro + Merrow** (parents, loud + louder) ← **Lysandra + Calyx** (siblings).

### Lysandro Ironveil *(father, ~58)* — **gate emeritus, S22 table canon**

| | |
|--|--|
| **Role tonight** | Holds the **Reach Gate walk** after tenth bell; foot traffic only; axle crisis on the apron. |
| **Look** | Rain-grey beard (went grey early), mismatched pauldrons (Mirathorn issue + Mireward patch), **no helmet**. |
| **Politics** | *“No crown without consent”* scratched on a **soup ladle** he keeps in the gate locker “for emergencies.” Runs **mock tribunals** on slow nights. |
| **Love language** | **Public storytelling**, **rules argued as folk songs**, calling her **Lysa** in front of soldiers. |
| **Secret** | Hides **Calyx’s fourth transfer petition** in his locker — hasn’t told Merrow Lysandra never signed it. |

**Table lines:**

- *“Road’s open. Town’s full. I’m not opening for wagons till dawn unless you’re bleeding or useful — and my **daughter** doesn’t count as useful till she says hello to her **father**.”*
- *“Write **foot only** — and if the axle folk want a miracle they can lift like the hymn says.”*
- On rank: *“You look like you’ve been **eating rank** again.”*

### Merrow Ironveil *(mother, ~55)* — **fuller, louder than the vats**

| | |
|--|--|

| **Toward Lysandra** | Fierce pride **weaponized as criticism**: *“Don’t you dare salute me, I’m your **mother**.”* Will **corner her in downwind lane** if party enters town. |
| **Toward Mirathorn** | *“Spire noise.”* Blames **Commander Thalia** for “stealing her sensible child.” |

### Torr Ironveil *(grandfather, ~78)* — **retired tithe clerk, quiet traitor to the bit**

## Relationships — soapy wires

### Commander Thalia Ashenvale *(mentor / mirror — partially filled)*

- **Thalia recruited** her out of Mireward youth militia after the tithe-barn incident.
- Lysandra **respects** her; does **not** confide. Thalia represents **everything Merrow hates** — merit, rank, Mirathorn ** permanence**.
- **Unspoken:** Thalia may know Ironveil family reputation; never mocks it to Lysandra’s face.
- **Knife:** If Thalia ** doubted** Lysandra after charm/promotion, Lysandra would **break** — not shown on table yet.

### Sara & Frank *(comms operators)*

- **Not blood** — **Mirathorn switchboard family** Lysandra **trusts**.
- **Sara** patches family line when **asked**; Lysandra’s **“don’t patch this week”** (S22) = **shame preemption**, not estrangement.
- Frank’s **Session 18** muffled meat/boundary traffic — Lysandra **on that wire** while Ironveils **would have said** “told you crown food rots.”

### Delwen Rast *(the one that stuck)*

- **Not tragic opera** — **ordinary wound**: she chose duty path, Delwen chose **someone who stayed**.
- If they meet: ** polite**, **hurt**, **no scene** unless player pushes.
- **Orrin Kettle** — decent drover; **not a villain**; makes Lysandra’s choice **feel small**.

### The Questionable Company *(why Mireward family might love them)*

- **Lysandro** **adores** anyone who **helps the axle line without rank**.
- Merrow **tests** them with **one rude question**; passes if they **answer plain**.
- Calyx **hero-worships** Stafl/Bonogo by proximity to Lysandra.
- Bib watches if they **treat Orin upstairs** at Last Dry Bed with mercy or cruelty.

## Embarrassment taxonomy — how to play “soapy”

| Trigger | Lysandra reaction |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Lysa** in public | Jaw tight; *“**Lysandra**.”* fails to land. |
| **Father’s story** | Pinches bridge of nose; **does not deny** story. |
| Merrow **mock reeve** mention | *“Mother.”* one syllable warning. |
| **Delwen** name | Goes **still**; changes subject to **patrol status**. |
| Calyx’s **petitions** | *“Not now.”* softer than she intends — **regret flash**. |
| Party **laughing with family** | **Joins or forbids** — 50/50; both are real. |
| **Salute** near family | Refuses; they **cheer**; she **hates that she agrees**. |
