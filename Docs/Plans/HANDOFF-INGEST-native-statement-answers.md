subject can read that subject's qualified source statements, synthesize an
answer, persist it through the existing APP turn transaction, and reopen its
original source clauses. This is the first consumer of the merged statement
endpoint, not a full native Graph cutover. Automatic subject discovery, reviewed
identity publication, full-World fixture publication and the unchanged fifteen

serialize a qualified statement as a pretend Markdown section.

## Frozen input, producing inclusion and output

1. Authenticate the managed World/campaign/role, active native binding and exact
   native subject selection. Load one immutable read revision R and its exact
   full domain/profile references. Reuse Core's governed context and the merged
   source_statements subject read; no second projector, ranker or identity map.
2. Preserve complete qualified payloads, semantic commitments, authorized about
   and reporter identities, source-local episode/order, partial/gap outcomes,
   exact admission/source revision/body digest and UTF-8 clause extent/hash.
   Require validated original publication child C and receipt/mappings for every
   statement used as qualified context; unavailable correspondence is a gap,
   never apparently complete support. Prove correspondence to R rather than
   equating selection parent P, C and R.
   A hidden/missing dependency cannot become a complete bundle or universal
   absence. Unknown identity or authority mismatch refuses before provider IO.
3. Persist the native support frame as a typed extension of the existing turn
   receipt/execution JSON: NativeStatementContextReceiptV1 and
   NativeStatementExecutionV1 contain the frozen writer policy, support
   observation, producing authorization/inclusion and lifecycle. Reuse common
   provider events and durable envelope capture/inclusion verification. No new authority ledger/table. A handle list alone
   is not inclusion: verify the full qualified frame and exact source clauses in
   the actual producing request. Untrusted source text remains data.
4. Permit source-attributed synthesis, hypotheses marked as proposals and honest
   limitations. Statements remain nonfactive context; about is discovery and
   reported_by is attribution, never endorsement. Do not restrict answers to
   literal quotation to make evaluation easier. Preserve qualification in the
   writer contract; semantic faithfulness remains independently assessed, not
   established by ID/digest checks alone.
5. Add a narrowly typed native source-clause citation family and corresponding
   completion contract. Bind each citation to the producing attempt/envelope,
   included support/read identity, managed/native World/campaign/fullrefs/R,
   native source artifact/revision/body digest and exact byte extent/hash. This
   is source attribution, not admitted World-fact or classic Graph authority.
   Genuine pre-existing APP section chains remain readable under their own
   historical contract, without forced new mappings or dual live dispatch.
6. Complete through the existing APP claimed-turn transaction, strict SQL JSON
   constraints and completion/reload validation. Version the new shape and
   reader fence; preserve retained old bytes. The frozen native execution policy
   requires native output proof regardless of caller-supplied result type:
   completion=None, plain TurnResult and legacy completions cannot bypass it.
   Reconstruct completion from captured raw output and producing request, then
   revalidate inclusion and citations; reload enforces the stored equivalent. Completed replay returns the
   original turn before current head/source/run queries or fresh provider work.
7. Historical clause opening authenticates current access but uses the pinned
   native revision/source proof and byte extent. Head movement cannot silently
   retarget a citation. The ordinary conversation citation UI opens these exact
   bytes, or truthfully explains lost access/unavailability; never falls back to
   a nearby paragraph or current revision.

## Required request and retry rules

The new native selection and Ingest selection are mutually exclusive. Conflicting
classic Graph scope or unsupported surface/capabilities refuse explicitly; they
are never silently discarded. Completed replay follows current authentication,
ownership and submitted-intent reconciliation, before fresh native/source/head
reads. Provider authorization, inclusion and lifecycle callbacks use the existing
claimed attempt fence. Entered/unknown outcomes retain the original incident and
cannot silently dispatch another writer. No success text can bypass missing
native proof. Ordinary historical replay never reexecutes generation.
