## Contract

- Preserve `_material()`'s complete immutable acquisition basis. Build an exact catalog from validated parent source receipts, captured exact passage bodies and saved-section receipts, not Graph assertion prose, hashes alone, current documents or latest reads.
- Add an explicit final `writer_selection` controller phase after admissible retrieval termination, before writer authorization. No implicit retention-phase reuse. Freeze a required selection policy descriptor in a new explicit APP policy subtype; historical execution policies remain historically exact.
- A genuine nonempty selection request is authorized and lifecycle-accounted through the parent SDK gate. Jev returns one closed typed judgment per exact source ID: direct support, material qualification/conflict, uncertainty, irrelevant. Unknown, missing, duplicate or malformed judgments refuse; no retry, fallback or invented response.
- Retain direct support, qualification/conflict and uncertain units. For every selected native passage, expand to the whole producing source-read event and all of its receipt bodies. Selected saved sections remain exact complete reads. Requested selection, expanded inclusion and exclusions are distinct recorded sets.
- Bind selection to question, scope, saved basis, terminal disposition, full acquired catalog digest, exact selector catalog, actual controller authorization/envelope and acknowledged response. Complete acquired evidence does not imply corpus completeness.
- Durably capture the exact normalized SDK selection response under an explicit controller-response item before recording a selection receipt. Do not misuse the writer answer-draft item. APP checks protected capture and transient actual request/response proof in the selection append transaction; it independently reconstructs catalog membership and whole-event closure. Missing/incomplete capture refuses. A driver dictionary or response hash alone supplies no authority.
- Persist one typed selection receipt, not a per-passage event family. Later writer authorization must match its selected sections and whole-event closure exactly. No acquisition or second selection after settlement, and no controller after writer authorization.
- Stage the source writer with no Graph payloads, selected whole source-event IDs and selected saved-section IDs. Generate finite citation selectors from actual writer bytes; retain existing request/completion reconstruction, source classes, historical citation opening, limitations and one-writer invariant.
- Empty selection follows existing honest insufficient-evidence output. An actually empty acquired catalog is a deterministic host case and must not manufacture a Jev response; pin its receipt semantics before implementation. Refused retrieval never becomes successful selection. Authoring/proposals keep their separate validated route.
