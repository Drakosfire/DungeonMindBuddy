Current page/latest unsaved draft remains primary authoring context, distinct from saved/World evidence and historical conversation. Capture the complete immutable draft at Send; retain exact writer-context and placement/save proof. Never truncate the draft, ignore unsaved changes, equate unseen metadata to evidence, reuse another draft/revision, invent new provenance authority or bypass configured provider-capacity admission. No new arbitrary character/token caps, legacy execution fallback, or global model/profile change.

Proposed architecture for critique: make decision context phase-specific. Navigation gets exact draft identity, structural section catalog and availability, not an automatic full body. Exact sections of that same frozen draft are accessible through controller-owned reads as needed, with section identity/offsets/content hashes derived from the captured record. Assessment sees the actually acquired sections and records coverage honestly. Full captured draft continues to reach the writing call once under its existing authority; controller judgments do not mint saved/World citations. Provider-capacity-aware partitioning must preserve all text and make every part accessible, including a long single line or headingless page; no sentence/heading/title/ID heuristics deciding truth. Avoid introducing a second store or independently mutable draft state. Old proofs may be readable but only the new current execution contract runs where a new contract is necessary.

Open design questions: minimum required current-draft read/selection contract versus use of existing captured-context proof; whether controller assessment needs explicit draft coverage policy; how to bind sections without treating them as World evidence; how to prevent related_pages from reintroducing same unconditional overflow.
