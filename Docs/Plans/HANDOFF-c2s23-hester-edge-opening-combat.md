## §2 Operator intent for Session 23 open (locked direction)

These are **GM choices**, not yet corpus — treat as design constraints unless the operator revises:

| Constraint | Operator say |
|------------|----------------|
| **Combat in the first scene** | Yes — open with a fight, not gate paperwork or inn RP first. |
| **Where** | **North side of town** — something crosses the wall / road from fen-ward. |
| **Guard model** | Mireward has **no formal guard culture right now** — decades of peace; garrison **thin** (festival recall + long patrol out per scaffold §A2). **Lysandro** models **mutual-aid / peace-forward** wall-watching (see seed). |
| **Interrupt** | Lysandro is **mid-explanation** to party + Lysandra when a **call goes up from the north**. |
| **Layout (v0)** | One **main road** through town — **south gate (where party is)** → interior → **north gate / fen apron**. Enough to orient; defer full gazetteer. |
| **Edge** | **Edge of the World** = last **Reach outpost before the swamp/fen** (north of Mireward). Hester’s tube/letter originated there or transited through there. |
| **Siege direction** | This arc is moving toward a **siege**: Edge is already under siege, and Mireward is trying to prepare for pressure it knows it is not ready to hold. |
| **Mireward festival** | Mireward had been preparing its own music festival: punkier, anarchic, anti-authority / pro-free-spirit counter-programming to Mirathorn. Not “down with Mirathorn” so much as “we thrive far from Mirathorn.” |
| **Bards matter** | The festival means extra bards, bands, road performers, and strange instruments are already in town — useful for clues, false alarms, morale, and sonic counterplay. |
| **Later breakout** | Lay groundwork for the roving **Celtic punk battlewagon** / battle-band to arrive later, help crack or bypass the siege, and escort the smaller party north toward Edge. This is likely **not** reached this session, but seed it now. |

**Design gap to close:** S22 has Hester carrying reports **to Mirathorn**. The operator now wants clarity on what **Edge → Mireward** communication already happened **before the party arrives** — separate dispatch, duplicate copy, earlier rider, or tithe-barn intake log. **You must propose and defend one chain-of-custody.**

**Strategic shape:** The opening combat should not be a random road fight. It is the first visible sign that Mireward is becoming a siege town: it lacks formal readiness, has too many civilians and performers, and must learn how to hold long enough for unconventional help and a small-party northward push.

## §3 Core brainstorm deliverables

Work toward these outputs in conversation; capture finals in session notes:

### A. Private Hester (buildout)

- Full name if needed (**recap: “Private Hester”** only — session notes once typo’d “Hester Mull”; do not invent Mull without operator OK).
- Age, voice, why she was riding south **that** fast, relationship to **Commander Vale** and Reach chain of command.
- **What was in the tube** beyond what she told the party aloud? Sealed vs spoken layers.
- Does she **reappear in S23** (already in town? second rider? name on a muster roll)? Operator has not decided — present 2–3 options.

### B. Edge → Mireward message (content + recipients)

Answer explicitly:

| Question | Your job |
|----------|----------|
| **What** did Edge communicate? | e.g. headcount, music incidents, missing patrol, refugee wave, wrong-verse hymn, request for grain/beds/escort — **pick a stack**, not one vague “bad north.” |
| **To whom** in Mireward? | Name **roles + people**: reeve/mayor, sergeant-emeritus, tithe clerk, Lysandro, ferry master, sheriff — scaffold §F lists slots **TBD**. Operator needs **named faces**. |
| **When** did it arrive? | Hours vs days before party; did town **act** or **stall**? |
| **Why hasn’t south heard?** | Scaffold §C — lean on capacity crush + festival deafness; don’t contradict. |

Cross-read: `Elderwyld/Cities and Towns/Edge of the World/README.md` (music rumor stub; position on Reach still **TBD** — you may **propose** Edge sits ~2–4 days north of Mireward on the fen road).

Assume the emerging answer is **not merely “Edge is worried.”** Edge is **under siege** or effectively pinned. The letter should communicate that difference: they are not asking for gossip management; they are asking Mireward to organize before the pressure rolls south.

### C. Mireward leadership + NPC cast (minimal)

Decide **who runs the town tonight** when crown and mayor disagree (scaffold knob #1). Strong candidates from scaffold:

- **Lysandro** — gate voice, not formal command (emeritus).
- **Sergeant-emeritus council** or one **veteran holding the post** while youth are south.
- **Crown reeve / tithe clerk** (Torr Ironveil flavor?).
- **Mayor** (civilian logistics).
- **Sheriff / constable** (law vs wall).

Pre-plan **3–5 NPCs** who can speak in the opening hour (including at least one **non-Ironveil**).

Include at least **one festival/bard NPC** and at least **one logistics/defense NPC**. Their disagreement should embody Mireward: free-spirit civic culture versus the hard reality that someone must allocate walls, food, bells, routes, and evacuation space.

### D. Layout v0

ASCII or bullet map:

```
[SOUTH GATE / mud apron] — party, Lysandro, Lysandra reunion continues inside
        |
   Main road (wagon-width)
        |
   [optional: Last Dry Bed, tithe barn, bell — name what’s ON the road vs side alleys]
        |
[NORTH GATE / fen smell / causeway head] — alarm origin, combat arena
```

### E. Opening scene + combat

Propose a **beat-by-beat** through:

1. Gate admitted or still on apron — operator preference: **just inside or still at south gate** when Lysandro talks.
2. Lysandro’s **peace guard** speech — 3–4 bullet beats (pamphlet energy, mutual aid, “we haven’t needed helmets”).
3. **North alarm** — what’s the call? (horn, bell pattern, runner, smoke?)
4. **Combat** — enemy type, count, terrain on north road; **why now** ties to Edge letter contents.
5. **Party hooks** — who acts first given table habits (§4).

Include **one sentence win condition** and **one complication** if they win fast (refugee panic, second wave, Orin upstairs singing, etc.).

### F. Siege arc / later northbound escape

Sketch but do not overbuild:

- What **Mireward needs to survive** the first siege pressure (bells, barricades, ferry closure, performer evacuation, food control, bardic morale / counter-music).
- Why a **small party** can get north after the siege beat better than a militia column can.
- How the **Celtic punk battlewagon** / battle-band could arrive as credible help: not an instant win, but mobile noise, morale, distraction, transport, or counter-hymn.
- What clue points from this session toward **Edge** as the next destination without forcing the table there before the players choose.
