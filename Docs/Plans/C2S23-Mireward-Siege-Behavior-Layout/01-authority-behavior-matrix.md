## Table-use notes

- Salla, Orric, and Nera should each be right about a real danger.
- Each should also be wrong about the full shape of the crisis.
- Lysandro is the bridge between abstract authority and named civilian action.
- Brin is not an exposition NPC. She is a stress fracture: she knows names, but her count is unreliable.
- Treat-first culture makes Mireward humane and vulnerable at the same time.
