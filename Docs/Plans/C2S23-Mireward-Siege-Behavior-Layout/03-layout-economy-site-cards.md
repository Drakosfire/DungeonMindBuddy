## Orientation notes

- The **festival commons** are on the **south apron**, not the center of town.
- The **Bell / Shrine** should be the memorable civic heart of the siege beat.
- The **tithe compound** has an outside yard and inside barn / store structure. It is a civic reserve, not a crown store.
- The **ferry-causeway** is inside the north gate and crosses an east-west river flowing out of the mountains.
- The **north gate** is not just a wall gap. It controls access to the internal river crossing, northern road, and fen-facing response.

## Bell / Shrine as remembered site

Make this the image players retain:

- A bell rope darkened by many hands.
- A small shrine crowded with wet blankets, lanterns, and treatment bowls.
- Orric trying to speak without sounding like a ruler.
- Lysandro pointing into the crowd and making people useful by name.
- One glassy-eyed refugee sitting near the shrine fire while someone insists, "No. We treat first."

## Bell code draft

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| One clear ring | Gather / listen at Bell / Shrine. |
| Two clear rings | Water / boil teams report. |
| Three clear rings | Wall / gate support needed. |
| Four spaced rings | Emergency consensus required. |
| Continuous ringing | Fire, breach, or monster inside the wall. |

## Emergency consensus candidates

Use the Bell / Shrine for consensus because it is visible and culturally civic rather than militarized.

Minimum emergency consensus group:

- Salla Vey for stores / counts.
- Orric Tane for public legitimacy.
- Nera Coalstep for gate / wall danger.
- Lysandro Ironveil for mutual-aid legitimacy.
- Brin Holloway if the decision directly affects Edge refugees.
- One party representative if deputized / entrusted in the moment.
