# 02 — Civilian Panic Model

**Status:** Planning artifact. Not corpus-promoted canon.

Mireward's first instinct toward the glassy-eyed is to **treat**. Panic should emerge from overloaded care, bad counts, and fear of neighbors — not from immediate cruelty.

## Panic modes

| Panic mode | Trigger | First visible sign | NPC / group who helps | NPC / group who makes it worse | Player-facing intervention that matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bed panic | Brin's 55 civilians reach the north gate and the wounded cannot stand | Families carrying blankets while shouting that there is no room | Maera Vell | Ordinary families hiding usable space | Declare a triage order: wounded to Last Dry Bed, children to Bell / Shrine, able-bodied to south-apron commons |
| Food panic | Salla locks the inner store bays until the count is stable | A crowd forms at the tithe-compound gate; someone shouts that Edge will be starved | Tithe-compound clerks | Delwen's haulers or festival toughs trying to force doors | Open one measured outer-compound ration bay now; audit later; place party seal / badge / visible guarantor |
| Water / well panic | A glassy-eyed refugee drinks from a public well or coughs into a bucket line | Buckets drop; people step away from the well and stare at whoever drank last | Lysandro | Tired wall volunteers spreading contamination rumors | Establish three water lanes: clean draw, boil station, refuse wash; name one local lead for each |
| Name panic | Locals recognize Edge refugees as kin, lovers, bandmates, debtors, or festival friends | People break the treatment line to embrace specific refugees | Brin Holloway | Salla demanding names before contact | Allow controlled recognition: one contact, one question, then mark wrist / ribbon / chalk token and return to line |
| Music panic | Performers start morale songs after everyone knows songs can charm | Half the crowd sings too hard; glassy-eyed mouths move on a delayed beat | Orric Tane | A bard who refuses to be silenced on principle | Approve drum / work cadence only; ban repeated wake / rise / shepherd / eyes motifs; ask Stafl or a trusted bard to audit |
| Glassy-eye panic | Someone says aloud that half the refugees look wrong | The refugee line divides itself; stones, knives, and tools appear in hands | Nera, reluctantly | Ordinary families and ferry workers | Create warm isolation: heat, water, blankets, healer access, guards outside only, no accusation language |
| Bad-count panic | Brin says 55, Salla counts 53, Maera has 3 extra upstairs | People realize the numbers do not match and start naming who is missing | Tithe-compound clerks | Affected people exploiting the mismatch | Run a bell count: name, origin, last-seen person, injury, treatment status; contradictions become clues, not proof of guilt |
| Edge-guilt panic | Mireward learns the support column was hit while the town prepared its south-apron counter-festival | Volunteers demand an immediate northern rescue before the gate is stable | Lysandro | Orric minimizing responsibility | Convert guilt into jobs: wall relief, stretcher teams, lantern teams, ration prep, water boiling, ferry readiness |
| Treatment panic | The first treated glassy-eyed person convulses, speaks in another rhythm, or briefly worsens | Helpers recoil; somebody asks whether treatment is making it worse | Maera, if a child is involved | Nera's tired volunteers calling it transformation | Make treatment visible and bounded: one healer speaks, one guard watches, one clerk records outcome, one family member stays nearby |
| Refugee-hidden panic | A family pulls a known Edge survivor out of line and hides them | The count changes, and no one admits why | Lysandro | Ordinary families protecting kin | Offer amnesty for returning hidden refugees to treatment before the next bell; after that, searches begin |

## Escalation ladder

Use this if the table drifts and the town needs to move.

| Step | Town state | Visible cue | Mechanical / prep consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Care surge | Blankets, kettles, volunteers, shouted names | Advantage / bonuses on organizing civilians if party names leads |
| 2 | Process strain | Lines tangle; counts mismatch; stores remain closed | Resource pressure clock starts |
| 3 | Suspicion | People ask who is glassy-eyed, who touched water, who sang | Social checks become harder without public procedure |
| 4 | Fragmentation | Families hide kin; ferry workers move people quietly; inn rooms lie | Bad-count clock advances; affected people can disappear |
| 5 | Civic break | Gate, tithe compound, or Bell / Shrine becomes a confrontation point | Requires emergency consensus or decisive party intervention |

## Treatment-first framing

Mireward should not start from cruelty. Its danger is that it tries to care faster than it can understand.

Good lines for NPCs:

- "Then we treat them. We count them while we treat them. In that order."
- "You do not put my cousin outside the wall because her eyes look wrong."
- "Warm room. Clean water. No knives. If that is prison to you, build me a better word."
- "If we stop singing entirely, they took more than our road. But no one sings alone. Not tonight."
