## How to use this packet at the table

### When the party arrives / after north-gate combat

Move pressure to three immediate questions:

1. **Where do the refugees go?**
   - Wounded / children / elderly toward Last Dry Bed and Bell / Shrine.
   - Able-bodied refugees toward warm count at overflow space.
   - Glassy-eyed toward treatment-first warm isolation, not prison.

2. **Who is allowed to decide?**
   - Nera can command the wall and lanes.
   - Salla can control the tithe compound.
   - Orric can speak for civic calm.
   - Lysandro can mobilize people by name.
   - Major gate / store / isolation decisions require emergency consensus.

3. **What image defines Mireward under pressure?**
   - Bell / Shrine, lanterns, blankets, treatment bowls, and a crowd trying to stay decent.

## Emergency consensus scene procedure

Use this when a decision is too big for one person: close / open gate, open stores, restrict movement, isolate glassy-eyed refugees, send people north, use ferry-causeway, or override a site lead.

1. **Ring four spaced bells.** Everyone nearby knows this is not a general announcement.
2. **State the decision in one sentence.** No speeches first.
3. **Each authority gets one concern.**
   - Salla: resource / count concern.
   - Orric: civil legitimacy / panic concern.
   - Nera: tactical / wall concern.
   - Lysandro: people / labor / kin concern.
   - Brin, if refugee-related: Edge survivor concern.
   - Party representative: field / monster / cult concern.
4. **Name the cost of delay.** What gets worse if they argue for 5 minutes?
5. **Let the party break the deadlock.** Persuasion, intimidation, evidence, tactical promise, magical proof, or direct assumption of responsibility.
6. **Assign visible ownership.** Someone must say, publicly, "I stand behind this."

## Scene emphasis notes

### Bell / Shrine

This is the civic heart. Use it often.

- Consensus happens here.
- Treatment vows happen here.
- Bell codes originate here.
- Children and elderly may be gathered nearby.
- The party can visibly change the town's behavior here.

Possible boxed image:

> The bell rope is dark with rain and hand oil. Beneath it, a low shrine is crowded with lanterns, blankets, bowls of boiled water, and people trying not to stare at the refugees whose eyes catch the light wrong. Lysandro points into the crowd and names people into courage. Orric stands near the bell, pale but smiling too hard. Someone says, "No cages." Someone else answers, "Then treatment. Now."

### Tithe compound

This is the food / legitimacy pressure point.

- Outer yard can handle counting, ration lines, cart weighing, and temporary distribution.
- Inner barn / stores should stay controlled unless emergency consensus overrides Salla.
- This is not a crown store. It belongs to Mireward's civic trust.

### South Apron Festival Commons

This is the cultural pressure point.

- Bards want to help.
- The crowd trusts rhythm.
- The Dustwalker precedent makes music scary.
- Orric can turn festival infrastructure into a volunteer board.

### Ferry-causeway

This is the later-north-action pressure point.

- If preserved, the party can leave north cleanly later.
- If compromised, the party can still go north, but it becomes costly and improvised.
- Orin and ferry workers should be defensive because boats are lifelines.

## Remaining open decisions

Keep these small and table-useful.

| Decision | Why it matters | Suggested default |
|---|---|---|
| What is the tithe compound's local name? | Helps make it feel non-crown and local. | "The Common Tithe Yard" or "The Share Barn." |
| Who are the first 12 named Edge refugees? | Makes Brin's count emotional and queryable. | Create by symptom / kin / contradiction hook. |
| Who are Lysandro's 8 named helpers? | Makes mutual aid concrete. | One blanket lead, one kettle lead, one cart lead, one child lead, one wall relief lead, one road-clear lead, one shrine aide, one suspicious holdout. |
| What does successful treatment look like? | Keeps glassy-eye care from being vague. | Tremors, vomiting black water, fever break, one true memory recovered. |
| What does failed / incomplete treatment look like? | Preserves horror. | Patient improves socially but begins repeating a wrong phrase. |
| What exact sound / phrase marks dangerous music? | Lets bards be useful and suspicious. | Delayed mouth movement on words like wake, rise, eyes, shepherd, gate, below. |
