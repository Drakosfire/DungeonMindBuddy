# 04 — Siege Pressure Interfaces

**Status:** Planning artifact. Not corpus-promoted canon.

This file connects the NPC behavior model to the physical town model. Use it to answer what happens next when the party chooses a site, ignores a pressure, or tries to leave north.

## Where glassy-eyed people disappear

Glassy-eyed affected people should not always behave like obvious cultists. Many are exhausted, frightened, or partially influenced. The horror is uncertainty.

| Disappearance site | Why it works | Clue if checked | Consequence if ignored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Last Dry Bed loft | Maera cannot bear to turn away known people | Blanket still warm, room tag altered, Maera avoids eye contact | Hidden affected person appears later near kitchen, stairs, or children |
| Tithe barn stacks | Crowds and sacks create occlusion | Sack dust disturbed, clerk count off by one, repeated phrase heard behind grain | Food panic becomes corruption panic |
| Ferry shed | Workers know side routes and boats | Wet footprints, rope moved, pole missing | Private crossing bypasses count / treatment |
| Festival tent backs | South apron has noise, cloth walls, and performers | Someone mouths a song half a beat late | Music panic spikes |
| Privies / wash sheds | People avoid checking them during crisis | Smell of river mud, scratched spiral, bucket overturned | Affected person returns at a different site |
| Family home | Treat-first culture plus recognition | Neighbor insists, "She just needs sleep" | Household barricade forms if confronted badly |

## Sites that must hold vs can fail

| Site | Must hold? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| North Gate | Yes | If it fails, the siege becomes town breach rather than pressure management. |
| Ferry-causeway | Yes, for later northern action | If it fails, the party can still fight but cannot cleanly scout / rescue / move north. |
| Bell / Shrine | Yes, symbolically | It is the emergency consensus and treatment-first heart. Losing it means civic identity cracks. |
| Tithe compound | Partially | A breached store is bad but not session-ending if food is recovered / rationed. |
| Last Dry Bed | Partially | It can hide a problem and still remain useful. |
| South Apron Festival Commons | Can fail without ending session | A chaotic commons is recoverable and can become a loud problem rather than immediate collapse. |
| Downwind craft lane | Can fail locally | Bad smoke / disposal creates future trouble but not immediate defeat. |
| Main road | Must remain passable | If it clogs, all other responses degrade. |

## Fast player levers

1. **Define authority lanes publicly.** Nera gates, Salla stores, Orric public words, Lysandro civilian labor, Brin refugee names.
2. **Make treatment visible and humane.** Warm isolation, family witness, guards outside, no accusation language.
3. **Use the Bell / Shrine.** Emergency consensus should happen there, not behind a closed door.
4. **Open one ration bay.** Controlled mercy prevents the tithe compound from becoming a riot image.
5. **Audit music without killing it.** Drum / work songs are allowed; suspect motifs are stopped.
6. **Keep the main road clear.** Three lanes: wounded, supplies, fighters / runners.
7. **Count in groups of five.** This gives Brin's 55 a physical rhythm and makes missing people obvious.

## Destabilizers if ignored

1. Salla locks the stores and becomes the face of starvation.
2. Orric preserves the festival mood too long and accidentally creates a charmable crowd.
3. Nera closes the gate before consensus and becomes morally radioactive.
4. Maera hides one glassy-eyed person out of love, making all shelter counts suspect.
5. Orin's workers smuggle kin across the river, breaking treatment / count logic.
6. Brin's unreliable count becomes the civic paranoia engine.
7. A bard repeats the wrong rhythm and every glassy-eyed person mouths it at once.
8. The Bell / Shrine becomes an accusation site instead of a treatment vow site.
