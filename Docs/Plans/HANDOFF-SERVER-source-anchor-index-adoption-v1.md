## Product adoption contract boundary

The original product experiment successfully froze a noninitial eligible anchor and completed the pinned saved-Plan route, but it exposed a receipt commitment boundary: APP-STATE V2 requires each frozen source-scope evidence ID inside `graph_packet.candidate_evidence_ref_ids`. Architecture ruled that the union of initial and full-index candidates is legal in the existing receipt shape only with an explicit `parent_initial_retrieval_with_bounded_source_index_v1` selection policy and a `retrieval_packet_sha256` over the canonical composite of the initial claim packet, exact index version/tuples, and policy. The experiment had hashed only the initial packet; relabeling alone would have been insufficient. The local experiment was saved as `/tmp/dmb-native-source-index-product-adoption-experiment.patch` and removed from this PR's code diff; this handoff preserves the concrete finding.

The successor must implement that composite receipt commitment and the narrow APP-STATE completion rule for evidence obtained by a successful indexed source read. Initial matched counts, fact sufficiency, and assembled dispatch membership stay tied to actual initial retrieval; index-only metadata leaves the initial packet omitted as insufficient. Index membership alone must never admit a claim or source IO. A later Graph expansion may admit a noninitial anchor to the active retrieval session; only then may the exact frozen index tuple authorize a read, and only a successful read in the producing provider envelope may support an opened citation or claim under the reviewed rule. No APP-STATE or product-route contract changes are included here. Buddy #1007 may proceed on the merged #1008 initial-anchor behavior without waiting for this successor.
