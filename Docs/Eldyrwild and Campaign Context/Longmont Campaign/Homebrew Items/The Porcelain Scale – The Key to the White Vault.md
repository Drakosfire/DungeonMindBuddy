### The Porcelain Scale – The Key to the White Vault

### When Stafl (or Another Player) Casts Identify…

At first glance, the scale appears **identical** to the one Stafl carries—large, **smooth**, and **immensely durable**. But when **Identify** is cast upon it, the spell **pauses**, as if unsure. Then, a single **crack** appears—so fine it was imperceptible before—running **down the spine of the scale**.

A deeper **truth is revealed**:

The scale is **not real**—it is a **forgery of masterful craftsmanship**, made from **white porcelain**, painstakingly **layered and reinforced** to appear **genuine**.

Inside, something **hollow** stirs. The magic of **Identify** can sense **a presence**, an object hidden within.

This is **not just any forgery**—it was **made with intention**, meant to be **passed as real**, possibly to deceive someone… or something.

The **weight** is slightly off. If shaken gently, something **clicks inside**.

It has **no magical enchantment**, but it **was crafted using magic**, likely to mask whatever is **within**.

### Breaking the Scale (Or Carefully Opening It)

If **broken open** (or carefully dismantled), **within the hollowed-out interior**, the players find **a key**—a **long, thin, delicate key of unknown metal**.

It is **pale silver**, with **strange, curling filigree**—**not elven, not dwarven, not human**.

The key’s **teeth** are strangely **angular**, almost **alien**, as if made for **a lock that does not exist in this world**.

If held, the key is **cold**, but **warms slightly in the hands of someone magically attuned**.

### The Mystery of the Key & Its Origins

The **forgery** was crafted at least **a hundred years ago**, if not **older**.

Whoever **made it** was a **master craftsman**, and they **wanted to ensure no one found the key unless they knew exactly what to look for**.

No **known locks** in the city match this key—**but legends of a hidden vault, lost beneath Mirathorn or even beyond, do exist**.

Some **artisans of Mirathorn** might recognize the style of the porcelain work—possibly someone **from the Stormspire Academy, or an old rogue guild**.
