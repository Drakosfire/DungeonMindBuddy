### Item Name: Silver Raven

**Visual Description:
** A palm-sized silver figurine of a raven, finely etched with feathers that shimmer faintly in moonlight. Its eyes are tiny black gems, and its beak is slightly open—as if caught mid-caw. When activated, it gleams briefly before taking flight in a swirl of metallic feathers.

### Stats:

## Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Legacy)

Use an action to speak its command word and toss it within 60 ft

Transforms into a living **raven** for up to **12 hours**

Raven is friendly, understands your languages, obeys commands

Can cast **Animal Messenger** on it at will

Reverts to figurine if reduced to 0 HP, dismissed, or time expires

Usable again after **2 days**
