### Item: The Friendship Chime

“When you need a friend, sometimes all it takes is a little jingle.”

**Item Type**: Necklace, Charm Bracelet, or Anklet (Uncommon, requires attunement)

### Appearance

The Friendship Chime is a playful piece of jewelry decorated with small, dangling charms shaped like smiling moons, stars, and quirky faces. Each charm jingles softly when Caelynn moves, producing a soothing, cheerful sound that seems to lighten the mood. Occasionally, one of the charms will wink or makFriene a silly face, especially when Caelynn is actively trying to make friends or entertain.

### Abilities

**Friendly Aura** (Passive):

While wearing the Friendship Chime, Caelynn emits a mild aura of friendliness within a 10-foot radius. Creatures within this range find themselves oddly charmed by her, giving her advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks to establish friendships or defuse tense situations. Once per short rest, this aura also slightly calms hostile creatures, making them pause briefly or even reconsider their actions if they’re neutral.

**Summon a Backup Buddy** (Once per Long Rest):

As an action, Caelynn can jingle the chime to call forth a magical “Buddy Spirit” in the form of a small, friendly animal or a cute, squishy critter. This Buddy Spirit is harmless and can’t attack, but it distracts enemies, provides moral support, or simply cheers on Caelynn and her allies with small dances or friendly sounds.

The Buddy Spirit can use the Help action each turn to give an ally within 5 feet advantage on their next roll. It lasts up to one minute or until dismissed, and it has a 50% chance to puff out of existence in a sparkle of confetti and laughter if anyone tries to attack it.

**Charm of Chums** (Unlockable):

When Caelynn makes a new friend or successfully helps a creature in need, the Friendship Chime gains a small charm in the form of a smiley face or animal. Each charm can be used once as a reaction to give Caelynn a “social boost,” adding a +1 to her next Charisma roll or casting a minor illusion (e.g., a heart or little burst of glitter) to make her even more likable. Up to three charms can be collected at a time, refreshing whenever she makes another friend.

**Friendship Manifesto** (Passive or Optional):

When Caelynn jangles the chime with intent, it produces a small burst of sound that inspires everyone around her. Allies within 10 feet who can hear the chime gain temporary hit points equal to her Charisma modifier and feel an inexplicable urge to hug, high-five, or laugh together. Enemies in the range might experience a split second of secondhand friendliness, making it harder for them to focus on attacking her (first attack against Caelynn on the next round has disadvantage).

### Possible Development and Roleplaying Hooks

**Make a Friend, Gain a Charm**: Caelynn can unlock new minor abilities or bonus charms as she bonds with more creatures. If she makes a significant friend—like a memorable NPC or even a redeemed enemy—the chime could grant her unique, friendship-inspired effects.

**Unpredictable Reactions**: The chime has a bit of a playful mind of its own. It may randomly jingle when Caelynn’s trying to be stealthy, give off encouraging sounds in battle, or emit a cheerful tune whenever she’s around someone it senses she could befriend, no matter how unsuitable they might be.

**The Chime’s Legacy**: Rumor has it that the chime was crafted by an eccentric half-elf who believed that friendship could overcome any odds. With each friend Caelynn makes, she continues that legacy—whether through goofy antics or true bonds, her friendships might be what ultimately make the chime’s magic stronger.
