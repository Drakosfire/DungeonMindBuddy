## 🏠 Scene: A Gift With Shadows – The Offer of the Wolf’s Estate

### 🎭 Setting the Scene

Late morning, Day 2 of the Festival. Just after the players have caught their breath and handled some downtime. There's a knock at their current inn room—or perhaps she intercepts them while they’re watching the trials.

**Captain Lysandra Ironveil**, armored and composed but clearly weary, approaches them with a small sealed scroll in her hand.

### 💬 Dialogue Hook

**Lysandra (low voice):
** “Thalia Ashenvale sends her respect—and her thanks. Without you, we’d be feasting on rot and corruption right now. We owe you more than coin.”
 (She hands over the scroll.)
 “The home of the late 'Wolf'—formerly Captain Drenic Vale—has been seized by the city. Ashenvale thinks it would serve better in your hands.”

## 🏛️ Property Details – “The Hollow Watch”

Once known as **Vale House**, the property is a narrow, three-story structure nestled at the edge of the **Watchward**—a district home to military officers, magistrates, and civic leaders.

**Architecture**: Stone foundation, reinforced timber. Arcane locks on upper floors. Stark exterior—built for privacy and defense.

**Location**: Overlooks a small plaza between the Watchward and Merchant’s Row. Close to a canal path and a defunct guard outpost.

### 🧭 Rooms & Features

**1st Floor**: Foyer, lounge, large dining room, small library.

**2nd Floor**: Bedrooms, study with a concealed safe (DC 18 Investigation to find), magical scrying mirror (currently inert).

**3rd Floor (Rooftop Access)**: Greenhouse/observatory, rooftop perch—great view of the city.

**Basement**: Secure storage, wine cellar, hidden trapdoor to a long-forgotten guard tunnel (currently caved in).

### 🔑 Hooks and Features for Story Integration

**Civic Reward**: Legitimizes the party as heroes and stakeholders in Mirathorn’s future.

**Investigation Thread**: Journals, hidden letters, and belongings from Wolf offer deeper insight into the cult and its plans beyond the city.

**Civic Entanglement**: Other guard officers or nobles may be displeased—why give traitor’s property to adventurers?

**Renovation Mini-Quest**: Some rooms are damaged or cursed. A “cleansing” might be needed. Maybe a lingering haunt?

### 🛡️ Lysandra’s Final Words

“I’ve walked its halls. Whatever he did there, it’s gone now. We stripped it of his command seals… but I left the wards untouched. Thought you might want to… investigate.”
 (She looks you over and nods once.)
 “It’s yours—if you’ll take it. Call it repayment. Or a responsibility.”
