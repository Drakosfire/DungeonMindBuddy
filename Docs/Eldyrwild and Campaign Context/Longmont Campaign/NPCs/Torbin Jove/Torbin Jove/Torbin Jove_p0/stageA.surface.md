Torbin Jove is a young, bright-eyed boy of about 10 years old with a mop of curly brown hair and a mischievous grin. He is filled with youthful exuberance and a fearless curiosity about the world around him. Having been inspired by the adventurers, he has developed a keen sense of ambition and dreams of becoming a hero himself one day. Torbin is known for his impulsive spending habits, often squandering any money he gets on things that catch his fancy, like expensive candy or shiny trinkets.

## ACTIONS


Candy Throw : 'Torbin throws a piece of hard candy at a creature within 20 feet. Make a ranged attack roll with a +2 bonus. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 bludgeoning damage and the target must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be distracted, giving the next attack roll against it advantage until the end of Torbin's next turn.


Impulsive Lash : 'Torbin makes a quick jab with whatever small object he has at hand. He makes a melee attack with a +2 bonus to hit, dealing 1d4 bludgeoning or piercing damage.


Quick Climb : 'Torbin can climb a vertical surface up to 15 feet without needing an ability check.
