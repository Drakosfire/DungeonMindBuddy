Elite Corrupted Guards were once honorable soldiers, but dark magic has warped their minds and bodies. They now serve their malevolent masters with fervor, their weapons steeped in malevolence. Often found commanding lesser corrupted guards, they are formidable opponents with skill and ruthlessness.
