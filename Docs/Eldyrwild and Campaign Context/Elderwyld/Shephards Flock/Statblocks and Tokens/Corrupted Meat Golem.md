THE CORRUPTED MEAT GOLEM IS A GROTESQUE AND NIGHTMARISH ABOMINATION, COMPOSED OF VARIOUS CHUNKS OF CORRUPTED FLESH, SINEW, AND BONE. THE FLESH IS AN UNNATURAL MIX OF COLORS, RANGING FROM SICKLY GREENS TO BRUISED PURPLES, WITH PATCHES OF GLISTENING, OILY OPALESCENCE THAT GIVE IT AN ALMOST OTHERWORLDLY SHEEN. THIS GLOSSY, IRIDESCENT QUALITY MAKES THE MEAT APPEAR BOTH ROTTEN AND BIZARRELY BEAUTIFUL, AS IF TAINTED BY SOME ELDRITCH ESSENCE. STRIPS OF MUSCLE AND TENDON ARE HAPHAZARDLY STITCHED TOGETHER BY AN UNKNOWN FORCE, CREATING A HULKING, MISSHAPEN FORM THAT STANDS OVER SEVEN FEET TALL.

Multiattack : 'The Corrupted Meat Golem can make two Slam attacks.
