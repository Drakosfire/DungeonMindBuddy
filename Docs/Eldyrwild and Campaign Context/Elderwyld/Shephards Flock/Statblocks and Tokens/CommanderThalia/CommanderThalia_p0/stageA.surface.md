
Commander Thalia Ashenvale
Medium, Humanoid, Human, Lawful Neutral

![](images/0.jpg)




Commander Thalia Ashenvale is a tall, statuesque Human with sharp, chiseled features and a stern expression. Her dirty blonde hair is pulled back into a tight braid, and she wears a polished breastplate bearing the crest of the city guard over a dark blue tunic. Her piercing gray eyes survey her surroundings with a calm but commanding intensity, and her posture is always upright and alert. She is disciplined and authoritative, fiercely protective of the city and its inhabitants. Unbeknownst to her, she has been ensorcelled by the Wolf to overlook the corruption within the guard, making her resistant to believing any accusations.
