
CORRUPTED PROFESSOR'S ASSISTANT:


DRAVEN WILLOWCREST


Medium, Humanoid, Human, Chaotic Neutral

![](images/0.jpg)




DRAVEN WILLOWCREST WAS ONCE A BUDDING NECROMANCER AT THE PRESTIGIOUS STORMSPIRE ACADEMY. WITH A SHARP MIND AND A THIRST FOR KNOWLEDGE, HE WAS AN INVALUABLE ASSISTANT TO HIS PROFESSOR, DELVING INTO FORBIDDEN TOMES AND INCANTATIONS. TRAGICALLY, HIS AMBITION LED HIM INTO THE CLUTCHES OF A SINISTER CULT, MASKED UNDER THE GUISE OF AN ACADEMIC STUDY GROUP. NOW CORRUPTED, DRAVEN'S PALE FORM MOVES THROUGH THE SHADOWS OF THE ACADEMY, SERVING AS A COVERT AGENT FOR THE CULT WHILE CONTINUING TO ASSIST HIS OBLIVIOUS PROFESSOR. HIS EYES, ENCIRCLED BY THE SHADOWS OF ENDLESS NIGHTS SPENT IN DARK REVERIE, NOW GLINT WITH A SUBTLE, SINISTER AWARENESS. PERHAPS SOME PART OF HIS NOBLE SPIRIT REMAINS, BUT IT IS SHROUDED BENEATH LAYERS OF MALIGN INFLUENCE.
