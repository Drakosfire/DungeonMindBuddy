## Grit and Grime


PULSATING HAVEN FOR GOBLINS AND THEIR KIN, GRITE AND GRIME ELECTRIFIES THE ATMOSPHERE WITH WILD ENERGY AND THE THRUM OF ELECTRONIC DANCE MUSIC. SPRAWLING GEARS AND SPARKING COILS GIVE IT A DISTINCTLY STEAMPUNK AMBIANCE. GRIT AND GRIME WAS ONCE AN ABANDONED FACTORY BEFORE A PARTICULARLY ENTERPRISING GOBLIN


NAMED GROBNOK SAW ITS POTENTIAL FOR PROFIT—AND RAMBUNCTIOUS REVERLY. GATHERING FELLOW GOBLINOIDS, HE TRANSFORMED IT INTO THE SENSATIONAL DANCE HUB IT IS TODAY. NOTORIOUS FOR ITS RAUCOUS PARTIES AND OTHERWORLDLY DJ SETS, GRIT AND GRIME IS WHERE GOBLINS AND ASSORTED CREATURES UNWIND LIKE NOWHERE ELSE.
