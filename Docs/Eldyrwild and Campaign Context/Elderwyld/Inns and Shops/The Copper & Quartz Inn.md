### The Copper and Quartz Inn

## Innkeeper:

**Name:** Barin Coppergleam

Bartender : Garruk “Two Mugs” Thaldrin

**Description:** Barin Coppergleam is a gnome with a warm and welcoming demeanor, known for his quick wit and charming hospitality. He has a sturdy build, with a thick mustache and twinkling blue eyes behind a pair of intricate, steampunk-style goggles. Barin dresses in finely tailored clothes that blend practicality with a touch of elegance, often adorned with small gears and trinkets that hint at his love for tinkering.

**Background:** Barin is not only the innkeeper but also a respected member of the city council of Mirathorn. His family has a long history in the city, and he takes great pride in maintaining the Copper and Quartz Inn, which has been in his family for generations. Barin is known for his innovative spirit, often incorporating new technologies and magical enhancements into the inn's design and services.

## The Inn:

**Number of Rooms:** 25 rooms

## Room Types:

**Standard Rooms:** 15 rooms, comfortable and well-appointed, suitable for travelers looking for a cozy place to rest.

**Deluxe Rooms:** 8 rooms, more spacious and luxuriously furnished, with additional amenities like enchanted lighting and private baths.

**Suite:** 2 rooms, offering the highest level of comfort and privacy, with separate living areas and panoramic views of the city and surrounding mountains.

**Pricing:** Due to the festival and the city's overflowing population, room prices have surged.

**Standard Room:** 3 gold pieces per night

**Deluxe Room:** 7 gold pieces per night

**Suite:** 15 gold pieces per night

**Availability:** Given the influx of visitors for the Festival of Expansion, all rooms at the Copper and Quartz are fully booked. Barin has a waiting list for any potential cancellations, but chances of securing a room are slim.

## Additional Services:

**Common Room:** A spacious area where guests can relax, socialize, and enjoy hearty meals and drinks. The common room is adorned with intricate decor that reflects the gnomish heritage and steampunk aesthetics.

**Workshop:** A small workshop for tinkers and inventors to work on their projects, equipped with various tools and materials.

**Entertainment:** Regular performances by local musicians and entertainers, adding to the lively atmosphere of the inn.

**Bathhouse:** A separate bathhouse with both standard and deluxe bathing options, featuring heated baths and magical steam rooms.

Barin Coppergleam runs the Copper and Quartz with a blend of efficiency and warmth, ensuring that all guests feel welcome and well taken care of, even in the bustling environment of the festival. His position on the city council also makes him a valuable source of information and influence within Mirathorn.
