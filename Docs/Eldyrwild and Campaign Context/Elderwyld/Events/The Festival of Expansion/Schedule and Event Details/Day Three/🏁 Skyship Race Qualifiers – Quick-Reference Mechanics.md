## 🏁 Skyship Race Qualifiers – Quick-Reference Mechanics

### Ship Stat Block (Choose at Build)

**Speed** (1–5): Spaces moved per success.

**Maneuver** (1–5): Affects DC to avoid hazards or thread tight gates.

**Durability** (1–5): Number of failures before you “crash.”

Player-built ships: assign two stats at 3, one at 2 (e.g., 3/3/2). Standard racers: 3/3/3.

### Crew Roles (1–4, No Duplicates)

**Pilot** (Dex or Vehicles): Steers the ship, makes primary checks.

**Navigator** (Int or Survival/Arcana): Finds shortcuts, grants bonus Speed.

**Engineer** (Con or Tinkerer’s Tools): Heals 1 Stress or boosts engines.

**Lookout** (Wis or Perception): Spots hazards, can lower DC or give Advantage.

**Solo Racer:** Only Pilot; missing roles = –2 penalty on those checks.

**Full Crew (4):** +1 bonus on all successes.

### Skill Challenge Engine (Run Per Segment)

**Declare Actions** – Each crew picks a check, Pilot acts first.

**Roll Checks** – Each rolls vs. segment DC (10–16).

## Results:

**Success:** Move your Speed in spaces.

**Failure:** +1 Stress (if Stress = Durability, you crash), no movement.

**Nat 20:** +1 space and either lower next DC by 2 or a free minor action.

**Engineer:** May heal 1 Stress between segments (burns their action).

**Last Place Catchup:** May roll DC 12 Piloting to catch up 1 space.

### Course Format

**A. Qualifiers:** 3 segments. Most spaces covered in 6 rounds advances.
 **B. Finals:** Head-to-head, 6 segments. First to 12 spaces wins.

#### Hazards & Obstacles Table

**Floating Rings:** DC 12 Maneuver. Success = +2 spaces; fail = 0.

**Gust Tunnels:** DC 14 Athletics/Con. Success = tailwind (+1); fail = –1.

**Lightning Cloud:** DC 15 Arcana. Fail = stunned for 1 round.

**Rough Air:** DC 13 Dex save. Fail = 1 Stress.

#### Crashes & Rescue

If Stress = Durability: Crash out. Can rejoin with 10 min repair (DC 15 group check, –2 penalty).

### Quick Example

**“Blinkwind” Solo:** 3/3/2, only Pilot—rolls at –2 for Engineer/Lookout checks.

**“Stormcatcher” (2 crew):** 3/2/3, Pilot + Navigator; auto +1 space in Gust Tunnels.
