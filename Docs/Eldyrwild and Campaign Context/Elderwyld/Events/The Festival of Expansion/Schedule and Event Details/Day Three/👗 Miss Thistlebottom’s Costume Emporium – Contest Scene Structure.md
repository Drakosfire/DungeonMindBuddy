## 👗 Miss Thistlebottom’s Costume Emporium – Contest Scene Structure

### 1. Arrival & Hook (Flavor)

Vivid description of the tower, the sound, the crowds, Miss Thistlebottom’s entrance.

“Welcome, darlings, to the only place in Mirathorn where reality gets the makeover it truly deserves!”

### 2. Registration (RP/Light Mechanics)

Each PC gives name, theme, and gets a Registration Gem (enchanted, records their parade walk).

## Roll 1d4 for a registration twist:

Wacky interview question (“If your soul was a color, what shade today?”)

Magical enhancement (sparkles, shifting color, animated accessory)

Free Mood Feather (tracks emotion, gives random advantage/disadvantage)

Miss T’s Advice (roll d4 for +1 or –1 on first contest check)

### 3. The Line & Drama

Queue full of rival NPCs, magical wardrobe malfunctions, goblins in disguise.

Allow RP, quick checks (Performance/Deception, Sleight of Hand for quick-fixes, Arcana to enchant, Insight for gossip).

## Drama hooks:

Sabotage (catch a rival in the act, intervene for a future favor).

Living costume causes chaos (Animal Handling or Strength check).

Secret cult rumor whispered nearby (Insight or Investigation).

### 4. Judging & Contest Mini-Game (Optional)

PCs roll Performance vs. DC 13 (vs. rivals).

Judging: **Creativity, Showmanship, Magical Flair** (rate 1–5 each, use rolls + description + crowd reaction).

Winners get:

1st: Magical costume upgrade (1/day minor spell or effect).

Runner-up: Flair Pin (+1 Performance for 1 hour).

Audience Favorite: Sparkle Token (Advantage on one social check).

### 5. Aftermath & Hooks

Winners get “backstage” access, extra RP with Miss Thistlebottom.

Plot seeds: Secret note, invitation to afterparty, or future quest.

NPCs to remember:

Miss Thistlebottom (host/judge)

FiFi Glitterboom (rival queen)

Grub the Goblin (sabotage artist)

Talwyn Tinkerhat (assistant)

## 🧑‍🎤 How to Run the Scene

Start with colorful description, then let each PC register and get their “twist.”

Throw in 1–2 drama beats (rivalry, malfunction, rumor).

Ask for a quick Performance or other relevant skill check for judging.

Announce winners, hand out minor rewards, and slide a plot hook or two for the next story beat.
