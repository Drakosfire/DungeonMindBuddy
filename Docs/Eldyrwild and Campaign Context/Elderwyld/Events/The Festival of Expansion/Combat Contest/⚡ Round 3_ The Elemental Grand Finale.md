# ⚡ Round 3: The Elemental Grand Finale

Colosseum of the Storm – The Championship Trial

## 🧱 Structure

**Phase 1 (Arena Threat):** Teams fight 3 summoned elementals while the Colosseum floor shifts each round.

**Phase 2 (Spectacle Trial):** Judges + crowd vote based on style, coordination, and flair, not just survival.

Victory requires **winning the crowd** as much as beating the foes.

## 🎲 Core Mechanics

### 1. Elemental Gladiators (Enemies)

## Ale Elemental (Medium CR, support/chaos)

Splashes zones of slippery foam (DC 13 Dex save or fall prone).

“Buffs” drunkenness → random advantage/disadvantage to nearby PCs/NPCs.

## Storm Djinn (CR High, striker/disruptor)

Illusions (disadvantage on first attack roll each round).

Lightning strike: +6 to hit, 2d8+4 lightning.

Counterplay for Ephanna’s fae magic—creates tension.

## Stone Colossus (Large, tank)

Huge HP pool, slow but devastating slam (2d10+5).

Can alter terrain: raise walls or collapse ground (DC 15 Str/Dex save).

Perfect opponent for Baergrom to stand toe-to-toe with.

(They don’t need to be fully statted CR-accurate—think “festival spectacle monsters.”)

### 2. Arena Hazards (Environment as an Enemy)

Each round, roll **1d4**:

**Lava Cracks:** Ground opens, 10 ft. line of molten flame (Dex DC 14 or 2d8 fire).

**Flood Surge:** Wave rushes in, pushes creatures 10 ft. (Str DC 13). Difficult terrain for 1 round.

**Storm Winds:** Ranged attacks/spells beyond 30 ft. at disadvantage. Chandeliers sway for stunts.

**Arcane Collapse:** Phantom pillars crumble. Dex DC 14 save or 1d6 bludgeoning + prone.

### 3. Trial of the Crowd (Scoring)

Each “team” (PCs or NPCs) earns **Crowd Favor Points** based on:

**Damage dealt / big moves:** +1

**Environmental stunt (swinging on chandeliers, keg-smash, etc.):** +2

**Flair (speeches, quips, witty lines, RP):** +1–3 (DM discretion)

**Teamwork move (setting up ally, saving someone):** +2

**Critical Hit / Nat 20:** Automatic cheer (+2)

**Critical Fail / Nat 1:** Boo (-1, unless hilarious, then +1)

🧮 At the end of the fight, tally Crowd Favor:

**≥15 points:** Roaring victory, the crowd carries them on shoulders.

**10–14 points:** Solid win, praised but less flashy.

**<10 points:** They survive, but the Silver Masks might “outshine” them in votes.

### 4. Judges’ Weights (Final Votes)

**Sergeant Dorn Havers:** Strength + Brutality. Favors Baergrom if he tanks well.

**Willa Brasscap:** Teamwork + coordination. Will notice Ephanna + Ogonob coordination.

**“Uncle” Spence:** Style + story. Loves Bonogo’s antics and one-liners.

Each judge grants 1 **final vote** → majority = Champion.

Crowd can override if they wildly favor the PCs (≥20 points = automatic Champion).

## 🔮 Twist Options (Use if you want drama)

**The Silver Masks face the same elemental trial after the PCs.** Crowd compares both. If the PCs scrape through but lack flair, the Silver Masks could snatch victory.

**Elementals go unstable at 0 HP.** Instead of dying, they explode into one final hazard, giving the PCs a chance to “finish big” or fail spectacularly.

**Mysterious Challenger tease:** A cultic influence subtly warps one elemental (fleshy cracks, whispers). The crowd dismisses it as illusion, but the party notices.
