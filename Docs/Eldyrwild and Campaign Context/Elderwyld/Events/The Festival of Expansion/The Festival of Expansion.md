The Festival of Expansion

# Setting

The adventure begins on the approach to Mirathorn. Passing out of the grassy plains into the canals, temperate forest, vibrant farmlands, villages on the outskirts and finally the sprawling OutTown.

# The Festival

**History and Purpose:** Commemorates the Goddess's sacrifice and the city's growth. Initially tied to expanding the Aspitome’s bubble.

**Key Events:** Opening Ceremony, Brewing Competition, Market Fair, Magical Demonstrations, Cultural Exhibitions, and a symbolic Ritual of Expansion.

### The Temple of the Nameless Goddess

**History and Construction:** Built with murals transported tile-by-tile from the Lundayell Empire, honoring the Nameless Goddess.

**Key Features:** The Great Hall, The Altar of Sacrifice, The Reflection Pool, and The Sanctuary.

**Role in the Community:** A center for worship, healing, and education about the Goddess’s teachings.

**Festivals and Rituals:** Festival of Remembrance, Ritual of Renewal, and Ceremonies of Healing.

### Key NPCs

**Mayor Elara Swiftwind:** Charismatic elf leader of the city council.

**Master Brewer Thrain Ironfoot:** Dwarf overseeing the brewing industry.

**Archmage Arin Lorien:** Human mage leading the Stormspire Academy.

**Captain Kira Stonefist:** Half-orc leader of the city guard.

**Priestess Liora Brightstar:** Human cleric of the Nameless Goddess.

### Plot Hooks and Encounters

**Sabotage in the Market:** Tainted goods causing illness among non-humans.

**Cult Recruitment:** Disappearances and new leaders preaching human supremacy.

**Magical Disturbances:** Natural disasters hinting at dark magic.

**Rescue Mission:** Freeing non-human captives from a secret hideout.

# Story Beats

## The Approach

### Agricultural and Industrial Landscape

## Agricultural Fields:

**Vibrant Farmlands:** As the players approach Mirathorn from the east, they first encounter expansive farmlands. The fields are lush and green, with crops like wheat, barley, and vegetables swaying gently in the breeze. Orchards with fruit-laden trees and vineyards with ripening grapes add to the pastoral scene.

**Livestock:** Grazing animals such as starling cows,Luminox sheep, and float goats dot the landscape. Herders and farmhands are seen tending to the animals and working the fields.

## Industrial Activity:

**Mills and Breweries:** Closer to the city, the players notice large windmills and watermills, their sails turning lazily in the wind. Breweries, including those of the renowned Wizard's Tower Brewing Co., are bustling with activity, preparing for the festival.

**Workshops and Smithies:** Workshops and blacksmith forges line the road, with artisans crafting tools, weapons, and everyday items. The rhythmic sound of hammers on anvils and the smell of smoke fill the air.

A mostly completed Giant Waterwheel can be seen from miles off, mounted next to the waterfalls pouring off the Stormspire Mountains.

## Population:

**Increased Traffic:** The road to Mirathorn is crowded with travelers, merchants, and farmers making their way to the festival. Carts loaded with goods, groups of pilgrims, and families with children create a lively and bustling atmosphere.

**Frontier Folk:** The players see a diverse mix of people from the frontier towns, each bringing their unique culture and goods to the city.

### City Fortification

## Walls and Gates:

**Ancient Walls:** Mirathorn is surrounded by ancient stone walls, built during the city's early days when threats were more frequent. The walls are tall and imposing, though they show signs of age and disuse, with ivy and moss creeping up their surfaces.

**Guard Towers:** Interspersed along the walls are guard towers, which now serve more as lookout points and symbolic reminders of the city's storied past than active defense posts.

**Main Gates:** The main gates of Mirathorn are grand and sturdy, made of reinforced wood and iron. The gates are flanked by statues of the city's founders, looking both welcoming and protective.

## OutTown

## Out Town/Campground:

**Temporary Structures:** Encircling the city are makeshift tents, stalls, and temporary shelters set up by the influx of visitors. This "out town" is a sprawling and chaotic mix of colorful tents, market stalls, and communal cooking areas.

**Vendors and Performers:** Street vendors sell food, drink, and trinkets, while performers entertain the crowds with music, acrobatics, and magic tricks. The atmosphere is festive and lively, albeit crowded and noisy.

**Communal Spaces:** Communal campfires and gathering spots are scattered throughout the area, where people share stories, food, and drink.

### Entry and Toll

## Gates and Toll:

**Crowded Gates:** The gates are heavily congested with people trying to enter the city. Guards manage the flow, checking goods and collecting tolls. The mood is a mix of excitement and frustration as people wait their turn.

**Steep Toll:** The toll to enter the city is steep, designed to manage the influx and ensure only those with means can enter easily. The high toll has caused discontent among many visitors. The toll is 15 gp, which is an exorbitant sum. The guards will explain that the toll will be going to ”fund the Waterwheel, a foundation for the future of Mirathron!”

## The Protest

**Protest Scene:** Outside the main gate, a group of Shepherd's Flock cultists is staging a protest. They are vocal and passionate, holding signs and chanting slogans against the toll, arguing it is unfair and exclusionary.

**Leaders and Agitators:** The protest is led by charismatic cult leaders who stir up the crowd, encouraging them to demand entry without the toll. Some protesters are distributing pamphlets with their message, trying to gain more support.

**Tension with Guards:** The city guards are visibly tense, trying to maintain order and prevent the protest from turning into a riot. Occasional shouts and scuffles break out, adding to the overall chaos.

### Shepherds

### Sleeper Cell of Cultists

**Leader:** **Name:** Lyra "The Serpent" Blackthorn

**Description:** Lyra Blackthorn is a charismatic and manipulative woman with a talent for stirring up emotions and leading crowds. She has long, black hair, often worn in intricate braids, and sharp, green eyes that seem to see through any facade. Lyra dresses in simple yet elegant clothing, blending in easily with the common folk but exuding an aura of authority.

**Background:** Lyra was a street performer and speaker known for her persuasive oratory skills. She was recruited by Eirik "The Wolf" Draven after he saw her potential to influence and manipulate people. Under the influence of the corrupting meat, Lyra's talents were twisted to serve the Shepherd's cause.

## Personality:

**Traits:** Charismatic, manipulative, and cunning.

**Quirks:** Often twirls a small, snake-shaped ring on her finger when speaking.

**Motivation:** To incite chaos and unrest, weakening Mirathorn from within.

### How These Will Be Used:

## Rallying Cry:

Lyra and her cell will use these arguments and slogans to rally the crowd, stirring emotions and uniting the people against the perceived injustice of the tolls. They will chant the slogans in unison, creating a powerful and unified front.

## Public Speeches:

Lyra will make impassioned speeches, weaving these arguments into her rhetoric to appeal to the crowd's sense of fairness, freedom, and unity. She will paint a vivid picture of the oppression they face and inspire them to stand up for their rights.

## Pamphlets and Signs:

The cultists will distribute pamphlets outlining their arguments and slogans. Signs and banners bearing the slogans will be prominently displayed throughout the protest, reinforcing their message visually.

## Encouraging Action:

The cell will encourage peaceful yet assertive actions, such as forming human chains to block the gates, chanting slogans loudly, and refusing to disperse until their demands are addressed. The goal is to create a situation where the city guards are forced to confront them, thereby escalating the tension and chaos.

When the players approach the gates, they will be walking up to a chaotic scene. There is a confrontation happening between a crowd of about 20 and the guards at the gate.  Lyra Blackthorn, a street performer and leatherworker from OutTown is leading the protest.
