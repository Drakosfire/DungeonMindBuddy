
Flesh Rat


Small, Beast, Corrupted, Chaotic Evil

![](images/0.jpg)




THESE MONSTROUS RATS, WARPED BY YEARS OF EXPOSURE TO TOXIC WASTE, HAVE DEVELOPED A SICKLY, HAIRLESS HIDE AND ARE PERPETUALLY DRENCHED IN A NOXIOUS, GOOEY SECRETION. KNOWN AS FLESH RATS, THESE VERMIN SKULK IN SEWER SYSTEMS, THEIR BODIES PULSING AND SEEPING WITH CAUSTIC ICHOR THAT BURNS EVERYTHING IT TOUCHES. DESPITE THEIR HORRIFIC APPEARANCE, THEY REMAIN NIMBLE AND QUICK, DARTING IN AND OUT OF SHADOWS TO SCRAPE BY ON WHATEVER SCRAPS THEY CAN FIND, OR ELSE FEED ON THOSE UNFORTUNATE ENOUGH TO STUMBLE UPON THEM.

## ACTIONS


Bite : ‘Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.


Caustic Ichor Spray : ‘The Flesh Rat can use its action to spray caustic ichor in a 10-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 5 (2d4) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The area becomes difficult terrain until cleaned.
