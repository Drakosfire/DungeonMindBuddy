The Elderwyld

Long ago, the gods lost to the other things. Beyond the planes, and the fae, and the gods was something else. First the Demons, Devils, and Chaos were allied with it. Growing their own domains on the complete besieging of all reality, growing with each god slain. Too late they understood,  too late to act, that Other thing, Other place, turned and consumed them too. All that was left was the Aspitome and it’s defenders. The nameless goddess gone, given their life to save everything. We remember her, we remember them. We remember our heritage.  Just as they forged a new land, and a new home for all, we will turn these Elderwylds to garden.

The Elderwyld is a continent on the edge of reality. Beyond the great waterfalls is something not Fae, or other planar, more like undefined. Mostly non-existence, mostly inert. But only mostly. A spark, the concentration of chaos and unreality. Something is born, crawls out. Sometimes it’s a giant fish, sometimes it’s a pack of wolves.

### Mirathorn Overview

#### You know you are approaching Mirathorn for a few reasons. The sound, you can hear it from at least a few kilometers.

### Origin and History:

Founded over 200 years ago by settlers fleeing the Lundayell Empire. They repaired an ancient airship

and discovered the continent, cutting off contact with the Empire to start anew.

### Location:

At the base of the Stormspire Peaks, by Lake Mirathorn, making it a strategic trade hub.

### Population:

Approximately 7000 residents of diverse races, including humans, dwarves, elves, gnomes, halflings, dragonborn, and a sprinkling of goblins and bugbears..

### Economy and Trade:

Major industries include brewing, craftsmanship, fishing, agriculture, and magical goods. It’s a key trade hub with access to rivers, mountain passes, and well-maintained roads.

#### Fantastic Livestock for the Elderwyld

#### 1. Luminox Sheep

**Description:** Luminox Sheep are fluffy, woolly creatures that emit a gentle, bioluminescent glow. Their wool, skin, and eyes all emit light, creating a beautiful and soothing spectacle at night. Their wool comes in a variety of colors, including soft blues, greens, and purples, each providing different intensities and hues of light.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Luminous Wool:** The wool is highly sought after for its natural glow. It is used to create luminescent textiles, perfect for clothing, blankets, and tapestries. These items are popular for both practical and decorative purposes.

**Example:** A luminescent cloak made from Luminox wool provides both warmth and light, ideal for travelers or nighttime activities.

**Luminox Milk:** The sheep also produce milk with a faint glow, believed to have mild healing properties. It is often used in special potions and recipes.

**Example:** A healing salve made from Luminox milk can soothe minor wounds and burns while providing a comforting glow.

**Bioluminescent Dye:** The wool can be processed to extract a dye that is used in various crafts and artworks, adding a magical touch to everyday items.

**Example:** Glow-in-the-dark tapestries depicting night scenes are popular in homes and inns.

#### 2. Tidal Turtles

**Description:** Tidal Turtles are small, amphibious creatures with shells that change color based on their mood and the surrounding environment. They thrive in both water and land habitats and have a unique ability to sense weather changes.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Turtle Shells:** The iridescent shells of Tidal Turtles are used for crafting decorative items, jewelry, and even lightweight armor.

**Example:** A pendant made from a Tidal Turtle shell changes color to reflect the wearer's emotions, making it a popular gift for lovers.

**Weather Predictors:** The turtles are often kept as natural weather predictors, their behavior and shell color changes providing early warnings for storms or other weather events.

**Example:** Farmers keep Tidal Turtles in their homes to predict rain and prepare their crops accordingly.

**Medicinal Salve:** The mucus secreted by Tidal Turtles is used in traditional remedies for treating skin conditions and burns.

**Example:** An ointment made from Tidal Turtle mucus helps heal and soothe sunburns and rashes.

#### 3. Starling Cows

**Description:** Starling Cows have hides adorned with starry patterns that shift and change, mimicking the night sky. They are gentle and calm creatures, known for their serene nature and celestial beauty.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Starry Milk:** Their milk has a faint, twinkling glow and is believed to enhance night vision and improve sleep quality. It is a key ingredient in many magical potions.

**Example:** A night vision potion made with Starling Cow milk is highly valued by adventurers and night-time sentries.

**Hide and Leather:** The starry hides are used to create stunning garments and accessories that captivate onlookers.

**Example:** A cloak made from Starling Cow hide appears to shimmer with a starry night sky, making it a favorite among nobles and performers.

**Celestial Cheese:** The cheese made from their milk has a unique, slightly sweet flavor and is served at special occasions and feasts.

**Example:** Celestial Cheese is a popular delicacy at festivals, known for its soothing effects and magical properties.

#### 4. Sunflower Ducks

**Description:** Sunflower Ducks have bright yellow feathers that resemble sunflower petals. They have a unique ability to photosynthesize, providing them with energy and a mild glow. They are friendly and often kept as pets and companions.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Glowing Feathers:** The feathers are used for decorative purposes and in crafting, adding a cheerful and bright element to various items.

**Example:** Sunflower Duck feather quills are popular for writing magical scrolls, believed to enhance the clarity of spells.

**Photosynthetic Eggs:** Their eggs have unique properties, often used in potions and alchemy for their energizing effects.

**Example:** An energizing elixir made from Sunflower Duck eggs is favored by travelers and those needing a boost of energy.

**Sunflower Oil:** Extracted from the ducks' skin, this oil is used in cooking and potions for its rejuvenating properties.

**Example:** Sunflower Oil is a key ingredient in rejuvenation potions, helping to restore vitality and health.

#### 5. Flutter Ferrets

**Description:** Flutter Ferrets are playful, curious creatures with butterfly-like wings. They are highly social and make delightful pets. Their wings come in a variety of colors and patterns, often sparkling in the sunlight.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Wing Scales:** The scales from their wings can be collected without harm and used in potions and enchantments for their light, airy properties.

**Example:** A potion of feather falling made with Flutter Ferret wing scales allows the drinker to descend gently from great heights.

**Companionship:** They are often kept as pets for their playful nature and are known to assist in finding hidden or lost objects.

**Example:** Flutter Ferrets are trained to help search for lost items, their keen sense of smell and playful nature making them excellent searchers.

**Magical Powder:** The dust from their wings can be used to create a magical powder that induces lightness and agility.

**Example:** An agility potion made from Flutter Ferret wing dust enhances the drinker's dexterity and reflexes.

#### 6. Float Goats

**Description:** Float Goats are whimsical, goat-like creatures with an extraordinary ability to walk on water. Their lightweight bodies and wide, webbed hooves enable them to stride effortlessly across the surface of lakes, rivers, and ponds. Float Goats have soft, curly fur that shimmers with a pearlescent glow, and their small, delicate wings aid them in maintaining balance. They are highly social animals, known for their playful and curious nature, making them both endearing companions and valuable livestock.

## Animal Products and Uses:

**Water-Walking Wool:** The fur of Float Goats is incredibly soft and lightweight, making it ideal for crafting airy garments and textiles. This wool has natural insulating properties, providing warmth without weight.

**Example:** Cloaks and garments made from Float Goat wool are popular among travelers and adventurers, offering comfort and mobility without added bulk.

**Buoyant Milk:** The milk of Float Goats has a unique, slightly effervescent quality, believed to have mild buoyant properties when consumed. It is often used in both culinary and magical recipes.

**Example:** Float Goat milk is a key ingredient in potions that grant temporary buoyancy or lightness, allowing users to float more easily in water.

**Floating Cheese:** The cheese made from their milk is light and airy, with a distinct, delicate flavor. It is highly prized in gourmet cooking and magical cuisine.

**Example:** Floating Cheese is a delicacy served at high-end feasts and gatherings, known for its unique texture and enchanting taste.

**Buoyancy Dust:** The dust that collects from their wings has minor buoyancy properties and is used in various alchemical concoctions and enchantments.

**Example:** Buoyancy Dust is a crucial component in crafting potions of water walking and other spells that enhance one's ability to float or swim.

**Companionship and Work:** Float Goats are often kept as companions and beasts of burden. Their ability to walk on water makes them ideal for transporting goods across bodies of water and navigating difficult terrain.

**Example:** Float Goats are commonly used in the Elderwyld to transport goods across lakes and rivers, providing a unique and efficient method of travel and transportation.

## Cultural Significance:

**Symbols of Joy:** Float Goats are considered symbols of joy and lightness of being. They often appear in festivals and celebrations, where their playful antics and water-walking abilities delight both children and adults.

**Spiritual Companions:** Some communities believe that Float Goats have a spiritual connection to water and nature, often featuring in local myths and serving as companions to clerics and druids who seek harmony with the natural world.

## Quirks of the Elderwyld

### Unit Name: Reaches

## Definition:

One Reach is equivalent to 8 kilometers (5 miles).

## Usage:

**Mapping and Travel:** "It's about 4 Reaches to the next village."

**Official Documents:** "The borders of Mirathorn extend 20 Reaches from the city center."

**Navigation:** "Follow the river for 2 Reaches, then head east."

### Narrative Integration

**Background:** The term "Reaches" originated from the early days of settlement, when every journey beyond the safety of the initial outposts felt like reaching into the unknown. Over time, as the settlers expanded their territory and established more secure routes, the once dangerous "Reaches" became a measure of progress and exploration.

**Cultural Significance:** In the lore of Mirathorn, tales are often told of the first pioneers who measured their progress in Reaches, facing peril and hardship to carve out a safe haven from the wild lands. The term now symbolizes the bravery and determination of those early settlers.

## Examples in Context:

**Travel:** "It's 6 Reaches to the ancient ruins."

**Trade:** "Merchants cover about 10 Reaches in a day's travel with their caravans."

**Exploration:** "The new map extends our known world by another 15 Reaches."

This unit of measurement not only serves a practical purpose but also embeds a sense of history and accomplishment within the narrative of your world.

## Approach to Mirathorn

### 1. Agricultural and Industrial Landscape

## Agricultural Fields:

**Vibrant Farmlands:** As the players approach Mirathorn from the east, they first encounter expansive farmlands. The fields are lush and green, with crops like wheat, barley, and vegetables swaying gently in the breeze. Orchards with fruit-laden trees and vineyards with ripening grapes add to the pastoral scene.

**Livestock:** Grazing animals such as cows, sheep, and goats dot the landscape. Herders and farmhands are seen tending to the animals and working the fields.

**Floatgoats:** Some farms have floatgoats, the whimsical creatures used for labor and transportation, adding a touch of magical charm to the countryside.

## Industrial Activity:

**Mills and Breweries:** Closer to the city, the players notice large windmills and watermills, their sails turning lazily in the wind. Breweries, including those of the renowned Wizard's Tower Brewing Co., are bustling with activity, preparing for the festival.

**Workshops and Smithies:** Workshops and blacksmith forges line the road, with artisans crafting tools, weapons, and everyday items. The rhythmic sound of hammers on anvils and the smell of smoke fill the air.

## Population:

**Increased Traffic:** The road to Mirathorn is crowded with travelers, merchants, and farmers making their way to the festival. Carts loaded with goods, groups of pilgrims, and families with children create a lively and bustling atmosphere.

**Frontier Folk:** The players see a diverse mix of people from the frontier towns, each bringing their unique culture and goods to the city.

### 2. City Fortification

## Walls and Gates:

**Ancient Walls:** Mirathorn is surrounded by ancient stone walls, built during the city's early days when threats were more frequent. The walls are tall and imposing, though they show signs of age and disuse, with ivy and moss creeping up their surfaces.

**Guard Towers:** Interspersed along the walls are guard towers, which now serve more as lookout points and symbolic reminders of the city's storied past than active defense posts.

**Main Gates:** The main gates of Mirathorn are grand and sturdy, made of reinforced wood and iron. The gates are flanked by statues of the city's founders, looking both welcoming and protective.

### 3. Outskirts and Campgrounds

## Out Town/Campground:

**Temporary Structures:** Encircling the city are makeshift tents, stalls, and temporary shelters set up by the influx of visitors. This "out town" is a sprawling and chaotic mix of colorful tents, market stalls, and communal cooking areas.

**Vendors and Performers:** Street vendors sell food, drink, and trinkets, while performers entertain the crowds with music, acrobatics, and magic tricks. The atmosphere is festive and lively, albeit crowded and noisy.

**Communal Spaces:** Communal campfires and gathering spots are scattered throughout the area, where people share stories, food, and drink.

### 4. Entry and Toll

## Gates and Toll:

**Crowded Gates:** The gates are heavily congested with people trying to enter the city. Guards manage the flow, checking goods and collecting tolls. The mood is a mix of excitement and frustration as people wait their turn.

**Steep Toll:** The toll to enter the city is steep, designed to manage the influx and ensure only those with means can enter easily. The high toll has caused discontent among many visitors.

## Shepherd's Flock Protest:

**Protest Scene:** Outside the main gate, a group of Shepherd's Flock cultists is staging a protest. They are vocal and passionate, holding signs and chanting slogans against the toll, arguing it is unfair and exclusionary.

**Leaders and Agitators:** The protest is led by charismatic cult leaders who stir up the crowd, encouraging them to demand entry without the toll. Some protesters are distributing pamphlets with their message, trying to gain more support.

**Tension with Guards:** The city guards are visibly tense, trying to maintain order and prevent the protest from turning into a riot. Occasional shouts and scuffles break out, adding to the overall chaos.

### Scene for Players:

As the players approach Mirathorn, they are greeted by a vibrant and bustling scene of farmlands, workshops, and a diverse population on the move. The ancient city walls loom ahead, a testament to the city's rich history. The out town around the city is a chaotic but festive mix of tents and stalls, alive with the sounds and smells of a massive gathering.

The main gates are a bottleneck of people and carts, with guards working diligently to manage the crowd. Amidst the throng, the players notice the protest by the Shepherd's Flock, adding a layer of tension and urgency to the scene. The high toll to enter the city is a point of contention, and the players may find themselves drawn into the unfolding drama as they navigate their way into Mirathorn for the Festival of Expansion.
