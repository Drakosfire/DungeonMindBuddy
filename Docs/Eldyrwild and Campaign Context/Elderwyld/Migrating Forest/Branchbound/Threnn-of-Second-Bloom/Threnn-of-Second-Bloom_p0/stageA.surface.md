
## Threnn-of-Second-Bloom


Medium, humanoid (half-elf), chaotic neutral

![](images/0.jpg)




Threnn-of-Second-Bloom is a half-elf from the Branchbound lineage, marked by their canopy-born heritage. With a slim frame and bark-like scar tissue, they embody the forest's impermanence while struggling to let go of attachments. Threnn's reflective amber eyes and moss-dyed leathers blend them seamlessly into their woodland home, but their refusal to move on from the overdue-to-be-abandoned Second Bloom has marked them as a defector. Though they are quiet and observant, their commitment to protection and discomfort with authority make them a complex figure, driven by a fear of unfinished business rather than mortality itself.

## ACTIONS


## Multitattack


Threnn makes two attacks with their leafblade shortsword or longbow.


## Leafblade Shortsword


Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage. Once per turn, if attacking a creature adjacent to foliage, roots, or natural terrain, deal an additional 2 (1d4) damage. Damage: 1d6+4 Range: Melee


## Longbow


Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage. If attacking from partial or full concealment, the first hit each combat has advantage. Damage: 1d8+3 Range: Range 150/600


## BONUS ACTIONS


## Canopy Step


Threnn moves up to 20 ft without provoking opportunity attacks, provided they start or end adjacent to natural terrain, roots, or trees. Recharge 4–6. Recharge 4–6


## TRAITS


## Root-Sense


Threnn has advantage on Perception checks to detect movement through natural terrain and cannot be surprised by creatures moving through plants, roots, or canopy.


## Witness Seed (Passive)


Threnn is affected by the Witness Seed at all times. Effects are detailed in The Witness Seed Document.
