## The Witness Seed

Branchbound Binding Mechanism

### What It Is

A Witness Seed is a living Fey construct—part plant, part memory, part covenant.

It is implanted into select Branchbound members to:

Anchor cultural continuity

Record decision-patterns

Ensure migration knowledge survives loss

Most seeds are stable.

Threnn’s is not.

### Normal Lifecycle (Branchbound Ideal)

**Implantation:
** Seed is placed beneath the collarbone during a rite. Painful but controlled.

**Witness Phase:
** Seed records choices made at thresholds: crossings, abandonments, refusals.

**Completion:
** At death or voluntary planting, the seed roots and grows into a **New Bloom**—a minor forest node seeded with Branchbound values.

This is considered a successful end.

### Threnn’s Deviation

Threnn resisted internally.

Not violently. Not consciously.

The seed never synchronized fully.

As a result:

It records **too much
**

It reacts to stress

It has not committed to an end-state

The seed is waiting.

### Mechanical Seed Clock (GM Tool)

Track the Witness Seed with a 6-step clock. Advance it when:

Threnn is critically wounded

Powerful magic is used nearby

Threnn experiences fear, grief, or forced obedience

The party enters major liminal spaces (swamp thresholds, planar scars)

## Stages:

Dormant — no visible effect

Warm — faint bark-veins, dreams intensify

Listening — forest entities notice Threnn

Pulling — terrain subtly shifts toward Threnn

Claiming — seed begins asserting decisions

Rooting — irreversible transition

### If Threnn Dies Violently

This is the catastrophic branch you like—and it’s good.

If Threnn is killed during a **failed assault on the swamp** and the corpse is exposed to:

Abyssal corruption

Flesh-growth rituals

The “dark god meat” phenomenon

Then the Witness Seed is **hijacked**.

Result:

The seed roots anyway

But what grows is not a Branchbound bloom

It becomes a **corrupted forest node**, blending Fey memory with abyssal hunger

This is not a new forest.

This is a wrong one.

It will:

Remember the party

Mimic Branchbound traversal logic

Spread flesh-root hybrids

Act as a foothold for something that understands growth but not restraint

This is a long-tail consequence, not an immediate boss.

### If Threnn Plants Themself Voluntarily

This is the bittersweet outcome.

A small, stable forest node forms.
 It is quiet.
 It does not expand aggressively.
 It remembers kindness accurately.

Branchbound culture would call this “success.”

Threnn would call it peace.

### Why Threnn Doesn’t Fit the Branchbound

Branchbound survival depends on letting go.

Threnn cannot stop caring specifically.

They remember individual animals.
 They name paths.
 They revisit places.

They want continuity, not just motion.

The Witness Seed noticed.

And now the forest is waiting to see what the party does with that flaw.
