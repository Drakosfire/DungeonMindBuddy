# The Migrating Forest — Keyed Hex Expansion

Designed for rapid table use. Each hex below provides:

What the forest is doing right now

One immediate pressure or choice

One strong sensory anchor

Use these entries as living scenes. They should bend under player action.

## 1. The Root Argument (Central Triple Spiral)

Three immense root spirals dominate this hex, coiling inward as if bracing against an unseen force. The ground subtly shifts every few minutes, not enough to knock someone down, but enough to make footing feel temporary. Water pooled at the base of each spiral rotates in different directions, carrying leaves in slow, deliberate orbits. The forest here is actively deciding how to redistribute its own mass.

**Pressure / Choice:** Lingering, camping, or using divination draws the forest’s attention. Leave quickly and unnoticed, or stay to learn something meaningful at the cost of being remembered.

**Sensory Anchor:** Whispered voices carry unnaturally far; shouted words feel swallowed by bark and soil.

## 2. The Shallow Spiral Pool

A single root spiral cups a basin of dark green water that never fully settles. Leaves and debris are pulled toward its center, vanish briefly, then resurface at odd angles. Reflections show different canopies, older skies, or storm-lit horizons that do not match the present moment. The forest here is shedding and reassigning memory.

**Pressure / Choice:** Submerge something to gain displacement or insight, knowing it may not return nearby. Or avoid the pool entirely and forgo a rare shortcut.

**Sensory Anchor:** The water smells faintly of rain that hasn’t fallen yet.

## 3. The Canopy Paths

Clear, walkable paths thread between thick trunks and low branches, curving naturally without straight lines. Fallen limbs form perfect steps and handholds, as if placed intentionally. No one is visible, but movement here feels guided, corrected gently before mistakes happen. The forest is allowing passage without committing to it.

**Pressure / Choice:** Travel lightly and respectfully to gain safe movement, or push speed and damage the terrain, causing the paths to erase themselves behind you.

**Sensory Anchor:** Footsteps land exactly where they should, even when eyes are closed.

## 4. The Fallen Spine

A colossal tree lies split and hollowed, its trunk forming a ribbed corridor through the hex. Roots claw upward like broken fingers, and younger growth crowds around the corpse. Sound carries strangely through the hollow wood, making distance and direction difficult to judge. The forest uses this place as a funnel.

**Pressure / Choice:** Pass through quickly and risk ambush, or detour and lose time while something hunts nearby.

**Sensory Anchor:** Every noise echoes twice, once real and once delayed.

## 5. The White-Leaved Tree

One immaculate tree stands apart, its pale leaves stark against the surrounding green. Its bark is smooth, unscarred by storm or age, and nothing grows at its base. Wind dies within its shadow. The forest here is holding something perfectly still.

**Pressure / Choice:** Act within its shade for precise, predictable outcomes, or step away and accept uncertainty.
DC 15 Wis save or else settle in for a good long sit. Sitting for 10 minutes counts as a short rest for free.

**Sensory Anchor:** The air is cool and unmoving, like a held breath.

## 6. The Moss Memory Ring

Trees form a rough circle around a moss-carpeted clearing. The moss is warm, springy, and inviting. Old footprints appear and fade within minutes, as if the ground is rehearsing the past. The forest here offers comfort it cannot sustain.

**Pressure / Choice:** Rest and recover at the cost of emotional exposure, or move on carrying fatigue but keeping distance from your own memories.

**Sensory Anchor:** The moss smells like places once called home.

## 7. The Storm-Scar Canopy

Shattered crowns and glass-fused branches mark where magical storms tore through the canopy. Leaves carry a blue-gray tint, and shallow pools glow faintly after dusk. The forest is still healing, and not gently.

**Pressure / Choice:** Use magic here and risk wild side effects, or rely on mundane solutions while predators grow bolder.

**Sensory Anchor:** Hair lifts with static even under clear skies.

## 8. The Wet Root Lattice

A web of thick roots crosses shallow, slow-moving water, forming natural bridges. The water is clear enough to reveal movement below but deep enough to conceal danger. This is a gathering point the forest has not yet erased.

**Pressure / Choice:** Harvest resources quickly and quietly, or linger and draw attention from beneath the surface.

**Sensory Anchor:** Water laps softly against roots like distant applause.

## 9. The Deadfall Cluster

Snapped trees lie tangled together, bark stripped and wood scored. The smell of iron hangs faintly in the air. Something large passed through violently, and the forest has not finished cleaning up.

**Pressure / Choice:** Investigate for clues or salvage, knowing predators may return, or move on before the site is reclaimed.

**Sensory Anchor:** Flies buzz despite the cool shade.

## 10. The Quiet Canopy

This hex is calm in a way that feels deliberate. Birds avoid it. Insects are scarce. The canopy is intact, but the forest feels watchful rather than empty. It is a pause, not a refuge.

**Pressure / Choice:** Rest briefly and remain alert, or settle in too comfortably and invite correction.

**Sensory Anchor:** The absence of sound presses on the ears.

## 11. The Broken Crossing

Roots and fallen trunks once formed a natural passage here, now carefully dismantled. The break is intentional, not the work of decay. Someone wanted pursuit to stop.

**Pressure / Choice:** Repair the crossing and announce your presence, or leave it broken and remain unseen.

**Sensory Anchor:** Freshly snapped wood still weeps sap.

## 12. The Edgeward Drift

The forest thins here. Roots float instead of anchoring, and water becomes more common than soil. The air smells faintly sour. This hex faces the swampward future the forest is being pushed toward.

**Pressure / Choice:** Push through quickly to escape the forest’s influence, or linger and risk contact with forces the forest cannot control.

**Sensory Anchor:** The ground squelches softly, even where it looks solid.
