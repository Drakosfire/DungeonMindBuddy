CAPTAIN LYSANDRA IRONVEIL (LEVEL-UP VERSION)


Medium, humanoid, human, lawful good

![](images/0.jpg)




RESPECTED AND RALLYING, CAPTAIN LYSANDRA IRONVEIL STANDS AT THE FORE OF THE BATTLEFIELD WITH HER BREASTPLATE POLISHED AND CLOAK SNAPPING IN THE WIND, HER PRESENCE A BEACON OF DISCIPLINE AND HOPE. A MASTER OF TACTICAL MANEUVER AND A SEASONED INSPIRATIONAL LEADER, SHE EXPERTLY BLENDS SWIFT SWORDPLAY WITH BATTLEFIELD ORDERS THAT TURN THE TIDE OF BATTLE, COORDINATING HER ALLIES WITH PIERCING SHOUTS AND CALCULATED STRIKES. HER INDOMITABLE WILL, FORGED THROUGH THE DEFENSE OF HER CITY AND THE TRIBULATIONS OF COMMAND, GRANTS HER NEAR-UNSHAKABLE RESOLVE—EVEN IN THE PRESENCE OF THE MOST HARROWING FOES—AND AN AURA THAT STEELS HER COMPANIONS AGAINST AMBUSH AND FEAR. UNDER LYSANDRA'S VIGILANT GAZE, CHAOS BECOMES COHESION, AND FALTERING HEARTS ARE STEADIED FOR VICTORY.
Armor Class : 16
Hit Points: 52 Hit Dice : 7d8+14

## ACTIONS


Multiattack : 'Lysandra makes two melee weapon attacks with her longsword.


Longsword : ‘Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) if used with two hands.


Commanding Shout (Recharge 5–6) : ‘Lysandra issues a powerful battlefield order. Up to 3 allies within 60 feet that can hear her may immediately (no reaction required): move up to half their speed or make one weapon attack of their choice.


Leadership Presence (3/Short Rest, Bonus Action) : ‘As a bonus action, Lysandra may inspire an ally within 30 ft. That ally gains 1d6 to add to their next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. This die may be rolled after the roll but before results are declared.


Tactical Strike : ‘Once per turn, when Lysandra hits with a weapon attack, she deals an extra 1d8 damage. If an ally she inspired hits the same target this round, her damage bonus becomes 2d8 instead.


Steadfast Will : ‘Lysandra has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened. When she succeeds on such a save, she can immediately end one such effect on an ally within 30 feet of her.
