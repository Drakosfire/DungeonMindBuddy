# [IDEA] Explore a connected campaign arc from a World object

**Captured:** 2026-11-14  
**Status:** Product goalpost, not scheduled or ready to dispatch.  
**Owners to consult:** Buddy Graph and retrieval for exploration; ingestion and the knowledge-contract owners only if evidence shows gaps at those boundaries.

## Goalpost

Start at a meaningful World object and follow what happened across sessions. A
moving, acting place should be explorable as a place, as a participant, and as
the subject of an unfolding story. The player should get a readable sequence
with reopenable citations, not a flat list of every event in the same sessions.

## Witness: the Wandering Forest

Longmont Campaign 2 Sessions 15–20 form the migration arc, and Sessions 16–18
cover the crossing. The graph should distinguish the persistent Forest from
this particular migration, and distinguish spatial containment (an ambush at
Fallen Spine, which is part of the Forest) from story membership (the diversion
at Mossford, outside the Forest). The same-session Inn subplot is a negative
control. Later evidence about Thrin and the Witness Seed is a follow-through,
not another event *inside* the Forest. Preserve source order separately from
fictional order and retain who reported a claim, especially when cause is
uncertain.

The retained November 13 Elderwyld graph, `rev:9a457bfed6bc517af83a7a719644970c`,
had 242 Event-typed entities cited to Sessions 15–20 combined, but no Event
directly incident to the Forest or to any of its six linked sublocations.
The Thinking Tree was unconnected, the Mossford diversion only pointed at the
campaign timeline, and the first seed mention and later named item were
separate objects. These are observations at that pin, not claims about current
serving state. The [Connected Events strip](https://github.com/Drakosfire/DungeonMindBuddy/pull/3321)
is a direct-neighbor view, not an arc traversal.

## What would count as progress

- From the Forest, recover cited witnesses for the Thinking Tree,
  the Fallen Spine encounter, the Mossford diversion, and the later
  Thrin/Seed disclosure. Distinguish where each event happened, why it belongs
  in this view, and which claims are reported rather than established.
- Reopen each citation at the pinned World, graph revision, source revision and
  admission view. Exclude unrelated same-session material, and do not assume
  that GM design references were admitted or played.
- Diagnose each miss before choosing a fix: source not admitted, object missing
  or split, relationship only present in prose, path not retrieved, or path not
  presented. Compare strong passage search with bounded graph traversal at a
  matched evidence budget before prescribing a new storage model. Keep writer
  output out of that retrieval comparison.

A parent Event is a candidate representation, not a schema decision. This
goalpost does not authorize hand-patching Elderwyld, re-ingesting the corpus,
adding direct edges to every event, or changing the active roadmap.
