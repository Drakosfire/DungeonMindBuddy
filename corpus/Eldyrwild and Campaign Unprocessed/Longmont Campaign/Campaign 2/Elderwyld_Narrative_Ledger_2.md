## I. Cosmological & Thematic Frame

Eldyrwyld exists at the unstable edge of expanded reality, preserved by the Aspitone and the divine sacrifice of Erathis, now known only as the Nameless Goddess. Mirathorn stands as a rebel city that escaped authoritarian utopia, now facing the risk of becoming something equally rigid under pressure.

**Campaign 2 Core Themes**
- Unity under strain
- Corruption disguised as fairness
- Institutions failing quietly
- Charisma without accountability
- Choice versus comfort

## V. The Dustwalker Paradox

### Revised Canon Understanding

**Confirmed Observations**
- Dustwalker manifested simultaneously in multiple locations
- Both instances were physically restrained or killed
- Each dissolution left behind cooked-meat residue
- Elimination does not end Dustwalker’s activity

**Conclusion**
Dustwalker is **not a singular being**.

He functions as:
- A distributed avatar
- A ritual interface
- Or a reusable vessel animated by an external will

**Rule of the World:**  
Killing Dustwalker is allowed.  
Stopping Dustwalker is not achieved by killing.
