MOONLIT MERINGUES


Medium, Construct, Fae, Neutral

![](images/0.jpg)




MOONLIT MERINGUES ARE MAGICAL CONFECTIONS ANIMATED BY ETHEREAL FAE FLOUR, GLOWING WITH A SILVERY LUMINESCENCE. ELEGANTLY STACKED IN A SPIRAL FORMATION, THEY MOVE WITH A SURPRISING GRACE, EXUDING AN AURA OF TRANQUIL MOONLIGHT. THEIR FRAGRANCE IS A BLEND OF FRESH BRIOCHE AND COOL MINT, ALLURING YET SERENE.


Armor Class : 12


Hit Points: 40 Hit Dice : 5d8
Speed: walk : 30

## ACTIONS


Whispering Frost : 'The Moonlit Meringues exhale a chilling breath infused with frostmint. Each creature in a 15-foot cone must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) cold damage and have their speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of the Meringue's next turn.


## LEGENDARY ACTIONS


The Moonlit Meringues can take 1 legendary action, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Moonlit Meringues regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.


Gravity Tumble : ‘The Moonlit Meringues collapse into a pile and then reform, instantly moving up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. All creatures within 5 feet of the new location must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 5 (1d10) bludgeoning damage from the impact of the reverberating confection.’
