# 🏛️ Round One – Trials of Steel

## Colosseum of the Storm | Qualifier Ruleset

## 💠 Overview:

A test of strength, finesse, and resolve. Competing teams face off in 1v1 wrestling duels within the ring. Each match is resolved when one combatant is knocked **out of the ring** or otherwise rendered unable to continue.

Teams may have up to **6 members**.

Duels occur **one at a time**. A team is eliminated when all members have lost a match.

Winners of each duel remain in the queue; defeated combatants are out for the round.

## 📜 Mechanics:

**Victory Condition**: Knock your opponent out of the ring (via shoving, grappling, or knockdown).

**Initiative**: Roll for initiative at the start of each duel.

**No Weapons or Magic**: Grappling, brawling, and clever tactics only.

### ⚔️ Knockdown Actions:

Choose one per turn:

**Grapple & Shove Combo**:

Step 1: Grapple → Athletics vs. Acrobatics or Athletics (standard rules).

Step 2: If grappled, a successful **DC 15 Athletics** check can throw opponent from ring.

**Takedown Strike**:

Roll to Hit (unarmed strike or improvised move). On hit, roll Athletics.

If **DC 14** is met, opponent is knocked prone. Follow-up shove may be attempted next round.

**Special Move**: Some NPCs have signature takedowns (see below).

## 🔧 Environmental Mechanics:

The ring may shift unexpectedly:

**Unsteady Ground**: Each round, roll 1d6. On a 6, footing shifts—DC 12 Acrobatics check to stay upright.

## ✅ Pass Criteria:

The **last team with fighters still standing** wins.

If any PCs are knocked out, they do **not** return this round.

Spectators cheer bonus antics, improvisation, or dramatic flair—DM may offer Advantage for style.

## 🛡️ Challenger Team: The Steel Fangs

A feared and seasoned team known for their unorthodox tactics and fierce arena presence.

### 1️⃣ Gorrun the Ironhide (Minotaur Brute)

**Role**: Enforcer

**Knockdown Action**: Ironhide Charge – DC 16 Strength save or be shoved back 10 feet & prone.

**Stay Standing**: Athletics +6 | Acrobatics +3 → DC 15

### 2️⃣ Seraphine "Bladewind" D’Lorien (Elven Duelist)

**Role**: Swift Striker

**Knockdown Action**: Precision Disarm – DC 15 Dexterity vs opponent’s Strength or Acrobatics.

**Stay Standing**: Acrobatics +6 → DC 14

### 3️⃣ Rurik "Stonebreaker" Thorne (Dwarven Shieldmaster)

**Role**: The Wall

**Knockdown Action**: Shield Bash – DC 15 Strength; failure by 5+ = stunned 1 round.

**Stay Standing**: Athletics +7 → DC 15

### 4️⃣ Valera Stormstrike (Tiefling Spear-Dancer) 25 HP

**Role**: Tactical Agile Fighter

**Knockdown Action**: Sweeping Hook – DC 14 Dexterity vs Strength or Acrobatics.

**Stay Standing**: Acrobatics +5 | Athletics +4 → DC 14

### 5️⃣ "Laughing" Yorrik Grent (Human Pit Fighter)

**Role**: Wild Grappler

**Knockdown Action**: Dirty Trick Trip – Deception DC 14 vs Insight, then Athletics DC 15 to drag down.

**Stay Standing**: Athletics +6 | Acrobatics +3 → DC 15

### 6️⃣ Morvian the Ashen (Half-Orc Monk)

**Role**: Acrobat/Counterstriker

**Knockdown Action**: Reaping Throw – Acrobatics DC 16 vs. opponent’s Strength.

**Stay Standing**: Acrobatics +7 → DC 16
