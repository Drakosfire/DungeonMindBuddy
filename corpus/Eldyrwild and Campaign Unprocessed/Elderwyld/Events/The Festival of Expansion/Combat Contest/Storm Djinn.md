THE STORM DJINN SURGES ACROSS THE BATTLEFIELD AS A DANCING COLUMN OF WIND AND ROARING ELECTRICITY. ITS SHAPE IS NEVER STABLE—ONE MOMENT IT'S A TOWERING NOBLE, THE NEXT A CACKLING SPECTER WOVEN FROM LIGHTNING AND LAUGHTER. THE DJINN RELISHES CHAOS, CONJURING ILLUSIONS TO BEWILDER FOES AND AMUSE ONLOOKERS. ITS ATTACKS ARE THEATRICAL, STRIKING WITH RADIANT BOLTS AND SENDING THE UNWARY TUMBLING ON FAKE GROUND. WHETHER HERO OR HINDRANCE, THE DJINN ADDS A CRACKLING JOLT OF SPECTACLE TO ANY CONTEST.
Armor Class : 13
Hit Points: 65 Hit Dice : 10d8+20

## ACTIONS


Illusory Double : 'At the start of each of its turns, the Storm Djinn creates a shimmering duplicate of itself. The first attack roll made against the Djinn each round is made at disadvantage, as attackers are unsure which form is real.


Lightning Lash : ‘Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 2d8+4 lightning damage, and sparks leap to a second creature of the Djinn’s choice within 10 feet of the target, dealing 1d6 lightning damage to that creature.


Stormburst (Recharge 5-6) : ‘The Djinn unleashes a howling sphere of storm energy in a 20-foot radius centered on itself. Each creature in the area must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d8 lightning damage and being pushed 10 feet away from the Djinn on a failed save, or half as much damage and no push on a successful save.


Illusory Ground (1/Short Rest) : ‘The Djinn alters a 20-foot square of terrain within 60 feet, making it look solid and safe. Any creature entering or starting its turn in this area must succeed on a DC 14 Acrobatics check or fall prone, possibly crashing through illusory ground or slipping comically.
