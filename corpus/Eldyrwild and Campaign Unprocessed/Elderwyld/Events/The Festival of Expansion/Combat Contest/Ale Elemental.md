BORN FROM CENTURIES OF TAVERN REVELRY AND ERRANT MAGIC, THE ALE ELEMENTAL LURCHES THROUGH THE WORLD AS A SLOSHING, FOAM-BODIED PRANKSTER. KEGS AND RIVERBANKS QUIVER AT ITS PASSING, AND FEW CAN WITHSTAND THE CHAOS OF ITS FROTHY ERUPTIONS AND INTOXICATING AURA. THOUGH NOT THE MIGHTIEST OF ELEMENTALS, IT LEAVES A TRAIL OF LAUGHTER, CONFUSION, SUDS, AND BRUISED EGOS. MANY AN ADVENTURER HAS SLIPPED, STAGGERED, OR FOUND THEIR VISION CLOUDED BY THE LEGENDARY ALE ELEMENTAL'S UNPREDICTABLE ANTICS.
Armor Class : 13

## ACTIONS


Sudsy Slam : ‘Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6+3) bludgeoning damage and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone as frothy ale gushes beneath their feet.


Foam Splash (Recharge 5-6) : 'The elemental belches forth a cone of frothy, sticky ale. Each creature in a 15-foot cone must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the creature falls prone and is soaked in ale, gaining disadvantage on Stealth checks for 1 minute. On a success, the creature remains standing but suffers the Stealth penalty.


Drunken Aura : 'A 10-foot aura of intoxicating vapor surrounds the elemental. At the start of each creature's turn in the aura, roll a d4: on a 1 or 2, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their turn


Ale Surge : ‘When the Ale Elemental is hit by a melee attack, it may burst in a spray of ale (no action required). The attacker must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be blinded (beer in the eyes!) until the end of their next turn.
