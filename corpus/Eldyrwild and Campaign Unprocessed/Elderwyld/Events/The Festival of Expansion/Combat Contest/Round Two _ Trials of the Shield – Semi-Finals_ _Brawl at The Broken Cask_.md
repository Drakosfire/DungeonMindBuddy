🛡 Trials of the Shield – Final Round: "Brawl at The Broken Cask"

**🥊 SEMI-FINAL MATCHES (Day 2 of the Festival)**

### Match 1: The Glorious Mess vs. The Whistlecranks

#### 🍻 The Glorious Mess (Team of 3)

**Bramble the Barback** – Half-orc monk, dual-wields broomsticks and flips off walls.

**Trixie Jinx** – Gnome rogue, throws silverware and uses sleight-of-hand to “steal” the floorboards.

**Durbin Groat** – Human bard, drunk and aggressive, inspires the team with off-key tavern songs.

#### 🛠 The Whistlecranks (Team of 3)

Steam-powered goblin engineers from the Foundry Quarter.

Wear ale-powered exo-bracers made of enchanted barrel hoops.

Use magically fizzing soda grenades and reinforced tankard-shields.

📣 **Highlight Moment:** Trixie throws a napkin that turns into a net midair while Durbin hits a perfect tankard slam with a thunderous belch.

### Match 2: The Ironmeads vs. Wildflower

#### 🪓 The Ironmeads (Team of 2)

**Grondar & Grumli**, dwarf twins, former pub security.

Fight in perfect sync, treat broken stools as paired shields.

Shout conflicting tactics mid-fight: “Shield wall!” “No, flank 'em!”

#### 🌿 Wildflower (Solo Fighter)

Half-elven druid/barbarian, fights barefoot with thorn-wrapped knuckles.

Wild Shapes into a bear with floral tattoos that glow during rage.

Previously disarmed the Ironmeads with a bar rug and a lantern chain.

📣 **Highlight Moment:** Wildflower kicks a stool into the air, then Bear Forms mid-jump and crashes down like a cannonball onto the twins.

### Match 3: The Bucklers vs. Bellowbill

#### 🛡 The Bucklers (Team of 4 halfling city guard recruits)

Train with teamwork-focused maneuvers.

Use serving trays and napkin ropes to trip opponents.

Ridiculously good at tumbling under furniture.

#### 🐗 Bellowbill (Solo)

Giant boar-shaped warforged with tavern-sign armor.

Can’t speak, but snorts and stomps to intimidate.

Charges through tables like a living wrecking ball.

📣 **Highlight Moment:** Bucklers pile up behind a counter, then vault simultaneously, trying to trip Bellowbill with a tablecloth. It works—for 6 seconds.

Each match has its own **fanbase** in the crowd, creating chants and waving enchanted signs. Players might hear:

“Mess it up, Glorious!”

“Twins don’t lose!”

“Bill! Bill! Bill!”

**Time:** 3:30 PM
 **Location:** Colosseum of the Storm
 **Format:** Team vs. Solo – Brawl with improvised weapons only
 **Tone:** Chaotic, raucous, and legendary—like a cross between pro wrestling and a gladiator match

### 🎖 The Contestants:

#### 💥 Team Gritspike

**Bonogo** – Bugbear Rogue: agile, sneaky, uses found objects as lethal implements.

**Baergrom** – Dwarf Fighter: tanky and tenacious, uses strength and terrain to his advantage.

**Ogonob** – Fey Spirit: copy of Bonogo, playful and unpredictable, mimics ally tactics with fae flair.

#### 🪓 Versus: Trainer Hagan Rook

**Human, Level 8 Barbarian/Druid**, last year’s champion and current Trainer of the Guard.

Calm, confident, and courteous—until the brawl begins.

**Combat Style:** Brutally efficient. Uses **rage**, **wildshape (Dire Wolf or Brown Bear)**, and **environmental control** via minor druid spells (e.g., Entangle, Mold Earth, Thunderwave flavored as barstool shrapnel).

Always fights "honorably"—which includes tactical use of surprise and terrain.

### 🍻 Arena Setup: "The Broken Cask"

The Colosseum transforms into a magically enhanced **tavern interior**:

Tables, chairs, kegs, chandeliers—all usable as improvised weapons or hazards.

Arcane illusions fill the air with tavern sounds, flickering lantern light, and phantom patrons who cheer or duck for cover.

Magical enchantments reinforce items: no real injuries, but dramatic visual "damage" (shattered chairs, explosions of ale, etc.).

### 💫 Magical Escalation: The Sudden Happy Hour Surge

At the **5-minute mark**, magical constructs of ale spirits and elemental “drunkenness sprites” begin appearing from kegs and bottles. They:

Fly around buffing or hindering contestants randomly.

Create wild zones: slick floors, gravity-shift bubbles, bouncing tables, etc.

Countdown to finale: The crowd begins chanting “One more round!” and a magical bell begins tolling—at the **third toll**, the round ends, and the judges declare a victor based on performance and crowd reaction.

### 🔧 Combat Rules:

**Improvised weapons only** (tables, stools, mugs, ropes, ale kegs, chandeliers).

No magic items or spells unless disguised as part of a tavern trick (e.g., Bonogo using Mage Hand as “bar flair”).

Non-lethal but spectacular. Players can use their class features flavored through tavern antics.

**Environmental Interactions encouraged**: leaping off balconies, swinging on chandeliers, or launching ale-blast surprise attacks.

### 👑 Judge Panel:

**Three City Guard Veterans**, a bit washed up, but full of opinions:

**Sergeant Dorn Havers**, old-school brute. Wants raw strength.

"He just bench-pressed a keg. That’s Guard material."

**Willa Brasscap**, sharp-eyed archer. Cares about teamwork and coordination.

"Taverns fall apart without rhythm. Let’s see how they dance."

**"Uncle" Spence**, drunk historian. Loves flair and backstory.

"That keg toss? Artistic. Reminds me of the Riot of '78."

### 🧠 Narrative & Outcome Hooks:

**If the team wins:** The crowd roars. Hagan bows, hands a sigil of the Trials to Bonogo, and nods to Baergrom: “You’re a tough bastard. Let’s talk recruitment.”

**If Hagan wins:** He helps each party member up with a grin. “Not bad. Come back next year… or join the Guard and train for it.”

Either way: **City Guard offers them honorary tokens** (usable as minor favors or proof of valor), and they become local legends.

## Proficient in Athletics, Intimidation, Perception

### 🧠 Combat Strategy Overview

#### Opening Gambit (Round 1–2)

## Starts in Human form, enters Rage.

Grabs a **table or keg** as an improvised weapon (1d8+STR), launches it at the biggest threat (Baergrom or Bonogo).

Uses **Entangle** flavored as a spilled beer slick + snaring broken chairs.

Keeps moving—tries to isolate one target for a brutal takedown.

#### Mid-Fight Wild Shape (Round 3)

**Shifts into Dire Wolf** or **Brown Bear** for crowd thrill—depending on how the fight goes.

Dire Wolf if controlling (trip tactics)

Brown Bear for raw offense (multiattack)

Swipes with paws or bites, flinging foes into barrels, furniture.

#### Happy Hour Chaos (Magical Interference)

Interacts with environment: throws chandeliers, topples tables, bites kegs to blast ale jets.

Tries to **slam** someone into the magical bell to trigger round-end sequence dramatically.
