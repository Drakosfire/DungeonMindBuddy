
Stone Colossus


Large, Elemental, Neutral

![](images/0.jpg)




THE STONE COLOSSUS LOOMS AS A SLOW-MOVING JUGGERNAUT OF FUSED BOULDERS AND GLOWING MOLTEN SEAMS, ITS STEPS SENDING TREMORS THROUGH EARTH AND BONE ALIKE. A LIVING MONUMENT TO RAW FORCE, IT CRUSHES BARRIERS AND COMPETITORS WITH UNWAVERING RESOLVE, SHAPING THE BATTLEFIELD WITH WALL-RAISING POWER AND SHATTERING THE GROUND BENEATH ITS FOES. THE MERE ACT OF ITS ADVANCE IS AN EVENT—INEVITABLE, IMPLACABLE, AND THRILLING TO BEHOLD IN THE ARENA'S THUNDEROUS ATMOSPHERE.
Armor Class : 12

## ACTIONS


Slam: ‘Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10+5) bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.


Earthshatter (Recharge 5–6) : ‘The Stone Colossus slams both fists into the ground. Each creature within 15 feet of it must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 13 (3d8) bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and not falling prone on a successful one. The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain until cleared.


Raise Wall (1/Short Rest) : ‘The Colossus raises a wall of stone (15 ft. long, 10 ft. high, 1 ft. thick) from the ground as an action. The wall provides total cover and blocks line of sight. After 3 rounds, the wall crumbles to rubble, creating difficult terrain.


Unstoppable Advance : ‘The Colossus is immune to being restrained, grappled, slowed, or pushed by non-legendary effects. At the start of each of its turns, it automatically ends any such effects currently affecting it.
