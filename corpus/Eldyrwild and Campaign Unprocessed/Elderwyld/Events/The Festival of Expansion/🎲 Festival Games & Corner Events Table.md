## 🎲 Festival Games & Corner Events (Roll a d20!)

## 1. 🍎 Apple Assassin

**What They See:** A sleek elf juggling daggers next to rows of bright red apples balanced atop scarecrow heads.

**Action:** Players throw daggers to skewer apples; precision rewarded.

**NPC:** **Celindra Swiftknife**, charismatic elf rogue, slyly encouraging friendly wagers.

## 2. 🥧 Pie Pursuit

**What They See:** Flour-covered children running frantically between pie stands, pursued by laughing adults.

**Action:** Race to deliver pies intact while dodging humorous obstacles (ducks, goats, flour bombs).

**NPC:** **Merrin Puddinfoot**, joyful halfling baker, cheering and offering pastries.

## 3. 🎯 Goblins & Goblets

**What They See:** Goblins with oversized bows, firing suction-cup arrows at wooden goblets hanging on strings.

**Action:** Hit moving goblets to win coins, candy, or ale tokens.

**NPC:** **Fizzlekeg**, cheerful goblin merchant, encouraging playful banter.

## 4. 🎈 Float Goat Rodeo

**What They See:** Crowds roaring in laughter as contestants cling desperately to bucking, floating goats tethered with ribbons.

**Action:** Test dexterity and athletics holding onto magical floating goats.

**NPC:** **Rizzy Bumblehoof**, jovial gnome rancher, wearing colorful ranch attire.

## 5. 🐟 Dockside Duck & Dive

**What They See:** Fishermen and dockworkers challenging festival-goers to catch slippery enchanted fish barehanded.

**Action:** Acrobatics or athletics to grab fish in a timed scramble.

**NPC:** **Old Gillis**, weathered human sailor, grinning with mischievous pride.

## 6. 🦋 Pixie Pursuit

**What They See:** A swirling swarm of glowing, playful pixies fluttering just out of reach.

**Action:** Players attempt to gently capture pixies with silk nets.

**NPC:** **Mistflower**, whimsical pixie whisperer, softly laughing at the chaos.

## 7. 🔮 Guess My Glamour

**What They See:** An enigmatic tiefling illusionist transforming her appearance behind a silk screen.

**Action:** Insight or Arcana to guess subtle changes in her glamour for prizes.

**NPC:** **Zelira Shadeskin**, mysterious yet charming performer.

## 8. 🥚 Eggcellent Juggle

**What They See:** Rows of contestants carefully juggling fragile wyvern eggs painted festive colors.

**Action:** Dexterity check to juggle eggs without dropping.

**NPC:** **Toby Eggflinger**, energetic halfling egg farmer loudly narrating each success or failure.

## 9. 🎨 Facepaint Frenzy

**What They See:** Children and adults lined up, receiving vibrant, magical facepaint designs that animate briefly.

**Action:** Players get painted, potentially receiving temporary minor enchantments.

**NPC:** **Juniper Moonbrush**, soft-spoken human artist with magical brush.

## 10. 💪 The Mighty Mallet

**What They See:** Strongmen and adventurers hammering a massive mallet onto a strength-testing bell contraption.

**Action:** Strength checks to ring the bell and win fame and ale.

**NPC:** **Grokk Ironfist**, exuberant dwarf strongman bellowing encouragement.

## 11. 🐉 Dragon’s Breath Chili Challenge

**What They See:** Participants fanning mouths, eyes watering from tasting absurdly spicy chili.

**Action:** Constitution checks to withstand increasingly spicy chili.

**NPC:** **“Spicy Sam”**, laughing dragonborn cook with flames flickering from his mouth.

## 12. 🥃 Ale-chemy Taste Test

**What They See:** Bottles labeled with cryptic symbols, each holding bizarre concoctions.

**Action:** Intelligence or Wisdom checks to identify ingredients by taste.

**NPC:** **Professor Tipsypip**, eccentric gnome alchemist grinning widely beneath oversized goggles.

## 13. 🎩 Hat Trickery

**What They See:** Rows of hats, constantly shuffling positions magically, hiding prizes or humorous surprises.

**Action:** Perception or luck to pick winning hats.

**NPC:** **Jasper Tumblehat**, playful halfling hatmaker performing minor hat-related illusions.

## 14. 🧀 Rolling Thunder

**What They See:** Contestants chasing huge wheels of enchanted glowing cheese through obstacle-filled streets.

**Action:** Athletics to chase and catch runaway cheese wheels.

**NPC:** **Madame Brie**, charismatic dwarven cheesemaker dramatically narrating races.

## 15. 🕯️ Candlemancer’s Challenge

**What They See:** Players rapidly lighting intricate patterns of candles, racing magical timers.

**Action:** Sleight of Hand or Dexterity checks to swiftly ignite candles.

**NPC:** **Lumira Brightwick**, gentle human candlemaker softly applauding each victory.

## 16. 📚 Bookwyrm’s Quiz

**What They See:** A wizened kobold scholar quizzing participants from stacks of rare books.

**Action:** Intelligence checks answering lore-based trivia.

**NPC:** **Scholar Zikzik**, excitable kobold librarian nervously flipping pages.

## 17. 🕷️ Webwalker’s Delight

**What They See:** Participants balancing across sticky rope webs suspended above soft hay.

**Action:** Acrobatics checks to traverse the web maze without falling.

**NPC:** **Silvi Arachnis**, graceful drow acrobat quietly offering tips.

## 18. 🔥 Firefly Catcher

**What They See:** Children and adults darting around after magical fireflies flitting through lantern-lit streets.

**Action:** Dexterity checks to capture quick, glowing insects.

**NPC:** **Elder Fina**, cheerful older elf gently coaxing fireflies to dance.

## 19. 🎻 Strings Attached

**What They See:** Puppeteers animating elaborate puppets who mock-fight participants in comedic duels.

**Action:** Performance or Deception to theatrically win mock duels.

**NPC:** **Marvin Strings**, animated halfling puppeteer enthusiastically narrating mock-battles.

## 20. 🪓 Axe Me Anything

**What They See:** Rugged lumberjacks and adventurers tossing axes at ornately painted wooden targets.

**Action:** Ranged attack rolls to stick an axe in bullseye.

**NPC:** **Hilda Logsplitter**, boisterous half-orc woodsman teasing and challenging passersby.
