## 🍵 3:00 PM – Herbal Tea Workshop at Evergreen Apothecary

**Location:** A moss-draped storefront nestled in the shade of flowering trees in the upper East Market district. Vines curl around open windows, and the air hums with bees, music, and herbal incense.

### 🌿 Setting & Atmosphere:

Inside, hanging bundles of dried herbs sway over polished wooden counters.

Tea kettles float lazily above warming glyphs, steaming with multicolored vapor.

A long central table holds tea bowls, mortars, flower petals, rune-etched glass vials, and tiny clay jars marked with regional sigils (Veiled Fen, Stormspire peaks, Mirathorn Grove, etc.).

### 🌱 Hosted By:

**Elara Greenleaf** – Elf druid and apothecary owner; serene, elegant, speaks in metaphors. Glowing green tattoos trail her forearms, especially when she brews.

**Talia Mossroot** – Her half-elf apprentice; younger, sarcastic, wears a belt of mismatched potion flasks. She gently teases participants, but takes brewing seriously.

### 🧪 The Workshop:

#### 🍃 Step One: Tea Crafting

Each participant chooses **three ingredients** to blend into a brew:

**Lavenderroot** (calm)

**Redcap Stem** (focus)

**Fenfruit Husk** (vitality)

**Candlebud Leaf** (glow-in-the-dark giggles)

**Ghostspice** (ethereal aroma, possible visions)

PCs roll **Nature or Arcana (DC 12)** to craft a unique tea:

**Success:** Gain minor effect (see below).

**Great Success (18+):** Uncover hidden properties or brew a one-use magical tea.

#### 🧊 Step Two: Steeping

Magical kettles animate, pouring hot water as runes glow in the base of each cup. Steam rises in **symbolic shapes** (based on the herbs or emotion of the brewer).

### 🍄 Caelynn-Specific Threads:

If she brings **glowing mushrooms**, Elara and Talia take deep interest.

**Arcana DC 15 or Nature DC 13** to safely infuse a piece into her brew.

Result: her tea glows faintly and grants **1 temporary HP per short rest for the day**, or **advantage on Arcana checks involving natural magic**.

Talia might whisper:
 “I’ve seen mushrooms like that near the old glyph circles in the Veil. You didn’t go near those, did you?”

### 💫 Magical Tea Effects (Roll 1d6 on Success):

## 🌫 Caelynn’s Vision – Tea of Ghostspice & Glowing Mushroom

As Caelynn blends the ethereal **Ghostspice** and adds the rare, pulsing mushroom into her tea, **Elara** gently warns:

“Visions are gifts… but they don’t always give answers. Sometimes, they ask better questions.”

### 🫖 Upon Drinking the Brew:

Steam curls up from the cup—not as vapor, but as **tendrils of misty rain**, drifting upward and forming vague **stormcloud shapes**. One of the floating shapes **shifts into a figure**, faceless and tall, standing in a grove where the trees lean inward as if listening.

Caelynn feels:

**A scent she remembers faintly** from her childhood: woodsmoke, wet leather, something like lilac.

A flicker of **music**, her panflute’s melody, but played by someone else. Slightly clumsy. Emotional.

### 🌩️ Vision Details (for Caelynn alone):

The grove is not familiar, but **the storm above it matches her own birthstorm** from her mother’s stories.

She sees **a silver medallion**, her father’s, **hung carefully on a low branch**, swaying like a wind chime.

A child’s voice echoes—her own, young: “Why didn’t you stay?”

A murmur from the faceless figure, too distant to make out fully. One word does rise: **“North.”
**

The wind lifts—not chaotic, but searching. The grove vanishes.

### 💬 Aftermath:

If Caelynn shares any of this, **Talia** (the apprentice) quietly says:

“Some spirits ride the wind because they’re lost. Others... because they’re trying to find their way back.”

Elara might offer a whispered blessing: “Your tea called out to something, child of storms. And something answered. Maybe not today. But soon.”

### Optional Thread Seeds:

“North” could connect to a ruin, town, or grove you seed later—Caelynn’s call to adventure.

Her panflute might harmonize differently after this vision—responding to unknown music.

The medallion on the tree could later appear in a dream, or even physically, much later.
