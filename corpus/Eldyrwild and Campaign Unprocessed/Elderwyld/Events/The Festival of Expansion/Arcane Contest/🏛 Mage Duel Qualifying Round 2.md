## 🏛 Mage Duel Qualifying Round 2

**Location:** Arcane Arena, Stormspire Academy
 **Participants:** 8 duelists (including PC: Ephanna & Caelynn)
 **Rounds Today:** 3 spell contests (2 scored, 1 elimination-style)
 **Format:** Individual 1v1, total score from first two contests determines if they move on to final duel.
 **Finalists:** 4 out of 8 advance to next round

## 🎓 Stormspire Academy Judges

## Professor Mira Glassmantle (High Elf, Divination)

Scoring Style: Analytical, precision-focused. Loves clean spellwork, control, and clever foresight.

Mechanical Edge: Bonus to spell attack accuracy, prediction, and use of divination or planning magic.

**Visual:** Tall and ethereal, Mira wears shimmering silver robes that ripple like moonlight. Her eyes are faintly mirrored—reflecting not the present, but possible futures. She never blinks during a match.

**Accessories:** A crystal monocle, perpetually floating near her eye, and a hovering tome of predictions that turns pages on its own.

## Catchphrases:

"Impressive… though they failed to see the obvious second-order consequence."

"Elegance is not optional. It’s the difference between control and chaos."

## Dean Bruun “Boomer” Embercleft (Dwarf, Evocation)

Scoring Style: Explosive power, flair, and crowd-pleasing. Rewards risky and high-damage spells.

Mechanical Edge: Grants bonus for spell effects that create strong sensory impact or AoE.

**Visual:** Broad-shouldered and grinning beneath a scorched black beard streaked with ember-orange. His robes are battle-scorched leathers etched with sigils of flame and force. He claps like thunder when pleased.

**Accessories:** A flask that constantly steams, and a sapphire-studded wand he uses like a pointer—or conductor’s baton.

## Catchphrases:

"Now **that’s** what I call spellcraft! Made my beard tingle!"

"If I can’t feel it in my chest cavity, it doesn’t count."

## Mistress Yna Valcorra (Tiefling, Enchantment)

Scoring Style: Charisma, creativity, and manipulation. Loves illusions, enchantments, and unexpected use of magic.

Mechanical Edge: Rewards Performance, Arcana, and “wow” moments or emotional shifts.

**Visual:** Elegant and mysterious, Yna’s crimson skin and spiral horns are offset by a gown of glamours that shifts colors with her mood. Her eyes glow faintly violet when judging strong emotional magic.

**Accessories:** A lace fan that doubles as a spell focus, and jewelry that murmurs with minor illusionary voices.

## Catchphrases:

"They made me feel something. I don’t even like feeling things."

"Theatrics aren’t optional, darling—they’re spell components."

Each judge scores each round from 1–5 based on how their preferences are satisfied.

## 🧮 Scoring System (Per Contest)

Each round is scored by all 3 judges (1–5 each), for a **max score of 15** per contest.

## Scoring Breakdown for NPCs or PCs:

**Spell Attack Accuracy (Hit / Miss):** +2 for a hit, +0 if miss.

**Spell Complexity (Spell Level Used):** +1 for a 1st-level spell, +2 for higher, 0 for cantrip.

## Style/Creativity (Performance or Arcana):

DC 10 = +1, DC 15 = +2

**Judge Preference Bonus:** +1 if the spell matches their style.

Total per judge is capped at 5. Add up all three for a final score per round (max 15).

## ⚔️ Round 1: Duel of Disruption (Combat)

**Setup:** Traditional 1v1 duel in a warded ring. Each contestant must land **2 successful spells** or **disable** the other to win. Lasts no more than 5 rounds.

## Rules:

No spells above 2nd level.

Damage reduced by arcane wards (non-lethal).

Bonus points for creative spell use or terrain interaction.

## Scoring Considerations:

Spell hits: +2 per hit

Avoiding damage: +1 for clever defense

Judge style: Adds +1 based on spell flavor

## 🧱 Round 2: Stone of Precision

**Setup:** Contestants face each other across the ring. A small enchanted stone floats between them. Each must use **magic** to strike the other with the stone—no direct attacks allowed.

## Objectives:

Move the stone using spell effects (e.g., Mage Hand, Gust, Catapult).

Hit your opponent 3 times to win, or win by judge scoring after 3 minutes.

## Creative Spell Use:

Spells like Mage Hand, Thunderwave, Catapult, Telekinesis, Gust of Wind, or even illusions to misdirect.

## Scoring Factors:

Hits scored: +1 per hit

Spell ingenuity: +1–2 (e.g., bouncing stone off a shield)

Judge bonus: Fits style? +1

## 🌟 Trial Three: The Manifestation Trial – “Shape the Shard”

### 🧩 Setup:

Each contestant steps onto a **levitating stone platform** with a cracked **Arcane Core** hovering in the center. The core pulses erratically with unstable energy—lightning arcs, shadowy rifts flicker, magical wind rushes in waves. The task:

## “Stabilize, suppress, or transform the core using only magic. No weapons, no physical touch. You have 2 minutes (3 rounds) to impress the judges.”

### ⚒️ Objectives & Player Creative Prompts:

They may choose to **contain**, **channel**, or **repurpose** the energy.

#### 🧙‍♂️ Ephanna’s Possibilities:

**Plant Growth** – Bind the platform in calming vines, rooting the core in arcane soil.

**Minor Illusion** – Mask the core’s dangerous glow or simulate a tranquil display to “trick” the instability.

**Calm Emotions** – Use on the crowd or illusion of a “sentient shard” if played up theatrically.

**Phantasmal Force** – Make the core believe it’s whole and repaired (judge-dependent).

**Mage Hand** – Suspend or stabilize parts.

**Summon Fey** – Bring a spirit to “commune” with the core and channel energy.

**Spider Climb + Invisibility** – Physically repositioning around the core mid-illusion for dramatic flair.

#### 🌩 Caelynn’s Possibilities:

**Gust** – Channel wild energy outward or spiral it upward for spectacle.

**Minor Illusion** – Cloak the cracked appearance, or create “guide lines” of energy.

**Dust Devil / Warding Wind** – Contain volatile energy in a storm barrier.

**Snilloc’s Snowball Swarm / Ray of Frost** – Cool overheating magic.

**Magic Missile** – Sacrifice stable arcane energy into the core to “seal it.”

**Thunder Step / Thunderwave** – Blow the core into a safe shape (high risk, high drama).

**Counterspell** – Use it theatrically to “deny” a final burst of chaos.

**Guidance** – Boost herself or dramatize collaboration with the crowd or judges.

## 🧠 Grimble Tatterleaf vs Ephanna

### Round 1 – Duel of Disruption:

**Grimble** surrounds himself with Slow fields and uses Mage Hand to trip Ephanna mid-Eldritch Blast. He blinks between locations unpredictably.

**Ephanna**, unfazed, summons Hex, warps the battlefield with Hunger of Hadar, and uses Misty Step to reposition and blast through the chaos.

Grimble tries to rewind a moment with a time echo—but Ephanna shatters it with sheer will and a perfect Eldritch Blast.

🏅 Ephanna wins narrowly—Boomer and Yna are impressed by the raw fey power and charisma.

### Round 2 – Stone of Precision:

**Grimble** conjures a series of invisible currents (Gust, Minor Illusion, altered gravity) to nudge the stone from unusual angles.

**Ephanna** starts simple with Mage Hand, then uses Phantasmal Force to make Grimble think the stone is somewhere else, while the real one strikes.

Both duelists push the stone like chessmasters—Grimble with physics, Ephanna with trickery.

🏅 Ephanna wins by one point. Judges commend clever deception.

### Round 3 – Shape the Shard:

**Grimble** slows time around the core using layered Mage Hand ripples and a time-stasis sigil. It pulses gently like a clock.

**Ephanna** summons a gleaming Fey spirit who dances around the core, weaving a web of magic (Minor Illusion + Phantasmal Force) to convince the shard it’s healed, then wraps it in Plant Growth vines that pulse to its heartbeat.

🏅 Ephanna wins by creativity and emotional impact. Yna is teary-eyed.

✅ **Ephanna Advances**

## 🎭 Caelynn vs Rashan Vel-Dun

### Round 1 – Duel of Disruption:

**Rashan** opens with Mirror Image and shifting terrain via Silent Image. He fires disorienting Chromatic Orbs through the illusions.

**Caelynn** keeps her cool, casts Warding Wind to scatter illusions, then Ray of Frost to strike the real Rashan.

She counters every image with tactical Starry Wisp and finishes with a dramatic Snilloc’s Snowball Swarm when Rashan missteps.

🏅 Caelynn wins handily—Mira gives her top marks for precision, Boomer for style.

### Round 2 – Stone of Precision:

**Rashan** uses Invisibility tricks and fake stones to confuse.

**Caelynn** uses Gust to launch the real stone, then Minor Illusion to reflect its path, catching Rashan unprepared.

At one point, she ricochets the stone off an arcane banner for a direct hit.

🏅 Caelynn edges ahead—Yna claps at the performance.

### Round 3 – Shape the Shard:

**Rashan** builds a mesmerizing illusion of the core splitting into butterflies and floating away. It's gorgeous but lacks functional logic.

**Caelynn** cools the core with Ray of Frost, surrounds it with spinning Dust Devil rings, then ignites it into a starry display using Magic Missile to pulse controlled bursts—she ends by casting Feather Fall on fragments to float down like snow.

🏅 Caelynn wins with showmanship and sound arcane principles. Crowd favorite.

✅ **Caelynn Advances**
