A CROTESQUE MASS OF PULSATING, ROTTEN FLESH, THE CORRUPTED HEAP WRITHS WITH A LIFE OF ITS OWN. MEATWORMS SPILL FROM EVERY CREVICE, EACH WITH A RAVENOUS HUNGER FOR LIVING MATTER. THE HEAP SLOWLY MOVES ALONG, LEAVING BEHIND A STENCH OF DECAY AND A TRAIL OF ACIDIC SLIME. ONCE AN UNRECOGNIZABLE ENTITY, IT NOW EMBODIES CORRUPTION ITSELF.

## ACTIONS


Meat Flail: 'The Corrupted Heap swings a tendril of putrid meat at a target within 10 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 bludgeoning damage and 1d4 acid damage as the meat flail secretes corrosive bile.


Worm Infestation : 'As an action, the Corrupted Heap expels a swarm of meatworms onto a target within 5 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be latched onto by 1d4 meatworms, taking 1 acid damage at the beginning of each of its turns until the worms are removed with an action.
