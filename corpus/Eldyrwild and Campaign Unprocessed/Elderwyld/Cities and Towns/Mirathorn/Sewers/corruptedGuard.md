CORRUPTED GUARD


Medium, humanoid, neutral evil

![](images/0.jpg)




ONCE PROUD DEFENDERS OF THE REALM, THESE GUARDS HAVE SUCCUMBED TO A CREEPING CORRUPTION THAT'S WARPED THEIR MINDS AND BODIES. THEIR ARMOR AND WEAPONS BEAR DARK, OILY STAINS, AND THEIR EYES FLICK WITH A SINISTER GLEAM. THEIR CORRUPTION MAKES THEM STRONGER AND MORE AGGRESSIVE THAN ORDINARY SOLDIERS, BUT ALSO CHAINS THEM TO A MALEVOLENT FORCE LURKING BEHIND THEIR FALL FROM GRACE.


Armor Class : 12


Hit Points: 16 Hit Dice : 3d8+3
