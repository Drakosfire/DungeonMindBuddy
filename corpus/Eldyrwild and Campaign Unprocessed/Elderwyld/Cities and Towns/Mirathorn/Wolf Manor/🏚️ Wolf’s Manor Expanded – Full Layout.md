# 🏚️ Wolf’s Manor Expanded – Full Layout

## Ground Floor (Main Level)

**Withered Garden & Main Courtyard** (already created)

**Grand Entry Hall:** Cracked marble floor, long-forgotten family banners. Large double staircase.

**Collapsed Parlor:** Shattered glass and ruined furniture.

**Trophy Hall:** Beasts with sewn mouths, whispered warnings.

**Servants’ Passageways:** Tight halls with scratched warnings in Thieves' Cant.

**Warded Study:** Fearful records hinting at the Shepherd’s cult.

**Children’s Room:** Haunting drawings with ancient glyphs.

**Shattered Fountain:** Hidden niche with keys and trinkets.

**Kitchen & Scullery:** Long-dead hearths and strange, dried herbs — some are not native to this plane.

## Second Floor (Upper Level)

## Master’s Chamber:

Rotting, once opulent.

Inset mirror cracked to spiderweb patterns.

Underneath the bed: a secret lockbox (Arcana or Lockpicking DC 16) containing:

Personal diary pages.

Map fragment hinting at the hidden tunnels.

A tarnished signet ring bearing the Wolf sigil—altered with strange abyssal script.

## Family Shrine Room:

Small personal temple.

Defiled statues — faces smashed or eroded away.

Passive magical unease (Detect Magic shows faint lingering enchantments).

## Guest Wing:

Multiple bedrooms.

One window frame has an almost invisible sigil etched into the frame, a very old **scrying anchor** left dormant.

## Observatory Room (Rotten Tower Top):

Collapsed telescope.

Arcane charts of the stars — strangely inaccurate as if drawn in fear of something moving beyond reality.

## Attic (Above the Second Floor)

## Dust-Choked Storage:

Trunks, cracked mannequins, moldering clothing.

Investigation DC 12: Discover hidden compartments with **ancient city maps** showing pre-expansion Mirathorn—**and marked “forbidden zones”** no longer publicly known.

## Whispering Rafters:

Low whispers heard at night if anyone sleeps in the attic.

Actually leftover psychic residue from past cultic rituals.

Insight or Arcana DC 15: Understand that a memory of a **hidden entrance** is embedded in the house’s psychic "skin."

## Secret Hatch:

Leads to a crawlspace to reach the **rooftop**, for perfect lookout potential.

Rusted pulley system once used to hoist signal flags—who were they signaling?

## Basement (Beneath Ground Level)

## Main Cellar Room:

Wine racks collapsed.

Strong smell of mildew and mold.

Investigation DC 12: One barrel wall is false; a tunnel mouth lies behind.

## Hidden Ritual Room:

Ancient, barely erased circle on the floor.

Abyssal symbols—faded but still potent.

Arcana DC 15 or Religion DC 14: Recognize that this room was used to **contain** something — not summon it.

## Collapsed Storage Room:

Dangerous collapse (Dex save DC 12 to avoid minor injury if disturbed).

Behind rubble:

Crates of rot-eaten cloth, strange statues that resemble human forms partially devoured by insects.

## Tunnel Mouth Entrance:

Secret door mechanism (Perception DC 14) hidden behind stacked barrels.

Leads to a **narrow stone passage** that joins the **ancient smuggling/cult tunnels** under Mirathorn.

Roughly aligned with old records about secret military tunnels used before the Festival of Expansion.

Potential access to Outtown or the Docklands — even the old city guard's hidden barracks.
