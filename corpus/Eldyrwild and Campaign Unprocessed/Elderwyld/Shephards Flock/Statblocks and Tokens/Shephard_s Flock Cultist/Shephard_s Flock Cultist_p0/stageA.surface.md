
Sheppard's Flock Cultist


Medium, Humanoid, Human, Chaotic Evil

![](images/0.jpg)




THE CULTIST MINION IS A GAUNT, PALE HUMAN FEMALE WITH DARK CIRCLES UNDER THEIR EYES AND A VACANT, ALMOST FANATICAL EXPRESSION. THEY WEAR TATTERED ROBES ADORNED WITH STRANGE, ELDRITCH SYMBOLS THAT SEEM TO WRITE AND SHIFT WHEN OBSERVED CLOSELY. A CRUDE, BLOOD-STAINED DAGGER HANGS AT THEIR WAIST, AND THEIR HANDS ARE OFTEN STAINED WITH DARK, OILY RESIDUE.

## ACTIONS


Chant of Dread: 'The cultist chants an eerie, unsettling incantation affecting those within 20 feet. Creatures that can hear the chant must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. Frightened creatures can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success.


Dark Whisper: 'The cultist whispers a single, disturbing word in an eldritch tongue. One creature within 30 feet must make a DC 12 Charisma saving throw or take 1d4 psychic damage and have disadvantage on their next attack roll or ability check.
