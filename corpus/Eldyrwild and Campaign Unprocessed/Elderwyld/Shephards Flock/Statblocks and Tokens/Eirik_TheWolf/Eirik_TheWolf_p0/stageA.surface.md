THE WOLF IS SEREN'S TRUSTED LIEUTENANT AND THE HIGHEST-RANKING INFLTRATOR WITHIN MIRATHORN ITSELF. ONCE A LOYAL SOLDIER OF MIRATHORN, THEY WERE CAPTURED BY THE SHEPHERDS, FED THE CORRUPTED MEAT, AND TURNED INTO ONE OF THE CULT'S MOST DANGEROUS ASSETS. THEIR POSITION WITHIN THE CITY GUARD ALLOWS THEM TO SUBTLY INFLUENCE THE CITY'S SECURITY AND SABOTAGE INVESTIGATIONS. WHILE OUTWARDLY APPEARING LOYAL TO MIRATHORN, THEY ARE SECRETLY ORCHESTRATING THE CULT'S OPERATIONS FROM WITHIN, CONTROLLING A NETWORK OF SLEEPER AGENTS AND PLANTING FALSE INFORMATION TO PROTECT THE CULT.
