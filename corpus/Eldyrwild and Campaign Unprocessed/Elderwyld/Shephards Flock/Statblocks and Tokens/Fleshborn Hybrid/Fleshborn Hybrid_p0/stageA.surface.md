DRIVEN BY PRIMAL INSTINCTS, THEIR RAVENOUS MAWS AND LASHING TENTACLES MAKE THEM FORMIDABLE OPPONENTS.

## ACTIONS


Claw : The Fleshborn Hybrid makes a melee weapon attack with its claws. +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8+3 slashing damage.


Bite : The Fleshborn Hybrid makes a melee weapon attack with its bite. +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2d6+3 necrotic damage.


Flesh Lash (Recharge 5-6) : The Fleshborn Hybrid lashes out with three tongue-tentacles at up to three targets within 15 ft. +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 2d6+3 psychic damage. Targets are grappled (escape DC 15).


Multiatack : 'The Fleshborn Hybrid makes two attacks: one with its claw and one with its bite.


Consume the Weak : 'If the Fleshborn Hybrid is within reach of a grappled, incapacitated, or unconscious creature, it devours the creature's flesh. This attack automatically hits, dealing 4d6 necrotic damage to the target.
