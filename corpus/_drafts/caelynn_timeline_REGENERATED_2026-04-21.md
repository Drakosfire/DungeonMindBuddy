| **14**  | **Caelynn** identifies a dusty vial as a **healing potion**; cannot decipher manor window sigils beyond “close to **Infernal**”; reads the **Wolf** ring well enough to believe she can activate it; arranges care for her mushrooms before departure.                                                                                                                                            | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 14 - Recap.md`                      |
| **15**  | **Caelynn** bottles migrating **moss**; uses the **rocky-talky** to reach **Professor Valin** and **Professor Tealeaf** about the off-schedule forest; with **Stafl** probes the trees’ magic shell; takes Tealeaf’s return call ordering a fast push to **vanguard trees** despite the dangers ahead.                                                                                            | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 15 - Recap.md`                      |
| **16**  | Her pack alone never hosts the stowaway spike lizards torturing everyone else; hearing a cry, she defends a baby **ankylosaurus** with **Dust Devil** and **Snowball**, leans on **Innate Sorcery**, feeds **Goodberry**, and drops the fleeing **Canopy Prowler** with **Magic Missile**.                                                                                                        | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 16 - Recap.md`                      |

| **18**  | **Caelynn** lobs **Snowball** into **Ephanna**’s **Hunger of Hadar** sphere to splash the **Mother**; channels **Innate Sorcery** into **Thunder Step** to rip **Bonogo** from the maw and shock the beast; later answers the fleeing **Mother** with three **Magic Missile** hits.                                                                                                               | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 18 - Recap.md`                      |
| **19**  | **Caelynn** reads the brewing storm in the sky for **Mossford**’s leaders; with **Ephanna**, **Karsemine**, and **Thrin** she tests **vanguard** reactions—**Ray of Frost**, then a wide **Sorcerous Burst** of cold that finally bends the forest line—and keeps experimenting as red gnats boil out of the trees.                                                                               | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 19 - Recap.md`                      |
| **20**  | The swarm engulfs **Caelynn** until she **Thunderwaves** it apart; her **bracelet** calms **Marla** before **Bonogo** gets brawled in the ditch line; she **rockie-talkies** **Sara**, reconnects with **Lysandra**, brews antidote **tea** when she sees **shimmer**-touched eyes, then reports tainted **jerky** while still holding for **Tealeaf**.                                           | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Session Recaps/Session 20 - Recap.md`                      |
