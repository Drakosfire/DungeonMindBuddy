| **7** | Her performance calms the toll riot so the party can reach **Captain Lysandra** and cut the gate line; picks **burgundy-and-teal** ribbons; with **Baergrom** visits **Elara Greenleaf**’s herb shop to trade a pound of glowing mushrooms for **Stormspire Academy** alchemy notes. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 7 - Passing Mirathorn Gates.md` |
| **8** | She and **Baergrom** run the street distraction meant to cover **Stafl** and **Karsemine**’s **guardhouse storeroom** infiltration until insults turn into a brawl. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 8 - Captain Lysandra Quest.md` |
| **9** | Rains **Snowball** on **Corrupted Meat Mounds** without spawning fresh **Meat Piles**; reads the cult **scroll** as planar **Abyssal**-like script; casts **Minor Illusion** so the busy tunnel junction looks empty while the party slips past. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 9 - Battle with the Meat Monsters.md` |
| **10** | Helps lift the crushing trap blocks, freezes the creeping goo so **Bonogo** can escape, briefs **Thraxx** on the quest; **Thunderwave** in the chilled meat warehouse; twice falls unconscious—including once when a **Charmed** **Bonogo** knocks her out again. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 10 - Battle with the Meat Monsters.md` |
| **11** | While **Ephanna** clears the council chamber, **Caelynn** stays with **Commander Thalia** talking through the plan until **Bonogo** ambushes **Wolf**. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 11 - Midnight Politics.md` |
| **12** | Opens the council fight with **Thunderwave** on eight guards and drops the chandelier; weathers the chamber’s anti-spell **runes** (dodges, paralysis, **Thalia** knocking her out); after **Baergrom**’s potion she **Thunderwave**s the last three standing guards; tells **Thalia** to bring a potion to **Lesandra** at **Stormspire Academy**. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 12 - One Persistent Bugbear or Sneaky Fucking Bugbear.md` |
| **13** | During the warded short rest she plays her mother’s **pan flute**; loose meat from **Caelynn’s** pack escapes into **Stormspire Academy** until she hunts it with **Baergrom** and **Karsemine**. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 13 - The Meaty and the Dead.md` |
| **14** | Opens with **Magic Missile** spread across the sewer blobs; layers **Sorcerer’s Burst** with the party against **Meat Flies**; snaps **Bonogo** and **Baergrom** out of **Charm** by deliberately **Magic Missile**ing both. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 14 - Into the Meat Grinder.md` |
| **15** | Proposes stealing cult **robes**; slows tendrils with frost while the chest is cracked; hears an old woman crying far below the meat cathedral; reads the **Fireball** scroll across the central platform; when grappled off the rail she chains **Feather Fall** with **Tempestuous Magic** to float back up as the guard falls. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 15 - Into the Meat Grinder.md` |
| **16** | **Magic Missile** finishes a guard and meatling; **Thunderwave** wipes a fresh cultist wave; **Snowball** clears sleeping foes and tags the climbing horror; **Feather Fall** catches **Idris** when the grate fails. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 16 - Recap.md` |
| **17** | **Arcana** (leaning on **Storm Sorcery**) flags the new tempest as the same pattern as **Stonebridge**’s flood; **Feather Fall** saves **Bonogo** after the centipede launch; **Ray of Frost** slows the centipede; bottles pooled goo; **Magic Missile** frees **Bonogo** from the meat wall; falls to a **Meat Mound** until **Baergrom** potions her; the recap credits the **Sprite** and **Caelynn’s charm friend** with hauling **Ethel** from the water. | `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 1/Session Recaps/Session 17 - Recap.md` |
