Ephanna grew up very close to a rift into the Feywild which allowed for no small amount of mischief and whimsy to enter their life from a young age. Their parents disappeared mysteriously one night, and Ephanna’s memory of that night is nearly blank. After a few years of living as a scammer and petty thief in the nearby city they returned to their hometown, and decided after a drunken conversation to venture into the Feywild. After a few weeks of confusion and mystery in the Feywild they met a wingless Archfey named Oberon. Oberon taught Ephanna much about the Fey and the ways of the forest, and invited them to live at his home. The cost of this arrangement was strange and enigmatic to Ephanna. Often Oberon would require some small task such as calling across a meadow with a particular mimicry of an animal sound or whispering a soft human voice along a river. This would be combined with leaving a particular rock or carved trinket along a path. Sometimes Oberon required them to live amongst the Court of another Archfey for a time and simply report back on the types of trees present within the realm.

On one of these “missions” Ephanna met a strangely luminous talking stag who motioned with his antlers and asked “May I borrow that if you’re not using it?” Ephanna had still learned little of the true nature of Faewild at that time, and assumed that being generous to forest spirits was the correct course of action. They held out their only possession, a crude lantern, towards the Stag and said “Of course, I don’t really need it.” The Stag nodded, and Ephanna felt a strange sensation as the Stag walked away slowly and disappeared into the woods. As they returned home that night Oberon gave them a peculiar glance and said “Interesting, I see you have given away your gender! I really must find you a teacher who can give you a better education in the way of Fae trickery.” Ephanna felt unconcerned, but nodded their assent.

The next week Ephanna found a satyr sitting in the kitchen drinking something steamy and green from a stein. Oberon introduced him as Hyrsam, another Archfey. They made an agreement that Ephanna would return to the material plane and perform some work for Hyrsam in exchange for the power to defend themself and an education in Fey trickery. Ephanna was already naturally skilled in disguise, forgery, mimicry, and the scam artistry, but learned many new skills from Hyrsam for charming, deceiving, and outmaneuvering their targets.

The price of their deal soon became clear. Hyrsam was looking for certain artifacts that had been taken by foolish mortals who had stumbled into his realm through hubris or luck. Ephanna was tasked not just with retrieving these objects, but finding out the means by which they had been acquired. The most important was one that Hyrsam had no leads on. It was a magical fiddle which he claimed could be used to charm and deceive even a god. For now Ephanna has only found a ring which had been stolen by a tiefling through a love affair with a jealous fae consort. The next artifact was most likely in Mirathorn.
