# Bubbles the Float Goat

Bubbles is Pippa Goldwhistle's float goat: a magical working animal used for river transport, beer delivery, and eventually festival spectacle. The table knows Bubbles through the StoneBridge flood rescue: beloved, useful, frightened, and memorable enough that "save Bubbles" becomes the emotional engine of the scene.

## Float-Goat Species Context

Float goats are whimsical goat-like creatures that can walk on water. The Mirathorn setting describes them as lightweight, soft-curly-furred, pearlescent, wide-hooved, and aided by small wings for balance. They are social, playful, and curious; their wool, milk, cheese, and horn shavings have buoyant or water-adjacent magical uses. They are common enough around Mirathorn and nearby waterways to serve as companions, livestock, beasts of burden, and festival delights.

## Bubbles at the Table

- **Working role:** Pulls Pippa's wagon full of kegs; paired with a barge/river workflow for brewery deliveries.
- **Personality signal:** Bleats and shakes her mane as if answering Pippa; not just equipment.
- **Fear trigger:** Fast, violent water. Bubbles can walk on water, but floodwater can tumble her and make her lose control.
- **Session 3 crisis:** Found wildly bleating on a rock in the river. Panics hard enough to bite Ephanna's mage hand and drop the rope. Eventually rescued and remembered as a local-hero beat.
- **Festival hook:** Later Mirathorn festival prep notes mention Bubbles showing up with a kid riding her and doing flips.

## Statblock-Generator Context

No canonical Bubbles statblock exists yet. If a statblock is generated, preserve the difference between "magical working animal" and "combat monster":

- **Core fantasy:** water-walking pack goat / river-delivery companion / festival performer.
- **Movement hook:** water walking should be normal on calm water; fast whitewater or flood debris is the meaningful danger.
- **Temperament:** social and playful when safe, panicked and defensive when trapped by rushing water.
- **Likely mechanics:** sure-footed/buoyant movement, pack-animal endurance, nonlethal shove/ram or bite only under stress, frightened/panic behavior around uncontrolled rapids.
- **Do not overstate:** Bubbles is not established as intelligent in a speaking-NPC way, not a combat-trained mount, and not a monster threat.
