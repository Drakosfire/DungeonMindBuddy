## How to use this with prep / statblock generation

- **Creature identity:** float goat, a magical working animal able to walk on water in normal conditions.
- **Bubbles-specific state:** floodwater speed overwhelms that gift; panic makes her bite and lose control rather than calmly water-walk out.
- **Scene role:** beloved rescue target, working companion, possible festival performer; not framed as a hostile monster.
