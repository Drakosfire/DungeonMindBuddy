## Canon summary

Riciberga is the blacksmith in Hempholm. After the caretaker attack and the party's rest, Bonogo goes to her with unusual metallic tree material. She is described as very sober and hungover after working all night and into morning, haphazardly shaping the material into a distinctive addition to Bonogo's leather armor. The result is awkward, misshapen, and perfect for Bonogo.
