## How to use this with prep / statblock generation

- **Voice and table energy:** bright, talkative, creative brewer; panic should come from love for Bubbles and practical fear of fast water, not helplessness as a default trait.
- **Scene role:** connective NPC between Wizard Tower Brewing Co, Stone Bridge, Mirathorn brewing culture, and Bubbles as a magical working animal.
- **Mechanics:** no canonical Pippa statblock exists yet. If generating one, treat her as a gnome assistant brewer/inventor first; use combat mechanics only to support that table role.
