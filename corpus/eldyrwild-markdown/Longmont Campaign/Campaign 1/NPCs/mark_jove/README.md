## Canon summary

Mark Jove is Torbin's father in Hempholm. During the grotesque tree crisis he appears angry, frightened, and justifiably distressed by the supernatural disaster surrounding his family and village. After the party explores beneath Hempholm and defeats the Guardian, they approach Mark Jove to discuss taking Torbin onward, possibly to Mirathorn for education, protection, or some other unclear arrangement.
