## Canon summary

Torbin is the boy from Hempholm connected to the grotesque tree crisis and the Jove family. In Session 4, the party finds him near the supernatural tree situation with his father Mark Jove. The recap describes him as sad and somewhat beaten, and Mark as angry and frightened. In Session 5, after the party defeats the Guardian beneath Hempholm and opens the heart of the tree, they approach Mark Jove to discuss taking Torbin onward, possibly to Mirathorn for an education or some other uncertain future arrangement.

By Session 13, Torbin is in Stormspire Academy's infirmary under Professor Tealeaf's care, and his condition remains unresolved in that recap.

## Known role and state

- Hempholm child tied to the Jove family and the grotesque tree crisis.
- First major continuity anchor for Hempholm's civilian consequences.
- Later patient in Stormspire Academy's infirmary.
- His future, care, and education remain an unresolved continuity thread.
