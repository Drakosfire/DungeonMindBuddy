## Canon summary

Hempholm is a village westward on the road from Stonebridge toward Mirathorn. The party reached it after meeting Torvak Hempdealer, who had left Hempholm quickly and warned them of a tree growing huge overnight and seeming to move.

When the party arrived, Hempholm was already in crisis around a grotesque, arcane, moving tree. They encountered Torbin, his angry and frightened father Mark Jove, and drunken townsfolk who wanted to attack or burn the tree. The tree proved dangerous, grew during the confrontation, and was eventually destroyed by the party. The victory revealed precious metallic leaves/branches and triggered a premature village celebration.

That celebration became a horror beat when root-like caretakers erupted from underground tunnels, attacked townsfolk, and turned the revelry into panic and fire. In the aftermath, the party rested in the Shacks, worked with Riciberga the blacksmith on Bonogo's spiked armor, explored the warm root tunnels beneath Hempholm, found glowing mushrooms and precious metal tree sap, fought the Guardian, and broke open the tree heart to reveal a potato.

## Canon location texture

- Rural village or small settlement on the road toward Mirathorn.
- Associated with hemp trade through Torvak Hempdealer and Hempholm's local identity.
- Contains shacks or poor dwellings where the party rested after the caretaker attack.
- Has a blacksmith, Riciberga, who drunkenly worked metal thorns into Bonogo's leather armor.
- Has at least one family home/garden site associated with Mark Jove and Torbin.
- Suffered a supernatural tree crisis, underground root/caretaker infestation, fire, injuries, and at least one confirmed civilian death.
- The surface tree was destroyed, but its underground root layer and aftermath became campaign-relevant.
