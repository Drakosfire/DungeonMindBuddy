## Looking Ahead :

**Recap:
**The party’s current situation: currently it is 2:30am. The BBQ starts at 9am and each member of the party is set to have 1 level of Exhaustion set in at 6am. The party takes a one hour rest at the Grit & Grime.

While discussing what all has happened, a small paper bird flies around Grobnok’s head. The paper is a summons for an emergency council meeting. Grobnok decides it is best to take the adventuring party with him.

The Council headquarters are in the very center of town. This is the original and oldest part of town and was originally a defensive position made of old stone. The party is taken inside where they find a large circular room where they meet the other Council members.

Elara Swiftwind - Mayor

Barin Coppergleam - Proprietor of the Copper and Quartz Inn

Grobnok FuckFight - Owner of the Grit and Grime

Headmaster Tinkerbright - Head of the Wizard’s College

Commander Thalia Ashenvale - Commander of the Guard

Torrin Flamescale - Master Smith and Representative of the Guilds

Merril Tealeaf - Leader of the Agricultural Union

Rurik Stonehammer - Chief Architect

The party also notices a ring of guards placed along the wall. There are 8 guards, 2 elite guards, and standing at Thalia’s shoulder is Wolf. The party checks each guard, including Wolf, and discovers all have an oily sheen to their eyes. However, they see that Thalia does not.

Elara, the Mayor, begins the meeting by asking Stafl (as Tans) about what the party has encountered so far in the city. After a brief recap, and avoiding any details that might alert the guards, Elara lays out the proceedings for how a plan will be chosen.

Each member of the Council will have a chance to present a plan on how to handle the tainted meat and citizens of the city. Then after some discussion a round of voting will take place. Any plan left with zero votes will be removed. When one plan has 60% of the votes it will be chosen as the plan to move forward.

Headmaster Tinkerbright will be first to present his plan. He plans to magically lock the city down and use a rune to find the corrupted meat.

Barin proposes a much more covert operation going to each guardhouse and using magic runes to locate and dispose of the meat.

Grobnok’s plan is to use guerilla tactics and use the goblins to search out anyone who may be involved and look for any stored meat.

Merril proposes a plant that involves purification to all members of the city.

Thalia believes, after consulting with Wolf, that this “problem” is overblown and that any issues should be handled by the guards.

Torrin’s plan is to alert the entire city and get everyone involved in finding the meat and the Cultists.

Rurik has a plan to activate the city defenses and lock everything down until all the meat can be found and burned.

After hearing each plan the party comes up with a plan of their own. They meet with the Headmaster and convince him to combine plans with Merril. Then with the votes from Barin and Grobnok they have enough to convince the Mayor of the best plan. Using a covert approach they will use the magic runes to locate the meat and the Cultists. They will then use the potions from Merril to purify anyone while at the festival.

Just as the plan is finalized the Bartender from the Copper and Quartz bursts through the door carrying a screaming Torbin. The group can just make out a few phrases: “They are calling me”, “I need to go” and “The Shepherd must rise”. Stafl quickly casts Sleep on Torbin and he is taken to the Wizard college.

After all the commotion dies down, Ephanna finally convinces Thalia to look at the eyes of all the guards. She now realizes how dire the situation truly is. Ephanna ushers the other council members out as Caelynn talks with Thalia about the plans. Stafl is set up to distract Wolf when suddenly, with a flash of a dagger, Bonogo attacks Wolf from behind, dealing major damage.

Now the group is in for a big fight…
