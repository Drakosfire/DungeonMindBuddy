## Recap:

The attack by Bonogo on the Wolf sets up a surprise round. Caelynn is first to attack, casting Thunderwave on one of the groups of 8 guards. This does great damage to all eight, but also causes the giant chandelier to fall from the ceiling, crashing right next to her. As soon as she casts her spell Runes begin to glow all along the walls around the room.

Despite this ominous sign, Ephanna applies Hex to one of the Elite Guards and hits them with Eldritch Blast for big damage. Stafl finally shows his true form, unfurling his incorporeal wings and casting Radiant Soul. He flies up near the ceiling and casts Heat Metal on the Wolf. The Wolf, sensing danger, downs an Invisibility Potion and disappears. Kasrsemine quickly uses Hunter’s Mark and deals a major blow to a guard.

Now Thalia has a chance to warn the group that the Runes are a protection against spells for the Council. They are set to target anyone not wearing an amulet. She then attacks a guard and then uses Commander’s Strike to direct Karsemine to kill one of the guards.

Just as the guard dies the Runes begin to glow, first purple then red. The purple runes create a force attack, the first of which Caelynn dodges, the second one she does not. The red runes create a paralysis spell. Again Caelynn dodges the first spell, but is hit and paralyzed by the second spell.

Baergrom, Bonogo and Karsemine each attack a guard; Bonogo and Karsemine each deliver a killing strike. Just then the Wolf speaks directly to Thalia, reminding her who she serves and instructing her to attack the group. She then attacks Caelynn, knocking her unconscious. Stafl is next to be knocked unconscious after being hit by the second round of Rune attacks. And even worse, the force spell pushed him all the way up and against the ceiling; almost completely out of reach.

Baergrom revives Caelynn with a potion then hits and kills another guard just before getting hit in the back with a massive amount of Necrotic damage from the Elite Guard. Ephanna attacks the Elite Guard with a deadly burst of Eldritch Blast. With a burst of fury Caelynn casts Thunderwave again on the remaining three guards, killing each of them.

Karsemine performs an impressive stunt to revive Stafl with her potion as Baergrom retaliates with a killing blow against a missed attack from the final Guard. Ephanna applies Hex to the last Elite Guard and then swiftly kills him. Stafl takes the chance to attack Thalia, thus ending her Charmed status. And with that, the battle is over…

However, while this was all happening Bonogo noticed a stone-on-stone grinding sound coming from behind a tapestry. He realizes it was the Wolf attempting to escape through a secret passage while still invisible. Bonogo quickly rolled under the sliding stone door and found himself in a hallway leading to stairs. He then rushes down the hall, attempting to brush against the Wolf and finds him at the top of the stairs. Bonogo throws down some caltrops to stop the Wolf, then throws a dagger in his direction. Nothing seems to hit and the only thing he finds is a pile of the Wolf’s armor.

Bonogo rushes down the stairs and then throws oil on the ceiling of the next hallway; which he then lights on fire. As he is searching the flames for any sound or movement he is knocked down by Wolf rushing by. When Bonogo catches up to him at the door Bonogo plunges his knife into the Wolf once again. In one final desperate move the Wolf attempts to disengage and make a run for hit, but Bonogo won't let him escape again.

As Bonogo deals the killing blow the oily sheen fades from the Wolf’s eyes and is replaced with only regret at betraying the city of Mirathorn.

Finally, Caelynn instructs Thalia to bring a potion to Lesandra at the Wizard’s College. Thalia creates a plan to make sure each guard in the city also receives a potion. Now the group must decide what to do with the Wolf.
