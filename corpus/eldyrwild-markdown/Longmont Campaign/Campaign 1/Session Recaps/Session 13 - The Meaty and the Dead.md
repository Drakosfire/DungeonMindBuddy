**Looking Ahead : **The players will be rewarded for asking for help with custom items. Mossglade comes with the leftover items from all the covert ops groups already laying claims to literally every item in the building. They just so happen to be ideal for each PC.

## Recap:

The group has decided the best course of action is to take the Wolf to Stormspire Academy and see if someone can help cast Speak with Dead so they can learn more about the plans Wolf had. But as soon as they step outside the disheveled Council Chambers they are stopped by guards who were alerted to the Rune alarms. Luckily Thalia is there to explain the situation and set the guard on alert for anyone with oily eyes. Bonogo, not wanting to carry an entire body, removes the Wolf’s head to take to the academy.

When they enter the street outside they find it busy with activity. Suddenly one of the covert ops groups shines a light on the group, demanding we tell them if we carry any meat with us. After Bonogo and Baergrom dump out hundreds of pounds of meat on the street the guards are quick to jump into action. As the group continues on to the academy the tainted meat is burned up by one of the mages.

The group arrives at the Stormspire Academy and finds it bustling with activity. Wizards are busy making potions, crafting runes and working on wards. The group approaches the desk of a Half-Elf: Head Clerk Mossglade. With a huff of impatience she asks how she can help. Professor Cinderbranch is very interested in the events that led to Wolf’s head ending up with the group.

Before departing with Professor Cinderbranch, Mossglade also tells the group about the “study room”, a place the group can use to rest and recharge. She also tells the group that Torbin is in the infirmary under Professor Tealeaf’s care.

So now the group must decide which to do first: visit the Necromancer to speak to Wolf, or head to the Infirmary to check on Torbin. The group decides to do both by splitting up. Ephanna and Baergrom decide to go check on Torbin, while Karsemine, Bonogo, Stafl, and Caelynn meet with the Necromancer.

Ephanna and Baergrom discover that Tealeaf has been unable to help Torbin and has not been able to help work on potions. They decide to stay with Torbin while Tealeaf goes into the next room to work on potions.

The rest of the group meet with the Necromancer. When shown the head of Wolf they become very interested in helping. Instead of casting a spell they decide to perform a ritual. While they get the ritual set up the group meets in the “Study Room” for a magical short rest. While taking this short rest Caelynn plays a wonderful song on her mother’s pan flute.

When the group meets up again outside the room they discover that a piece of meat from Caelynn’s bag escaped and is now on the loose in the Academy. Again the group decides it is best to split up and take care of two things at once. Stafl and Bonogo will go meet the Necromancer for the ritual while Caelynn, Baergrom and Karsemine will hunt for the meat. Ephanna will stay with Torbin.

Stafl and Bonogo enter a basement morgue and find the head of Wolf in the center and the ritual about to begin. As with all Speak with Dead spells they are granted 5 questions. Stafl promised one question for the Necromancer, so that left them with 4.

1: Tell us what killed you. He nods towards Bonogo.

2: Where are the strongholds for the meat storage? Hidden in the walls, the guardhouses and underground.

3: How many are working on this plot against the city? Everyone operates in small groups so the number is not known, but Lira will finish the plot before she dies.

4: Where is the Shepherd? Everywhere! In all the minds of the people.

Fifth question asked by the Necromancer: What is the plan and how will we know when it is complete? There is no stopping it! They are gathering now and will soon erupt as something new, led by the Shepherd. All will die and be consumed. You will know at the break of dawn as the Shepherd rises from below.

After the ritual Professor Cinderbranch leaves the room, just as Draven closes and locks the door. Suddenly one of the walls opens up and 2 Elite Guards enter with a large and disgusting Sewer Meat Monster. The disgusting monster moves to the center of the room and oozes goo in a 10 foot radius.

Draven begins to monologue about cooking and eating Stafl and Bonogo as he casts Fear on Bonogo. Luckily Stafl helps and forces the spell to miss. One Elite Guard attacks Stafl and deals damage as well as poisoning him. Stafl counters with a radiant burst and casts Sleep on Draven, who falls asleep. Bonogo sneak attacks the sleeping necromancer and deals massive damage resulting in a killing blow.

With his bonus attack Bonogo then strikes out at Elite Guard 2, but misses. That guard then attacks and hits Bonogo, poisoning him as well. Just as he is being poisoned, the Sewer Monster bites Bonogo, dealing more damage. The other guard makes two attacks against Stafl, who then decides to run for the door.

As soon as he is outside the door he yells for help then casts Sleep on the remaining enemies. Bonogo also runs for the door, but takes the opportunity to attack the sleeping guard, dealing massive damage and racking up another kill.

After explaining to Cinderbranch what happened, the group finds the ritual room empty, a bad sign of what is to come. With the help of the Mages the party can stock up on items before they head down the tunnel to see what awaits…
