## Session 15: Into the Meat Grinder

## Major Beats:

## Next Beats :

**Recap**:

The fight is not over yet. Bonogo cuts the fingers of the guard hanging on and the guard is quickly swept away by the rushing water. The rest of the group surrounds the sleeping guards. Karsemine attacks both for critical damage. Thus ending this fight.

It is now 5:30, inching closer to the dreaded sunrise. The group watches as the Cultists continue to file by, oblivious to the fighting. To help stem the flow, Stafl suggests the group destroy the bridges. After unsuccessfully trying spells and moving carts, they end up burning the bridges and decide to follow the Cultists to see where they are going.

Caelynn has the idea to try and take some of the robes to use as disguises. She finds a kindly old woman in the endless stream of human cultists, and is able to get her to stop long enough to talk, however Bonogo has other plans and knocks her off the bridge and into the rushing water. A furious Caelynn grabs some meat and instructs the group to find a place in line and head down the tunnel.

They can see growths on the walls and the chanting and drumming is getting much louder. As the line of cultists move ahead of the group they can occasionally see one of them being grabbed by the tendrils and absorbed into the wall. Finally, the tunnel opens slightly into a wider area. Again the walls are covered in tendrils, all except spots along the wall where there are glyphs.

The tunnel splits into two paths. In the center is an old grate. Karsemine peers in and is able to see large hinges on the other side. Far in the back are large crates and she can hear voices. Stafl yells to the people, encouraging them to come and talk. The group is shocked to discover a grizzled Captain of the Guard, Captain Idris, and some of his remaining soldiers. He informs the group that what is waiting in the next room is terrifying. So much so, that all his remaining men are too terrified to continue. Only Captain Idris and his mage, Zora, will continue on and help.

Before the group moves into the large room, Bonogo decides to check out the other path, which ends at a locked door. The lock doesn't prove to be any challenge for Bonogo and he discovers a storage room with various crates and a chest half buried in tendrils. Too tempted to let it go, Bonogo reaches out to grab it, but is attacked by other tendrils and is grappled in the process. In a desperate attempt to escape, he uses his new item and vanishes from the creature’s grasp.

Karsemine is next into the storage room and attacks the tendril, cutting it from the wall. She can see that slowly other tendrils are closing in. Caelynn, also seeing the walls slowly closing in, casts frost, further slowing the spread. Without delay she attempts to open the chest, but cannot get it to budge. Baergrom borrows the crowbar from Bonogo and pries open the chest. Inside they discover rolled up scrolls and a very old sealed letter. Ephanna gives one last Eldritch Blast to a storage box, revealing some fabric. He sends his Sprite to grab the garment and is rewarded with a mysteriously dark cloak.

While the group heads back to the main tunnel they take a quick glance at the items they picked up from the chest. They find another fireball scroll as well as a counter spell scroll. Stafl opens the very old letter to find a flowing script from the time of the founding of Mirathorn describing the construction of the temple in this area. The group continues down the other tunnel, slowly the tunnel widens until they reach a very large room.

From the tunnel the party can see that the room is very large, about 100 feet across. There is a giant platform in the middle with walkways connecting to the outside walls. Thirty meters below they can see soft blue light coming from the churning water. Caelynn can faintly hear the cries of an old woman down below. The walls along the outside are covered in meat and several large tendrils connect to the giant mound of meat in the center of the platform. The Cultists that were in line walk up to the giant meat mound and throw themselves on it, being absorbed.

The group backs up into the tunnel to assess the situation. They can see 3 Cultist Leaders positioned on the outside walkways, 4 Elite Guards stationed around the center, 4 more regular guards near the tunnel entrance, 4 meat monsters, and 3 tendrils attached from the walls to the giant meat in the center platform. As the group is just getting spells and oils prepared, the meaty tunnel squelches and begins to contract like an intestine squeezing them out and throwing them prone on a metal grid walkway.

And so begins the biggest fight yet. Caelynn immediately seizes the opportunity to cast the Fireball scroll on the center platform, dealing massive damage to the Cult Leaders and two of the Elite Guards are instantly incinerated. Bonogo uses his chance to leap back over to one of the Elite Guards by the tunnel and stabs him for massive damage before throwing him off the walkway to the depths below.

Just then a guard grapples Caelynn and, to everyone’s shock, they tumble over the side as well. But Caelynn was prepared. She immediately cast Featherfall which then let her cast Tempestuous Magic and float back up, leaving the guard to plummet to his doom. Karsemine attacks a guard and then a meatling. And with her bonus action kills the guard with a second slash of her scimitars.

Which way will the flow of the battle go? Could this be the finale for one of the characters? Or will the group finally put an end to the corruption (or at least a very large impact)?
