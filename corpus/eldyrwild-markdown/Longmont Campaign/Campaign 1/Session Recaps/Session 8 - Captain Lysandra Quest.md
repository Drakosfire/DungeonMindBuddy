## Session 8: Captain Lysandra's Quest

When we resume we find our party (except for Ephanna) wrapping up their activities at the Copper and Quartz. It is getting later into the evening, Stafl is still playing when Captain Lysandra and a few of her guards enter the Inn. She seems anxious to the group as we come downstairs after our meeting with Berin about the cultists activities in town. We grab a table in the corner so we can talk quietly with Lysandra. She tells us that she feels there is something wrong happening in the city, and worse, something happening to her own guards. She asks for our help taking a look at the guards she brought.

Bonogo and Baergrom can sense something wrong with one of the guards (Bill). His eyes appear to have a slight oily sheen to them. Lysandra also informs us that she is also being kept out of the city guard's store room, which never happened before. She believes something is happening between some guards and the cultists.

So she lays out a plan to break into the storeroom. She will help us get into the locked door, but cannot be seen helping us. She can offer us 50 gold and connections in the city. She draws us a map that shows us the locked door she will open that leads under the jail and office of the guardhouse. A hallway passes by the barracks and ends at the storeroom. There is a guard here at a small desk. The guard rotates, but all seem to have the same oily eyes.

Here we jump back to Ephanna, who is currently back in a cell, asleep, and having bad dreams. Faintly Torbin can be heard a few cells over being forced to eat some meat again. Suddenly a Fey appears to Ephanna. Ephanna is shown a flash of memories and the Fey is not happy with him. However, the Fey does offer Ephanna a deal, otherwise certain death awaits.

The first deal the Fey offers is to help Ephanna escape, but Torbin will be turned over to the Fey and raised by them. The second deal is to teach a lock picking spell, however it will cost 1d10 life to cast it. After some deliberation, Ephanna chooses option 2 and receives the new spell.

Fortunately for Ephanna all his gear (minus any gold) are in an unlocked chest next to the cell. Hanging above the chest is the key to Torbin's cell. Quietly they move up the stairs, but there is no sign of anyone left in the building. They quickly exit the front door and find themselves back in Outtown. As they make their way back toward the main gate they can faintly hear a song (one of Stafl's performed by someone else). Torbin does a masterful job of acting and they are allowed through the gate and finally back to the Copper and Quartz to meet back up with the full party.

Now that the party is back together we need a plan. Stafl comes up with a plan to walk right in with Karsemine. She will use her Thaumaturgy spell to change her eyes to match the guard's and he will have brought his artifact to add to the storeroom. Ephanna will start in our room across the street, keeping an eye on the street and on Torbin (who also started to show signs of the oily eyes). Bonogo will guard the back door, while Baergrom and Caelynn will create a minor distraction in the street.

At the unlocked door Bonogo blends perfectly with the shadows. Stafl and Karsemine walk right up to the guard, who is surprised to see anyone so late. Stafl rolls a great persuasion and they all enter the storeroom. He tells the guard that they need to have everything in place for the next morning when Wolf (the higher up). Everything seems to look like a normal storeroom, until the guard moves a bookshelf to reveal a staircase heading down into a cold room. The room is filled with meat, however the meat does not look normal. At the back of the room Karsemine stumbles upon a hidden wall that leads to a long tunnel. This is where they decided to grab some of the meat and head back.

Meanwhile, back on the street, Caelynn and Baergrom's distraction is not going according to plan. What started out as a minor distraction turned to insults and then violence. They rejoin Stafl and Karsemine on their way back to the Inn to discuss further plans with Captain Lysandra.

Back at the Inn the party meets up with Lysandra and Berin to discuss the meat and the tunnel they found. Berin has no knowledge of any tunnels, but Lysandra tells us they were used in the past for military movements. She is also concerned about the meat. The next day is supposed to be a city wide barbeque and she is unsure where all the meat may have been stored. She will take 3-4 guards she trusts to meet us in the Warehouse District where she believes the tunnel comes up. An arcana check reveals the meat to be from another plane of existence. Berin will have the orc bartender take a sample of the meat to Stormspire Academy for analysis.

The party heads back to the guard house. This time we will be much more direct. Bonogo puts out the lights in the hallway and Stafl casts Sleep on the guard. Bonogo and Baergrom split up the meat to take with them. Ephanna, using his Sprite, leads the way down the tunnel, past all manner of traps. At the end of the tunnel the party finds a ladder leading up to a trap door. Sending up the Sprite, Ephanna can see a large warehouse with stacks and stacks of meat. Close to the trapdoor are 5 Cultists sitting in a circle with cages. Inside are Lysandra and her guards. They are being forced to eat the meat.

The party quickly jumps into battle and makes quick work of two of the Cultists. They seem to go down easily and things seem straightforward, until the remaining Cultists split open to reveal huge mouths full of teeth. Before the party can react the Cultists jump on the guards in the cages. Falling upon, gorging on, and merging with the guards in cages something corrupted and fleshy is born. Captain Lysandra, barely able to hold off the giant meat maw trying to consume her, screams for help while fighting for her life.
