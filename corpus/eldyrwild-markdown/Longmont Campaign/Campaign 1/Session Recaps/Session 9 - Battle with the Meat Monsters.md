## Recap:

A fierce battle begins. Bonogo is first to strike, and he makes sure it counts. With deadly precision he kills one of large disgusting meat creatures - a Corrupted Meat Mound. However, with his final slice against the creature a chunk of meat falls off to the ground to form a smaller creature - a Corrupted Meat Pile. It quickly crawls toward the nearest rack of meat and disappears inside. Stafl casts Light on the center cage and fires off a bolt from his crossbow for a nice clean hit. However, another chunk falls off and again crawls toward the rack of meat.

All the while Caelynn is forming a spell to cast Snowball it splashes against the Meat Mound and both Meat Pilese. Caelynn notices when attacking with the spell, no new Meat Piles break off. All other damage gushes a poisonous cloud. Carsemine is forced to dodge the rank cloud and casts Hunter’s Mark on the Meat Mound.

Baergrom dives in to the frae, heedless of the gas, dwarves don’t fear poison. Crushing the mounds with his axe he hews off more meat piles. Taking a cue from Caelynn, Ephanna casts Shatter for 14 damage exploding the disgusting piles.

Lesandra, barely able to fight back, pulls a torch from Bonogo’s pack and casts Leadership on him. In a daring feat of swashbuckling,Bonogo jump on to the top of one of the cages, slips on some ice and falls to the ground, tossing the torch over to Karsemine and Baergrom.

Stafl casts Tasha’s Hideous Laughter and watches the meat jiggle with no effect. Desperate, he reveals his divine nature and runs into melee range. Wisely Caelynn keep raining ice down on the corrupted mounds, tearing at them without spreading the corruption.

Karsemine, inspired by the torch begins pouring oil on the meat rack, and tosses the torch to start a blaze, then slashes out at the Meat Mound and cuts off another chunk.

Ephanna nauseated and unable to focus, channels his fae powers blasting out at the mound, and hitting rack of meat. His Sprite frantically searchesfor any missed meat piles. Lesandra collects herself and begins swinging her sword wildly, only to cut off more piles. Nearby, a disgusting pile of offal rises up from the flaming meat rack that Karsemine had started and bashes into Karsemine who rebukes with flames that spread the fire, consuming smaller piles. The fire spread and the desperate fight continues for several more heartbeats. Finally, surround by smoke and clouds of poisonous ichor, the meat all seems to have stilled.

With the battle over and the fire out, the hard part starts. What to do about all this bullshit. Looting the cultists and searching the room reveals a second underground entrance, and a scroll in a strange language. Caelynn’s aptitude with languages allow her to know it is written in something like Abyssal from other planes than this one. Without much recourse, Lesandra is dispatched with one of the scrolls, heading to the Mages College at Stormspire Academy.

Bonogo takes point and directs the party around tunnel after tunnel of traps. They arrive at a prison packed with humans and cultists leading another feasting ritual. Bonogo and Karsemine sneak in, and take out the three cultists, and bar the door hoping to protect the prisoners for a time.

At the next junction the party finds where meat is being packedand loaded into carts. There is a crowd of workers, guards and carts. While considering next steps, Ephanna hears something in the tunnel behind the party. The barred door must not have held. Stafl and Karsemine take up their prior disguise sand confront the cultists. The distract them and learn some new things. The cultists are working for Captain Fairfield and that Captain Blart is coordinating the meat distribution at the central office. Further, the rioter Lyra is nearby and leading the distribution project.

After a lengthy deliberation on how to get past the junction, a delay the party can not afford to have, Caelynn casts Minor Illusion to have the tunnel appear normal as the party sneaks past. Now the party must decide which path to take next: to the Mountain Gate or to the Docks.
