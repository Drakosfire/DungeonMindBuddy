### Session 5 : Underneath Hempholm

Major Beats:

Defeated the caretakers on the surface.

One grandmother was killed during the assault

Got a long rest, got spiked leather armor

Explored the underground root layer

Found precious metal tree sap

Found glowing mushrooms

Fought the Guardian

Baergrom went down, then was blasted by shockwave by Caelynn failing two death saves. Brought back by Stafl

Caelynn’s shockwave annihilated the caaretakers.

Gather a bunch of gold and the potato

Next Beats :

Traveling to the city, stuck outside the gates, the Shephards

Looking Ahead : Getting into the city, the festival, the cultists at the BBQ contest. The Wolf.

Recap :

After a night that started with sweet drunken celebration that became fire, and horror that culminated in the heartbreaking loss of Grandma Thistlebloom, RIP and the orphaning of little Annie Thistlebloom. The group poked their noses and tossed a torch to do just enough exploration of the mouth of a tunnel to convince them that it was a problem for another day. Heading over to the Shacks, Bill the Belly was more than ready to give them a room for free and some much needed rest. 

Bright and early the next morn, Bonogo march straight to Riciberga the now very sober and hungover blacksmith. She had drunkenly worked all night and into morning haphazardly shaping metals thorns to add to his leather armor. It is awkward and misshapen, and perfect. Thorns of organic metal sticking out in all directions. Without wasting time, they began to explore the tunnels.  Barely squeezing through the opening, the tunnels expanded into warm and dank darkness broken by the glow of mushrooms.  They could hear they were surround by chittering and skittering caretakers, who seemed to have learned their lesson and left them be. 

Caelynn became fascinated and began collecting a glowing mushroom of unknown name or purpose, and collected many, many pounds of them. Ephanna and Bonogo found a lump or precious metal tree sap in a branch of the tunnels. Bonogo keeping 25 gold and Ephanna sharing out the other 25. Finally making their way through, they found the heart of the tree and it’s Guardian.

The enormous metal spike armed insect like root and metal creature immediately began to assault the invaders. Downing Baergrom and stabbing him viciously it redirected it’s focus to the others. Bottlenecked in the tunnels, Caelynn  who had charged in to get space and was surrounded, used a shockwave to clear the space of caretakers.  The Guardian and Baergrom’s dying form were thrown together against the wall of the cavern. Nearly dying from the blast, and nearly dying again slamming into a wall Stafl rushed to Baergrom with some quick healing touch.

The situation clearly turning, the Guardian became truly agitated and began growing and exploding with metal thorns, that weakened it and the party. With a final burst of effort ____ took down the metal and root nightmare.
Examining the room, they busted open the glowing sack of the heart of the tree which oozed precious metal and revealed a ordinary appearing potato. After collecting their loot, the group approach Mark Jove to discuss taking on his son as a ward, or slave, or snack, or maybe clerk. It was unclear. There were promises to take him to Mirathorn and get him an education.
