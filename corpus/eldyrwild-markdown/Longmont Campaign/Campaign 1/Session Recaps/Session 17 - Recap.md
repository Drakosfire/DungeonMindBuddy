Session 17:

The centipede-like creature begins to vibrate, trying to shake off its adversaries. Bonogo just manages to hang on, however the creature begins to pull Ephanna further into the giant pile on the center platform until only Ephanna’s head remains visible. As Bonogo clings to the creature climbing the wall, a large lump begins shifting around and suddenly a head materializes right in front of him. It pushes against the skin of the creature, revealing itself to be the Wolf and begging to be killed. Dark energy pulses through the room and along the creature, curing a large portion of damage.

There is a sudden flash and the smell of ozone before water starts pouring in from every tunnel and every crack in the ceiling above. Caelynn, using her background in Storm Sorcery, makes an Arcana check on the storm suddenly raging outside. She shares the disturbing news that this storm is exactly like the storm they encountered in Stone Bridge when Bonogo and Bubbles were almost swept away.

Bonogo, hearing enough out of the Wolf’s head, stabs it right in the face with his dagger; feeling the blade easily slide in, but hitting something hard inside. The centipede creature writhes and slams its body, launching Bonogo 80 feet back and over the rest of the battlefield. Caelynn casts Feather Fall, saving Bonogo from almost certain death. Bonogo springs back to action, and using sleight of hand, cuts Ephanna free and pulls them out.

Caelynn quickly casts Ray of Frost on the centipede, slowing its movement speed. She then dumps out some of her water and collects some of the goo that has pooled up on the floor. One of the nearby Cultists begins to fall into a trance. Despite Stafl landing a hit on them, they continue until they reach the center where they spill some of their blood onto the meat where it is absorbed and heals the large creature.

Karesmine focuses her next attack on the tentacle, hitting it for nice damage before casting Zephyr Strike to escape being surrounded by Meat Mounds. As she rushes by she uses Horde Breaker on the nearest Meat Mound, dealing damage again.

All the while Stafl is working on a plan to deal with the Wolf’s head. He yells insults to the head attempting to taunt it closer. He can see a protrusion inside the meat creature shift and move until it slides right next to him. It pops up out of the creature and Stafl grabs it. He can hear something rattling around inside. Stafl shakes it until a seed falls out and lands in his hand. The small tentacles wrap themselves around his hand and he can feel dark energy emanating from it as it tries to burrow into his skin.

After a quick debate about what to do with the seed Stafl decides to just stab it. There is an immediate crack of lightning and the entire space is filled with screams coming from all the meat connected in the room. The meat creature appears to have taken a critical blow.

One of the Meat Mounds slides up to attack Ephanna. The first attack misses, but the second hits. Ephanna casts Shatter on the Meat Mound, hitting it, the tendril and the center grate. The tendril appears to be just barely hanging on now, as well as the grate. Ephanna then downs a Greater Healing Potion, recovering almost all hit points. Idris attacks the same tendril and finally severs it, causing the meat creature to shake the entire room again. The party is dealing massive damage to the creature. Baergrom uses Commander’s Strike with Bonogo, but the attack misses. After using Second Wind he lands a decent hit.

Sensing that both Bonogo and Karsemine are hanging on to the creature, the walls of meat reach out and grapple them. Karsemine dodges the grapple, but is attached to the creature as it continues to climb up the wall. The creature flips around and crushes Karsemine, downing her well above the reach of any of the party. Worse, the creature is very close to reaching the ceiling and breaking through.

In a desperate attempt to save her friends Caelynn casts Magic Missile on the wall holding Bonogo. Bonogo climbs up the creature, reaching Karsemine, and healing her with a potion. He follows up with a stab from his dagger. Karsemine is now able to break free and uses her bonus action to attack, and Stafl sends a bolt from his crossbow. The centipede-like creature looks to be finally on its last leg.

The Meat Mound attacks Caelynn with both attacks, dealing a massive amount of damage and knocking her unconscious. Baergrom rushes to revive her with a potion, but misses with his follow up attack. Ephanna attacks the creature with Eldritch Blast, severing it in two!

Suddenly Ephanna’s vision fills with Fey stories of heroes past. As the creature’s lower half crashes to the water below, the top half rips open a hole in the ceiling. The storm starts to fade, the rancid smell of meat dissipates, the remaining meat creatures die, and the glaze disappears from the eyes of the remaining Cultists. Thus ending the final battle!

A huge crowd begins to form around the hole, looking down on the heroes below. The party realizes that the group is made up of all Cultists, but they see Lesandra at the front. The Sprite and Caelynn’s charm friend even help Ethel out of the water. When the party works their way out of the hole and back up on the street, Lesandra orders everyone to keep quiet about the events of the night so the festival can continue as planned.

The feeling in the air quickly changes from despair to celebration, but the party begins to feel their exhaustion. Before the party can join in on the fun of the festival they all plan to have a nice long rest. Then, it is time to cut loose and have some fun.

There are, however, many loose ends to still tie up. Where will the party head next? Will they continue to hunt down the Shepherd? Explore some of their own personal stories? Or is there an entirely new adventure awaiting them?
