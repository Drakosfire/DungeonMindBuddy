# Session 16 Recap

The group finds themselves deep into the battle. Assessing the situation, Stafl decides the best action is to cast Sleep on the Cultist Leader. Both Elite Guards attack members of the party. Karsemine is attacked first and sprayed with corruption. The other Elite Guard attacks the prone Baergrom for a critical hit. Some meatlings attempt a follow-up hit but fail miserably and plummet over the side into the depths below.

Ephanna sees another perfect opportunity for Fireball and again casts it in the center of the room, dealing massive damage. The Cultist Leaders perish, the meatlings are incinerated and the enormous Meat Pile in the center is now on fire. Baergrom turns to the attacking Elite Guard and shoves them over the side to the water below. Zora, the mage, casts Magic Missile on each of the remaining meatlings and the remaining Elite Guard while Idris, the Captain, charges forward and slashes the Meat Pile.

The room quakes, the tendrils squirm, and then begin to swell with energy. The energy is passed down to the Meat Pile. The flames go out and it now looks as if it never took any damage at all. Stalf rushes to Zora to help pull back from the tendrils, but they continue to pull her towards the wall. Then a voice coming from the Meat Pile, the disgusting head of the Wolf appears begging for someone to release him as the Meat Pile spews out more meatlings.

Bonogo attacks the Elite Guard, dealing a large amount of damage, but not quite killing him. Caelynn follows up with Magic Missile finishing off the guard and killing a meatling. Suddenly, without warning, Stafl is stabbed in the back by a group of Cultists that have made their way down the tunnel. Seeing that Zora could not be saved by Stafl, Karsemine rushes to her rescue and slices through the tendrils. Stafl decides to use his brand new item: the Peacemaker Fiddle. Casting Song of Shattering kills the remaining Elite Guard and injures all of the Cultists.

The group seizes the chance to push back the enemy. Ephanna casts Shatter on the large tendril and kills several meatlings. However, the spell damages and weakens the grate. Baergrom moves to the center of the platform and uses his new item, planting his shield and providing temporary health to the team within range. Idris rushes forward to attack the large tendril, hearing an ominous creak in the grate. Zora follows up the attack with Magic Missile, severing the tendril in a spray of goo.

Again the ground shakes and pulses flow down the tendrils to the Meat Pile in the center. Again the meat swells and spawns a group of Meat Flies. Bonogo immediately kills one before it can get too far as Caelynn casts Thunderwave on the group of Cultists, killing them all. Realising that the tendrils are the top priority Karsemine rushes over to another large tendril and uses both swords to deal major damage.

More Cultists arrive from another tunnel, but don’t get very far into the room before Stafl casts Sleep. Seeing Bonogo attack and kill one of the meat flies, the remaining meat flies all attack at once against him. Luckily Bonogo is able to dodge all but one attack and takes only minor damage. Baergrom uses his shield’s ability, providing temporary healing for the team again.

Ephanna uses Eldritch Blast and Zora casts Magic Missile on the tendril, combining for some good damage. Idris moves around the center platform to make an attack, but takes a step on the broken grate and falls through, barely hanging on. One of the Cultist Guards makes an incredible leap across to the central platform, preparing to attack. The meat creature’s tendrils lash out against Ephanna, but Baergrom intercepts the attack with his shield. The Cultist Guard hits Bonogo with two attacks dealing a significant amount of damage.

Caelynn casts Snowball on the sleeping Cultist group, killing 2 of them instantly. Karsemine then leaps to the remaining Cultists and uses Horde Breaker to kill a few more. Baergrom lifts up his shield and then uses Commander’s Strike to have Bonogo deal massive damage to the meat pile. Idris is able to climb back up while Zora again casts Magic Missile on the tendril for more damage.

That's exactly when the meat pile spawns a giant disgusting centipede-like appendage that slowly starts to climb up the side of the room towards the ceiling. The only thing separating the townspeople above and this horrible creature is the ceiling and the group of adventures.

Bonogo leaps onto the creature, slicing with his dagger. Caelynn casts Snowball again attempting to deal damage and slow the creature; also destroying the last tendril. The Guard grabs Ephanna and pulls them on to the meat pile, but misses their attack. Karsemine uses Horde Breaker on the meat flies and kills both. Stafl uses a potion to heal some damage and then uses his final spell slot to cast one more Sleep spell on the remaining meat flies and meatlings.

One of the meat flies that was still active ends its transformation and turns into a meat mound and attacks Karsemine for big damage. Ephanna continues to be stuck and is pulled in further. Baergrom lands a critical hit on the centipede and then follows up with a second attack with Action Surge. Idris and Zora both attack the centipede, however this does little to stop it from moving closer to the ceiling. Because it begins to move Bonogo gets in an opportunity attack for massive damage. Idris falls off the creature, likely receiving major damage, but is saved by Caelynn casting Feather Fall.

This battle has been really back and forth, up and down. Which way will it end? Will the heroes stop the centipede in time to save the city?
