# Session 3 Recap

Adventure hook, hear about a mysterious artifact while celebrating.
Unseasonable torrential rains floods wherever the players are, non combat problem solving. They have to keep themselves alive, and save townsfolk.

Big beats :

Rode with Pippa and fell in love with Bubbles.

Helped Kirfan pull up debris from the broken structure from upriver. (River and Upriver town need a name)

Got comp’d beer and board from Grishna.

Stafl wrote and played an incredible song that woo’d the town in the retelling of the Wizard’s Tower Brewery adventure.

Horrible storm kicks up and gets worse.

Players heard Pippa yelling and immediately ran out to help find Bubbles.

Ephanna uses mage hand to lasso Bubbles on the rock. Bubbles is too panicked and bites the mage hand.

Caelynn uses ice to freeze the base of the rock and make a platform.

Bonogo dives in the water, Baergrom loses the rope.

Bonogo flows downstream and has a wonderful zen underwater adventure.

Stafl gathers town support, nets are dropped across the bridge flow ways.

Ephanna’s mage hand succeeds the second time and lassos Bubbles, lead her to Ephanna.

Karsemine uses Zephyr strike,

Ready for some rest, ready to not be covered in the sweat and ichor of the day, Caelynn, Karsemine, Stafl, Baergrom, and Ephanna accepted the offer of a ride to StoneBridge with Pippa. Pippa a bright and talkative Gnome obsessed with crafting and understanding beer, hitched up Bubbles the Float Goat to her wagon full of kegs and led the crew to a dock and barge on the river. Along the way down river, they encountered an elderly fisherman, whose net was stuck. Without missing a beat Bonogo out of selflessness and hopes for a giant fish to eat, dove into the water to find the damned net didn’t have a fish, stupid useless net. With some trial and error and stradling of boats Stafl and Baergrom I think pulled the net to surface to see it looked to be the corner beams of a roof.

Once at StoneBridge and unloading beer, it became clear a storm was coming. Hitching up Bubbles and the barge, into the River’s Edge Pub for a welcome round of beers and some bragging to Grishna about their adventures. Inside was the evening crew, Grishna, patrons, and another bard! Who Stafl immediately joined in a jam with that undoubtedly demonstrated his superior song craft and bardic talents. After some excellent drinks and music, Pippa realizes the loud drumming isn’t just the bard, but a torrent of rain on the roof excuses herself to check on Bubbles. Gone for a few moments, during a pause in the retelling of the day’s events, weeping is heard from another table, followed by a piercing yell in between cascades of rain off the roof.

“BUBBLES!”

Dropping everything and rushing outside, you found Pippa, soaked and panicked. Gripping onto the front of the pub holding on in rushing water up to her waste. “Help! I can’t find Pippa” she babbles about a broken lead. Rapidly searching around Bubbles is found wildly bleating on a rock in the river. Out on a rickety dock, shaking in the flood and downpour, the river frothing, rapids crashing all around as it wildly rushed.  a lasso was made and handed to a mage hand Ephanna created. Stafl runs for help, and gathers townfolk to set nets across the river. Bonogo and Baergrom prepare for Bonogo to dive in. Caelynn desperately fires ice bolts to freeze a platform around Bubble’s rock. Stafl began banging on doors and assisting townfolk escape the flood and gather at the pub on high ground.
Sending the mage hand and rope out to Bubbles, the Float Goat panics and bites, destroying the mage hand and dropping the rope into the water. Desperate to save (maybe to eat, raw? Or maybe in a curry? Thoroughly fermented) Bonogo dives into the river with full trust in Baergrom to hold the rope, which is immediately torn out of Baergrom’s hand. Unprepared for the speed with which the river carries Bonogo away, the rope and Bonogo are gone. The only remenant, the end of the rope whipping across the top of the river, vanishing out of sight.

In the face of a rapidly deteriorating situation, Karsemine cast Zephyr strike and ran down the bank, shooting arrows at wear Bonogo probably was. Stafl and Caelynn cut loose a dinghy and let the river take them after Bonogo, with only a net with light cast on it and hope. Ephanna and the mage hand coaxed Bubbles to the lasso and were able to get her to the dock across the ice path and rushing river. Where Ephanna hopped on Bubbles back and followed after the runaway boats.

Bonogo was rescued, the wet adventurers headed to the Pub to find a damp, but generally relieved, safe, and drying town. Collapsing to sleep, they woke up the next day with the most important question of all. What now? Sure they were heroes of Stone Bridge, and definitely the saviors of Bubbles the Float Goat, but so what? What about that city Pippa was talking about, where they were having a festival? Mirathorn, that might be interesting… Long way on foot, but not that far. Maybe a bit more than a week. Not much to do around Stone Bridge unless you are into rebuilding and staying still.
