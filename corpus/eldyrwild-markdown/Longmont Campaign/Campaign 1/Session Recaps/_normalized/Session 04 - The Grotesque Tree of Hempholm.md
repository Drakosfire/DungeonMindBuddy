# Session 4 Recap

With little interest in being part of the rebuilding of Stone Bridge the heroes begin their ~5 day trek on foot to the West toward Mirathorn. After some time on this bucolic walk past fields, with an ominous forest to the south they encounter the hemp caravan led by Torvak Hempdealer. In between pitches of all the glorious ways hemp could improve the heroes lives Torvak also mentioned that his crew felt they had left the town of Hempholm just ahead of disaster, telling a story of a tree growing huge overnight and seeming to move. Without so much as considering buying any of Torvak’s incredible Hemp, the team marched on eager to investigate this bizarre tree.

Camping overnight, being disturbed by some wild board while definitely being 100% awake during watch, then starting the next morning with bacon, the crew makes their way to Hempholm. Where they find a tall tree, a sad, somewhat beaten boy named Torbin, his rightfully irate father Jove, and set out to study this bizarre and grotesque arcane moving tree with no leaves.  While studying the tree and chatting with Torbin and Jove they learn the tree has a huge reach and swats Karsemine knocking her to the ground. Undeterred the group continues discussing and considering the tree when a group of drunken townsfolk wander from the shacks, bolstered by drink and sick of the fear of the tree, they began to set about attacking it and burning it to the ground. They are talked out of their insane plan by Stafl who makes friends and distracts them. This distraction only lasts a few minutes, when the ground begins to shake and the tree suddenly grows 10 additional feet, which become 10 additional range, and the tree sets about trying to pummel anyone it can reach. Baergrom is lifted off his feet, and just before being flung into the distance, smashes the branch gripping him into splinters. Ephanna making the decisive choice, starts blasting. In very little time, the tree is felled and a number of precious metal leaves are found.
Bonogo, always looking for something of value drags the broken semi organic metal branch to the blacksmith in town and begins to dicker and trade to have it worked into spiked metal for his leather armor.

The town is overjoyed at the death of the tree and gathers for a drunken revelry that stretches into the evening hours and gets the crew good and drunk. Caelynn abstains from the drink but not the festivities. At some point some asshole human named Steve makes some shitty remarks about non-humans. As the the hours of party stretch and folk are gathered around bonfires the ground begins to rumble anew. The shaking turns to bellows and screams as dozens on dozens of root like beatles crawl up from tunnels underneath the town. Townsfolk begin to fall, Steve the racist is shredded and knocked into a fire and a night of revelry turns to nightmare.
