# Session 14 Recap

The party gathers together with Head Clerk Mossglade to gather some items. Mossglade hands out some valuable potions, scrolls and oils. Then she goes to look for the important items she promised the group. But bad news. She discovers that all the items have been given out to the covert ops teams that already left. However, she does discover some old dusty items that may be useful.

The items are legendary. After each of the party members looks over the items and picks one for themselves, they discover that these new items are incredible. With the new items, potions and scrolls the team feels confident in starting their journey into the tunnels.

The group enters a dark tunnel. Bonogo and Ephanna’s Sprite take the lead and look for traps. Before too long they encounter the first trap, a spot on the floor that can easily be avoided. Just past this trap Bonogo finds a locked door. Looking through the keyhole he is able to see another long dark tunnel beyond. Using his skills and some tools, Bonogo picks the lock and the group continues on.

What they find at the end of the tunnel is a large cylindrical room with old storage crates and supplies scattered around and rotting. They can hear water below them rushing toward a giant fan at one end of the room. There is a stone bridge over the water directly in front of them and an old wooden bridge near the fan. Faintly, they can also hear drums and chanting, but they cannot pick out the direction.

Among the rotting crates and supplies they notice the remnants of a recent battle. Everywhere they look they see glowing runes detecting corrupted meat. Karsemine’s new Scimitars begin to glow, something only she can see, indicating humanoids are present. She discovers a Half-Orc barely clinging to life. Baergrom uses a health potion to revive him, hoping to gather some information or even a new ally.

The Half-Orc is very relieved to see reinforcements, but he is visibly shaken. He describes a terrible battle with a monster that killed everyone. He tells the group that they had made their way down the tunnel, but had retreated to look for backup before they were attacked. Before he goes back up the tunnel to the Mage’s tower he has one final warning: trust no one.

Ephanna begins searching the bodies for anything useful. The Sprite frantically returns to Ephanna to warn of a bomb attached to the underside of the bridge. As the group begins looking for the tripwire, the door on the opposite end of the bridge opens. Two guards in full armor enter with two flesh blobs following. Then four enormous corrupted sewer rats climb out of the water on each side of the party.

Immediately one of the blobs throws a glob of goo, missing Baergrom but hitting Bonogo for 1 acid damage. Stafl attempts a crossbow shot, but also misses. Caelynn then casts Magic Missile at each of the blobs. Next Ephanna casts Eldritch Blast on the blob dealing huge damage. As the blob is destroyed it explodes and deals 6 acid damage to the nearby guard.

Bonogo attacks the nearest 2 rats. He makes quick work of both rats. Karsemine and Baergrom attack the opposite group of rats. Karsemine slices up the first rat and Baergrom smashes the second rat right through the grate. Meanwhile, the Sprite continues to look for the tripwire, finding it halfway across the bridge. Both guards attempt to throw spears at Karsemine, but one is blocked by Baergrom and the other falls harmlessly into the water. Seeing the situation is not going their way, the guards quickly run back through the door and lock it behind them.

After a hit from Stafl’s crossbow and another Eldritch Blast from Ephanna the second blob is destroyed. Bonogo is first to the locked door and tries again to pick the lock, but cannot manage to get open. Karsemine also has thieves' tools, but not enough movement to make it to the door before the guards get away. She is able to jump on Baergrom’s back and then make the final movement to the door, and she is able to get the door unlocked and immediately casts Hunter’s Mark on one of the guards. Stafl again gets a hit with the crossbow, but Caelynn misses a frost attack. Ephanna hits with another Eldritch Blast and Bonogo finishes off the guard.

Now the group finds themselves at another locked door at the end of the tunnel. Beyond the door they can hear loud drumming and chanting. Looking through the keyhole they can see a large circular room where meat is being loaded into carts by a line of cultists guarded by Elite Guards, several other guards and a couple of sewer meat monsters. After several different attempts at picking this lock with no success Ephanna uses his lock spell to unlock the door, but taking 9 damage in the process.

Now it's time for something big. Karsemine cracks open the door and, using one of the scrolls from the Mages, casts Fireball! The spell lights up almost the entire room dealing major damage to all but two enemies. Each of the Elite Guards take damage, the monsters take damage, all the Cultists in the room die and the large pile of meat in the center catches on fire. Immediately Caelynn casts Sorcerer’s Burst and deals damage to an Elite Guard. Stalf then casts Sleep on the other Elite Guard while Ephanna casts Eldritch Blast on the third Elite Guard.

Bonogo kills the sewer monster in one strike. With his bonus action he deals major damage to the second one, but causes a small chunk of meat to fall off. Karsemine, using her new scimitars, kills the first Elite Guard then kills the second one that was asleep. Then she is hit by a spear from one of the guards. Before she can even react, she is hit with a second spear thrown by another guard. Reeling from the damage she backs up into the remaining sewer monster. The first attack misses, but the second hits, and worse it grapples her and slams her to the ground.

Just as she is about to be thrown over the side and into the rushing water she casts Hellish Rebuke, covering the monster in hellfire. Things are starting to look up when suddenly the meat in the center platform turns into a monster; sending out flaming tentacles and releasing Meat Flies. Baergrom throws a potion of healing to Karsemine, then smashes one of the meat flies. The meat flies start digging into the corpses around the room, some attack Karsemine, and one Charms Baergrom.

Caelynn casts Sorcerer’s Burst, Ephanna casts Eldritch Blast, and Stafl lets loose another bolt from his crossbow on the flies. Karsemine uses her Horde Breaker skill and deals damage to multiple flies. Bonogo attacks the flaming meat pile, however he becomes Charmed. Luckily for Karsemine, Baergrom misses his attack on her. Caelynn, seeing the charmed allies, decides to attack both of them with Magic Missle to end the Charmed effect.
