## Where the players left off (GM reflection)

The **forest crisis at Mossford is resolved** in the town’s favor: the ditch-fires worked; the trees **withdrew and turned east**. The Questionable Company got their public thank-you — and then **named their own departure**, cutting off Marla’s ask for justice on Bonogo before it could become a town trial.

**Lysandra** is **with the party again**, physically found at a **half-unpacked wagon** in the wild. She was **under cult-tinted influence** (shimmer eyes); **Caelynn’s tea** broke the surface of it. Lysandra remembers **voices in the dark** after the party entered the forest, **gaps in time**, **meat-smell** while trying to sleep — and she had been **drawing** something she called a **tower** and “where the voices are.” That drawing is still **player knowledge** as a lead, not necessarily literal truth.

**Supplies:** **Stafl** confirmed **tainted meat mixed into the jerky**; the compromised lot has to be **burned** (bacon was clean). That raises **Mirathorn trust** questions — not abstract anymore.

**Comms:** **Caelynn** reached **Sara** on the rockie-talkie; Sara is **shaken** about who in the city can be trusted. The line was **transferred to Professor Tealeaf** — **no answer yet**. Last beat: Caelynn **still waiting on the line** while the camp settles.

**Weather (canon):** The **magical storm** is **building to the northeast** and **moving on a southwestward track** — **Karesmine** reads it as carrying **shimmering rain**. The party is **pitching real shelter** for **people and animals** — this is not a lazy long rest in a dome; it’s **exposed camp** with pressure incoming.

**Ephanna’s declared intent (end of session):** **Disguise** and a **supply run back to Mossford** — not yet played at the table when Session 20 stopped. Session 21 can open **at that moment** (camp fire, line ringing, storm on the horizon) or **after a short skip** (Ephanna already in town — use `Session 21 - Mossford Saltfen rumor shop.md`).

## Player-facing intro — full read-aloud

Use after greetings, before “What do you do?”

> Mossford is behind you — still cheering, still digging in, still wondering what it means that the forest **turned away** instead of swallowing them. Out here, the world feels bigger and less impressed by your victory.
>
> The camp is **real work**: lines tightened, canvas snapping in a wind that has opinions, animals complaining in that honest way that means they’re scared of the sky. **Karesmine** keeps glancing **northeast**, where the mass is still assembling — a system **riding southwest** like something driven by a hand on a map. It doesn’t look like weather you outlast by ignoring it — the clouds have a **shimmer** in them, like heat… or like something **remembered wrong**.
>
> **Lysandra** sits wrapped in a blanket she didn’t ask for, hands still smudged where she tried to **scrub dirt** off ideas that weren’t entirely hers. She’s present. She’s quiet. Every so often she looks at the ground like it might **answer** if she stares hard enough.
>
> Nearby, the smell of **smoke** isn’t comfort-food — it’s **provisions dying on purpose**. What you can’t safely eat anymore is being fed to the flames so it can’t feed anything worse.
>
> And then there’s the **rockie-talkie**, stubbornly alive in **Caelynn’s** palm: line open, city distant, **patience wearing thin**. You got **good news** and **bad news** through **Sara** — relief and suspicion in the same breath — and then the handoff to **Tealeaf**… and the kind of silence that isn’t the same as *nothing happening*.
>
> It’s still **early enough** in the day that choices feel expensive. You have **road** ahead — more than a day’s worth if you’re honest — and you have **people** here who will need **gear, cover, and luck** before that sky arrives.
>
> **Ephanna**, you already said the shape of your next move: **a face that isn’t quite yours**, and a walk back into a town that thinks you’ve **left**. The rest of the company can breathe, plan, argue, listen to the wind — or follow you into the edges of that decision.

**Then:** Ask the table **where they want the camera first** — camp, the line, Lysandra, the fire, Ephanna’s departure — and run from there.
