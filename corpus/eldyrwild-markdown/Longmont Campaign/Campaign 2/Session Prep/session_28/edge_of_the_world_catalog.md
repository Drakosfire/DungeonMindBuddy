# Session 28 — Edge of the World catalog

## Purpose

Collect what has actually been established or deliberately decided about **Edge of the World** before expanding it for the Campaign 2 endgame.

Labels:

- **PLAY CANON** — happened or was stated at the table in a session recap.
- **PLANNING LOCK** — deliberately fixed in prior campaign planning but not necessarily revealed at the table.
- **GM LOCK (S28)** — fixed by the GM during Session 28 planning.
- **OPEN** — not yet decided; do not silently fill.

---

# 1. Core identity and geography

## Name

**PLAY CANON:** The settlement is called **Edge of the World**. Sheriff Roderic Marr first named it to the party in Session 21 while warning about strange reports from farther north.

**GM LOCK (S28):** The name refers to its strategic/civilizational position: Edge is the **last place where Mirathorn and its military institutions meaningfully project influence** before the swamp/fen and the much less controlled north.

This makes the name partly literal and partly political. Beyond Edge, Mirathorn's ability to patrol, maintain infrastructure, enforce policy, or reliably protect settlements falls away.

## Position

**PLAY CANON / prior reference:** Edge is north of Mireward, on the outskirts of the swamp/fen.

**PLANNING LOCK:** Edge is the last Reach outpost before the swamp/fen.

**OPEN:** Exact map coordinates and exact travel time from Mireward are not cleanly fixed. Older travel material contains rough estimates that should not be promoted into exact geography without review.

Relevant source tension:

- Edge hub: Sheriff Marr estimated roughly a week to reach Edge from Mossford.
- Journey tracker: lists Edge at roughly 7–8 days from Mirathorn, explicitly as an estimate.

Treat both as loose travel-era estimates, not precise geometry.

# 2. Edge before the siege

## A neglected frontier outpost

**PLANNING LOCK:** Edge had become visibly **run-down** before the current crisis.

Crown / Mirathorn maintenance support had thinned. Mireward had also spent years resisting the levies used to support the northern defensive network. The local political sneer was effectively:

> "We won't pay to arm a ghost fort."

Mireward later sent a civilian support column north partly from guilt, pride, and the desire to prove that it had not actually abandoned Edge.

**PLANNING LOCK:** That support column consisted primarily of civilians rather than a military relief force: cooks, menders, tally-clerks, teamsters, shrine-tenders, and charter folk.

## Military / Reach presence

**PLAY CANON:** Private Hester wears the **Elderwyld Reach** uniform, which Stafl recognizes from his own former service.

**PLAY CANON:** Hester tells Lieutenant Lysandra to speak with **Commander Vale** before going into the swamp.

**INFERENCE ONLY / DO NOT PROMOTE YET:** These facts strongly suggest a formal Reach command presence associated with the northern frontier and probably Edge, but the existing sources do not yet establish Commander Vale's exact post, Edge's garrison size, chain of command, fort layout, or whether Vale is physically inside Edge during the siege.

# 3. The warning signs

## Session 21 rumor stage

**PLAY CANON:** Sheriff Marr reports rumors from Edge of **cultists using music to convert people**.

The original Edge location stub preserved an ambiguity about whether this was Shepherd activity, Raucous Saints activity, both, or misreporting.

**CURRENT CONTINUITY:** Later play makes the original broad uncertainty substantially stale. Edge is now tied directly to the same meat/corruption phenomenon the party already knows from Mirathorn.

Do not delete the old rumor as history: it represents how little the south understood when the first reports arrived.

## Session 22 field warning

**PLAY CANON:** Private Hester is riding south at extreme speed carrying a sealed tube of reports for Mirathorn.

Her spoken report includes:

- mysterious music;
- people walking into the swamp;
- those people failing to respond when stopped;
- concern when shown the familiar glassy / oily eye symptom;
- the judgment that the party is far too small for the scale of what is happening;
- instruction to speak to Commander Vale before entering the swamp.

**PLAY CANON:** At the same time, the broader Reach is showing related anomalies: planar bleed, savory mist/rain, the Dustwalker melody traveling without an obvious source, and the party involuntarily humming it.

## Edge field packet

**PLANNING LOCK:** Before the party reaches Mireward, Hester first delivers a **detailed local Edge packet** to Mireward, then continues south with the separate sealed Mirathorn tube.

The local packet includes:

- Edge pinned / siege pressure;
- missing patrols;
- wrong-verse / anomalous music;
- glassy walkers;
- concrete logistical requests.

Named Mireward recipients:

- Reeve **Salla Vey**;
- Mayor **Orric Tane**;
- **Nera Coalstep**;
- **Lysandro Ironveil**;
- **Delwen Rast**.

Hester specifically insisted that Lysandro hear the warning.

# 4. Edge under siege

## What Mireward learns

**PLAY CANON (Session 23):** Shortly before the party arrives at Mireward, a dire message from Edge states that the settlement is **under siege by horrific monsters** and that survivors are heading south.

The message's creature descriptions match threats the party fought in Mirathorn: slimy flesh creatures, flying meat creatures, and changes to people's eyes.

Mireward initially reacts badly because decades of peace and lack of recent contact have made people complacent and skeptical.

## Refugee / survivor wave

**PLAY CANON:** A southbound group reaches Mireward's north gate while a pursuing flesh force closes behind them.

**PLAY CANON:** Brin Holloway is a cook from Edge and becomes the clear leader / spokesperson for the arriving survivors.

**PLAY CANON:** Brin says Edge was **surrounded and overrun by horrible meat monsters**.

**PLANNING + REFERENCE LOCK:** The arriving group is the returning **Edge support column** originally sent north from Mireward.

Count/state used in planning and then substantially reflected in play:

- about **55 civilians** arrive / are housed in Mireward;
- Brin's deliberately unreliable trauma-count is **58** at the last mile marker, **51** still answering to their names, and **fifty-odd** when he stops pretending the categories are stable;
- roughly half show glassy / worsening eyes in the planning model;
- Session 25 confirms Brin has separated clear-eyed people from oily-eyed people and that 55 refugees are housed at the Ironveil warehouse.

## What happened to people who fell behind

**PLANNING LOCK:** Brin's account is worse than ordinary pursuit. People who fall behind are not always simply killed. Witnesses describe some of them **joining the herd**, standing back up wrong, and walking north again.

This is consistent with Maelthor's assimilation rather than conventional predation.

# 5. Scale of the attack

**PLANNING LOCK:** Edge is **pinned** by a much larger Maelthor flesh force.

The monsters that pursue the refugees south toward Mireward are explicitly **not the main army**. Brin's framing is that they are what the siege line did not need.

Useful line from his seed:

> "The things behind us aren't the whole army. They're what the siege didn't need. That's what makes this worse."

This establishes an important strategic scale:

- the Mireward attackers can be deadly while still being only a flank / surplus force;
- the main concentration remains farther north around Edge;
- Edge is therefore the first known settlement facing a true large-scale Maelthor siege rather than an isolated outbreak or infiltration.

# 6. Relationship to Maelthor and the Meat Mind

## Current GM truth

**GM LOCK (S28):** The siege of Edge is an outward expansion of the **Maelthor infection centered on the former human swamp settlement**.

The human settlement / flesh-pit colony is the deeper source. Edge stands between that source and the more stable Reach / Mirathorn sphere of influence.

**GM LOCK (S28):** The **Mireward Meat Mind is not an independent new Maelthor colony or unrelated intelligence.** It is an **extension / forward organ** of the same Maelthor organism / flesh network involved in invading and sieging Edge.

A useful strategic picture is therefore:

`Maelthor breach + converted human settlement` → `mature flesh colony / invasion mass` → `siege of Edge` → `forward extensions / flanks / Meat Mind pressure at Mireward`

Do not interpret the arrows as requiring one simple chain of command. Maelthor remains distributed and biological. The point is provenance: the Mireward pressure belongs to the same northern growth front.

## Why Edge matters to Maelthor

**INFERENCE, NOT YET LOCKED:** Edge may not have been selected because Maelthor hates Mirathorn or understands military geopolitics in a human way. It is simply the first major organized concentration of people, infrastructure, defenders, and resources encountered as the northern colony expands south/outward.

The Shepherd-derived human cognition inside the network may make Edge's military significance increasingly legible to Maelthor, but the degree of deliberate strategic understanding remains open.

# 7. What Edge is *not yet* defined as

The corpus currently does **not** give us a proper Edge gazetteer.

Still open:

- exact population before the siege;
- species / demographic makeup;
- exact age of the settlement;
- street / district layout;
- wall, fort, palisade, tower, trench, or gate design;
- exact garrison strength;
- exact relationship between civilian government and Reach military command;
- Commander Vale's full identity, rank history, personality, or precise current position;
- who the civilian leader of Edge is;
- major businesses, shrines, inns, crafts, or landmarks;
- exact refugee count originating in Edge itself versus the returning Mireward support column;
- exact casualties / remaining defenders;
- whether Edge is merely surrounded, partially breached, or already substantially occupied;
- exact travel time from Mireward;
- exact location of the road relative to fen / swamp geography.

Do not accidentally infer these from the words **outpost**, **ghost fort**, or **under siege**.

---

# 8. Current concise GM model

**Edge of the World is the last meaningful outpost of Mirathorn / Reach influence before the swamp.** It had been allowed to decay through distance, complacency, reduced maintenance, and southern political unwillingness to keep funding a remote frontier post. Early reports of strange music and people walking into the swamp were initially treated as dubious frontier stories. By Sessions 22–23, the truth is unmistakable: the same flesh-corruption phenomenon known from Mirathorn has expanded outward from the converted human swamp settlement and placed Edge under a full siege. Mireward's civilian support column fled south, many already infected, while only a surplus flank of the attacking force followed them. The larger Maelthor mass remains concentrated around Edge. The Meat Mind now threatening Mireward is a forward extension of that same northern invasion network, not a separate origin.
