# Session 22 — travel to Mireward (runbook)

**Session endpoint:** Party **arrives Mireward** (gate line or first night inside walls) unless table chooses delay.  
**Start:** Conical-hill camp north of Mossford (~**5 days** to Mireward at S21 end).  
**Design source:** `Making Travel Sessions Memorable and Fun in TTRPGs.md` (repo root) — principles below, not random-hour grinding.

**Companion docs:** `session_22_planning_anchor.md` · `session_22_prep_brief.md` · `Mireward_PLACE_BUILD_SCAFFOLD.md` · `Journey - Mireward Reach (Campaign 2).md`

## 1. Design contract (what we're doing / avoiding)

| From travel guide | This session |
|-------------------|--------------|
| **Sensory narration every ~10 min** | Rotate **Reach sensory palette** (§4) **and weather beat** (§3) each march beat |
| **Discoveries & landmarks** | **1 player road roll per march day** (§6 **R5** + beat band); not hourly rolls |
| **Point-crawl structure** | **4 march beats + arrival** (§5) — each node = **rolls + mandatory NPC** |
| **Meaningful choices** | **1 player dilemma roll per march day** (§7 **T-DIL**); **gate roll** at arrival (**T-DIL-G**) |
| **Avoid roll-to-punish** | Weather (§3.4) and comms raise **pressure**; players choose **response**, not endless saves |
| **Campfire RP** | **1 prompt per night camp** (§8) — tie to Mirathorn silence / north pressure / wrong rain |
| **Dungeon room 0** | Road + approach **foreshadow Mireward** — apron looks fine, haylofts don't (scaffold §B) |
| **Avoid** | Fighting every d100 row; confirming Dustwalker; resolving Mirathorn off-screen |

## 3. Weather spine *(first-class — not garnish)*

Weather is **literal pressure** (mud, visibility, camp quality) and **metaphor** (north closing in while Mirathorn sings). Treat it like a **second clock** running beside distance to Mireward.

### 3.1 Table canon at Session 21 end

| Fact | Source |
|------|--------|
| Party caught the **edge** of the gathering storm — soaked, not destroyed | S21 recap |
| Rain pools show **magic sheen**; smells **savory**, not petrichor | S21 recap |
| **Caelynn** felt the storm **still building** after drake nest | S21 recap |
| **Ephanna** felt **strange magic forming** on the wind | S21 recap |
| Party sought **shelter** on conical hill; **Thrin** competence relevant | S21 recap |
| **Double rainbow** → drake detour (weather tied to wonder, not combat grind) | S21 recap |

**Campaign context (reference, not player lecture):** Ledger §3 — **magical storms over Mirathorn** felt *wrong* to casters. This Reach front may be **the same system unwinding north**, a **cousin storm**, or **fen weather** — keep ambiguous until you want a reveal.

### 3.2 Geography — why weather matters here

- **Stormspire west:** Clouds **stack against the range**; the Reach is **open plain** — no city walls, few hedgerows. When it breaks, the party **feels it first**.
- **Conical hills:** Only reliable **high camp** (S21 pattern). Pond crowns **sympathize** with storm magic (night camp d100 — wet stone smell, pressure pulses).
- **North (fen-ward):** Humidity rises before you see reeds — **smell before sight**. Fog sits **low** while sky clears wrong.
- **Mireward:** Walls mean **refugees compress** when it rains — hay in chapel, queue in mud (scaffold §B).

### 3.3 Five-day weather track (narrate one beat per march day)

Use **one row per day** as default narration spine. Pull detail from **Reach d100 §21–30** or **conical hill night camp §21–30** when a watch needs texture.

| Day | Beat | Sky & wind | Ground & body | Magic tell | GM tool |
|-----|------|------------|---------------|------------|---------|
| **1** | Aftermath | Bruised anvil clouds **west**; wind picks up by afternoon | Shoulders **mud**; wagon ruts hold **shimmer puddles** (savory) | **Shimmer in raindrops** — Mossford echo (R5 **#82**) | Thrin reads **wrong bird silence** before gust |
| **2** | Main front | **Sideways rain** (R5 **#74**); optional **hail** (#78) | Ditch crews filling washouts (#32); **T-DIL** roll | **Static** in hair/banners (#90); thunder **before** lightning (#80) | **T-NPC** + **T-DIL** at camp |
| **3** | Wrong calm | **Heat lightning** horizon (#86); **mirage** spires (#60) | Road **steams** then **fogs** at dips (#72) | **Ephanna:** magic “not storm-colored” | Optional **double rainbow** callback (#87) — promise under it? |
| **4** | Fen breath | Low **fog**; wind carries **reed/decay** | Boots soft; animals **skittish** at dusk | Well-water **copper** rumor if you hit hamlet (#150) | Refugees **huddle** — storm last night as backstory |
| **5** | Arrival | **Squall line** at gates **or** brittle clear cold | **Gate queue in mud**; smoke hangs low | Rockie-talkie **static** peaks; bells compete with thunder | Mireward **interior** = dry sought after wet |

**Pace knob:** Compress days 1–2 into one beat if time is short — still hit **shimmer rain + one hard choice**.

### 3.4 Weather-driven choices (agency, not saves)

| Situation | Choices | Consequence |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Front arriving early | **Hill camp** (Thrin) vs **roadside ditch** vs **push to hamlet** | Rest quality; encounter type; time loss |
| Sideways rain day | **Wagon cover** vs **walk beside** vs **Bonogo scout-flight** (gust hazard?) | Visibility; flight cooldown spent |
| Shimmer puddles | **Collect sample** vs **avoid contact** vs **Arcana read** | Ledger foreshadow; Ephanna/Caelynn spotlight |
| Static / comms | **Retry Mirathorn call** in lull vs **wait** vs **send rider south** | Lysandra beat; no city truth without R2 |
| Arrival squall | **Wait out rain** (lose half day) vs **queue in mud** vs **bribe/skip line** | Gate scene tone; Maera floor space |

**Avoid:** “Make a CON save every hour or take damage.” **Prefer:** “The road is closing — what do you do before night?”

### 3.7 Mireward arrival — weather as place

Layer **before** §10 district walk:

1. **Smell shift:** rain on **lime/tannin** (Bracken & Lime) — acrid wet smoke, not sweet fen yet  
2. **Sound:** bell **duels** thunder; refugees **shout** under eaves  
3. **Sight:** **Palisade dark**, **mud to the ankle** at gate; smoke **too low**  
4. **Social:** “We’re fine” gate talk **while** families **drip** in tithe barn line  

**Image:** *The town looks like it’s weathering the storm. The haylofts say it isn’t weathering the people.*

## 4. Reach sensory palette (rotate; don't read all at once)

**Fixed geography:** **Stormspire mountains west** (same range since Mirathorn). **Grass sea east**. **Road** = crown-maintained braid, mud shoulders after rain. **North** = grass → reed → **fen smell** (sweet-rot, standing water, smoke wrong for the hour).

| Sense | Southbound (where they've been) | Approaching Mireward |
|-------|----------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Sight** | Conical hills, pond crowns, wagon ruts, mirage spires (hot mud) | **Palisade + bell** on horizon; **smoke too much** for one town; refugee lines on berm |
| **Sound** | Wind in grass; ditch crews; distant thunder without lightning | **Bell for Mirathorn festival** prayers; queue noise; **maybe** bass on wind (ambiguous — prep brief §6) |
| **Smell** | Savory/shimmer rain (S21); horse sweat | **Downwind craft** before walls — lime, tannin, charcoal (§10 Bracken & Lime) |
| **Touch** | Static in hair (storm inheritance); mud to the knee | Crowd press; **mud queue**; rain on lime kilns |
| **Feel** | Scale — sky big, city small behind | **Storm drove everyone inside** — apron cheerful / interior strained |

**Narration habit:** When ~10 minutes pass in real time or you finish a beat, pick **one** row from this table **or** advance **§3.3 weather day** — often both.

## 5. Point-crawl map (abstract)

Each **node** = one compressed march beat. At every node **except arrival**, the table rolls in this order:

1. **Weather beat** (§3.3 — GM narrates day row; not a die roll unless you add **T-WX** later)
2. **T-NPC** — mandatory NPC spotlight (player rolls)
3. **R5** — road encounter (player rolls; beat band in §6.2)
4. **T-DIL** — travel dilemma (player rolls)
5. **Night:** **R6** (per watch or once per night — §6.4) + **T-CF** campfire (player rolls)

```
[S21 camp] ──beat 1──► [Conical hill night] ──beat 2──► [Open road] ──beat 3──► [Approach road] ──beat 4──► [MIREWARD GATE]
     │                      │                      │                      │                      │
  T-NPC + R5(b1)        R6 + T-CF              T-NPC + R5(b2)       T-NPC + R5(b3)       T-DIL-G + §10
  + T-DIL               (repeat per night)     + T-DIL                + T-DIL
```

**Beat 0 (pre-march):** No rolls — name Mireward, set weather aftermath (§3.3 Day 1).

**Optional detour:** Pre-era hill pond (`pre_era_conical_hills_d20_find.md`) — costs half a day; **skip next R5 roll**; player still rolls **T-NPC** if you want the beat.

**Pace knob:** Merge beats 1–2 → one **T-NPC**, one **R5**, one **T-DIL**, one night block. Still roll — don't GM-pick rows.

## 6. Roll table registry *(metadata only — rows live in corpus files)*

This section describes **what to roll, when, and how tables are ordered by theme**. It does **not** author encounter text. Build missing tables as separate corpus files; register them in `Session Prep/session_22/README.md` when filed.

**Authoring contract:** `Event Table Design Guidance.md` (repo root). Apply §6.0 below when writing **T-NPC**, **T-DIL**, **T-DIL-G**, **T-CF**; use it to audit **R5** / **R6** at table.

### 6.0 Design guidance mapping

| Guidance principle | How Session 22 applies |
|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **Tailor to setting** | Reach = maintained crown road + storm + north undertone + Mireward crush foreshadow. No generic monster roster. |
| **Category / nested tables** | **R5** = top-level d100 by **band** (§6.2); beat routing = sub-window. **T-NPC** / **T-DIL** = ordered blocks, not flat random. |
| **Right-size for journey length** | ~5-day compressed session → **d12 / d8 / d6** for daily spotlight + dilemma + campfire; **d100** only for road (R5) and camp texture (R6) where variety earns prep. |
| **Weight common vs rare** | **R5:** bands 01–40 = mundane/common; 51–80 = uncommon/weird; 81–100 = rare hard turns (20 rows). **Small tables:** use block order (early rows = common daily beats) or **2d6→d12** map if you want a bell curve when authoring. |
| **Beneficial entries** | Require **≥25%** benign or helpful on **T-DIL** and **R5 beat windows** (aid, shelter, gossip, safe camp)—not a harm gauntlet. **T-NPC** rows should include competence wins (Thrin shelter, Caelynn read). |
| **Action-oriented rows** | Every row = **situation + spark** (who/what + why it matters + what players can do). Not lore paragraphs. |
| **Escalation on repeat** | **R5** file rule: same band twice → raise stakes. **T-NPC** / **T-DIL:** if duplicate in one journey, intensify (static worse, more witnesses, shorter time to decide). |
| **Player rolls at table** | Beats use **player dice** (§7)—not GM pre-roll. GM adjusts **framing and escalation**, not which row “should” have happened. |
| **Plot connection** | Rows may **foreshadow** Mireward / Shepherd / Mirathorn silence; must not **resolve** off-screen canon. |
| **Special slots** | **R5 81–100** = reserved rare / story-weight. **T-DIL 12** = highest-stakes north-trust. **T-DIL-G 6** = wildcard gate path. |

**Entry format (all new tables):**

```text
{Headline — one line} · {Sensory tag} · {Spark — question or choice} · {If ignored / repeat}
```

**Tone mix target (T-DIL d12):** ~4 benign/neutral · ~5 pressure · ~3 ominous (no auto-combat).

## 7. Roll procedure (quick reference)

**Per march day (beats 1–3):**

```
1. GM — weather day row (§3.3)
2. Player — T-NPC (d12)
3. Player — R5 (d100 within beat band, §6.2)
4. Player — T-DIL (d12)
5. Night — Player R6 (d100, §6.4) + Player T-CF (d8)
```

**Arrival (beat 4):**

```
1. GM — §3.7 weather at gate
2. Player — T-DIL-G (d6)
3. GM — §10 place reveal (district walk, Maera/Orin as needed)
```

**Minimum session bar:** 3× **T-NPC**, 3× **R5**, 3× **T-DIL**, 1× **T-DIL-G**, ≥2× **T-CF** (or 3 if three nights played out).

## 10. Mireward arrival — sense of place toolkit

**Weather first:** §3.7, then read order below.

### 10.1 First from the road (before gate)

1. **Smell:** lime-and-tannin **downwind** (**Bracken & Lime** yard — working name; charcoal kilns behind it). Not sewage — **honest stink-trades**.
2. **Sound:** charter **bell** (not alarm); **two** prayer rhythms — one for Mirathorn festival, one impatient queue clatter.
3. **Sight:** **Palisade dark against sky** — Stormspire still west; **smoke columns too many**; wagons parked **half a mile out** (capacity).
4. **Feel:** "We've been fine for **years**" energy — veterans at gate, **not** panic.

### 10.2 Gate scene (short-handed garrison)

**Gate voice (working):** **Sergeant Hald Voss** — emeritus, festival took the young spears, patrol **still out** (`[ name TBD ]`). Line: *"Road's open. Town's full. If you're Mirathorn heroes, help or wait — your choice."* Frame from **T-DIL-G** roll.

**Tell in 3 beats:**

- **Apron:** Clean-ish toll scale; **cheerful** sign — *Last dry bed before the smell*.
- **Truth:** Family with **goats in the chapel** story; clerk at tithe barn **won't open granary**.
- **North:** Fen reek beyond inner lane — **ferry** horns.

### 10.3 First steps inside (pick 2–3 districts)

Walk them past **named texture** — no map required:

| District | One line | Tool |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Reach Gate apron** | Wagons, mud, crown scale | **T-DIL-G** |
| **Downwind lane** | **Bracken & Lime** — lime kiln hiss, tanner vats, workers **not** hiding | §A2 stink-trades |
| **Well square** | Queue; **copper taste** rumor (optional 51–60 #150 callback) | Capacity |
| **Bell / shrine** | Candles for **Mirathorn festival** — town prays south while south ignores north | §C deaf south |
| **Tithe barn** | Hay in chapel; **overflow families** | Refugee crush |
| **Ferry head** | Reeds, fen smell, **horns** — "last hard ground" | Geography lock |

### 10.4 The Last Dry Bed (if party needs beds)

**Maera Vell** — tired, protective. **No rooms** — floor space if they **don't upset Orin**.

**Orin (upstairs):** humming **wrong verse**; family calls it **fen shock**. Maera: *"We don't turn folk away for being different — that's Lundayell talk, and it's the best thing they ever taught us."*

**Scene tools:**

- Singing through floorboards during watch
- Maera asks party to **take a message to Mirathorn** (they're going north — irony)
- Orin mutters **`[ one garbled true thing TBD ]`** — e.g. "the pit breathes" / "they're still singing"

**Do not** confirm cult at table unless players invest Investigation/social time.

### 10.5 End-of-session images (choose one to close)

- Party on **inn floor**, bell ringing **for a festival they're not at**
- **Gate closed at dusk** behind last refugee wagon — **still** not enough room
- Lysandra on wall walk, **no answer** on rockie-talkie, **Mireward** louder than Mirathorn ever was
