# Mireward Gate Dilemma d6

**Use:** Roll once at Mireward gate after the weather layer and before the first district walk. If the gate is skipped in play, use the same row at the first inn desk, tithe barn, or wall checkpoint.

**Row shape:** `{gate pressure} · {choices} · {what this reveals about Mireward}`

**Repeat rule:** If reused later, keep the same pressure but move it deeper into town: gate apron → tithe barn → Last Dry Bed → ferry head.

| d6 | Gate dilemma |
|----|--------------|
| 1 | **Help the queue: the axle line** · A refugee wagon breaks sideways in the mud and blocks the gate mouth while rain starts again. The party can help clear it, organize bystanders, wait, or demand the guards solve their own line. · Reveals Mireward’s garrison is not cruel, just short-handed; competent help earns quiet favor. |
| 2 | **Help the queue: the chapel goats** · A family argues that their goats were promised chapel shelter, while the clerk insists animals go outside the wall. The party can mediate, use coin, use rank, or side with policy. · Reveals the town is already rationing mercy by category: people, animals, locals, outsiders. |
| 3 | **Wait like everyone else: mud gossip** · The party waits in the same ankle-deep line as everyone else. They can listen, comfort people, trade supplies, or keep apart. · Reveals rumors before authority does: ransacked hamlets, strange meat, missing patrols, and resentment that Mirathorn notices late. |
| 4 | **Wait like everyone else: Last Dry Bed is full** · A runner announces that Maera Vell’s floor is nearly full before the party reaches the gate. They can reserve space by favor, look for public shelter, sleep rough inside the wall, or accept that someone else gets the dry floor. · Reveals Mireward’s crisis through logistics rather than exposition. |
| 5 | **Name-drop Mirathorn** · Someone recognizes the Questionable Company or Lysandra’s authority and the line parts too quickly. The party can accept, refuse, use the access to bring others through, or ask who recognized them. · Reveals local resentment: Mirathorn names still open doors, but people notice who gets to be exceptional. |
| 6 | **Side path wildcard** · Bonogo sees a scoutable wall gap, Lysandra can try rank at a side gate, and a tired guard clearly expects a bribe without quite asking. The party can scout, invoke authority, pay, expose the guard, or return to the main queue. · Reveals the first crack in Mireward’s self-image: it believes it is orderly, but pressure is already making little exceptions breed. |

## Table notes

- Sergeant Hald Voss can frame any row with: “Road’s open. Town’s full. If you’re Mirathorn heroes, help or wait — your choice.”
- These rows should not resolve the town’s core problem. They expose capacity, resentment, and the difference between apron order and interior strain.
- If players create a strong new fact at the gate, promote it after play through the Session 22 recap and Mireward scaffold checklist.
