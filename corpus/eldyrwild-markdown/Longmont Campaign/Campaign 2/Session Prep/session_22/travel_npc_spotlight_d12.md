# Travel NPC Spotlight d12

**Use:** Roll once per march day before the road encounter. This table forces one companion or PC spotlight so the journey stays inhabited instead of becoming weather plus distance.

**Row shape:** `{npc} · {trigger} · {ask of party} · {one action that changes scene}`

**Repeat rule:** If a row repeats during this journey, keep the same NPC but intensify the pressure: static worsens, the omen becomes easier to notice, more witnesses are present, or the decision must be made faster.

| d12 | Spotlight |
|-----|-----------|
| 1 | **Thrin Branchborn** · The grass goes quiet before the wind changes; even insects stop rubbing their legs. · He asks whether the party trusts his read enough to leave the road for a higher, uglier camp. · If they follow, he builds a lean-to in miserable ground that holds through the first hard gust; if they refuse, he marks where the storm will break and says nothing else. |
| 2 | **Thrin Branchborn** · A line of birds crosses the road, then wheels back south as if it struck glass. · He asks Ephanna or Karsemine to watch the horizon with him and name what they see that he cannot. · He finds a dry animal run beneath the hill-grass that can hide tracks, shorten the next mile, or shelter one frightened traveler. |
| 3 | **Caelynn** · Shimmer rain beads on her sleeve without soaking in, reflecting a sky that is not overhead. · She asks whether to test the droplets now, bottle them for later, or avoid touching them entirely. · A careful read gives the party the next weather shift before it arrives; ignoring it lets the weirdness remain only a smell and a bad feeling. |
| 4 | **Lysandra Ironveil** · Rockie-talkie static rises exactly when the road opens clear toward the south. · She asks for privacy to retry Mirathorn, or for someone to stand close enough to hear if her voice changes. · She either logs a clean failed attempt with soldierly precision or hears one half-syllable that makes her tighten her hand around the device. |
| 5 | **Lysandra Ironveil** · A refugee cart mistakes her rank for authority over the road and starts asking questions she cannot answer. · She asks the party to decide whether they are guards, escorts, witnesses, or merely travelers today. · Her chosen posture sets how the next strangers treat the group: as help, threat, bureaucracy, or rumor made flesh. |
| 6 | **Stafl** · Thunder answers the cadence of his footsteps, one beat late, like an untrained drummer learning his song. · He asks whether to make a joke of it, test it musically, or shut up before the road starts listening. · A tune can steady the group, draw a rumor from nearby travelers, or briefly make the wrong rhythm answer from the north. |
| 7 | **Ephanna** · The wind carries magic that does not feel storm-colored; it is colder in the teeth than on the skin. · She asks Thrin whether forests can lie, or asks the party whether magic gets judged before it hurts anyone. · Her read can separate weather, fen, and otherworldly pressure into three imperfect threads. |
| 8 | **Ephanna and Thrin** · A thorn snag catches Ephanna’s sleeve, then releases only when Thrin looks away. · One of them asks the party to decide whether this is a sign, a joke, or a boundary. · If honored, the road gives up a safer camp approach; if mocked, every branch near the next rest keeps clicking without wind. |
| 9 | **Thrin Branchborn** · He refuses to approach a hilltop pond that should be ideal shelter, staring at its still water as if it has spoken first. · He asks the party to choose: camp lower in mud, test the pond, or ask him what he heard. · If pressed gently, he gives one honest forest-rule answer; if pushed hard, he acts anyway but stops volunteering warnings for the next beat. |
| 10 | **Bonogo** · A break in the rain opens a clean scouting line, and his Boots of Crowing Wings practically beg to make a ridiculous noise. · He asks who wants him to scout, show off, or stay grounded because the gusts are mean. · A flight reveals road traffic, smoke, or a weird landmark; a poor or reckless flight turns into a very loud omen that everyone on the road remembers. |
| 11 | **Karsemine** · Fog gathers in the low ground while the high road remains clear, creating two routes that should not both be possible. · She asks whether the party values speed, visibility, or avoiding watchers. · Her navigation choice changes which table row gets framed first: public road pressure, private weirdness, or a delayed but safer camp. |
| 12 | **Party pick** · The fire, rain, or road singles out the quietest character: a shadow points toward them, a bug refuses to bite them, or a puddle reflects them older. · The player chooses who gets the question: “What about this journey is already wearing on you?” · Their answer gives the GM the next sensory motif to echo once before Mireward. |

## Table notes

- Thrin appears in rows 1, 2, 8, and 9; row 1 is the mandatory omen shape.
- Lysandra appears in rows 4 and 5; both keep Mirathorn unresolved.
- Rows are prompts, not scripts. Move them between march and camp timing as needed.
- Do not confirm Dustwalker, resolve Mirathorn, or make planning beats canon without Session 22 recap promotion.
