# Night watch d12 (T-WATCH)

**Use:** After camp is set on a **conical hill** (S21 pattern). Standard night = **four watches** (~2–3 hours each in-world; ~5–8 minutes at table per watch unless players lean in).

**Who rolls:** The player whose PC is **on active watch** rolls **d12** at the **start** of their watch. Read the row to that player first; others learn only what the watcher shares unless the row wakes the camp.

**Row shape:** `{what the watcher notices} · {optional check or action} · {wake camp? / quiet handle?}`

**Repeat rule:** Same row on one journey → intensify: closer sound, second witness, same omen on two watches in a row, static that repeats a phrase.

**Pair with:** `conical_hills_night_camp_d100.md` (**R6**) — optional **one** R6 roll per **night** (not per watch) if you want extra cone/pond weirdness after watches. **T-CF d8** once per night when the party is **together at fire** (after watch 1 ends, or before sleep).

| d12 | Watch |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | **Static in the dry** · Rockie-talkie crackles though the sky is clear; pattern almost regular, then stops. · **No check** — watcher chooses: wake Lysandra/Caelynn, log time, or ignore. · Quiet handle: one line of static that sounds like a name fragment; wake if they investigate. |
| 2 | **Wrong bird hour** · Insects or frogs stop mid-chorus for six heartbeats, then resume. · **Survival DC 12** (watcher only): omen before wind, or normal night noise. · Wake camp on fail by 5+ or if Thrin is sleeping nearby and fails — he sits up without being asked. |
| 3 | **Light on the road braid** · A lantern or firefly-bright speck moves **with** crown traffic below, not toward camp. · **Perception DC 13**: convoy, rider, or nothing there when looked at directly. · Do not wake unless speck climbs toward camp or party chose high visibility tonight. |
| 4 | **Pond crown pulse** · Hilltop pond dimples once when a coal pops in the fire — not from sound. · **Arcana DC 13** (or ask Ephanna if awake): geomantic sympathy vs weather. · Wake on success if watcher reads it as **warning**; otherwise note for journey tracker. |
| 5 | **Footing on the cone scree** · One pebble shift above camp, then nothing. · **Perception DC 14**: animal, person, or scree settling. · Wake on humanoid result or second shift next round; else log. |
| 6 | **Savory mist thread** · Thin ground mist smells of **broth and iron** (S21 echo) though rain is not falling. · Watcher chooses: circle camp, wake Caelynn/Ephanna, or mark direction on R1. · Wake if mist crosses the fire line. |
| 7 | **Comms lull** · Static drops; rockie-talkie sounds **almost clean** for one minute. · Watcher only: try call, fetch Lysandra, or let the minute pass. · If ignored, next watch **T-WATCH** or **T-CF** may carry half-voice residue — not Mirathorn truth. |
| 8 | **Two-knock rhythm** · Something knocks once from the grass, once from the opposite slope, then silence. · **Perception DC 12** to locate; **Stealth DC 13** to investigate without waking camp. · Failed Stealth wakes light sleepers; success gives one clue (animal, branch, refugee hiding). |
| 9 | **Visitor at the edge** · Eyes at bush line — fox by gait, or **[U]** wrong spacing between blinks. · No fight unless watcher pursues alone. · Wake if eyes approach bedrolls; otherwise fox/deer log. |
| 10 | **Dream bleed** · Watcher's thoughts drift to **gate queue mud, chapel hay, or Mirathorn lights** — not asleep yet. · **Wisdom DC 11** to shake off; on fail, watcher loses 10 minutes and cannot recall the gap. · Optional tell another PC at watch change; do not confirm as prophecy. |
| 11 | **North sound wrong** · Faint bass or drum on the wind, **not** storm direction; lasts three breaths. · **Survival DC 13**: weather, distant convoy, or **unidentified harmony** (Saints ladder rung 1 — no name). · Wake if party previously chose to investigate music; else watcher logs bearing. |
| 12 | **Watch chooses the sign** · Roller picks: shadow, smell, insect behavior, flame shape, or reflection. · GM gives **one** small sign aimed at that sense tied to today's **T-WX** row or an open thread (comms, north, Thrin). · May wake one named PC if sign touches their kit — player's call. |

## Table notes

- **Four watches:** Assign order at camp (e.g. Karsemine → Stafl → Ephanna → Lysandra). Bonogo flight scout does **not** replace a ground watch without cost (gust, noise).
- **3+ hour sessions:** Run **full four rolls** on nights 1–2; on arrival night in Mireward, swap to gate/inn watches or skip T-WATCH if scene is social.
- **Combat:** Only if watcher investigates alone and escalates, or an **[E]** R6 optional roll fires the same night — default T-WATCH stays atmosphere and choice.
- Do not resolve Mirathorn, confirm Dustwalker, or name the cult from watch rows alone.
