# Session 22 — Mirathorn Comms Retry d100

## How to use

- Roll when **T-DIL 7**, a night-watch comms lull, a Lysandra comms spotlight, or an explicit player call creates a real chance to reach Mirathorn.
- Player rolls **d100**; GM reads only the *Heard* portion aloud or paraphrases it as a wire fragment.
- Do not use this to replay the immediate S21 Frank hang-up or Lysandra no-answer unless at least one travel beat has passed.
- Pair with weather/static texture from the runbook or night-watch table when the call happens during storm pressure.
- Same band twice in one journey means shorter call, more static, worse tone, or a second overhear; never repeat the exact same words.
- The *GM truth* field is planning-layer guidance, not player-facing exposition.
- The wire never fully explains Mirathorn, Dustwalker, or the city-side rite. It gives fragments, pressure, and choices.
- If a call result becomes table fact, promote through the Session 22 recap after play.
