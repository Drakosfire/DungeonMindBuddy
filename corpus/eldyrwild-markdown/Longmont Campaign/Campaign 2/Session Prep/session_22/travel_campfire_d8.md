# Travel Campfire d8

**Use:** Roll once per night camp after R6, or instead of extra R6 rolls when the table needs a quieter scene. These prompts are for sensory texture and light player opportunity, not mechanics or NPC monologues.

**Row shape:** `{campfire image} · {open prompt} · {optional weird echo}`

**Repeat rule:** If a row repeats, ask the same kind of question through a different sense: sound, smell, touch, insect behavior, flame-shape, reflection, dream, or static.

| d8 | Campfire prompt |
|----|-----------------|
| 1 | **Static under the coals** · The fire pops in a pattern almost like the rockie-talkie trying to clear its throat. Who reaches for the device, who pretends not to notice, and who watches Lysandra? · In the last ember, a tiny red coal pulses once like a distant signal and goes dark. |
| 2 | **Bugs choose favorites** · The biting gnats are relentless, except around one person they refuse to touch. Who is spared, who is being devoured, and what theory does the group invent? · A ring of insects briefly forms the shape of a road, then scatters when named. |
| 3 | **NPC wants a small talk** · Thrin, Lysandra, or a player-nominated companion asks for help with something mundane: drying socks, sharpening a blade, fixing a strap, making tea. Who helps, and what question slips out while hands are busy? · The ordinary task works too well, as if the camp wanted that conversation finished. |
| 4 | **Sound beyond the firelight** · Something knocks once from the grass, then once from the opposite direction, then stops. Who goes quiet first, who jokes, and who listens past fear? · If nobody investigates, the same rhythm returns in a dream but softer, like a lullaby with the wrong bones. |
| 5 | **Vision in the flames** · For a few seconds the fire shows a place none of them are standing: a gate line, a wet chapel floor, a crowded inn stair, or Mirathorn lights through rain. Who sees it clearly, and what do they admit they hope it means? · The image vanishes if treated as prophecy; it lingers if treated as worry. |
| 6 | **Wrong rain smell** · Rain misses the fire but the smoke smells savory, metallic, and sweet by turns. Who remembers the first time this smell felt wrong, and who is tired of everything meaning something? · In the morning, one covered pot or cup tastes faintly of storm even if it was sealed. |
| 7 | **Festival elsewhere** · Far south, Mirathorn is probably lit and singing; here, socks steam on sticks and mud dries in ugly flakes. What part of civilization does each character miss least, and what part do they miss more than expected? · A stray spark rises like a festival firework, then curves north instead of up. |
| 8 | **Player chooses the quiet** · The roller chooses a character, NPC, object, or unanswered question. The camp gives it one small sign: a shadow, a smell, a reflected face, a restless animal, a sudden silence, or a bug bite in the shape of a mark. What scene does the player want for two minutes? · The GM echoes one detail from this answer once during the next march beat. |

## Table notes

- Keep scenes short unless players lean in.
- Good outcomes are allowed: warmth, laughter, useful honesty, better rest, or a shared joke can matter as much as dread.
- Weirdness should be present but not always hostile. The road is strange; it is not only cruel.
