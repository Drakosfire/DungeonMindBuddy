# Session 21 prep — brainstorming (unrefined)

Lets start with an brainstorming idea dump, not everything here works, this is just ideas. I don't want model assistance here, I want to have my unrefined prose stored and flagged as brainstorming.  This is prep for Longmont Campaign 2 Session 21

The experience that I want to set up for and I hoep to execute on or generally put in front of the players is to start rapidly upping the pressure. Working to drive them forward at a reckless rate. I want them to have to question whether they can do more "good" or "impact" by staying where they are and helping or if the most effective action is to press on towards the swamp, to try an work out what this drawing Lysandra made is, to get to the swamp. 

At this point in my imagination what was once the swamp community has become not a tower as they interpreted the map but a fleshy lovecraftian hole in the ground that becomes a dimension and mind bending trip to somewhere else that is not fun. I want the players to get there, see the hole this maddening maze into a dirt hole, that shows signs of becoming some kind of living corrupted meat thing. To ask where did all this meat come from and not like the answer. 

The ideas and thoughts I want to run through their heads is "if we had come sooner, this wouldn't be so bad, we took too long". I think 3 of the players introduce the idea at the beginning of campaign 1 that their villages had been destroyed. I used that to be the foundation of the Shephards Flock, in my imagination the Shephards flock has been assaulting the various far reaching frontier communities and enslaving and bringing their people back here to work on this pit and be consumed. So we need some breadcrumbs that will tell that story, NPCs and notes to find etc. 

To make any of that meaningful we gotta get there. Which means more travel. 

Some things I want them to encounter while traveling are deserted towns, that look like they were abandoned in a rush. 

So the planning we really need to do is, Have a storm start kicking up, but how to use that to get them to move on instead of hunkering down? Or do we just have storm building as a metaphor for things getting more tense.
