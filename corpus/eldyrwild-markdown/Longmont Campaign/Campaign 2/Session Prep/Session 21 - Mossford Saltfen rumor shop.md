# Mossford — Saltfen Dry Goods (Ephanna supply run)

**When to use:** Early in Session 21 (or first Mossford beat after camp). Ephanna has come back under **disguise** for **notions, cloth, dyes, ironmongery, weatherproofing** — whatever your table decided she needed before the storm.

**Why this shop:** Close to the **wagon yard / river landing** where word arrives before it gets polished by the mayor’s office. Locals who *talk* shop here.

**GM pointer — who the rumor really is:** If the party bites hard on “cult,” see `Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Factions/Raucous_Saints_of_the_Rolling_Longhouse.md` (counter-hymn road fellowship, **not** Shepherd doctrine).

---

## The shop

**Name:** **Saltfen Dry Goods & Notions**  
**Sign:** A salt-crystal painted white on teal board; smaller line underneath: *Thread · Tack · Tea · Trouble (no credit)*.

**Interior (read aloud):**

> The bell gives a thin jingle, like it’s apologizing for existing. The air inside is **dry** — paper, hemp, metal, and the sharp sweet of cheap dye cakes. Shelves climb to the ceiling on runner’s legs; a ladder on wheels blocks one aisle like it’s guarding a fortress. Behind the counter, bolts of cloth lean like tired soldiers. Someone has pinned a **charms-against-mice** above the scale: not magic, just superstition and confidence.

**What Ephanna can plausibly buy here:** Fabric for disguise work, needles, waxed thread, cloak pins, lamp oil, waterproofing grease, a kettle, trail biscuits, “respectable” gloves, a hat that isn’t heroic, cheap cosmetics or charcoal for weathering, pack patches, a secondhand cloak one size wrong (perfect for cover).

---

## Who runs it

### Yulla Saltfen (she/her), proprietor

**Age:** early forties. **Species:** halfling. **Vibe:** fast hands, faster mouth — the crisis week in Mossford left her **tired**, **richer than usual**, and **starved for novelty**.

**Want:** Move inventory, yes — but also **process** what just happened. The forest turned; the heroes shouted about leaving; the town is vibrating between **relief** and **shame**. Yulla talks because silence feels like another kind of storm.

**Hook for Ephanna specifically:** Yulla doesn’t need to recognize her. She *does* need to **perform** for whoever walks in — and if the customer is guarded, clever, or “too calm,” Yulla doubles down to get a **human reaction**. She’s also heard **enough secondhand Mirathorn gossip** to name-drop performance-trouble in a way that can snag anyone who lived through the Dustwalker beat.

**Voice sketch:** Warm, a little mocking, never cruel to children — **cruel to lies**, especially the polite kind.

## The rumor (core truth dressed as gossip)

**What actually happened (offscreen, GM):** The **Rolling Longhouse** (or a forward scout of the Saints) passed a **crossroads hamlet** or **ferry landing** a few days east along the main drift of travel — the direction your party is *thinking* about anyway. Locals experienced: **pipes**, **drums**, **call-and-response**, **free cups**, **laughter**, **a wagon that should not move**. Someone who only knows **fear-music** from cult stories misfiled it as **“another hymn like Mirathorn.”**

**What Mossford hears:** Third- and fourth-hand, embroidered.

## Scene structure (drop-in)

### 1) Threshold (1–2 min)

- Ephanna enters; Yulla looks up — **one long inventory of the stranger** (clothes, hands, whether they flinch at the bell).
- **No roll required** unless the disguise is actively contested; if it is, run your system here and continue either way — Yulla can be suspicious *and* chatty.

**Yulla opener (player-facing):**

> “Saltfen’s. If you need it and it don’t breathe, we’ve got it or we can insult you honestly about where to find it. What’s the road doing to you today?”

### 2) The purchase (play as you like)

Let the player state intent. Yulla **bundles** while she **talks** — the rumor lands *between* folds of cloth and counts of coin.

### 3) The rumor delivery (juicy — aim for Ephanna’s attention)

Use **one** of these angles depending on what your player leans toward:

**A — Fear-forward (Dustwalker-shaped):**

> “You’ve got careful eyes. Good. We just got careful all week and it still wasn’t enough. Listen — **Merrow’s boy** came in yesterday with a story from his uncle’s caravan run. Says they passed a **town** — not ours — where folk were **standing in the square like sleepwalkers with their mouths open**, all facing the same direction. And there was **music**. Not pretty music. *Organized* music. The kind that **makes your feet decide before your head does**.”

She taps the counter once, hard.

> “Now — Merrow’s boy is a dramatist. But his uncle isn’t. And the uncle said there was a **performer** in front of it all — tall, loud, **bell** like a priest who lost an argument with god. People kept saying it felt **holy**. That word scares me worse than ‘bandit.’ Bandits don’t ask you to **mean it** when you sing.”

**B — Hope-forward (same facts, different lighting — still hooks “cult” paranoia):**

> “Other travelers say different. Say it wasn’t a taking — it was a **feeding**, yeah, but like a **feast**: bread, beer, some fool on a stage making people **laugh** until they cried. Say a **wagon** came through big as a barn, wheels like millstones, **pipes** you feel in your teeth. That doesn’t sound like our problem… except Mirathorn taught me **joy can be a hook too**.”

She slides Ephanna’s parcel across.

> “So which story do you want it to be? Because my gut says both. That’s what makes it **interesting**.”

**C — Direct Mirathorn callback (if you want the Dustwalker ping explicit):**

> “They’re comparing it to that **red-string business** in the city — you know the type — **eyes closed, crowd breathing together**. Only this time someone swore they heard **bagpipes** and a **drum like a heartbeat**, not… whatever your trouble was called. I don’t know if that makes it **better** or **worse**.”

### 4) Optional second beat — another customer (30 sec)

If you want **pressure** without a fight: a **tired farmer** in the doorway, still muddy from ditches, mutters:

> “If it’s saints, I’ll take ‘em. If it’s *his* kind of saints, I’ll take a rope.”

Yulla doesn’t shush them — she **marks** it, eyes back on Ephanna.

> “See? **Mossford’s nerves** aren’t finished just because the trees turned east.”

### 5) Exit hooks (choose what the table earns)


| If Ephanna…            | You can offer…                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Presses for **place**  | A name: **“Hobble Reach”** / **“the old ferry at Callow’s Bend”** / **“two days toward the smell of standing water”** — pick what matches your map; keep it *directional*, not GPS. |
| Presses for **time**   | “Three days if you’re kind to your horse. Two if you’re lying to your horse.”                                                                                                       |
| Shows **alarm**        | Yulla softens: “Could be nothing. Could be **traveling church** without a leash. Could be **exactly** what you think it is.”                                                        |
| Buys her off with gold | Yulla adds a “gift”: **cheap saint-charm** (folk religion, not mechanical) — doubles as token someone might recognize later.                                                        |
| Keeps cold             | Yulla shrugs, already moving on: “Well. If you hear **pipes** and your heart gets **lighter**, tell your heart to shut up.”                                                         |

## Why Ephanna might care (table hints, GM-only)

- Party has **lived** the lesson that **music + crowd + ritual** can be a **weapon**.
- This rumor is **ambiguous on purpose**: it rewards **investigation** and punishes **assumption** — same thematic knife as “press on vs stay.”
- If she’s packing **disguise** for a longer road, she’s already in a **planning mind**; juicy intel is a **lever**, not noise.
