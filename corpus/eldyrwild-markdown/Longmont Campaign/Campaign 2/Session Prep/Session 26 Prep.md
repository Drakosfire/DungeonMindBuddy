# C2 Session 26 Prep

## Session intent

This session is about upping the pressure on the players, making them feel that doom is approaching. The walls wont hold as they are being attacked from underneath.

## Opening Scene

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> The bells and noise of [Mireward Reach](#dmb-ref:graph-node:loc_4) feel far away compared to the grinding beneath your feet.
>
> For a moment, the wall stills. The monsters that dragged Thrin under are gone from sight, swallowed by the earth they came from. Dust hangs in the air in a pale curtain, settling over shattered stone and churned mud. Somewhere below, something shifts with a wet, heavy scrape — not gone, only hidden.
>
> Then the ground gives a low, distant groan.
>
> It starts as a vibration in your boots, almost easy to miss if you’re not listening for it. A slow tremor runs through the wall, through the packed earth at its base, through every loose stone left standing from the first impact. The rumble grows a little louder, a little deeper, like some vast thing turning over beneath the town and finding purchase again.
>
> There’s just enough silence for the fear to settle in.
>
> Then the crack comes.
>
> A line of mortar splits along the wall, sharp as a whip-crack, and a section of stone buckles outward with a roar of collapsing weight. Dust and rock burst into the open as the wall caves in, and for an instant the darkness beyond it seems to breathe back at you.
