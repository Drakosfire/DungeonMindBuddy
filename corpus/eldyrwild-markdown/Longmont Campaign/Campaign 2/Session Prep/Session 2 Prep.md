### 🌆 Evening Events – Day 1

#### 🪕 5:00 PM – Bardic Showcase at the Glimmering Globe

A rotating stage with shimmering lights enchanted to shift color based on the music’s tone. Competing bards from across the frontier and Lundayell exiles perform for coin, contracts, and clout.

📝 **Sign-Up Bonus**: PC bards or performers can enter. Top 3 get:

1st: Platinum Chime of Harmony (Grants Advantage on Performance once per day).

2nd: VIP seat for the Magicians’ Midnight Show.

3rd: Free drinks for the next 24 hours at Copper & Quartz.

🎭 **Rumor/Hook**: One performer sings a haunting song about “The Shepherd’s Eyes”—the lyrics are veiled prophecy, hinting at cult movements or secret tunnels under the city.

#### 🎆 8:00 PM – Fireworks Display over Lake Mirathorn

Boats gather like stars on the water, and the skies above explode in magical color. Glittering dragons made of light, faerie-lights spiraling in celestial dances, and a final burst that silhouettes the Stormspire.

💥 **Interactive Elements**:

A child cries out, claiming to see “faces” in the fireworks. Possible hint at psychic interference or illusion magic gone awry.

Ephanna’s or Caelynn’s attunement to the arcane might let them detect subtle magical tampering—glyphs hidden in the sky pattern.

### ☀️ Day 2: Feasts & Forges

#### 🍰 9:00 AM – The Great Bake-Off Preliminary Rounds (Market Plaza)

Three enchanted cooking tents, each with magical ovens, guide contestants through a series of increasingly chaotic baking challenges.

🎂 Rounds:

Pies of Precision (sleight of hand, cooking tools).

Pastry Peril (dexterity-based baking during minor magical surges).

Secret Challenge: Incorporate a strange glowing fruit (from the Veiled Fen?) without poisoning the judges.

🍴 **Rumor/Hook**: One contestant disappears between rounds—no one notices at first. A trail of icing leads toward the alleys behind the butcher stalls.

Smiths from the Guilds demonstrate mastercraft techniques. Participants can try forging their own dagger or pendant.

🔨 PC Opportunity: Craft a +1 weapon hilt or infused trinket (1-hour activity, DC 15 Smith's Tools or Arcana).

📜 Lore Drop: A forged weapon on display bears ancient Abyssal runes—an old banner from the War of Boundary.

#### 🤸 1:00 PM – Acrobatic Extravaganza (Glimmering Globe)

Rope-walkers, enchanted spring boots, illusion trapeze—pure joy and danger.

🐐 Bubbles Sighted: The float goat, now a minor local legend, appears with kids doing tricks on her back.

🎪 Bonogo could absolutely join in—goblin and bugbear performers would love him.

#### 🍵 3:00 PM – Herbal Tea Workshop (Evergreen Apothecary)

Elara Greenleaf and Talia guide guests in blending herbs into calming brews and potions.

🌿 Tea of Clarity: Removes 1 level of Exhaustion if brewed successfully.

🧪 Optional Challenge: Identify a mushroom brought by Caelynn, which starts to pulse when brewed.

#### 🛡️ 3:30 PM – Trials of the Shield (Colosseum of the Storm)

Teams engage in a rotating gauntlet:

**Formation Defense**: Hold a shield line against summoned monsters.

**Rescue Trial**: Escort a “wounded” ally through obstacles.

**Final Clash**: Face a surprise elite enemy in the ring.

💬 The crowd cheers and bets; the city guard recruits from this event.

🪧 Watch for subtle sabotage—shield bindings cut, or illusion walls misdirecting runners.

#### 💃 6:00 PM – Unity Dance Rehearsal (Community Hall)

A coordinated multi-racial, multi-tradition dance meant to close the Festival the next day. Outsiders encouraged to join.

💞 Great RP scene for character bonding or goofy missteps.

🎼 Music faintly resonates with Caelynn’s panflute—there's a harmony of older magic in the Unity Dance.

#### ✨ 8:00 PM – Magicians’ Midnight Show (Stormspire Academy)

The climax. A secretive event attended by invitation or… infiltration.

**Attendees**: Archmage Lorien, Elara Swiftwind, emissaries from Stormspire.

🪄 Themes: Portals, illusions, forbidden knowledge.

**Plot Hook**: A magical act is sabotaged—an extra-dimensional rift tears open briefly, revealing a glimpse of Maelthor’s realm.

## Thread Fireworks Events

## 🌩️ Interruption at the Fireworks Show

### 🎇 Scheduled: 8:00 PM – "Fireworks Over Lake Mirathorn"

**Original Setup**: The city's magic and technology combine in an awe-inspiring pyrotechnic display over the lake. Animated lanterns drift, music plays, and reflections shimmer on the water’s surface.

### 🌩️ Sudden Disruption: The Sky Cracks Open

**8:17 PM** (Mid-show): Without warning, the skies twist into unnatural shapes. Clouds churn violently, forming a spiral above the lake. Cold rain lashes down.

Thunder rolls without lightning, and nearby mages or attuned PCs may detect something is wrong—this is not just weather.

**Arcana DC 14**: This isn’t a random storm. It echoes planar energy—something akin to the storm that flooded Stone Bridge.
 **Arcana DC 18** or relevant experience: It’s partially being channeled or amplified by a ritual from elsewhere. Not just chaotic—directed.

### 🧙‍♂️ Mage Response: Skyweaving

A trio of prominent mages from Stormspire Academy (perhaps led by Professor Cinderbranch or a new public-facing evoker) step up on floating disks.

They form a quick magical triangle, casting Skyweave, a high-level collaborative spell to **force clear skies** over Mirathorn for 1 hour.

Visual: Blazing white lines of glyphs arc upward, weaving like threads in a tapestry. Rain halts midair, parting in a dome over the city.

**NPC Flavor**: Elven Skycallers and human evokers mutter about how “this storm has a signature,” or “this wasn’t supposed to happen again…”

### 🎭 Opportunities for the Party

**Recognize** the magical storm from Stone Bridge. (Ephanna, Stafl, or Bonogo might be most attuned.)

**Speak with the mages** post-show—could open new investigations or side quests.

**Scout OutTown** where rain still falls—perhaps there's a ritual or cult anchor out there.

**Foreshadowing**: During the next day’s events, there are reports of other regions also being hit by strange weather. It's spreading.

### 🧩 Narrative Payoff / Links to Hanging Threads

Brings Back: The mysterious magical storm and its unnatural effects.

Hints At: The Shepherd or Maelthor cult reaching into elemental or planar forces.

Adds Pressure: The mages are strained. This storm could’ve ruined the festival—and something or someone tried to.

Political Impact: Some on the Council might see this as weakness or excuse to seize more power.

## Thread : Wynna, Karsemine and Zyla

## 🎓 Scene: Stormspire Academy – Post-Crisis Quiet

Zyla is overseeing the reorganization of magical wards and scroll distribution in the central atrium. The usual bustle is subdued. She’s tired, but alert. An arcane light flickers overhead as Karsemine approaches.

### 💬 Opening

**Karsemine**: “Professor Cinderbranch, I know this isn’t a priority… but there’s someone I want to speak on behalf of. Clerk Mossglade. She bent the rules for us once—helped get us into that duel.”

**Zyla** (without looking up):
 “Hm. Wynna? Model of rigidity, that one. If she bent a rule, I assume you bent the rest of the table to convince her.”

### 🧭 Middle Beat – Roleplay & Light Humor

**Karsemine** might mention the Bardic Inspiration, the blood-tile creation, or that Mossglade had been terrified of the consequences.

**Zyla** (smirking slightly, still focused on a levitating scroll): “She probably drafted her own disciplinary form in triplicate before helping you. Honestly, that girl should’ve been a Paladin, not a paper pusher. Loyal to rules, rigid to the core.”

She pauses for the first time, then turns to Karsemine.

**Zyla**: “But I’ve been watching the aftermath of your work. And I’ll be frank—I trust your judgment more than the rulebook right now.”

### ✅ Resolution

**Zyla** (soft but certain):
 “If she made a call outside protocol and it helped this city, then that’s the kind of instinct I want cultivated. I’ll see to it she isn’t reprimanded. Might even consider moving her off clerk duty. She’s got potential—just needs to stop being afraid of her own spine.”

She waves a hand as if closing the subject.

**Zyla**: “Tell her she’s fine. But if she ever tries to requisition ritual chalk with the wrong filing rune again, I’ll polymorph her into a pigeon.”

### 🎁 Optional Reward or Hook:

**Mossglade** later delivers a neatly wrapped magical item to Karsemine with a stiff note: “Thank you. Please do not mention this again.” Inside is a **Scroll of Sending**, or a magically bound parchment titled “Emergency Application for Arcane Favor” usable once.

## Thread : 🎤 Event: Bardic Showcase at the Glimmering Globe

**Time**: 5:00 PM
 **Location**: A floating, magically rotating circular stage under illusion-charmed lanterns that respond to tone and tempo. The audience sits in a crescent-shaped amphitheater spilling out into the square. Light musical enchantments subtly enhance ambient sound, but not the performances themselves—those must shine on their own.

### 🧪 Performance Mechanics

Each bard or performer must make a **Charisma (Performance)** check with the following modifiers or twists:

🎭 **Theme Bonus**: Choose a performance theme (Joy, Sorrow, Mystery, Protest). Gain **Advantage** if your performance resonates with the mood of the crowd (DM’s call or DC 15 Insight to guess).

🎶 **Instrument Use**: Using a proficient instrument grants +2.

🎵 **Originality Bonus**: PCs who create or perform original material get an extra +1.

💃 **Stage Flair**: Include acrobatics, illusions, dancing, etc. Make an extra check (e.g., Acrobatics, Arcana, or Sleight of Hand DC 14). Success adds +1 to the final Performance score.

🎼 **Scoring**: Total modifiers added to the Performance check. The top 3 performers are determined by the highest final totals. In case of a tie, a “Bard-Off” is called.

### 🪙 Audience & Judging

**Crowd Response**: You can add flavorful cues—cheers, gasps, or murmurs of “That’s the one from the Wolf fight!” for Stafl.

**Judges**: A mix of retired performers, magical patrons, and one mysterious masked benefactor.

### 🏆 Prizes

🥇 **Platinum Chime of Harmony
** Once per day, grants **Advantage** on a Charisma (Performance) check. Subtle harmonic magic resonates with the wielder’s song.

🥈 **VIP Seat – Magicians’ Midnight Show
** Includes backstage access and time with faculty of the Stormspire Academy.

🥉 **Copper & Quartz Coin
** Good for free drinks for 24 hours. Also makes you instantly popular with festivalgoers.

## 🎶 “The Shepherd’s Eyes” (The Swamp Calls)

A hushed, droning melody spreads like fog from the stage—an old, cracked-voice bard sings, eyes shut tight, swaying with a rhythm only they seem to hear.

**🎵 Verse 1:
 Out where the fen grows thick and wide,
 The Shepherd waits beneath the tide.
 With every bone and breath we give,
 We feed the flesh that learns to live.

**🎵 Chorus:
 The Shepherd’s eyes still see the way—
 Through mire, rot, and bloodied clay.
 Return, return, your skin is due.
 The seed must bloom in what was you.

**🎵 Verse 2:
 The cities spit and cast us down,
 But deeper roots ignore the crown.
 The swamp forgets not what it claimed—
 Each step you take, it learns your name.

**🎵 ****Bridge (Whispered Chant):
** Back to bog, to bone, to brood—
 To slough our names and make the food.
 Wade the fen and seek the nest,
 Be devoured and be blessed.

**🎵 Chorus (Reprise):
 The Shepherd’s eyes are wide and wet,
 His hunger burns, but not yet met.
 So bring the meat, the soul, the kin—
 He stirs to life… from deep within…

### 🕯️ Performance Notes

The bard finishes in silence. No applause—just distant whispers and glances toward the north.

**Insight DC 14**: The song is a message, encoded with directions or dogma.

**Arcana DC 16 or Religion DC 18**: The lyrics carry an enchantment—subtle, but real. A compulsion lingers in the air, like the faint scent of swamp water.
