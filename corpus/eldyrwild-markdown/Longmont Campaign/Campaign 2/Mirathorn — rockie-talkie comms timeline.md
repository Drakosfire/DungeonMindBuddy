## 2. Shape of Mirathorn while the company is away

*Planning — authoritative lock in `Campaign 2 — Dual Front Shepherd Arc (GM planning).md`. Not table canon.*

**Single root cause:** Tainted **party rations** and Mirathorn’s **city crisis** share one **Shepherd meat pipeline** (logistics → guard → north pit).

**Festival lie:** Table canon is Frank’s **“festival resumed, long night”** (S21). GM truth: **festival is not actually back on.** **Fresh guard corruption** (subtle second front) feeds operators a **celebration script** so Questionable Company **keeps marching to the swamp** instead of turning back.

**Parallel clocks:**

| Front | Cult assumption | Party experience |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|
| **North (swamp)** | Primary — **flesh pit** must fall | Lysandra’s **tower map** → pit crawl → **local big bad** |
| **South (Mirathorn)** | **Summoning** underway while heroes are north | Wire **fragments**; return reveals **transformation or mid-ritual** |

**Second front vs Globe:** Quieter — **wrong meat in legitimate channels**, choral-oath revision (ledger §8), static **“Mael—”** (§37), **no barn sermon on the wire**.

**Operators:** Frank/Sara **relay guard-authored reassurance**; Sara’s S20 **trust crisis** may be **closer to truth** than Frank’s festival register. **Not cruel** — **used**.

**Design guardrails (Session 22+):**

- **Do not confirm** festival lie or summoning on static alone — **proof** needs return, infiltration, or **pit evidence**.
- **Do not resolve** Mirathorn off-screen; wire beats are **fragments** and **steer-north nudges**.
- **Lysandra’s city investigation** (P1 knob) — tie to **guard corruption** or **supply signer** when named.
