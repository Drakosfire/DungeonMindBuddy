### 🟧 BEAT 2: The Guard Tower Performance

**Setting**: A neighborhood guard station. During the Festival of Expansion, the Mirathorn Guard hosts traveling performers—**The Guardhouse Cabarets**, a long-standing tradition of morale and outreach.

## As the party takes their seats (or stands in the back, observing):

A gaunt human bard in fine, dusty robes steps onto the stage, eyes half-lidded, smile faint. No instruments—just a **single-stringed lyre** and an unnatural reverb to his voice.

### 🎶 Dustwalker Performance: "The Shepherd’s Wake"

“They call us the forgotten, yet build on our backs.
 They call us unclean, yet drink from our cracks.
 They call us few—but we are many.
 We are the children of the Shepherd.”

The crowd hushes. Some murmur along.

Minor **charm effect** is in the air. (Arcana/Insight DC 14)

Illusions accompany verses: human workers crying, a guard beaten by elves, a broken statue of a human leader toppled by goblins.

🎤 **Final Line** (as Dustwalker looks into the crowd):

“If your blood burned at my words—if you felt seen—then maybe you were meant to hear more.”

## He lifts a parchment, spiral symbol visible.

“We gather later tonight. We speak truth. No city council. No foreigners. Just us. Seek the Spiral Gate. Speak the name: **Maelthor**.”

### 🟥 BEAT 3: Descent into the Spiral Gate

**Location**: A water barrel beside the tower. Spiral scratched into the wood.

A robed cultist (or disguised guard) watches for those who repeat **“Maelthor.”** Once the word is spoken, the barrel’s base reveals a stone trapdoor.

## Tunnel System:

### 🟫 BEAT 4: The Fleshbarn Sermon

**Emergence**: The tunnel opens behind a hay wall in an **old ranch barn**, secluded outside Mirathorn.

**Float goats** graze in a paddock under moonlight.

Inside the barn: flickering lanterns, spiral motifs, meat laid out on tables (faintly moving).

30–40 humans, mostly young, angry, and malleable.

**On a dais, under an inverted float goat skull**, a priest begins the **Sermon of the Flesh**:

**Preacher** (zealous): “The old gods abandoned us. The city forgets us. But the Shepherd feeds us. He gives us the true flesh—so that we might be remade. So that we might rise!”

They bring out a **shackled non-human (maybe goblin child, or a known NPC)**.

Preacher raises a blade.

(At this point, the crowd may fall into silence, or begin whispering "Maelthor" in slow waves as the preacher ascends to the dais.)

### 🕯️ Sermon of the Flesh

(The preacher stands tall beneath the inverted skull of a float goat, its eye sockets filled with two black and red candles burning with green candlelight. Their voice echoes off the wooden rafters—smooth at first, then building like thunder.)

## "The old gods… watched as our children went hungry."

"They turned away when we knelt in the fields, back broken under stone, our pleas swallowed by the mountain wind. They gave us nothing. Not bread. Not protection. Not justice."

**"The city?"** (a sneer)
 "It builds towers to the sky, gilded with your labor. It gives titles to the undeserving, riches to the undeserving, power to the unworthy. And it calls you equal, while it feeds its council with roasted lamb and leaves you to chew on bone."

(The crowd murmurs. Some nod. Others weep.)

## "But the Shepherd… he does not forget."

## "The Shepherd sees every slight, every indignity, every drop of your blood that watered their empire."

"He does not feed you lies. He feeds you **truth**."
 "He does not whisper promises. He offers **flesh**."

(raising his hands high)
 **"The true flesh—not the frail meat of this world, but the seed of new becoming. In his name, we are unbroken. In his name, we rise!"**

(A drumbeat begins. A minor illusion dances like tendrils of muscle over the preacher’s arms.)

"To partake is to be chosen. To feast is to be changed. Shed your unworthy skin—leave behind your half-life—and be remade."

(Two robed acolytes drag forth the **sacrifice**—a goblin child, small and shivering. The crowd stills. The preacher lowers his voice to a dangerous whisper.)

## "Even now, there are those who defy us. Who would pollute the flock. But the Shepherd teaches us this:"

## "That the impure may still serve a purpose… as kindling for the divine."

(He raises a cruel ceremonial blade—bone-handled, etched with abyssal sigils. The crowd chants:)

## “Shepherd guide us. Shepherd feed us. Shepherd rise.”

**This is your turning point**.
