Campaign 2 Session Recaps

Session 1 Recap:

Session 1: Let the Games Begin Despite all the merriment of a huge festival beginning, the group decided to head back to the Inn first and get some much needed rest before heading back out to partake in the plethora of events. As the group weaved their way through the crowd to the center of the city they could see all the paths leading off in every direction; over-filled with people and noise. The paths were packed with colorful tents. As they passed by groups they could overhear joy mixed with some unease. Some people had begun to notice fewer guards were patrolling and it seemed far fewer party-goers than expected. The adventurers took their long rest and awoke with excitement, ready to take in the remainder of the first day’s activities. While each member of the party looked over the list of things to see and do, Stafl began casting Identify on a scale he had been carrying around for a long time.
 The dragon scale turned out to be a real dragon scale, however it was not of a dragon that currently exists. It is possible the scale was from an ancient dragon that is long extinct. While holding the scale Stafl could feel vast and unseen power as well as a steady vibration, almost like breathing. Meanwhile, Ephanna and Caelynn decide to see if they can still make it into the Mage’s Duel. As they approach the Arcane Arena outside the Stormspire Academy they can see statues of Mages from famous duels and an overly enthusiastic apprentice yelling to every passerby. As they enter through the doors they can hear the boom of magic spells as they take in the beautiful surroundings. They also see a friendly face from their previous trip to the Academy: Head Clerk Mossglade. She is standing behind an enormous desk placing name cards on a wall forming a large tournament bracket as other apprentices come running in and out with results from the qualifying rounds.

After a few failed attempts to get her attention, Caelynn (with some Bardic Inspiration from Stafl) finally manages to pull her away from the wall long enough to ask about entering the duel. They are informed that the process to make the name tiles is very time consuming and that Mossglade is afraid of getting into further trouble from the night before. Stafl convinces her that Karsemine will take care of any past trouble if she can help get Ephanna and Caelynn into the Duel. Mossglade finally agrees and, after taking a drop of blood from each, creates the name tiles for them both. The Qualifiers consist of three rounds and test whether they have what it takes to duel. The test is to hit a floating target with a spell. The second test is to hold a special ice crystal and summon an Ice Elemental. The Elemental will attack and they attempt to hold their Concentration on the spell. And the final test is, when given a special stone, can they decipher the magic pattern within. First up: Ephanna. As a Warlock the spell choice was obvious: Eldritch Blast. The second test was not too difficult, even with an attack from the Ice Elemental. The third test turned out to be very simple for Ephanna. The mystical stone, when held, turned out to be a Fey toy, something that Ephanna was very familiar with and took no time at all to solve. The other wizards taking notes during the qualifiers became very excited at this new participant. Next up was Caeylnn. Feeling a little nervous about not passing the qualifying round she chose the easiest spell she could think of for the first test: Magic Missile. Again there are excited whispers from the other wizards. The second test went easily for her as well. The final test, however, was much more difficult. Having never seen this stone before it took a long time for her to decipher the pattern, but in the end she succeeded. While Ephanna and Caelynn were working hard in the qualifiers Stafl again decided to use Identify on another object. This time it was on an old fossil that Ephanna had been carrying around. What Stafl identifies immediately is that this fossil is a fake. It is in fact only made of porcelain. But, while shaking the fossil, he discovers something rattling inside, just like the seed from the slain meat monster. Ephanna cracks it open to discover a small pale silver key. After a close inspection they determine that this is not human, or dwarven, or elven. And with help from Caelynn they also discover it is not infernal or primordial. The key is cold to the touch, but always begins to warm up when held. Now the group (minus Karsemine) heads over to the Forge and Fire district in search of the Colosseum of the Storm. Bonogo and Baegrom are interested in testing their mettle in non-lethal combat. Again the outside is adorned with statues of past champions and the inside is lined with magic. All around the arena are seats for people to watch and bet on the action. This competition also has a qualifying round. Teams of up to 6 fighters must beat the other team 2 out of 3 times. At the end the winners will receive a prize. And soon even some mystery challengers will be announced.

# Session 2 Recap:

Ephanna and Stafl took their seats in the stands, but not before Ephanna casts Summon Fey and creates a nice copy of Bonogo - affectionately called “Ogonob”. Bonogo, Baergrom and “Ogonob” enter the arena to face their first challenge: a one-on-one wrestling match. Across from the team enter the challengers - a feared and seasoned team known for their unorthodox tactics and fierce arena presence.

The Steel Fangs consist of 6 members: a massive and imposing Minotaur Brute known as Gorrun the Ironhide, a lithe Eleven Duelist named Seraphine "Bladewind" D’Lorien, a gruff and no-nonsense Dwarven Shieldmaster named Rurik "Stonebreaker" Thorne, the mysterious and striking Tiefling Spear-Dancer called Valera Stormstrike, the renowned Human Pit Fighter "Laughing" Yorrik Grent, and a formidable Half-Orc Monk called Morvian the Ashen.

The first match appears to be very lopsided - mountain dwarf against a massive Minotaur. Baergrom rushes forward intent on grabbing the Minotaur by the horns and throwing him off balance, however the attack has the opposite effect and the Minotaur flips Baergrom up and over his head towards the edge of the circle. Baergrom executed an aerial maneuver, grabbing the horns then using the momentum to throw the large Minotaur out of the ring. Stafl had used a little of his luck to help turn the momentum. The crowd erupts in thunderous applause - the battle is off to a great start.

Next, Bonogo moves into position across from the Elven Duelist “Bladewind”. Bonogo grabs some sand and throws it into the face of the Elf, but misses on his grapple. The Elf expertly twists her body and counters with her own grapple. Bonogo counters and spins out of her grasp. Just then the ground begins to shake. Between rounds the magically enhanced arena will randomly shift, throwing off the fighters and changing up the battle. Bonogo makes a dexterity save while the Elf is thrown off balance. As she is stumbling Bonogo uses a shove, then a punch, throwing the Elf out of the ring and out of the competition.

Next up is “Ogonob”, Ephanna’s fey. They are facing a very difficult challenger - the Dwarven Shieldmaster. The dwarf hunkers down, as if with a shield, then launches at Ogonob pushing them back to the edge of the fighting circle. Ogonob tries to grapple and carry the dwarf, but he is much sturdier than he appears. Just as they come to a stalemate, the ground heaves up and changes, throwing off both competitors as they are locked in battle. As one starts to fall, both fall, neither with an advantage. Both competitors land outside the ring, thus being eliminated.

With just Baergorm and Bonogo making it through it's now up to them to eliminate the rest of the challengers to move on as a team. Now Baergrom lines up across from Valera Stormstrike - an acrobatic looking Tiefling. Valera strikes first with a solid punch, but Baegrom shrugs it off and follows up with his own strike. Then a second, staggering the Tiefling. Valera then sprints forward ready to push Baergrom out of the ring. With another display of strength, Baergrom uses her momentum to lift and throw her over his head, pausing long enough to give the opposing dwarf a wink, and throws Valera clear out of the ring for another victory.

The excitement is building in the crowd - will Bonogo and Baegrom survive the last two challengers? Both bouts look very tough. First Bonogo vs the Human Pit Fighter. As soon as the match begins, with maniacal laughter, the pit fighter tries to throw sand at Bonogo and rush to tackle. With incredible dexterity Bonogo is able to dodge both attacks and then land a punch. However, all it seems to produce from Yorrik is a grin. Then the ground begins to change and throws both fighters off balance. Bonogo is able to catch his footing, but the pit fighter falls, grabbing onto Bonogo’s legs. Bonogo walks to the edge of the ring, carrying the pit fighter with him. Yorrik tries to pull Bonogo down from here, but gets his head slammed against the ground and then followed up with a well placed knee strike sending the pit fighter out of the tournament.

After the last two matches the crowd erupts in wild applause. The crowd is still going crazy, encouraged by Stafl, as the last match is about to begin - Baergrom vs the Half-Orc Monk Morvian the Ashen. Baergrom starts throwing punches then shoves the monk back. The monk attempts to retaliate, but misses. Again the ground shakes and morphs and sends both competitors sprawling, neither one able to keep their feet. Baergrom is first to react, jumping up and then with a mighty shove, pushes the monk out of the ring, ending the qualifying round.

All the while, Karsemine was on an important mission - helping Winna Mossglade get out of the trouble. Karsemine had promised to speak to someone at the Academy on her behalf as a thank you for getting Ephanna and Caelynn into the Mage’s Tournament. The first thing that she notices is how empty Stormspire Academy is. She discovers light coming from an office.

Inside she finds Zilla Cinderbranch, the Necromancer, with her face buried in some notes. Zilla finally notices Karsemine. To her surprise she discovers that Winna is not in any trouble at all. In fact the issue is that she is a strict rule follower and that she only thought she was in trouble. Kasemine cannot go back empty handed. Instead she asks Zilla for a note explaining the situation - she is not in trouble and should relax and enjoy the festival.

Just as Kasemine was leaving she ran into Caelynn who had come to help. Kasemine explained all that happened with Zilla as they made their way back to the Arcane Arena, determined to clear things up with Winna and maybe even convince her to join the group for the evening. And that's exactly what they do. After explaining all that happened with Zilla, Winna agrees to join the group for a drink and watch the Bardic Showcase at the glorious Glimmering Globe.

Situated at the edge of Lake Mirathorn, the Glimmering Globe is a masterpiece in Artificer craftsmanship. As Karsemine, Caelynn and Winna approach the beautiful, flower shaped building, they can already hear the steady murmur of voices. They find Ephanna, Bonogo and Baergrom already grabbing drinks from a familiar smiling face - Pippa! Winna squeals with delight when Caelynn takes her to Bubbles and then Karsemine tells her the tale of her daring rescue. Everyone gets their Ether Foam Ale from a very busy Pippa and they all head for a table together at the front near the stage.

Then Stafl appears on stage while seeming to direct people and reassure them - clearly he just jumped up on stage out of turn. Then, pulling Bonogo up on stage, begins a rousing original song about the Bugbear. The crowd goes wild, jumping to their feet and cheering for more. However, his turn is over and up next is a Human Bard - Dustwalker.

The Bard begins to play a melancholy song titled “The Shepard Eyes. Several other humans stand up in the crowd and begin to sing along to the slow and unsettling song. Finally, as the song ends, the crowd is completely silent, chilled by the lyrics. Stafl, seeing the murder in the eyes of the crowd, quickly jumps into action and ushers the bard off the stage. The last band quickly changes the mood as they play an upbeat local drinking song. With the competition over the group heads outside for the final event of the day - the Fireworks Display over Lake Mirathorn.

The fireworks display is a beautiful combination of magic and alchemy. The crowd was enjoying it until a sudden change in the wind brought in yet another terrible storm. Caelynn quickly checked to see if the storm was the same magical storm as before - explaining to Winna about how the group encountered storms just like this twice before. This time however, a trio of mages step in to cast complicated spells to dispel the storm in a circle around the city allowing the fireworks to resume and the crowd to stay dry. Zilla notices Willa sitting and having drinks with Karsemine and Caelynn - giving a smile and nod of approval.

As the day comes to an end the group discusses what to do next. The festival has been such a nice change of pace that they agree to continue with the festival the next day. But they all agree - they need to track down that bard, Dustwalker, and get some answers about the Shepard.

# Session 3 Recap:

Here are the session notes as written by a player.

Before the group heads back to the Inn Caelynn wanted to speak to the Mages about the storms. As Caelynn approached the three Mages she could tell that they seemed unsettled by the storm - this was an unexpected event. Zilla tells Caelynn that storms had been happening every day for weeks and no one knew where the magical storms came from.. Zilla becomes even more concerned when Caelynn shares that the group encountered these storms in other towns. The only thing the Mages can agree on - the storms carry a sense of evil.

The party heads back to the Inn. Before heading to sleep Stafl asks if anyone else has an item that they would like him to Identify. Caelynn, Kasemine, Baergrom and Bonogo all have items. Stafl identifies Caelynn’s item as a magical Sprite trapped in a bottle, Karsemine has a cursed idol - possibly giving the group nightmares, Baergrom has a raven figurine that can briefly transform into an actual raven, and Bonogo has a beautifully crafted, but cursed, invitation. After opening and reading the invitation Bonogo is now Cursed, however Ephanna quickly casts Remove Curse and the curse was removed, but not from the object. With some time before the festival begins they agree to check on Torbin. They find out he shares a floor with Ethel, the elderly woman they saved from the giant monster. Torbin jumps out of bed, excited to tell the group his exciting news - he will be starting school soon. Wondering how long Torbing must remain in bed, Ephanna picks up a magical slate revealing that the mages believe he was poisoned or magically cursed. Ephanna again casts Remove Curse and they can all see an immediate improvement in Torbin. He goes into a deep sleep and they can tell that he will now make a full recovery.

Caelynn discovers that Ethel is in the next room and goes in to greet her. Ethel is so happy to see Caelynn, but her smile quickly turns to a frown when she sees Bonogo. It is revealed that she is from Hempholm and that she knows Torbin as well. She was a seamstress in the town and all she remembers from the last time she was there was guards arriving at her house. The next thing she remembers is waking up to see Caelynn. She also remembers terrible dreams - a dark space with oily swirls. And then giant tentacles wrapping around her and a perverted connection that she doesn’t want to be a part of. A name that is repeated over and over again, Maelthor. And finally a face - The Shepherd.

The group now heads back to the festival. First up is the Bake-off in the Market Plaza. Situated right in the middle of the plaza there are huge tents set up all around the square with large magical stone ovens. There are teams set up including a group of goblins. Rather than participating in baking, Bonogo wants to be a judge - simply because he wants to eat free food. He approaches the judges table, attempting to just sit down and join in, but is immediately called out for it. As he is about to be removed Grobnok tells the other judges that he was added as guest judge. Surprisingly for Bonogo all the dishes he likes are quickly eliminated by the other judges, but he is more than content eating all the dishes (and leftovers). So much so that he ends up getting sick all over the table. Grobnok quickly ushers him back behind all the tents. Bonogo asks Grobnok about the mysterious letter he has been carrying. Grobnok doesn’t have any other details about the letter, but can tell that it is very expensive paper. The only place in the city that would have that is the Gilded Fold.

As Bonogo is walking with Grobnok, Karsemine asks Stafl for advice on something personal. She tells him about her feelings for Winna and asks whether or not she should try to pursue a relationship. Stafl is so taken aback by the sudden sharing of personal details, something Karsemine almost never does, that all he can manage to get out is a mumble about moving on.

Meanwhile, Caelynn makes finding Lysandra her next priority - Ephanna and Baergrom join her in her search - but where to start. Caelynn and Ephanna come up with a plan to start asking at guard houses while Baergrom uses his new raven figurine to send out a description of her to see if that can help. After no luck at a couple of guardhouses they decide to head to the first guardhouse they know she was stationed at - across from the Copper and Quartz Inn.

They find Captain Lysnadra and her group of guards, excited and happy to see the heroes of the city. The work that the group did with Lysndra really helped boost her reputation as well. Suddenly her eyes light up and she burst out of the room. She quickly returns with a document in her hand and excitedly hands it to the group. It is the deed to Wolf’s Manor. After the losses the city suffered during the Cult activity, lots of people from Outtown have been given land inside the city. This location was set aside for the adventures by the Mayor, however it will need some work. Now the adventures have a home base inside Mirathorn.

Caelynn then pulls Lysandra aside and tells her that she has been looking for someone for a while and could use her help tracking them down if they are in the city. She gives a very detailed description, despite not revealing the relationship to this person. Lysandra is more than happy to help - something to focus on and forget the trouble of the past week. She will work through her contacts in the city and use her position to access old records.

Baergrom asks her about the most pressing issue - The Shepherd. Lysandra pulls out a draft of the report she wrote and explains that the city guard has been exploring all the underground tunnels. Ephanna then asks about the cultist bard Dustwalker. Lysandra is embarrassed to say that they are so short staffed they have not had time to follow up on the bard or any of the others that were singing along. Seeing the situation she is in she reluctantly agrees to “deputize” the adventures to handle tracking down the Shepherd.

Sargent Dunberry goes into a back room and returns with badges for each of the adventurers. For the members present, he flashes the badge up to their face and magically the face appears on the front of the badge. He then begins to drone on about all the rules that must be followed while wearing the badges. He hands the group the remaining 3 blank badges to scan when the party is all back together. The badges work for 2 weeks before they reset and have to be brought back.

The next thing the group sees are festival booth games. The group is immediately drawn to Axe Me Anything, an axe throwing booth. There are others already throwing axes and being heckled by a woman who appears to be running the booth. Hilda Logsplitter enthusiastically pulls the group in to try their luck at the simple game. For 2 silver you get to throw 3 axes at targets that get increasingly farther away. Hit all three and you earn tokens for the festival and can move on to the more challenging targets. Kasemine, Baergrom, Bonogo and Ephanna all agree to play, while Stafl settles for betting on those that are throwing.

Of the three throwing areas - traditional targets, city council targets and mythical creatures - Kasemine chooses to throw at the council “officials” targets. Her first throw is almost perfect, hitting the target with a satisfying thunk. Her second throw is even better, almost dead center. But her last throw sails just wide, earning her a good razzing from Hilda. Ephanna is next to throw the axes, this time against the mythical creatures. The first throw is really good and lands on the target. The next throw lands dead center, earning a gasp from the crowd gathering to watch. However, just like Karsemine, the last throw flies over the top of the last target.

Next is Bonogo - determined to hit all three targets. He takes on the council targets as well. His first throw is great, hitting the target dead center. The second throw however flies over the target. Determined not to be out so early, Bonogo lines up an incredible throw. The axe hits the top of the second target and flies over the top and hits the third target, earning cheers from the crowd and a chance to head to the advanced round. He has the chance to gamble his 10 magic Mayor Marks - the festival tokens - and play for 30 marks and an “extremely rare” commemorative spoon with his name on it. Bonogo does not hesitate, he wants the spoon. However, his first throw sails wide and with no chance to earn the spoon he gets a good razzing again from Hilda. His next throw lands with a clean hit, but he misses the final throw.

As Karsemine is watching Bonogo she notices a box over where Hilda is standing. As she gets closer she can see it is piled high with the “rare” spoons. While she is talking with Hilda and distracting her, she deftly uses her tail to steal a spoon for Bonogo. She is overjoyed to see the big grin on Bonogo’s face when she hands him the spoon.

Last up is Baergrom who is confident that he can hit all the targets. He decides on the mythical creatures and makes quick work with the first three - hitting all three almost dead center. With his confidence high, he decides to gamble his tokens for the advanced round. The first throw lands, but the last two sail wide. No spoon for Baergrom either.

With the morning activities ending the party decides to participate in the afternoon events - another round of fights at the Coliseum of the Storm and another round of Magic Duels at the Arcane Arena.

# Session 4 Recap:

With the sun almost hitting noon, the group heads to the next event on their list - the Acrobatics Extravaganza at the Glimmering Globe. Stafl begins busking on the street, hoping to gain some intel on the Shepherd and his “Flock”. Earning 33 gold in the process. Karsemine is very excited to participate, until she finds out that it is not a competition, but a show. Ephanna convinces her to slip into the show and participate. They walk around to the back of the Globe and find a door standing open. As Karsemine and Ephanna walk through the back door, they are greeted yet again by a familiar face behind a desk - Winna. Dressed in all black and with a magic earpiece, she is clearly helping to run the event. Her expression at seeing Karsemine and Ephanna is exasperation. She is again stressed out that they would like to bend the rules to get into the show. Ephanna quickly comes up with a plan. While Karesmine performs some acrobatics to show off, they will forge her name into the list of performers. With an impressive leap, Karsemine is able to parkour off three sets of beams and lands on a beam high up above Winna. Here she begins to juggle some unlit torches, dropping one and barely managing to catch her balance. While Winna is distracted, Ephanna sneaks over to the list and quickly writes in Karsemine’s name. Karsemine again attempts to juggle, using her hands as well as her tail, and drops a torch again, nearly falling in the process. After failing another time at juggling, Karsemine makes her way back down.

Winna notices the name on the list and is momentarily relieved that her name is on the list and she won’t have to break any rules. Just as Karsemine and Ephanna believe that they have succeeded, Winna notices that the ink is still wet, proving that it was added. She kicks them out and they both join back up with the group. That’s when a guard approaches Stafl. The guard is not happy about him performing without a permit. Just as the guard is about to escalate things with Stafl, the rest of the group joins in and shows them their badges. Stafl uses this opportunity to ask if he knows who we all are - “Questionable Company”. He immediately recognizes the group as the saviours of the city and explains that everyone is still on edge about all the missing guards. Now that they have cleared things up the group decides to head to Wolf Manor to check out the house they have been given. Outside the inner city ring, where you would find the Academy and the Council Headquarters, is an outer ring featuring a series of hills. Originally a defensive position for the inner ring, the outer ring now consists of very nice - and very old - houses. The group approaches one of these - a very rundown and ivy covered manor. Large sections of the outside have collapsed including the huge oak front doors, each featuring the emblem of the Wolf family. As the group walks through the broken gates they can see old tarnished statues across an area of about 30 feet, a crypt, a large garden and then the 3 story manor. When they enter the manor they find 3 doors, but only the first two are unlocked. Caelynn is on a mission to find the door to the conservatory.

She checks the first door and finds a dirty room filled with furniture and a large fireplace. Everything is covered with sheets to protect from the dust. Behind the second door she finds more doors and a staircase leading up. When she climbs the stairs she finds another dusty room filled with furniture and another staircase leading to the third floor. On the third floor she finds the conservatory - a huge glass room with tarnished metal shutters. A very old and weathered crank (turned by the collective effort of Caelynn, Karesemine and Baergrom) opens the shutters and turns a telescope into view. With a cap on the end of the telescope, and it being close to noon, the telescope does not reveal anything useful. Meanwhile, Bonogo is in search of the master bedroom. While looking he finds a locked door, but with his lockpicking skills it was easy work. This bedroom doesn’t have nearly as much dust, no sheets covering any of the furniture and nice paintings of what appear to be family members. With little decorum Bonogo begins to ransack the room. Grabbing anything that doesn’t look important and throwing it onto the floor. Finally, after several minutes he finds a lockbox under the bed. Again he picks the lock and discovers a diary, some old papers, an old ring featuring the Wolf sigil, and family notes. Bored with his findings Bonogo takes the lockbox and resumes searching the manor for anything else that could be useful.

The next room the group discovers is the library. In an old parlor there are sitting chairs, a lectern and a wall full of books. Bonogo, convinced that there is a secret room here, pulls all the books off the wall hoping to find a lever. All he does is make a huge mess of books on the floor. Karsemine sifts through the books until she finds one on the history of the manor and Wolf family. The group does discover a “hidden” door set off to the side and find the servants stairs leading up to the attic. Bonogo rides the dumbwaiter back down to the first floor, discovering a storage room that leads into the kitchen. On their way out Ephanna casts Unseen Servant and assigns them the task of cleaning up as much of the manor as they can. Now it is perfect timing to head back to the festival and get ready for the next round of the Mage’s Duel. Before entering the arena, Stafl strums a little tune and gives some Bardic Inspiration to Ephanna and Caelynn. The next phase of the Mage’s Duel consists of 3 rounds. Judges will award points each round for attacks landed and for creative style. The first round is a 1 on 1 duel to land 2 hits or disable the opponent. The second round is also 1on 1, but this time they have to use spells to move a small magical stone and hit the opponent. The final round is to stabilize an erratic levitating stone.

Caelynn is up first - facing off against a Drow Illusionist. She casts the first thing that comes to mind - Magic Missile for an automatic hit. The Drow strikes back with Hold Person. But just as the spell is beginning to form it suddenly dissipates as Caelynn casts Counter Spell. The judges are impressed with her two opening spells. Caelynn then casts her trusty Thunderwave. Not only does the spell hit, the Drow is pushed back to the edge of the arena. Caelynn easily wins the opening round with a score of 7 points to 2 points. Ephanna is up next - paired against what looks like a very seasoned spellcaster named Gimble. Gimble casts Charm Person. Ephanna fails to make the save, however they feel a sudden swell of inspiration (thanks to Stafl) and they succeed in not being Charmed. Ephanna casts Witchbolt, but misses. However the link to Gimble stays for the next round. Gimble then uses Magic Missile and succeeds in breaking the concentration spell. With a final spell Ephanna casts Create Bonfire right on top of Gimble, but Gimble jumps out of the way and then casts Shocking Touch on Ephanna - ending the duel. Ephanna loses the first round 4 points to 6 points.

In an exciting third match, much to Caelynn’s delight, a chair beat a nameless mage. Then it was time for the second match between Caelynn and the Drow. This time they had to use spells to push a small pebble and hit their opponent to score points. The Drow casts Gust to try and push the pebble into Caelynn, but she is able to dodge. She then casts Thunderwave to push the pebble at the Drow, but he dodges. The Drow then casts Mage Hand in an attempt to grab the stone and then hits Caelynn, but she dodges again. Using her arcane knowledge and her gift for storm magic, Caelynn then casts Dust Devil in front of the stone and sends it flying at her opponent, landing a hit and major style points. The Drow tries the Mage Hand again and hits this time. And with the final test of her magic skill, Caelynn casts Warding Wind and floats toward the opponent, hitting them with the pebble. The crowd, and the judges, go wild. She easily wins the round 15 points to 3 points. Ephanna, with some tricks up their sleeve, was ready for the second round. Gimble quickly cast Shape Water then propelled the water forward, but Ephanna dodged the pebble. Ephanna conjures a Fey and the Fey teleports with the pebble behind Gimble and lands a hit. After the teleportation the Fey cast Darkness in a cube around Gimble. Blinded by the darkness Gimble cast Gust in the direction of Ephanna and manages to land a hit. Deceptively, Ephanna moves the Fey above Gimble and drops the pebble from above - landing another hit. The crowd again goes wild and the judges award maximum style points and a big win for Ephanna. And in the last duel the chair wins again, moving on to the next round with Caelynn and Ephanna.

With the second round of the Mage Duel complete, the group heads over to the Colosseum of the Storm for the second round of the combat trials. This time the team - composed of Bonogo, Baergrom and Ogonob - face off against a fearsome Human Barbarian Druid in a magical Tavern Brawl. To the combatants the arena has been magically transformed into a tavern, complete with all the items normally found in a tavern - tables, chairs, barstools, glasses, and darts. As soon as the fight starts Hagan rushes forward with a shoulder charge, smashes into Bonogo and knocks him prone. Bonogo, quick as ever on his feet, jumps up and grabs a dart from the dart board. He moves out of striking range then throws the dart directly at Hagan’s head, earning a hearty laugh from Hagan. Bonogo then triggers his sneak attack, landing a decent hit. Ogonob flies into the fight with a series of punches, all landing for a great amount of damage, but triggering Hagan’s transformation into a giant bear. Using his experience as a fighter, Baergrom launches a flurry of punches, landing 3 out of the 4 for a nice amount of damage. However it does little to slow down the bear who then takes a powerful swipe at Bonogo, landing big damage. The bear then jumps over to the other side of the bar to use it as cover.

Bonogo grabs a handful of darts and starts throwing. Ogonob lunges for another attack, but misses. Baergrom grabs a barstool and breaks it to create a makeshift shield and club, but also misses his attack. Surrounded, the bear lashes out, but misses any attacks. Bonogo then grabs a bottle from the bar. He breaks it then slashes at the bear dealing a good amount of damage. The bear is beginning to slow down.
