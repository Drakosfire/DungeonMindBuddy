## Class & subclass features (Warlock / Archfey)

- **Pact Magic** — short-rest spell slots.

- **Eldritch Invocations** (5 known): **Agonizing Blast** (add Cha to Eldritch Blast), **Devil's Sight** (120 ft), **Fiendish Vigor** (cast False Life on self at max die without slot), **One with Shadows** (Invisibility on self in Dim/Dark, no slot), **Pact of the Tome** (conjure **Book of Shadows**: 3 cantrips + 2 ritual-tagged 1st-level spells from any class list, doubles as spellcasting focus).

- **Archfey Spells** — always-prepared list per Archfey Spells table.

## Species traits (Kenku)

- **Mimicry** — accurately mimic any sound including voices; DC 15 Insight to tell.
- **Kenku Recall** — proficiency in two skills of choice; 3/long rest, give yourself Advantage on a skill check you're proficient with (before rolling).
- **Expert Duplication** — Advantage on checks to copy writing/craftwork exactly.
