# Ephanna — Questionable Company (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Ephanna** (player **Dan**) is a **warlock-flavored eldritch caster with a Fae companion (Ogonob)** in **Questionable Company**: recaps show heavy use of **Eldritch Blast**, **Misty Step**, summoned Fae spirits, and reactive utility (Remove Curse, Identify-adjacent slate work, disguise). The **Porcelain Fossil → Silver Key** is Ephanna's defining campaign artifact.

## Disambiguators

- **Not** an NPC dossier hub: **no CR, HP, or DC block here** — use the **live character sheet** at the table.
