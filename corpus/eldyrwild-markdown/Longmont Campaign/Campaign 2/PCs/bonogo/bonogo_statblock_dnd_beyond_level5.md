## Class & subclass features (Rogue / Assassin)

- **Expertise** in two skill proficiencies (Stealth & Sleight of Hand are at +10 — both Expertise).
- **Sneak Attack** 3d6 (Finesse / Ranged + Advantage *or* ally adjacent).
- **Thieves' Cant** + one extra language.
- **Cunning Action** — Bonus Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Steady Aim** — Bonus Action self-Advantage on next attack (Speed 0 until end of turn).
- **Cunning Strike** — spend Sneak Attack dice for **Poison** (DC 15 Con or Poisoned 1 min), **Trip** (DC 15 Dex or Prone), **Withdraw** (move ½ Speed without OA).
- **Uncanny Dodge** — Reaction: halve incoming attack damage.
- **Assassin: Assassinate** — Advantage on Initiative; first round, Advantage on attacks vs. anyone who hasn't taken a turn; Sneak Attack on a hit during round 1 deals +5 damage of weapon type.
- **Assassin's Tools** — proficient Disguise Kit and Poisoner's Kit (gained as kit).
- **Enhanced Dual Wielding** — extra Light-weapon Bonus Action attack with a *different* Melee weapon (must lack Two-Handed); no ability mod on extra attack damage unless negative.
- **Weapon Mastery** (Dagger Nick property in use).

## Species traits (Bugbear)

- **Long-Limbed** — melee reach +5 ft on your turn.
- **Powerful Build** — count as one size larger for carrying capacity / push-drag-lift.
- **Sneaky** — proficiency in Stealth; can move through and stop in Small-creature spaces without squeezing.
- **Surprise Attack** — +2d6 on a hit vs. a creature that hasn't taken a turn yet in combat.
- **Fey Ancestry** — advantage to avoid/end Charmed.
- **Darkvision** 60 ft.

## Background feature (Criminal)
