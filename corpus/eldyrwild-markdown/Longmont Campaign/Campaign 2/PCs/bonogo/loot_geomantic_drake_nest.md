## GM reskins (table → table truth)

Bonogo grabbed and ran from the nest without deep investigation. **Session 21 at the wagon:** he tried the boots and learned the name **Boots of the Crowing Wings** (see recap). The **metal slab** remains unidentified. GM mechanical truth:

| Slot | Looks like (Bonogo's read) | Actually (GM truth) |
|------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Item A** | A flat **slab of metal** with **ivory-coloured raised spots** along one face. Heavier than it should be; cool to the touch. | **Cloak of the bat** (rare, attunement) — folded into a leather wallet that drakes had hammered shut with their own scales until the "spots" of bone-coloured plating fused on top. The cloth is intact inside; the wallet pops open with one Investigation success or a careful unfolding. |
| **Item B** | Beat-up boots; **brass-gold** and **crow-violet** feathers in the filigree. | **Boots of the Crowing Wings** — printed card: double jump + flight, **1/short rest** each (Jump and Crow / flap wings). **250 gp.** |

> **Why the swap?** The table rolled *ring of warmth* on slot B; GM authorial liberty replaced it with **winged boots** (table entry **65** flavour) so Bonogo gets a memorable **flight** prize fitting his "go look at the shiny thing" arc. Treat the swap as **canon** for this campaign; the world-canon d100 table is **unchanged**.

## Table notes

- **Don't volunteer the truth** — let Bonogo (or another PC) decide to spend a short rest on each.
- The **chicken-wing filigree** on the boots is a player-facing tell; once anyone investigates, it becomes the obvious clue toward "wings."
- The **slab/wallet** is a deliberate pause — reward whoever opens it with an "oh, *that's* what this is" beat.
