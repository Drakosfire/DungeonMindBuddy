# Bonogo's drake-nest haul (canon)

**Source table:** `Elderwyld/Wilderness/geomantic_drake_nest_loot_d100.md`.

## The roll

| Step | Result |
|------|--------|
| **First roll** | **100** — *Double haul.* Roll twice more, keep both. |
| **Second roll** | **93** — *Cloak of the bat* (table entry). |
| **Third roll** | **48** — *Ring of warmth* (table entry). |

## GM reskins (table → table truth)

Bonogo grabbed and ran. He **did not investigate**, so neither item has been **identified** at the table. The corpus-canon truth is:

| Slot | Looks like (Bonogo's read) | Actually (GM truth) |
|------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Item A** | A flat **slab of metal** with **ivory-coloured raised spots** along one face. Heavier than it should be; cool to the touch. | **Cloak of the bat** (rare, attunement) — folded into a leather wallet that drakes had hammered shut with their own scales until the "spots" of bone-coloured plating fused on top. The cloth is intact inside; the wallet pops open with one Investigation success or a careful unfolding. |
| **Item B** | A pair of **beat-up boots** with **mostly destroyed gold filigree** — only a few flakes of gilt left along the seams, hinting at little stylised wings. | **Boots of the Crowing Wings** — reskinned **winged boots** (per *Boots of the Crowing Wings*, see Item Card). Chicken-wing motif in the filigree; otherwise mechanically identical to standard winged boots. |

> **Why the swap?** The table rolled *ring of warmth* on slot B; GM authorial liberty replaced it with **winged boots** (table entry **65** flavour) so Bonogo gets a memorable **flight** prize fitting his "go look at the shiny thing" arc. Treat the swap as **canon** for this campaign; the world-canon d100 table is **unchanged**.

## Identification state

- **Neither item identified.**
- *Identify*, an hour of attentive examination + Investigation/Arcana check, or a city sage will resolve them. Suggested DCs:
  - **Item A (cloak):** Investigation **DC 12** to realise the "slab" unfolds; Arcana **DC 14** to identify the cloak; or *identify*.
  - **Item B (boots):** Arcana **DC 13** to recognise winged-boot enchantment; or *identify*.
- Until then, treat both as inert curios in Bonogo's pack.

## Table notes

- **Don't volunteer the truth** — let Bonogo (or another PC) decide to spend a short rest on each.
- The **chicken-wing filigree** on the boots is a player-facing tell; once anyone investigates, it becomes the obvious clue toward "wings."
- The **slab/wallet** is a deliberate pause — reward whoever opens it with an "oh, *that's* what this is" beat.
