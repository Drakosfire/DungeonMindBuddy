## Class & subclass features (Bard / College of Lore)

- **Spellcasting** — Charisma; Musical Instrument as spellcasting focus.
- **Bardic Inspiration** — Bonus Action, target within 60 ft, **1d8** die; **4/short rest** (Font of Inspiration regains all on Short or Long Rest; can spend a slot to regain a use).
- **Expertise** in two skill proficiencies (Performance and Persuasion at +10 — both Expertise).
- **Jack of All Trades** — add ½ proficiency bonus (1) to ability checks using non-proficient skills.
- **Font of Inspiration** — regain all Bardic Inspiration on Short or Long Rest; spend slot for one BI use back.
- **College of Lore — Bonus Proficiencies** — gain three skill proficiencies of choice.
- **College of Lore — Cutting Words** — Reaction: spend Bardic Inspiration to subtract roll from a creature's damage / check / attack.

- **Celestial Resistance** — Resistance to Necrotic and Radiant damage.

- **Light Bearer** — know Light cantrip; Charisma is its spellcasting ability.
