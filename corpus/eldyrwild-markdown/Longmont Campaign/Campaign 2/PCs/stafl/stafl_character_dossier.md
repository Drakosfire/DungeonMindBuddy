# Stafl — Questionable Company (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Stafl** (player **Scott**) is **Questionable Company’s** **bardic face and magical utility engine**: recaps show **Identify** marathons, **crowd leadership**, **Bardic Inspiration**, and high-stakes **control magic** in the **cult barn** and **Globe** crises. Continuity emphasizes **performance**, **luck**, and a still-emerging **personal backstory** (notes + Session 1 **dragon scale** beat).

## Disambiguators

- **Species framing:** Campaign 1 Session 1 recap lists **Stafl the 'Human' Bard** with **scare-quotes** around Human. Corpus truth from the handed sheet and Session 6 prose: **Stafl is an Aasimar** who can **reveal wings** in crisis. Treat **"Human"** in old recap shorthand as **in-universe misread / passing**, not a second character.
- **Not** **Bonogo** (Kyle’s rogue) — different PC; they scheme together (keg plan Session 6).
- **Not** **the Dustwalker** — Stafl opposes him in the bardic competition and the cult arc.
- **Not** an NPC dossier hub: **no CR, HP, or DC block here** — use the **live character sheet** at the table. Optional numbers snapshot: `stafl_statblock_dnd_beyond_level5.md`.

## Tools, items, and techniques established in play

- **Ancient Dragon Scale (Session 1 + notes):** **Identify** reveals a **real dragon scale** from a dragon that **does not currently exist** — possibly **ancient / extinct**; holding it, Stafl feels **vast power** and a **breathing-like vibration**. Campaign notes echo this under **Ancient Dragon Scale (Stafl)**.
- **Bardic support:** **Bardic Inspiration** on **Caelynn** before the **Mage’s Duel** clerk conversation (Session 1).
- **Identify service (Session 3):** Party items including **Caelynn’s sprite-in-a-bottle**, **Karsemine’s cursed idol**, **Baergrom’s raven figurine**, **Bonogo’s cursed invitation** — exact mechanics live on the sheet; recaps name the objects and caster.
- **Cult rooftop / barn (Session 6):** Throws the missed **dart** to puncture the **keg** per **Bonogo’s** plan; later caught in **Shepherd’s Wake** charm; **Hideous Laughter** on the priest at the sacrifice beat; **reveals wings**, flies, draws **Peacemaker Fiddle**, plays **Song of Shattering** to disorient the barn.
- **Globe (Session 12):** Leads crowd from **Copper and Quartz** to the **Globe**; pulls other performers on stage; spots **Dustwalker**; **Suggestion** (recap truncates spelling) used quickly in the fight sequence.
- **Forest / storm front:** With **Caelynn**, probes sparkly tree husks using **Identify** on material (Session 15); helps the party sneak past the **wyvern** (Session 17); **Hideous Laughter** on the **Mother of Broken Branches** once the apex fight opens (Session 17).
- **Mossford preparations (Session 20):** Directs workers from a **wagon** “throne”; audits provisions and finds **tainted jerky** mixed into crates (burn-the-lot call).
