# Caelynn — Questionable Company (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Caelynn** (player **Danielle**) is a **storm- and ice-flavored arcanist** in **Questionable Company**: recaps show heavy use of evocation/thunder magic, storm reading, and increasingly **tea-, mushroom-, and comms-based** problem solving tied to **Mirathorn** and **Stormspire**.

## Disambiguators

- **Not** **Karsemine** (Vanessa’s ranger/lock dual-wield investigator)—different PC. Recaps sometimes spell her name **Karesmine**; that is a **transcription variant**, not a second character.
- **Not** the **Dustwalker** (cult bard)—Caelynn fights his charm theater and (in one public crisis) destroys a **decoy** “Dustwalker” at the Globe while another body remains elsewhere.
- **Not** an NPC dossier hub: **no CR, HP, or DC block here** — use the **live character sheet** at the table.
