# Karsemine — Questionable Company (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Karsemine** (player **Vanessa**) is a **stealth-first martial / ranger-flavored investigator** in **Questionable Company**: recaps show her acting as scout and tracker, dual-wielding scimitar and short sword in the field, and carrying the party's quietest social thread (Wynna at Stormspire Academy, the Devon Redshield / sister-and-village backstory).

## Disambiguators

- Recaps spell her name **Karsemine** and **Karesmine** interchangeably — these are **the same PC**, not two characters. Caelynn's dossier flags this same variance.
- **Not** **Caelynn** (Danielle's storm/ice arcanist) — different PC. Recurring co-scout, but they are distinct characters.
- **Not** an NPC dossier hub: **no CR, HP, or DC block here** — use the **live character sheet** at the table.
