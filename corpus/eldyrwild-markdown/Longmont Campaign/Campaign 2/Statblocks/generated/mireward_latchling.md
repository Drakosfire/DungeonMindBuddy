## Traits
**Amphibious.** The latchling can breathe air and water.
**Ditch Stalker.** The latchling has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while in mud, shallow water, or heavy vegetation.

## Actions
**Many-Jointed Assault.** The latchling makes one Bite attack and one Ankle Latch attack.
**Bite.** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Poison damage.
**Ankle Latch.** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage, and the target is Grappled (escape DC 12). Until the grapple ends, the latchling can't use this attack on another target.
**Blackwater Drag.** The latchling moves up to 15 feet, dragging one creature Grappled by it. The dragged creature moves with it, and the latchling can move the creature into or through water. The latchling can't drag a creature that is more than one size larger than itself.

## Description
A low, many-jointed scavenger from the Mireward ditches, the latchling hunts by fastening onto ankles and dragging prey into black water.
