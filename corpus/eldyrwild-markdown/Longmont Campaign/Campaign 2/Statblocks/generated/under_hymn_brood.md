## Traits
**Earth Meld.** The Brood can move through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone as if it were difficult terrain. It leaves no tunnel or sign of its passage.

## Actions
**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d6 + 4) piercing damage.
**Tunneling Swarm.** The Brood burrows at an incredible pace, creating difficult terrain in a 20-foot radius centered on itself. Structures within this area take 10 damage at the start of each of the Brood's turns.
**Shepherd's Hymn.** The Brood emits a low hum that reverberates through solid objects. Each creature within 30 feet that can hear the hum must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be deafened for 1 minute.

## Description
The Under-Hymn Brood is a swarm of small aberrant creatures that undermine fortifications and structures. They operate as a single-minded entity, digesting the foundation of walls and barricades rather than directly attacking defenders. Their presence is marked by the unsettling sound of their humming chorus and the ground shifting beneath feet.
