# Session 3 Recap:

Here are the session notes as written by a player.

Before the group heads back to the Inn Caelynn wanted to speak to the Mages about the storms. As Caelynn approached the three Mages she could tell that they seemed unsettled by the storm - this was an unexpected event. Zilla tells Caelynn that storms had been happening every day for weeks and no one knew where the magical storms came from.. Zilla becomes even more concerned when Caelynn shares that the group encountered these storms in other towns. The only thing the Mages can agree on - the storms carry a sense of evil.

The party heads back to the Inn. Before heading to sleep Stafl asks if anyone else has an item that they would like him to Identify. Caelynn, Kasemine, Baergrom and Bonogo all have items. Stafl identifies Caelynn’s item as a magical Sprite trapped in a bottle, Karsemine has a cursed idol - possibly giving the group nightmares, Baergrom has a raven figurine that can briefly transform into an actual raven, and Bonogo has a beautifully crafted, but cursed, invitation. After opening and reading the invitation Bonogo is now Cursed, however Ephanna quickly casts Remove Curse and the curse was removed, but not from the object. With some time before the festival begins they agree to check on Torbin. They find out he shares a floor with Ethel, the elderly woman they saved from the giant monster. Torbin jumps out of bed, excited to tell the group his exciting news - he will be starting school soon. Wondering how long Torbing must remain in bed, Ephanna picks up a magical slate revealing that the mages believe he was poisoned or magically cursed. Ephanna again casts Remove Curse and they can all see an immediate improvement in Torbin. He goes into a deep sleep and they can tell that he will now make a full recovery.

Caelynn discovers that Ethel is in the next room and goes in to greet her. Ethel is so happy to see Caelynn, but her smile quickly turns to a frown when she sees Bonogo. It is revealed that she is from Hempholm and that she knows Torbin as well. She was a seamstress in the town and all she remembers from the last time she was there was guards arriving at her house. The next thing she remembers is waking up to see Caelynn. She also remembers terrible dreams - a dark space with oily swirls. And then giant tentacles wrapping around her and a perverted connection that she doesn’t want to be a part of. A name that is repeated over and over again, Maelthor. And finally a face - The Shepherd.

The group now heads back to the festival. First up is the Bake-off in the Market Plaza. Situated right in the middle of the plaza there are huge tents set up all around the square with large magical stone ovens. There are teams set up including a group of goblins. Rather than participating in baking, Bonogo wants to be a judge - simply because he wants to eat free food. He approaches the judges table, attempting to just sit down and join in, but is immediately called out for it. As he is about to be removed Grobnok tells the other judges that he was added as guest judge. Surprisingly for Bonogo all the dishes he likes are quickly eliminated by the other judges, but he is more than content eating all the dishes (and leftovers). So much so that he ends up getting sick all over the table. Grobnok quickly ushers him back behind all the tents. Bonogo asks Grobnok about the mysterious letter he has been carrying. Grobnok doesn’t have any other details about the letter, but can tell that it is very expensive paper. The only place in the city that would have that is the Gilded Fold.

As Bonogo is walking with Grobnok, Karsemine asks Stafl for advice on something personal. She tells him about her feelings for Winna and asks whether or not she should try to pursue a relationship. Stafl is so taken aback by the sudden sharing of personal details, something Karsemine almost never does, that all he can manage to get out is a mumble about moving on.

Meanwhile, Caelynn makes finding Lysandra her next priority - Ephanna and Baergrom join her in her search - but where to start. Caelynn and Ephanna come up with a plan to start asking at guard houses while Baergrom uses his new raven figurine to send out a description of her to see if that can help. After no luck at a couple of guardhouses they decide to head to the first guardhouse they know she was stationed at - across from the Copper and Quartz Inn.

They find Captain Lysnadra and her group of guards, excited and happy to see the heroes of the city. The work that the group did with Lysndra really helped boost her reputation as well. Suddenly her eyes light up and she burst out of the room. She quickly returns with a document in her hand and excitedly hands it to the group. It is the deed to Wolf’s Manor. After the losses the city suffered during the Cult activity, lots of people from Outtown have been given land inside the city. This location was set aside for the adventures by the Mayor, however it will need some work. Now the adventures have a home base inside Mirathorn.

Caelynn then pulls Lysandra aside and tells her that she has been looking for someone for a while and could use her help tracking them down if they are in the city. She gives a very detailed description, despite not revealing the relationship to this person. Lysandra is more than happy to help - something to focus on and forget the trouble of the past week. She will work through her contacts in the city and use her position to access old records.

Baergrom asks her about the most pressing issue - The Shepherd. Lysandra pulls out a draft of the report she wrote and explains that the city guard has been exploring all the underground tunnels. Ephanna then asks about the cultist bard Dustwalker. Lysandra is embarrassed to say that they are so short staffed they have not had time to follow up on the bard or any of the others that were singing along. Seeing the situation she is in she reluctantly agrees to “deputize” the adventures to handle tracking down the Shepherd.

Sargent Dunberry goes into a back room and returns with badges for each of the adventurers. For the members present, he flashes the badge up to their face and magically the face appears on the front of the badge. He then begins to drone on about all the rules that must be followed while wearing the badges. He hands the group the remaining 3 blank badges to scan when the party is all back together. The badges work for 2 weeks before they reset and have to be brought back.

The next thing the group sees are festival booth games. The group is immediately drawn to Axe Me Anything, an axe throwing booth. There are others already throwing axes and being heckled by a woman who appears to be running the booth. Hilda Logsplitter enthusiastically pulls the group in to try their luck at the simple game. For 2 silver you get to throw 3 axes at targets that get increasingly farther away. Hit all three and you earn tokens for the festival and can move on to the more challenging targets. Kasemine, Baergrom, Bonogo and Ephanna all agree to play, while Stafl settles for betting on those that are throwing.

Of the three throwing areas - traditional targets, city council targets and mythical creatures - Kasemine chooses to throw at the council “officials” targets. Her first throw is almost perfect, hitting the target with a satisfying thunk. Her second throw is even better, almost dead center. But her last throw sails just wide, earning her a good razzing from Hilda. Ephanna is next to throw the axes, this time against the mythical creatures. The first throw is really good and lands on the target. The next throw lands dead center, earning a gasp from the crowd gathering to watch. However, just like Karsemine, the last throw flies over the top of the last target.

Next is Bonogo - determined to hit all three targets. He takes on the council targets as well. His first throw is great, hitting the target dead center. The second throw however flies over the target. Determined not to be out so early, Bonogo lines up an incredible throw. The axe hits the top of the second target and flies over the top and hits the third target, earning cheers from the crowd and a chance to head to the advanced round. He has the chance to gamble his 10 magic Mayor Marks - the festival tokens - and play for 30 marks and an “extremely rare” commemorative spoon with his name on it. Bonogo does not hesitate, he wants the spoon. However, his first throw sails wide and with no chance to earn the spoon he gets a good razzing again from Hilda. His next throw lands with a clean hit, but he misses the final throw.

As Karsemine is watching Bonogo she notices a box over where Hilda is standing. As she gets closer she can see it is piled high with the “rare” spoons. While she is talking with Hilda and distracting her, she deftly uses her tail to steal a spoon for Bonogo. She is overjoyed to see the big grin on Bonogo’s face when she hands him the spoon.

Last up is Baergrom who is confident that he can hit all the targets. He decides on the mythical creatures and makes quick work with the first three - hitting all three almost dead center. With his confidence high, he decides to gamble his tokens for the advanced round. The first throw lands, but the last two sail wide. No spoon for Baergrom either.

With the morning activities ending the party decides to participate in the afternoon events - another round of fights at the Coliseum of the Storm and another round of Magic Duels at the Arcane Arena.
