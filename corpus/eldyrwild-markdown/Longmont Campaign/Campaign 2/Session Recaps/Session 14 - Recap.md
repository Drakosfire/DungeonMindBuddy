# Session 14 Recap:

Karsemine is rushing to the Academy with Winna, fearing more corruption from the guards watching the Dustwalker. Winna is even more stressed out than usual and she is very short with the young sorcerer at the front desk. They are informed that a group of guards came in shortly after Karsemine left earlier. After some back and forth, Karsemine eventually reveals what really happened with her, Bonogo, and the guard (minus the fact that they attacked the guard and she knocked him out). Even though the story doesn't sound good for Karsemine, Winna trusts her judgment and believes that she did the right thing in the situation. And despite still being upset about being told the guards were corrupt, Winna is convinced that everything is under control and she can finally go get some rest. Karsemine leaves her danger necklace with Winna then hurries out to join up with the other members of Questionable Company. The group, led by Lysandra, head for the old guard tower where they will be able to stock up on supplies for their journey. Despite the bad weather and late hour, the old guard station is fairly busy with soldiers. An old gnome gives a questioning look to Lysandra as the group enters the building. Again Lysandra begins to break down and the group is suddenly surrounded by guards. Stalf, seeing Lysandra is unable to defend the group, speaks up and demands Sergeant Fabio speak with the Mayor and all the questions will be sorted out.

While Stafl is contacting the mayor with the sergeant, Ephanna and Karsemine help Lysandra recover. With everything sorted out, the grizzled old soldier, with beautiful flowing hair, takes the group down the steps into the basement to see the bursar. They find dusty shelves, mostly empty, with papers, forms, and assorted items left by the city for storage. Plume, the old man bustling around the room, brightens up seeing the group enter the room. He agrees to prepare the basic travel supplies and will have them ready in the morning. The group then heads back to the Copper and Quartz Inn for a good night’s rest. The morning starts with a request for some barrels of ale for the long trip. With some convincing the barkeep finally agrees to ask Barin on the group’s behalf. On the way out Bonogo slips his “living slime” into the sewer. Ephanna wants to stop at the Academy to let Torbin know the group is leaving for a long trip. With that decided the group head back to Wolf Manor, eager to get things finished before the start of the trip to the swamp. Despite the decaying look of the estate most of the structure is sound. The team takes the opportunity to finally explore some of the rooms in the house. As the group heads for the manor, Bonogo heads for the crypt. Stafl checks out the library, looking for any useful maps or spells. He finds a very old scouting map that details the wilds outside of the capital.

Ephanna does a quick check out the master chamber, but nothing immediately jumps out. They meet up with Karsemine in the Trophy Room where they find lots of dust and wild animal heads mounted on the walls. They both make their way back to the kitchen, finding old herbs for potions. Next, they go up to the attic. Here they find old storage trunks and a safe hidden in the wall bearing the Wolf family seal. Ephanna attempts to pick the lock, but is unable to. Inside the old trunk is a dusty old bottle with an unknown liquid inside. They take it to Caelynn to analyze and she can tell it is a healing potion. Back in the crypt, Bonogo discovers a very beautiful large family crypt. He pries open the door and there is a sudden flash of runes, but nothing else happens. The ring he found last time in the box under the master room bed disabled any traps. Upon seeing rows of sarcophaguses he starts prying them open, looking for anything of value. The first holds just a skeleton, the next a skeleton and an iron amulet, the next, child sized, is empty, and the last two he is unable to open by strength alone. He begins to form a plan to use a pulley system to lift the tops off. Heading back down, Ephanna goes back through the master bedroom and notices a beautiful envelope on the bed, addressed to Bonogo. They use Mage Hand to grab it and open it and find a scrawling message ending with “I’ll keep watching”. Ephanna places the letter back on the bed and moves on to the children’s room. Here they find some old clothes, a locked chest and, most notably, strange symbols etched on the windows. Unable to decipher the symbols, they write them down and then pick the lock on the chest. Inside are some old toys and an old scratched up orb with constellations on it. They show the symbols to Caelynn, known to speak the most languages in the group, but she is unable to identify them as well, though they appear to be close to Infernal.

Bonogo is now able to lift the tops of the last two using a pulley system he rigged up. He finds more skeletons with wolf pelts and a glimmering carving of a wolf devouring sheep. In the final sarcophagus he finds two copper rings and a faded stone tablet. Finished, he heads back to the locked safe and unlocks it to find 40 silver, a broken compass, and a key in the shape of a wolf. Then reads the letter on the bed. The group gathers back in the kitchen doing one last check before they are ready to leave. Ephanna notices a very old and worn handle, but is unable to budge it. In fact, no one is able to get the handle to budge. At a loss of how to make it move, they remember the wolf-head key that Bonogo found. With a click the handle moves easily revealing a trap door to the basement. Inside the room they find a collapsed wall and another room beyond. The room is covered in the strange symbols and the floor is painted to create a summoning circle, the very same circle they have seen once before used by the Necromancer. Stafl, then Ephanna, each examine the room and discover the strong smell of cooked meat. Karsemine discovers constellations that match the rooms upstairs as well as the orb Ephanna found. Caelynn examines the ring and is able to determine how it works and believes that she would be able to make it work. Despite a tunnel leading off toward the city, they all decide to lock the room back up and return to the Academy. They are able to talk with Torbin and Caelynn is able to find someone to check on her mushrooms. Suddenly they hear a very upset goblin arguing about payment for an order. They find Blizzy arguing with Winna about payment for a huge potion order. Stalf uses Suggestion on Winna, but fails to convince her to pay for the order; they would need to request the payment from Headmaster Tinkerbright. Then Stafl persuades Blizzy that all will be taken care of and she can leave the cart of potions with him.
