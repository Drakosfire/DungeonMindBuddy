# Session 12 Recap:

The Battle of the Bards By now the party is in full swing at the Copper and Quartz. Almost all the patrons are drinking and enjoying stories and music. Bonogo, using the paper he acquired earlier in the city, makes some “Wanted” posters of the hooded figure. He hangs up a couple around the Inn then hangs on to the rest. Stafl then whips the crowd up and he leads them down the street to the Glimmering Globe for the Bardic Storytelling Competition. By the time the crowd enters the building Stafl is being carried in and anyone on the street is curious about the competition. Once Stafl is on stage the crowd finds seats or joins a group up near the front, standing. Caelynn moves through the crowd getting them involved and hyped up. Bonogo takes the opportunity to view the seats from the top row and, for only a brief second, sees the hooded figure down in the front. Baergrom joins a group of dwarves, attempting to sway them to Stafl.

Meanwhile, on the stage, Stafl calls for all the other competitors to come out and join him. The group includes a halfling, half-orc, a very old human and a shadowy figure who remains just off stage. The first Bard to compete is the Halfling. They perform using juggling to tell an old story. Stafl gets the other Bards to provide backup music. Then the old man performs a somber poem. While the next Bard is performing with a sort of glass xylophone, Stafl asks about the shadowy figure. He is informed that it is the Dustwalker! Stafl quickly calls all the members of Questionable Company back to stage as the Half-Orc begins to play a gigantic chain harp. Stafl alerts the group of the figure. When Ephanna and Karsemine check the shadowy figure he just smiles and tips his hat. Bonogo immediately throws a dagger at the Dustwalker, but is intercepted when one of his red cloaked entourage jumps in front of the dagger. Bonogo throws two more daggers, one finally finding its mark. Caelynn then runs to the guard, asking for them to contact Lesandra through their “rocky-talky”. He connects with the city’s twin operators, Sara and Frank, and asks for help. Then the battle begins.

The Dustwalker took a good amount of damage from the dagger throw, and is now also poisoned. Bonogo rushes in for an attack with his dagger and lands a large amount of damage. He follows it up with an attack with his short sword, landing two more hits. He gets a bloody smile in return. Caelynn casts Snowball on the group of red cloaks surrounding the Dustwalker, killing half of them with one spell. The Dustwalker pulls out a guitar, forming an attack on Bonogo, but the string snaps and his attack misses. He turns to the crowd around the front of the stage and casts Word of the Shepherd and the crowd becomes charmed, along with Karsemine and Baergrom. Both of them break out of the spell, but are unable to attack. There is an arcane hum building as the song continues. The red cloaks, called the Crimson Chorus, cast a spell on Bonogo then attack him for big damage. Blood erupts from his eyes and nose. Ephanna uses Hex on the Dustwalker, but misses both attacks. Stafl quickly uses Suggestion on the Half-Orc and the crowd near the stage and has the orc lead a large part of the audience up and out of the building. He then casts Celestial Revelation and uses Necrotic Shroud to frighten the remaining front row of the crowd, creating a barrier to the stage.

Bonogo makes another dagger throw at one of the red cloaks then lands a hit with his short sword on the Dustwalker. Caelynn again casts Snowball, killing all the remaining red cloaks. She also kills the Dustwalker! Finally, the spell over the crowd is broken, but many in the crowd begin to panic. Ephanna casts Calm and soothes the tensions. Each of the red cloak bodies remain, however all that is left of the Dustwalker is a puddle of goo (which smells like cooked meat). Lesandra arrives and begins taking notes, starting with Caelynn. She advises the group to stay inside the city. Karsemine and Bonogo, realizing that the Dustwalker must have broken out of his cell, rush to the Academy. When they enter the front door someone new greets them. Karsemine heads to Zilla’s office while Bonogo heads to the cell, but Zilla is out to see the competition so Karsemine rejoins Bonogo. They discover the Dustwalker sitting in his cell, right where they left him! They tell the city guard to open the cell, but he refuses. Bonogo then threatens him, but the guard is unaffected and calls for backup. Thats when Bonogo strikes him, attempting to knock him unconscious, but fails to. Against her best judgment, Karsemine also hits the guard, finally knocking him out. Bonogo picks the lock and opens the cell door. The Dustwalker calmly looks up at them and nods his head. In a flash Bonogo cuts his throat. Again, all that remains is a puddle of goo smelling of cooked meat. They both rush back out into the city, sticking to the shadows as a group of guards arrive at the Academy.
