# Session 4 Recap:

With the sun almost hitting noon, the group heads to the next event on their list - the Acrobatics Extravaganza at the Glimmering Globe. Stafl begins busking on the street, hoping to gain some intel on the Shepherd and his “Flock”. Earning 33 gold in the process. Karsemine is very excited to participate, until she finds out that it is not a competition, but a show. Ephanna convinces her to slip into the show and participate. They walk around to the back of the Globe and find a door standing open. As Karsemine and Ephanna walk through the back door, they are greeted yet again by a familiar face behind a desk - Winna. Dressed in all black and with a magic earpiece, she is clearly helping to run the event. Her expression at seeing Karsemine and Ephanna is exasperation. She is again stressed out that they would like to bend the rules to get into the show. Ephanna quickly comes up with a plan. While Karesmine performs some acrobatics to show off, they will forge her name into the list of performers. With an impressive leap, Karsemine is able to parkour off three sets of beams and lands on a beam high up above Winna. Here she begins to juggle some unlit torches, dropping one and barely managing to catch her balance. While Winna is distracted, Ephanna sneaks over to the list and quickly writes in Karsemine’s name. Karsemine again attempts to juggle, using her hands as well as her tail, and drops a torch again, nearly falling in the process. After failing another time at juggling, Karsemine makes her way back down.

Winna notices the name on the list and is momentarily relieved that her name is on the list and she won’t have to break any rules. Just as Karsemine and Ephanna believe that they have succeeded, Winna notices that the ink is still wet, proving that it was added. She kicks them out and they both join back up with the group. That’s when a guard approaches Stafl. The guard is not happy about him performing without a permit. Just as the guard is about to escalate things with Stafl, the rest of the group joins in and shows them their badges. Stafl uses this opportunity to ask if he knows who we all are - “Questionable Company”. He immediately recognizes the group as the saviours of the city and explains that everyone is still on edge about all the missing guards. Now that they have cleared things up the group decides to head to Wolf Manor to check out the house they have been given. Outside the inner city ring, where you would find the Academy and the Council Headquarters, is an outer ring featuring a series of hills. Originally a defensive position for the inner ring, the outer ring now consists of very nice - and very old - houses. The group approaches one of these - a very rundown and ivy covered manor. Large sections of the outside have collapsed including the huge oak front doors, each featuring the emblem of the Wolf family. As the group walks through the broken gates they can see old tarnished statues across an area of about 30 feet, a crypt, a large garden and then the 3 story manor. When they enter the manor they find 3 doors, but only the first two are unlocked. Caelynn is on a mission to find the door to the conservatory.

She checks the first door and finds a dirty room filled with furniture and a large fireplace. Everything is covered with sheets to protect from the dust. Behind the second door she finds more doors and a staircase leading up. When she climbs the stairs she finds another dusty room filled with furniture and another staircase leading to the third floor. On the third floor she finds the conservatory - a huge glass room with tarnished metal shutters. A very old and weathered crank (turned by the collective effort of Caelynn, Karesemine and Baergrom) opens the shutters and turns a telescope into view. With a cap on the end of the telescope, and it being close to noon, the telescope does not reveal anything useful. Meanwhile, Bonogo is in search of the master bedroom. While looking he finds a locked door, but with his lockpicking skills it was easy work. This bedroom doesn’t have nearly as much dust, no sheets covering any of the furniture and nice paintings of what appear to be family members. With little decorum Bonogo begins to ransack the room. Grabbing anything that doesn’t look important and throwing it onto the floor. Finally, after several minutes he finds a lockbox under the bed. Again he picks the lock and discovers a diary, some old papers, an old ring featuring the Wolf sigil, and family notes. Bored with his findings Bonogo takes the lockbox and resumes searching the manor for anything else that could be useful.

The next room the group discovers is the library. In an old parlor there are sitting chairs, a lectern and a wall full of books. Bonogo, convinced that there is a secret room here, pulls all the books off the wall hoping to find a lever. All he does is make a huge mess of books on the floor. Karsemine sifts through the books until she finds one on the history of the manor and Wolf family. The group does discover a “hidden” door set off to the side and find the servants stairs leading up to the attic. Bonogo rides the dumbwaiter back down to the first floor, discovering a storage room that leads into the kitchen. On their way out Ephanna casts Unseen Servant and assigns them the task of cleaning up as much of the manor as they can. Now it is perfect timing to head back to the festival and get ready for the next round of the Mage’s Duel. Before entering the arena, Stafl strums a little tune and gives some Bardic Inspiration to Ephanna and Caelynn. The next phase of the Mage’s Duel consists of 3 rounds. Judges will award points each round for attacks landed and for creative style. The first round is a 1 on 1 duel to land 2 hits or disable the opponent. The second round is also 1on 1, but this time they have to use spells to move a small magical stone and hit the opponent. The final round is to stabilize an erratic levitating stone.

Caelynn is up first - facing off against a Drow Illusionist. She casts the first thing that comes to mind - Magic Missile for an automatic hit. The Drow strikes back with Hold Person. But just as the spell is beginning to form it suddenly dissipates as Caelynn casts Counter Spell. The judges are impressed with her two opening spells. Caelynn then casts her trusty Thunderwave. Not only does the spell hit, the Drow is pushed back to the edge of the arena. Caelynn easily wins the opening round with a score of 7 points to 2 points. Ephanna is up next - paired against what looks like a very seasoned spellcaster named Gimble. Gimble casts Charm Person. Ephanna fails to make the save, however they feel a sudden swell of inspiration (thanks to Stafl) and they succeed in not being Charmed. Ephanna casts Witchbolt, but misses. However the link to Gimble stays for the next round. Gimble then uses Magic Missile and succeeds in breaking the concentration spell. With a final spell Ephanna casts Create Bonfire right on top of Gimble, but Gimble jumps out of the way and then casts Shocking Touch on Ephanna - ending the duel. Ephanna loses the first round 4 points to 6 points.

In an exciting third match, much to Caelynn’s delight, a chair beat a nameless mage. Then it was time for the second match between Caelynn and the Drow. This time they had to use spells to push a small pebble and hit their opponent to score points. The Drow casts Gust to try and push the pebble into Caelynn, but she is able to dodge. She then casts Thunderwave to push the pebble at the Drow, but he dodges. The Drow then casts Mage Hand in an attempt to grab the stone and then hits Caelynn, but she dodges again. Using her arcane knowledge and her gift for storm magic, Caelynn then casts Dust Devil in front of the stone and sends it flying at her opponent, landing a hit and major style points. The Drow tries the Mage Hand again and hits this time. And with the final test of her magic skill, Caelynn casts Warding Wind and floats toward the opponent, hitting them with the pebble. The crowd, and the judges, go wild. She easily wins the round 15 points to 3 points. Ephanna, with some tricks up their sleeve, was ready for the second round. Gimble quickly cast Shape Water then propelled the water forward, but Ephanna dodged the pebble. Ephanna conjures a Fey and the Fey teleports with the pebble behind Gimble and lands a hit. After the teleportation the Fey cast Darkness in a cube around Gimble. Blinded by the darkness Gimble cast Gust in the direction of Ephanna and manages to land a hit. Deceptively, Ephanna moves the Fey above Gimble and drops the pebble from above - landing another hit. The crowd again goes wild and the judges award maximum style points and a big win for Ephanna. And in the last duel the chair wins again, moving on to the next round with Caelynn and Ephanna.

With the second round of the Mage Duel complete, the group heads over to the Colosseum of the Storm for the second round of the combat trials. This time the team - composed of Bonogo, Baergrom and Ogonob - face off against a fearsome Human Barbarian Druid in a magical Tavern Brawl. To the combatants the arena has been magically transformed into a tavern, complete with all the items normally found in a tavern - tables, chairs, barstools, glasses, and darts. As soon as the fight starts Hagan rushes forward with a shoulder charge, smashes into Bonogo and knocks him prone. Bonogo, quick as ever on his feet, jumps up and grabs a dart from the dart board. He moves out of striking range then throws the dart directly at Hagan’s head, earning a hearty laugh from Hagan. Bonogo then triggers his sneak attack, landing a decent hit. Ogonob flies into the fight with a series of punches, all landing for a great amount of damage, but triggering Hagan’s transformation into a giant bear. Using his experience as a fighter, Baergrom launches a flurry of punches, landing 3 out of the 4 for a nice amount of damage. However it does little to slow down the bear who then takes a powerful swipe at Bonogo, landing big damage. The bear then jumps over to the other side of the bar to use it as cover.

Bonogo grabs a handful of darts and starts throwing. Ogonob lunges for another attack, but misses. Baergrom grabs a barstool and breaks it to create a makeshift shield and club, but also misses his attack. Surrounded, the bear lashes out, but misses any attacks. Bonogo then grabs a bottle from the bar. He breaks it then slashes at the bear dealing a good amount of damage. The bear is beginning to slow down. Ogonob lands a terrific punch, ending the temporary health of the bear and forcing Hagan to transform back into a human. Seeing Bonogo’s strategy, Baergrom also breaks a bottle into a makeshift dagger and attacks with both weapons - slashing with the bottle and attacking with the club. Hagan is now looking pretty rough. But that doesn't stop him from also slashing with a bottle on Ogonob. With a final push Bonogo lands a slash on Hagan and then trips him, knocking him prone. While he is trying to stand up Ogonob jumps on Hagan landing a devastating punch, bouncing Hagan’s head off the floorboards and ending the fight. We also enjoyed the following delicious food items: orange/cranberry scones, ham and potato soup, shepherd's bread, some cheese and crackers, kettle chips and cupcakes
