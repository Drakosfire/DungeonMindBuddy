# Session 6 Recap:

The Dustwalker’s music not only seeps into every inch of the rooftop, it also seems to invade the mind. Little parchments begin floating around proclaiming that any true believer should stick around after the party for a chance to be enlightened. It is clear by looking at all the eyes that something needs to be done to keep the guards from transforming into monsters. The most likely, and in some ways most unlikely, member of the Questionable Company is chosen for this task - Bonogo. They must get the antidote to all the people at the party and the best way is through the beer. With just those sparse details, Bonogo comes up with a plan. First, still being invisible, he hands Stafl a dart and instructs him to miss the dartboard and hit the beer keg instead, creating a small opening that he can exploit. Despite the looks Stafl gets he does hit the keg, but only leaves a tiny hole. Bonogo then uses a file from his pack to make the hole a little bigger until beer starts to flow out. That’s when he initiates phase two of his plan: touching his Slinkstone he disappears only to appear again inside the keg!

Stafl is trying to figure out what just happened. He had a vague sense of where the invisible Bonogo had gone after he was handed the dart, but only pieced it together when he saw the beer stop flowing. Knowing the antidote was only going to be from this keg - along with an interesting Bugbear aftertaste - he shouts to the crowd that the next round was on him. Almost every person on the rooftop grabs a drink just as the Dustwalker begins to play. The group then starts to spread out. Ephanna, also still invisible, moves closer to what looks like a group or 3 merchants standing behind the Dustwalker. Ephanna is able to see their oily eyes as they begin to chant along with the music. Stafl tries to bring them drinks, but they flat out refuse. Caelynn is able to get her poster signed just before the Dustwalker reminds the crowd that after this final song they can seek the Truth. He begins to play “Shephard’s Wake” and all the guards on the roof join in.

Immediately Stafl and Ephanna can tell that this is no ordinary song, it’s a Charm. Stafl is caught in the spell and is compelled to follow the large group of guards to their “meeting”. They must seek the spiral gates and speak the password: Maelthor. Caelynn notices that Lysandra is also caught up in the spell. The party, desperate to not lose Stafl and Lysandra, follow the large crowd headed down the stairs to the basement. Baergrom, seemingly out of the kindness of his heart, volunteers to take the “empty” keg down to the back alley. When he removes the top he only finds beer, but a strong smell of wet “something” tells him that Bonogo is still invisible. Just as Bonogo shakes off the remaining beer clinging to his fur, Karsemine runs up to inform them that Caelynn and Ephanna are following a group with Stafl and Lysandra down into the tunnels.

As the large group traverses the narrow tunnels, often with dark paths branching off here and there, Baergrom notices that the tunnels are slowly leading out of the city. Ephanna notices that their Invisibility spell will soon end, so they recast the spell. And further back Bonogo pops out of thin air, the Invisibility spell that was cast on him abruptly ending. Luckily, he uses one of the dark branches and successfully hides from the rest of the cult group. Further on the tunnel finally ends at what looks like a dead end. And standing there is a stern looking human in full Cultist garb demanding a password. Quickly the group remembers the name “Maelthor” and the Cultist cracks a huge smile and invites them in - telling them all about the events of the evening: drinks, steaks to eat, music and at the end - a ritual. Then the tunnel opens up right into a field outside the city - complete with a pond of float goats and a huge barn with spiral doors and gates. The party can see several small groups gathered together - for a total of about 30 humans. Karsemin, determined to remove the Charm on Stafl, trips him as a form of attack, however she did not expect to leave him with a bloody nose. Meanwhile, Bonogo sneaks out of the tunnel and heads for the far end of the field, trying to remain as hidden as possible. Everyone, except for the three cultists cooking some meat, have all made their way into the barn. As soon as everyone is in, including his own party, Bonogo sneaks up to a corner of the barn and pours out oil, then lights it on fire. Ephanna, who is also outside, but still invisible, begins to smell the smoke.

Inside the barn, the music starts to die down and everyone finds a seat. The priest at the front of the barn begins to speak and each of the humans, including Lysandra, start eating the meat. Alarmed, Caelynn hurries to grab something to drink and pours in the antidote for Lysandra, then uses Shocking Grasp to knock her out of the Charmed effect. After taking substantial damage and some convulsing on the floor, Lysandra comes to and Caelynn explains the situation. Just then the Cultist priest begins to chant, joined by each human in the crowd, as well as music from the Dustwalker. Two robed Cultists walk down the center aisle with a cart - twin goblin children inside. With a final decree, the Cultist demands that someone impure is needed for a sacrifice and raises a dagger to strike down the goblin children. Stafl, reacting quickly, casts Hideous Laughter on the priest and he falls to the ground laughing hysterically. The Dustwalker starts to strum a slow song and begins a monologue: he knew the Questionable Company was at the party at the guardhouse, he knew that we would follow the cultists to the barn, he knew they would fall right into his trap. As he finishes 6 Cultists sitting near the group explode and morph into hideous creatures with giant tentacles and large mouths filled with teeth - they become Fleshborn. Other Cultists begin forming up around the party, while the rest of the room goes into a panic as time starts to stutter. The group prepares for a fight.

Karsemine uses Hunter’s Mark on Dustwalker then hits him with a Fire Bolt. He smiles and says he is looking forward to the fun. With a huge burst of light, Stafl reveals his wings, flies up to the center of the barn and pulls out the Peacemaker Fiddle and plays the Song of Shattering. Every Cultist and monster in the barn takes damage and becomes disoriented.
 Outside Bonogo and Ephanna, on opposite ends of the barn, remain hidden to the Cultists. Bonogo makes for the barn doors behind the priest, he pushes the cart back into the barn and then slams the doors shut. On the opposite end of the barn, and now seeing the fire growing, Ephanna yells to the rest of the Questionable Company that the barn is on fire and to make a run for it. They fire off an Eldritch Blast at the Dustwalker, but miss. Back inside, one of the horrible flesh monsters lashes out with a savage tentacle attack on Caelynn, Baergrom and one of the random humans that was nearby. Baergrom avoids the attack, however Caelynn is struck with necrotic damage, but avoids being pulled into the creature’s grasp. After being struck and killed by the attack, the human is pulled into the monster’s body and absorbed. Some Cultists move in on Lysandra and begin a chant. Luckily the group is able to avoid being Charmed again.

Recovering from being attacked, Caelynn and Baergrom go on the offensive. With anger crackling all around her Caelynn floats back into position and then unleashes a devastating Lightning Bolt in a line from one end of the barn to the other - killing several Cultists, dealing major damage to the Fleshborn, dealing huge damage to the Dustwalker and blowing a hole out the other end of the barn. Baergrom draws his longsword and trusty Hearthwarden Shield and quickly strikes a monster four times before bashing it with his shield and knocking it prone. Seeing an opening, he calls upon his maneuver Evasive Footwork and moves outside the barn doors. Just as Baergrom exits the barn the Cultists outside move in and slam the doors shut, locking Baergrom and Ephanna out and everyone else in. The flesh monsters team up to attack the remaining humans and Lysandra. Sheis able to withstand the damage, but the humans are killed and absorbed just as before. Lysandra turns and makes a run for the doors with Caelynn, but finds them locked.

Grinning, the Dustwalker begins to play a new song, clearly building up to something big. The flesh monsters corner Lysandra and Caelynn, attacking both for major damage and, in her weakened state, Caelynn is grappled. Another monster attacks the pair, this time also grappling Lysandra. Things are looking desperate as both of their remaining health is running dangerously low. Seeing these two down and almost out, the remaining monster turns its sights on Stafl who is still floating above the carnage. The monster leaps into the air and lands an attack with its long tentacles dealing psychic damage and pulling Stafl back to the ground. The Cultists begin another round of Dark Wispers, luckily but still have no effect. Outside, the fire begins to grow - moving up the walls of the barn and spreading out in every direction.
