# Session 24 Recap

The disgusting hybrid monsters continue their march toward the wall, but unable to reach it yet. One of them reaches the magically dark cube with Ogonob inside and lashes out with a tentacle, managing to hit Ogonob three times, but avoiding being grappled by the monster. Back on the wall, Karsemine launches a Firebolt at the tripod monster, dealing massive damage igniting the many escaping gases.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] Whether it’s the sounds of gases ignited or screams of pain, the monster writhes in agony, but is still alive. Ephanna and Bonogo both fail to resist the effect of the meatwings and are now compelled to only move to the monsters and consume them.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] Ephanna climbs over and down the wall in an attempt to get to the closest meatwing, but still manages to resist long enough to attack the hybrid monster attacking Ogonob.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] With two powerful Eldritch Blasts the monster is destroyed, leaving behind a smoldering pile and crackling purple energy. Ogonob then uses Misty Step to get within striking range of the tripod, but misses the attack.

Bonogo uses his Steady Aim ability and lines up a powerful shot at the hybrid.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] The arrow does massive damage, pinning the creature to the ground, but the monster pulls itself back up, ripping and shredding its back. Thrin tries to steady himself, but just starts to let panic set in and misses both of his shots at the monster.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/] Undeterred by the battle around them, the giant sewer monsters split up and head in different directions, but both continue toward the wall. Baergrom readies himself and takes aim with his crossbow, only for it to jam.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/] Frustrated, he throws the crossbow down and grabs a short bow, loads an arrow and fires at the hybrid monster, lodging an arrow in its head.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/]

One of the tripod creatures lashes out at Ogonob now that they are in range, hitting them all three times before continuing on to the wall. Before the monster can get far Ogonob reacts with an attack, landing a glancing blow. The other tripod creature continues to be asleep; under the influence of Stafl’s spell.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/stafl/] The group of meatwings all begin to fly closer to the wall, except one that goes straight for the sleeping tripod. It slams itself into the creature, killing itself, but awakening the monster. The rest of the group create a wafting smell of meat, enchanting Thrin, Karsemin, Ephanna, Bonogo, Caelynn and Baergrom.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/] Again they are compelled to find and eat the monsters, but cannot attack them directly. Two of the meatwings attack Ephanna and two attack Caelynn.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/]

Seeing the team in danger, Stafl jumps up from being prone and casts Bardic Inspiration on Ephanna then casts Enemies Abound on the golem, attempting to confuse and have it attack its own allies.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/stafl/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] The golem ignores everything around it and dashes to the wall, ready to start to break it down. Seeing this, Lysandra recharges her Commanding Shout and commands Bonogo, Karesmine and Baergrom to all attack.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/] Bonogo and Baergrom attack meatwings; Baergrom deals a decent blow with his bow, while Bonogo uses his dagger to slice one to pieces.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/] Karsemine attacks the tripod, dealing damage with her short bow.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] Lysnadra then removes the charm affecting Caelynn, who then uses a level 2 version of her spell Magic Missile, heavily damaging two meatwings and exploding a third.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/]

One of the hybrid creatures finally reaches the wall just below Bonogo and near Ephanna, where it begins to attack the wall, slowly chipping away the foundation. Karsemine again attacks the tripod monster with a Firebolt, this time fully igniting the flesh, causing it to burst into flames and die.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] She then transfers her Hunter’s Mark to the golem and learns it is immune to poison and charm, but weak to fire.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] So Ephanna uses Eldritch Blast twice on the golem, dealing big damage, its skin crackling with purple energy.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] Ogonob attacks one of the meatwings that's charming its allies, and with one hit turns the creature into a cloud of flesh and gore. Seeing the golem attacking the wall, Bonogo uses Steady Aim and throws a dagger at its leg, dealing damage and causing the monster to fall prone.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] Then Bonogo turns his attention to one of the remaining meatwings for another kill.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] Thrin takes aim and fires an arrow at the prone golem, and just as the arrow lands, whatever energy was holding the creature together finally collapses.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/] Parts begin to fall off as animal bodies and slime slough off and slide away.

It appears the battle is going well so far, until one of the giant sewer monsters finally reaches the wall. It uses its length to slide up the wall and reach the top, then pulls the rest of its body up and on top, right in the middle of Lysandra, Thrin and Caelynn.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/][NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/] The other sewer monster moves in to attack Ogonob through the magical darkness, but misses. However, both monsters ooze out a sticky gel that now makes it difficult to move away from the creatures. Seeing the immediate danger on the wall, Baergom uses his Bait and Switch ability, moving Thrin out of harm's way and boosting his own defence in the process, then uses his warhammer to deal a thunderous blow.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/][NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/] Turning to Bonogo, Baergrom then uses Commander’s Strike to direct him to attack.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] Bonogo chooses the sewer monster attacking Ogonob and with a well thrown dagger, skewers and kills the monster.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/]

The remaining tripod creature turns its attention to Ephanna, loading up its third leg again, but this time launching a massive spike, impaling Ephanna and pulling them toward the disgusting monster.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] The proximity to the monster causes Ephanna to lose the ability to pinpoint where the creature is.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] But before the creature can attack again, Stafl casts Sleep on the monster, who almost breaks out of the spell until Stafl uses Luck.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/stafl/] Lysandra can see that they have the advantage now and can push for a final assault on the remaining monsters.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/] She recharges her Commanding Shout and directs Bonogo, Baergrom and Karsemine to attack.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] Bonogo shoots an arrow into where he saw the hybrid monster enter the darkness, and manages to hit his target.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] Baergom is about to bring his hammer down again on the sewer monster when an arrow from Karsemine flies past, impaling the monster, but in killing it sprays poison on Baergrom, Thrin and herself.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/] Caelynn holds her attack until she can see the hybrid or the tripod wakes up.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/]

The remaining hybrid moves in to attack Ephanna, but is still blinded by the magical darkness. It swings wildly and misses the first attack, but hits with the second, dealing necrotic damage. After seeing Ephanna in trouble, Karsemine moves to the edge of the wall and takes aim at the hybrid monster.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] Her first arrow sails past the creature, hitting Ephanna instead.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] She adjusts her aim and shoots again, this time landing a killing blow on the monster.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/] With one monster left on the battlefield, both Ephanna and Ogonob join Caelynn in holding their attack until the team coordinates an attack on the sleeping tripod.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/]

Bonogo readies the team, then takes the first attack.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] He deals critical damage on the incapacitated creature, dealing massive damage in his first attack before following it quickly with two more attacks.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/bonogo/] All at once Caelynn, Ephanna and Ogonob attack.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] Caelynn’s Magic Missile and Ephanna’s Eldritch Blast pile on the damage.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] Finally Thrin finishes off the writhing creature, ending the battle.[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/thrin_branchborn/]

Surveying the field, Karsemine and Ephanna decide the first thing they should do is burn as many remaining piles of meat as they can.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] However, as soon as they cast Firebolt and Bonfire the remaining piles turn to goo and are sucked into the ground.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/karsemine/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/] With the battle over and full night upon them, the group must decide what to do next. Something needs to be done with the refugees from Edge, while some members need a much needed rest to regain spells. The group decides to split up along those lines: Ephanna, Stafl and Baergorm will stand watch at the wall above the gate, taking a short rest and recovering.[Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/ephanna/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/stafl/][PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/baergrom/][Location][Elderwyld/Cities and Towns/Mireward/] The others will go check on the town and create a plan for the refugees.[Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/][Location][Elderwyld/Cities and Towns/Mireward/]

As they are just about to head out, Caelynn makes a quick call to Grobnok.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/] After some muffled noise, he answers her call and she tells him about both Edge and Mireward Reach.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/][Location][Elderwyld/Cities and Towns/Mireward/] He is very frustrated, not used to not being able to make a plan and get things done. She attempts to get Grobnok’s rockie-talkie connected with Lysandra’s, but this will take some time.[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/PCs/caelynn/][NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/captain_lysandra_ironveil/] He will call them back the next day to check in. In the meantime, there is work to be done.
