Session 17 Recap: 

Caelynn and Bonogo approach the large dinosaur-like creatures. At over 15 feet long they appear intimidating, but welcome Caelynn while Bonogo picks up a broken piece of plate armor from the baby. The ankylosaurus family then wanders back into the trees and the group looks for the next path forward. Darkness is starting to fall over the forest, but a clear path is illuminated by patches of bioluminescence in the trees. Just as the group decides on a path forward, Karsemine and Bonogo see a stealthy shape climbing down a tree in the distance. In one quick motion, Bonogo releases an arrow from his short bow. The shot flies true and the shape falls from the tree. The rest of the group approach the humanoid form writhing on the ground, speaking in a series of clicks. The figure looks like a woodland elf and they can see it is wearing a cloak of shimmering leaves. It also carries a short bow and a leaf shaped short sword. With some broken words in Common he begs for peace and Stafl casts Healing Word to close the wound. Both Caelynn and Karesmine try to determine the language it is speaking, but cannot. Through gestures and a few words, Stafl is able to determine his name is Thrin, he lives in the trees and wants to leave the forest with us because of some rules that he broke. He tries to convince the group to stop and sleep for the night, but the group must keep moving if they are going to save Mossford.

Thrin shows the group handholds along the trees and then up into the canopy they find the atmosphere very different. It is very humid and the temperature decreases dramatically. They also sense a lot of movement among the branches. They move along the natural bridges into the next section of the forest. Far below they can see the spines of a large creature moving through the bushes. Thrin fixes some of the bridges as they continue on to the next area, one that is completely silent with the feeling of being watched. They discover a camping spot in the next clearing and Thrin demands they stop and camp. He tells the group that it is too dangerous at night with giant flying bugs that, when killed, will attract even bigger creatures. Ephanna sends up a magical owl to see over the forest and determine they are about half way through the forest. Despite feeling like it is around 7pm the owl is able to see that dawn is breaking. The group cannot delay any longer.

As they continue to the next area, Karesmine asks Thrin about why the forest is moving. He explains that the forest felt “gross” and needed to move after a “shiny rain” fell over the forest. As soon as the group crosses into this area Thrin becomes very frightened. His eyes get big and he motions for everyone to stay quiet. Down below they can see the deadfall section they crossed before, but now, among the piles of branches and bodies, is a gargantuan wyvern, the largest living creature any of them have seen. The group, not wanting a fight, attempts to sneak by in the branches above. With the help of Stafl and Caelynn the group, including Baergrom (with Thrin stuffing leaves in his armor) manage to sneak through without any trouble. The group crosses into the next area of the forest called the Broken Crossing. Thrin stops the group at the edge of the decaying and broken bridge. Seeing how Thrin fixed the bridges before, the group quickly comes up with a plan to build a makeshift rope bridge. Ephanna teleports across the gap and then Baergrom tosses rope to create the bridge. After they all clear the gap they are off to the next part of the forest. Not long after crossing the bridge Thrin stops abruptly and motions for everyone to stay quiet. Caelynn can just make out another creature like Thrin. Bonogo draws his sword, but Thrin motions for everyone to sneak by instead.

The group pushes through the next area and comes to the end of the bridge again. This time it is because there is no canopy above them and a giant swirling pool of water below. Just when they are deciding which path to take next, Thrin tells the group to quickly and quietly climb down the trees. The group can hear the high pitched buzzing of a giant creature flying above them. Once the buzzing fades, the group picks a side path to take around the pool of water. Pushing through some brush the group enters a new area: The Fallen Spine. Dominating the clearing is a massive fallen tree, the roots ripped out of the ground and the trunk creating a path up and across a low area. The group sends Thrin out first to cross the giant log. They can hear the thumping of his feet on the seemingly hollow wood, despite his effort at stealth. Karsemine goes next and deftly makes it across. Baergrom follows, also attempting to be as quiet as possible. Unfortunately, even with some help from the team, he takes a bad step and crashes through the trunk. He is able to grab the sides of the walls before falling completely inside the old log. Karsemine then notices sounds and Thrin starts to panic.

Stafl quickly rushes to help pull Baergrom back out and onto the top of the log. Caelynn follows right behind, staying as quiet as possible, but only making it halfway across before the group can all hear something massive moving in the mist along the treeline. Suddenly the gigantic head, long neck, and massive claws appear as the wyvern that has been tracking them emerges from the mist. The Apex of the Canopy, or the Mother of Broken Branches, launches herself at Caelynn. Using her wings she attempts to grab and take off with Caelynn as she glides past the fallen tree. But she misjudges Caelynn as easy prey and misses, only to be hit with a crackling Lighting Bolt as retaliation. All stealth forgotten, Bonogo leaps into action and attacks twice, dealing impressive damage. Karesmine casts Hunter’s Mark on the beast and discovers resistances and abilities that are tied to her being near her nest. Karsemine then fires off two shots from her long bow. Stafl quickly casts Hideous Laughter, causing the Mother to go prone, half hanging on the log and half on the ground below, while Caelynn launches another Lightning Bolt. Seeing the creature down for the moment, Ephanna casts Hunger of Hadar, creating a terrible sphere of darkness over the creature and dealing cold damage followed by acid damage. Thrin, seeing that the group intends to stand and fight, fires two arrows from his long bow. Finally, seeing just the end of the tail sticking out of the spell of darkness, Baergrom attacks with three mighty swings of his axe, severing the tail as the beast slides off the log and to the ground.
