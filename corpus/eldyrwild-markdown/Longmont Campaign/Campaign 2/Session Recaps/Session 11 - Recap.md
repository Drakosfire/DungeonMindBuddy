# Session 11 Recap:

The Coliseum Championship Suddenly the terrain changes: a huge meteor crashes down into the arena, splashing smaller pieces all over the ground. The teams make impressive dodges, but the ground is now covered in burning piles of rock. Bonogo uses his short bow to attack the Storm Elemental. Despite passing through it, he does deal some good damage, however the elemental now cracks with lightning and separates into two identical forms. It forms a whip of lightning and attacks Ogonob dealing massive lightning damage. The lighting then arcs to Bonogo, dealing damage to him as well. Ogonob teleports behind the elemental then attacks using force damage. Finally, Baergrom uses his action surge and swings his warhammer at the Stone Elemental 3 separate times, dealing a good amount of damage. The Stone Elemental attempts to hit him back, but misses.

Again the ground changes, this time a large hole opens up under Bonogo and he is forced prone and burned. He is able to climb to his feet and this time attacks the Storm Elemental with his short sword and whip. He misses the sword, but the whip deals big damage again. The elemental retaliates by casting some form of magic on Bonogo who now believes the ground is collapsing all around him. Then both Bonogo and Ogonob are hit with lightning. Ogonob again teleports behind the elemental, then striking with a swift blow, removes the head of the Storm Elemental. Baergrom takes the chance to swing his hammer again, only to miss completely and watch it fly out of his hands, landing somewhere off to his left. Undaunted, he pulls out his longsword for another attack, this time only hitting the wall, not the elemental. He uses Evasive Footwork to disengage and recover his hammer. The Stone Elemental slams its hands together, dealing huge damage to Baergrom and attempting to crush him. Baergrom, using his strength and his shield, is able to avoid being crushed. The terrain of the arena changes again. Suddenly lava erupts out of the walls. Bonogo attacks again with the short sword and whip, this time on the remaining elemental. With an incredible attack he is able to rip off one of the stone hands and smashes it. Ogonob follows this up with an attack, dealing big damage. They can start to see that the magic holding the elemental together is starting to fail. Baergrom unleashes multiple hammer attacks. The Stone Elemental wildly swings its remaining arm and catches Ogonob for a devastating blow. They wink out of existence.

The terrain changes one last time. A huge magnetic field manifests near Bonogo and Baergrom. Bonogo is pulled in and damaged, while Baergrom is able to dig in and withstand the pull. Bonogo unleashes a series of devastating attacks on the elemental, snuffing out the magic and destroying it. The crowd erupts in cheers and Karsemine leads the familiar Bonogo chant. As the group leaves the arena they can hear the next team starting. They are informed that the results will be announced the following day. Bonogo takes one final look back to the stands where he saw the hooded figure, but the seat is empty. Meanwhile, Caelynn heads back to the Stromspire Academy to find Professor Tealeaf. After watching her attend to some mushrooms, Caelynn asks about own. She encourages Caelynn to enroll at the academy, then explains the potion she can make from her mushrooms: a potent paralysis potion that can also cause Fear. They exchange mushroom spores and she instructs Caelynn on the best way to grow them. Caelynn asks about the professor that left. She learns that he was obsessed with storms and their effect on plants, especially at high altitude. He left about 30 years ago.

She heads back to the manor and sets up a small area in her room for the mushrooms to grow, then heads into the city to find a proper furniture store. She finds the Spore and Timber, a store growing right out of a giant stump. The store has beautiful, very expensive, furniture. Caelynn convinces the owner to exchange some of her mushrooms for a series of herb boxes. She will return for more in the future. She sets them up on her balcony at the manor then heads for the Inn. Back at the Copper and Quartz, Ephanna, Bonogo and Baergom are looking for a short rest, when they encounter Stafl. He has been "practicing" all day (drinking) and launches into a loud introduction of the fighters to the tavern crowd. The crowd goes wild and drinks are passed around.
