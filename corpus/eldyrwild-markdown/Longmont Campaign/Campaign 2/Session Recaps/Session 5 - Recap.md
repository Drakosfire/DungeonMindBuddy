# Session 5 Recap:

The fighters exit the arena to thunderous applause from the crowd. As Ephanna, Bonogo, Stafl and Baergrom are outside they are approached by Lysandra. The group almost mistakes her for someone else. She is dressed in very casual looking clothes, a stark contrast from her usual military outfit. She looks to be very uncomfortable and very anxious. The small group tells her that they are headed over to the Evergreen Apothecary for the Herbal Tea Workshop to catch up with Caelynn and Karsemine. Lysandra decides to join them on their walk to the Apothecary. As Ephanna, Bonogo, Stafl and Baergrom are walking to the tea workshop it becomes clear that Lysandra is trying to tell them something important, but uncomfortable. She reveals that she needs help from the group again as there is no one else she can trust. She explains that during the festivals each guardhouse hosts a music festival, however this time it is for guards only - something that has not happened before. And to make matters worse, only humans are invited. She explains that she needs help infiltrating a guardhouse to determine what is actually happening. Caelynn and Karsemine enter the courtyard of the Evergreen Apothecary and are greeted by a very enthusiastic elf named Elowyn Vireleaf. She will be leading the workshop and explaining all about the leaves in the surrounding area and how to get the most out of any brew. The smell is almost overpowering. There are numerous tables set up around the courtyard with bowls of leaves and pots of steaming water. Each table is also equipped with a mortar and pestle with which to grind up their leaves. (edited)

Karesmine is first to pick her leaves and brew her own tea. She picks Blue Cap mushrooms and brews a pretty nice tea, impressing Vireleaf. Caelynn chooses her leaves, and after watching Karsemine, brews the perfect tea, really impressing Vireleaf. Caelynn impressed her so much that she asks for her to brew a tea that even she has been struggling to make - Stormbark Tea. Again Caelynn makes a perfect tea, smelling of light char and cedar, and in the process makes another friend. They exchange tips about teas and leaves from various areas and Caelynn gains the ability to extract bonuses from brewed teas. Karsemine decides to try her luck again with a different tea, this time using Rattle Leaf, and, taking extra time and care, brews a wonderful tea. Vireleaf’s apprentice Bramblewood is very impressed with her skills with the mortar and pestle and offers her some “special” mushrooms to take. Caelynn is so happy with her new brewing skills that she purchases a traveling tea set. Caelynn is beyond excited to see her friend Lysandra, but immediately senses that something is off. It seems that not only is Lysandra anxious she looks exhausted. She further explains that she has not been able to get any rest. That’s when Baergrom suggests that everyone head back to Wolf’s Manor and then Lysandra can get some rest while the group decides what to do about the guardhouses. While everyone is taking a much needed rest Stafl asks Lysandra for a rough layout of the guard tower and then the group begins to form a plan.

Meanwhile, Bonogo leaves the manor in search of the Gilded Fold - the paper shop in the Artisans Quarter. This is where he hopes to find answers about his cursed letter. Right away Bonogo notices side-glances - it is not very common for Bugbears to be seen in this fancy part of town. Bonogo spares a glance for the sculpture of the origami phoenix over a scroll above the door as he enters the shop. Without missing a beat he walks straight up to the counter and slaps down the letter. Without even looking up, the owner, a snooty Elf named Volen, demands that Bonogo leave. Bonogo produces the blank badge of the city guard - knocking over a stack of pristine paper in the process. Believing that this must be some elaborate prank, Volen again dismisses Bonogo, becoming more agitated about the disrespect to his paper. In a final attempt to get the elf to relent, Bonogo informs him that the letter is cursed and, to his eye, looks to be worth much more than any of the paper he knocked over. Unable to resist, Volen takes a closer look at the letter. It is clearly something he made by hand and reluctantly tells Bonogo that he made this paper for an anonymous elf in a land far away over the desert. In exchange for gold and some dried boar meat, he sells Bonogo some paper and reveals that the paper is enchanted to resist burning and the name of the tiefling he worked with on the letter is named Merella. Back at the manor, Ephanna is helping Karsemine and Baergrom with human-looking disguises to help get into the guardhouse later that evening. Ephanna has a lot of experience helping Karsemine blend in to many different places so her disguise looks perfect. Though not nearly as good as Karesmine’s, Ephanna creates a great disguise for Baergrom as well. They test it out with Stafl and he can hardly tell that neither one is human. The group then heads to the library for a short rest.

Seeing that there is time before the music festival at the guardhouse, Caelynn enthusiastically encourages the group to head to Community Hall for the Unity Dance rehearsal. As the group enters the outdoor amphitheater they are met by the director - Glinter. Glinter is overjoyed to see new members wanting to join in on the dance. They explain that the dance is a reenactment of the battle for the city of Mirathorn. Caelynn is the first to join in, followed by some backup singing from Ephanna and Stafl. Karsemine and Baergrom use this opportunity to see if they pass as humans - blending right in. The group is ready to get Lysandra, who decided her disguise was terrible and changed back into her armor, and they all head to the guardhouse. At the entrance to the guardhouse the group takes a moment while Caelynn brews some tea to increase Insight for Lysandra (who is looking very nervous), Stafl, and Ephanna. The group easily pass the initial check as humans and head for the party on the rooftop, passing several floors where people are mingling, eating and drinking. The rooftop is an elegant setup and features more food and drink booths. In the center the Dustwalker is performing. After an initial check, Lysandra seems to think things look ok so far. Bonogo, who chose to approach the rooftop while invisible, looks for places where the money is kept at the booths. Seeing no easy targets he circles back to the group who are now deciding what their next plan of action is.
