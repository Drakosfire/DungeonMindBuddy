# Session 16 Recap:

At this point it is getting late in the day and starting to get dark. The group must make a final decision on the next steps. Stafl asks Lysandra about any landmarks between where they are and the town of Mossford and she is able to provide a good picture of the land around them. But much of the path ahead is broken up and very difficult to navigate thanks to the large roots of the trees. Based on their current distance to the town and how fast the trees appear to be moving, the trees would reach Mossford in a day and a half. As the group looks into the dark and mysterious forest, they decide to enter together and, using Karsemine’s ability, sneak through to the other side. As the group enters the first thing they notice is the amount of plants and other vegetation hanging from the trees, making it difficult to see very far. There is no immediate threat, but there is a feeling they are unwelcome. There is lots of movement and activity, just outside line of sight. Even the sounds are distorted, movements in the trees take much longer to hear after they are seen.

Not wanting to bring any fire into the trees, Stafl casts Light on his torch, but is extinguished when he is reminded they all possess dark vision. Ephanna immediately feels a Fey presence and can feel that the forest also is made of Fey magic. They cast Invisibility and they scout ahead for the group. Everyone else stays close to Karsemine who, through her spell, makes everyone almost completely invisible. After walking for a bit they suddenly come to a small clearing. In the very center is a pure white tree standing alone. Almost at once Karsemine feels totally at peace and moves to sit down in the bowl of roots at the base of the tree. Ephanna can feel the strong Fey magic here as well and joins Karsemine. Bonogo also moves closer to the tree looking for bones, but after appearing to be caught in a spell, curls up in a ball in the same bowl of roots. Stalf becomes very concerned that all three are unwilling to leave the tree and seem to be caught in a spell. He casts Identify and then touches the roots of the Thinking Tree. This is the place where the entire forest gathers all its thoughts and is absorbed by the Thinking Tree. Stafl discovers that this tree is not dangerous and that after about 10 minutes the spell is broken. In that time he also sat down and played a tune to invigorate the group for the next part of their journey in the forest. Karsemine leads the group further on into the forest.

After some time the group again comes to a sort of clearing, this time they find an enormous web of roots acting as a bowl holding water. At about 40 meters across they have two ways forward: cross the water directly or take the longer path around. While discussing what to do next, Stafl grabs his pack for a snack, only to discover it is infested with little spiked lizards. Everyone, except Caelynn, discovers the lizards in their packs as well. Ephanna and Bonogo grab the lizards, smack them on the ground, and then eat them. Meanwhile, Karsemine is further investigating the water. She can see that the water is very clear, but very deep, with only a small amount of algae forming in the very center. She puts her head under the water to get a better look and discovers that the water is very deep. The progress they have made through the forest has been steadily going up an incline. Suddenly there are 20 to 30 dark shapes all moving together in a swarm under the water. Ephanna throws a lizard out onto the surface of the water and in a flash of scales and splashing water the lizard is ripped apart by aggressive fish with teeth. After a brief debate about building a raft, the group decides to take the longer way around.

After another 10 minutes of walking Karsemine suddenly feels like she has lost her sense of direction. They find the path leads up to a giant wall created by deadfall from huge trees. Ephanna sends Ogonob up to take a look over the wall. They cannot see much further, but discover natural looking bridges in the canopy created from vines and fallen trees. The group decides to climb over the wall, and at the top Karsemine stops to take a look at the forest ahead. Oddly, there is no movement or life in the forest ahead. Her spell is ending, so she casts Pass Without a Trace again. She can see more “bridges” above and can now see that they appear to be made with footholds to climb. The trees appear to be unnaturally groomed. The group decides to continue on along the path. As they enter the next part of the forest time begins to feel distorted. No one can agree on how much time has passed and even the sun seems to be in the wrong place. The trees begin to thin out and the ground is covered in volcanic rock. After closer inspection, the rock is not actually the ground, but part of the forest itself. Baergrom and Karsemine determine the rock is not natural to the area and must have been carried by the forest. Suddenly Caelynn hears an animal cry from a clearing nearby. She finds a small Ankylosaurus in the center of the clearing surrounded by 5 large moss covered reptiles with large beaks, roughly resembling velociraptors.

Caelynn acts fast to defend the baby, casting Dust Devil and striking two of the attacking Canopy Prowlers. With the element of surprise the rest of the group attack. Bonogo launches an attack with his short bow, but misses, then vanishes again. Ephanna summons Ogonob who uses Misty Step and then casts a cloud of darkness around the baby ankylosaurus. Karsemine uses her long bow to launch two arrows, landing both shots on the same Prowler. Baergrom lands a hit with a crossbow on the Prowler, but it remains standing. Stafl casts Blade Ward on himself, preparing for an attack. Now that the surprise attack is over, the group attacks again. Caelynn uses her Innate Sorcery ability, further increasing her spell damage. Bonogo fires off another arrow from his short bow, this time landing a powerful blow. Baergrom, losing the protection of Karsemine’s spell, rushes forward with his axe. After landing a hit on his first target, he uses Cleave to land a second attack on another Prowler. He then brings the axe around for another set of attacks, landing a devastating blow and then using Cleave again on the second Prowler. Karsemine lines up her two arrows from her long bow and, with a shot through the eye, takes down the first Prowler.

Ogonob uses Misty Step again and lands between two Prowlers before casting Darkness again then they attack. Stafl fires off a shot from his crossbow, but misses. The baby ankylosaurus backs up on the rock it's standing on and takes a defensive position. Then the Prowlers attack. They attack the baby and rip off a plate of armor. Another attacks Baergrom, but misses. Then a second attacks Baergrom, dealing big damage, and tears a huge gash in his armor. The last two attack Baergrom and the baby again, missing Baergrom but scoring another hit on the ankylosaurus. Caelynn moves her Dust Devil to hit the Prowlers and then casts Snowball on the attackers. Bonogo lands another attack with his short bow then blends back into the shadows. Karsemine charges the Prowler attacking the baby and lands a killing blow. Ogonob follows that up with a devastating attack, turning another Prowler into a fine mist of gore. Baergrom, also in a rage after seeing the damage done by the Prowlers, charges up a mighty swing of his giant axe. He obliterates one of the Prowlers then, cleaning straight through the first body, lands a critical blow on the second Prowler. Finally, Ephanna launches an attack with several Edritch Blasts, dealing huge damage and killing another Prowler. The remaining Prowler turns tail and makes a run for it. Caelynn easily takes it down with Magic Missile before it can reach the edge of the trees.

Caelynn conjures a Goodberry and feeds one to the injured ankylosaurus. That's when Ephanna notices the two other, larger ankylosaurs standing just outside the clearing.
