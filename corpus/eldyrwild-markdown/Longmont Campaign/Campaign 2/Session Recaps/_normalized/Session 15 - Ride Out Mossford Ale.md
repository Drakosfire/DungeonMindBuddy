# Session 15 Recap

After negotiating the transfer of the potion cart from Blizzy, Stafl and Baergrom quickly get the cart back to the guard house to load up on the wagon. They are met by Lysandra, now in her new uniform and sporting her new rank. Sargent Fabio takes Bonogo back in to get his badge updated. A “picture” is taken using magic then burned on to the blank badge he was given. This magic will last for two weeks. With everyone present and ready to go, Lysandra reminds the group that she is in command and gives a speech about the quest they have been given.

They are reminded that the task is to only investigate the swamp; they are not to engage anyone there, no side quests or other distractions. Their first goal is to head north until they get to the town of Mossford. While each member of the team is getting acquainted with their horses, Stalf suddenly remembers the most important item for the trip: the barrels of ale. He conscripts Baergrom again to pull the cart and the two pick up and return with the 6 barrels of ale. By now Lysandara is starting to become annoyed and threatens a long conversation with Stafl later.

While Baergrom and Stafl meet their horses Lysandra describes Mossford. It's a small town along a river that has been dammed to create a reservoir. The town is famous for growing lumber created from growing special moss. This lumber is used all over, including Mirathorn. Baergrom and Stalf attempt to mount their horses, but they both underestimate their skill needed. Baergrom is kicked in the head; luckily he is still wearing his armor and does not suffer any damage. Stafl also has trouble with his horse, which looks much more like a pony with its short legs, and he ends up just walking along beside it.

The group finally heads out through the 10 foot thick walls, a stark reminder of Mirathorn’s past, and arrives at Outtown. Much less lively now that the festival has been postponed, some in the crowd begin to perk up at seeing Questionable Company. Seeing the crowd begin to gather around the departing group, Stafl announces that the group will leave a gift: two barrels of ale from the wagon. The crowd cheers as the group leaves Outtown and heads north.

In only a short amount of travel the group begins to notice changes around them. The land becomes more wild, the air is more humid and they can see moss aggressively growing all along the path. With even just a short pause the moss begins to climb up the sides of the wagon and choke the path ahead. Karsemine takes a closer look at the moss and discovers that it is rich in minerals and is even edible. She takes some and adds it to the supplies in the wagon, while Caelynn adds some to a bottle in her bag.

Continuing along the path into the early afternoon the air becomes much more humid and the moss begins to thin out, replaced by larger and thicker roots. Ahead they can see the road is becoming more and more difficult to navigate. Lysandra is becoming uneasy; this was not in any of the notes she was given. Stalf can see the tops of a sea of trees in the distance, swaying as if by the wind, despite the air being still. Karesmine scouts ahead and discovers a wandering forest. She is reminded of tales of a wandering forest destroying parts of Mirathorn in the past. The enormous trees all migrating together appear to be blocking the path to Mossford, with no easy path around them.

With some experience with the trees, Lysandra informs the group that this is an unusual situation. The tree migration usually follows a schedule and a usual path, but this migration is following neither. Caelynn decides to use the “rocky-talkie” to contact Professor Valin at the Academy to learn more about the unusual forest. She is connected with her, and Professor Tealeaf, and learns this event is very unusual and that the group should keep their distance while Tealeaf looks for additional information. The group moves slowly behind the forest, waiting for Tealeaf to contact Caelynn. The group is close enough to get a good look at the trees. Each over 100 feet tall, the forest makes an imposing sight. Kasemine examines the bark and discovers the trees are “shedding” something sparkly, similar to what she has seen before during the magical storms in Mirathorn and StoneBridge.

Caelynn and Stafl test the trees for magic and discover that the sparkly outside is magic, but not much else. Karsemine and Ephanna scoop up some of the shedded material and then Stafl uses Identify to discover that the husk is a sort of carapace infused with magic; the same magic causing the storms and the oily eyes in the cultists.

Caelynn receives the call back from the academy and is given some grave news. The migration is about 2 years early and the group needs to get to the other side as quickly as possible, there they will find vanguard trees that they can force to change direction and the forest will follow. But the group must be careful of  the dangerous creatures inside the forest. Ephanna sends up a fey spirit to observe the size of the forest and any possible paths around. The forest is too wide to go around, but not too deep to cross directly. Stafl and Karsemine come up with a plan. They will use Karsemine’s Pass without a Trace ability and move quietly as a group through the trees until they reach the vanguard trees at the front. Lysandra will stay back with the supplies and horses, while summoned Feys will help if a distraction is needed.
