# Session 21 Recap

While still waiting for a response Caelynn studies the sky once again. She estimates that the gathering storm will arrive in about seven hours. The group then decides to load up the wagon and head out in hopes of beating the storm. They plan to head back up the main road through town and continue north. This will give them a chance to warn the Mossford of the approaching storm and gather any supplies they might need. Stafl and Lysandra, both in need of a good rest, lay down in the back of the wagon, using this time to recharge while they travel. Caelynn prepares some tea while the rest of the supplies are loaded in the wagon. As the wagon approaches town it is clear that they are having a celebration. Stafl begins to shout, in rhyme, to the crowd about the storm. He is quickly approached by the sheriff, who is not happy about the news during the celebration. It also becomes clear he is not a fan of signing, or rhyming in general. He warns the group about rumors coming from a town to the north about cultists using music to convert others. The town, called Edge of the World, is about a one week trip to reach. They warn the sheriff about tainted meat and Karsemine uses her Thaumaturgy to show them what to look for when people begin to change.

They continue on north with Karsemine and Caelynn keeping an eye out for high ground for a place to stop for the night. While on this short trip everyone is able to relax and have a short rest. Because of their timing leaving the town and their navigation skills, the wagon is able to make it far enough north to just hit the very edge of the storm and only get soaked. They are able to see the sheen of magic as the rain pools up and instead of the usual petrichor the rain smells savory. Karsemine and Caelynn spot a good camping spot on the top of a conical hill. They leave the wagon and the animals at the bottom and make their way up until they reach a giant bowl of water surrounded by a grove of trees. From this position they can see out along the plains for a fairly long distance. After conferring with Lysandra, they determine that they are 3 days from Mirathorn and the next little town north along the road will be Mireward, about 5 days out. Everyone gets comfortable, sentries are posted, and they get a peaceful night’s rest. After another uneventful night, the group grabs their belongings and begins to walk back down to the wagon. As soon as they start Bonogo sees a huge double rainbow. Thrin is so excited to see it that he thanks the group again for taking him out of the forest. Bonogo is convinced that there is something on the next hill over where the rainbow appears to end. Ephanna reminds him of an old Fey legend about making promises under a rainbow. The group decides to make a short detour to the hill Bonogo thinks the rainbow ended on. What they find at the bottom of the hill is a very old trail covered in thick brush. After only a brief discussion about continuing, the group slashes their way up the trail.

Karsemine, always keeping a sharp eye on her surroundings, is the first to notice something just off the trail. She picks up an iridescent scale, still warm to the touch. She shows it to Caelynn to make an arcana check, but she can’t tell anything about it. As the group continues up the path the number of scales laying around continues to grow until it becomes obvious something is up ahead. Karsemine becomes very nervous about reaching the top and finding something waiting. While Caelynn creates Good Berries for everyone, Karemine scouts ahead. The top of the hill is again shaped like a giant bowl, just as before, but this time instead of water this bowl is filled with detritus and the horrible smell of decay. Karsemine sneaks back to the group and warns them that something is definitely living here. Bonogo does not want to leave without taking a look himself, so Karsemine casts Pass Without a Trace and they both sneak back up. They slowly make their way back through the remaining brush to the lip of the bowl and suddenly Bonogo is overwhelmed with the scent of reptiles. Down below on the floor of the bowl they can see a large amount of winged reptiles, between 40 and 50, and the remains of countless animal corpses along with human corpses with some remaining gear. The reptiles appear to be the size of a very large dog and their iridescent scales make them appear to be covered in rainbows. Despite the danger, Bonogo is convinced the “treasure” is on the human remains and tells the rest of the group that this is a big score waiting to be picked up.

As the group spends the next few minutes deciding what to do next, the majority want to leave right away, suddenly a large group of the reptiles fly up and away. Karsemine is able to tell now that they are young drakes. Based on their size five of these would be a tough battle for the group, so it doesn't look good if they are caught fighting the whole nest. Bonogo decides now is a good time to check again on how many remained in the nest. What he finds is nine young drakes remain, but he also discovers two large elder drakes and seven more sitting on smaller nests. Because of the spell from Karsemine he is able to sneak even closer without being seen. He is able to find a few valuable things right away: a metal slab with buttons and a pair of beat-up old boots. He decides not to push his luck further and heads back to the group. He is disappointed in the loot until he tries on the boots and discovers that they are the Boots of Crowing Wings. They allow him additional jumping distance. Back at the wagon the sky still looks good, but Caelynn can feel the storm starting to build and Ephanna feels a strange magic forming. The group decides that it is best to find a spot quickly where they can make any sort of shelter from the storm. They continue up the path until they reach the next small hill. Again they leave the wagon and hike half way up the hill before finding a suitable spot. Thrin shows his craftiness by helping to create a shelter from the surrounding trees.

Stafl is on first watch, with little happening, until the sky darkens further and he can just make out a huge swarm of wings. Ephanna takes the next watch, and the only notable thing that happens is they find a strangely flat rock, which they put into their pack. Shortly after sunrise Caelynn decides to call Mirathorn again. After a pause she is finally connected with Frank, who sounds very hung over. He apologizes to her, telling Caelynn that the city resumed the Festival and it was a long night. She is very upset at the news about the festival, but before she can tell Frank he abruptly hangs up on her. Now she is devastated. And things don’t appear to be improving. In need of a friend, she approaches Lysandra to tell her what just happened, but Lysandra doesn’t appear to notice that Caelynn is barely holding back tears. Frustrated, Caelynn turns and walks away to gather her things. Lysandra then notices Ephanna talking with her while she is brewing some tea. She takes the chance to approach Caelynn again and asks for some tea and finally notices the state that Caelynn is in. Now it's Lysandra’s turn to burst into tears. After collecting herself, she tells Caelynn that she has done a little investigation into the person they spoke about back in the city and how she finally sees how much the group does for her. Again Caelynn turns and walks away, clearly not ready to talk.

Ephanna moves in to speak with Lysandra and tell her about the earlier call to Mirathorn. Lysandra decides to make a call herself, but does not even get an answer. Something seems wrong back at the city, but they cannot know for sure. Now the group must decide: continue on their mission to the swamp, or turn back and see what is happening at the city. After a brief discussion, they agree to press on to the swamp.
