# Session 8 Recap

Stafl moves within range and casts Healing Word on Caelynn, thus saving her from death. Karsemine turns her attacks on the nearby rip in space. After a flurry of attacks, including a critical hit, she destroys the portal and severs the arms. Ephanna moves within range of the monster attacking Bonogo and hits it with two Eldritch Blast attacks, killing the monster and freeing Bonogo. Ephanna’s Fey attacks one of the remaining Cultists and lands a killing blow after using Misty Step and Charming one of the monsters.
 While remaining prone, Bonogo uses Steady Aim with his crossbow to attack the nearest space rip, dealing massive damage. Caeylynn uses Sorcerer’s Burst on the same space rip, closing it and again severing the monstrous arms. She then speaks directly to the priest and, through the power of friendship, convinces him not to attack us. Baergrom attacks multiple times with his sword and shield, hitting the first but missing the second attack. Again the Dustwalker fails both saves and remains prone with laughter. One of the monsters attacks Baergrom, but the attack just glances off his armor.

A second monster attacks Karsemine, dealing damage and pulling her in. The priest doesn’t attack. Lysandra attacks and almost kills the monster attacking Karsemine, while Karsemine drinks a Potion of Greater Healing then finishes off the monster. Bonogo attacks and kills the priest, with a dagger through the eye and a witty quip - “Now I’m in your head.” Stafl waits and holds a spell, waiting for the Dustwalker to break free. Ephanna and Fey both attack another monster and kill it. Baergrom uses multiple attacks on the final monster and kills it, ending combat. Everyone gathers around the Dustwalker, deciding what to do. Bonogo ties him up with the chain and the group decides to take him back to town. After looking over the maps with Bonogo, Lysandra leads the way back, bringing the goblin children along. The group makes their way back to the Mountain Iron Vale Gate and Lysandra begins to coordinate guards to take care of the children and the fire burning. In the guardhouse she discovers a file on someone named Sorin Holdrom, discovering the identity of Dustwalker. She also discovers he was kicked out of the Academy for extremist ideas and creation of the rips in space. The group decides to take him back to the Academy to hold him there in a cell.

The group is greeted by Winna again, unhappy to see another problem again. She knows who Dustwalker is, but is not sure why he is dangerous. After a brief recount of our fight she runs to get help. Stafl takes the opportunity to Identify the sitar he took from the Dustwalker. It appears to be a custom sitar created in the Mirathorn. Originally bronze in color, now stained red with runes hand carved by Dustwalker. Winna returns with Zilla Cinderbranch, who appears to have been woken up and is frazzled. She places magical restraints on Dustwalker and locks him in a room. Winna is placed in charge of the door. The group decides to head to Copper and Quartz for the night. Even at the late hour it is packed and there is a huge party. They are greeted by a very drunk Barin who insists that Stafl keep the party going with some songs. Everyone grabs a drink while Stafl sings a song about the downfall of the Dustwalker. Karsemine asks for some caffeinated tea and runs it back to Winna, while the rest of the party head upstairs to take a long rest and prepare for another festival day.
