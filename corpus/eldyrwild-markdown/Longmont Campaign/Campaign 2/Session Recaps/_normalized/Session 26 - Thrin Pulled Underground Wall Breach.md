# Session 26 Recap

The Questionable Company watches in horror as Thrin is pulled into the ground by another hybrid monster. To the west alarm bells begin to ring out and everyone can hear rumbling and the ground begins to shake. Suddenly a large crack forms in the wall as the ground below it begins to swirl, almost as if it is liquified. Shadows are moving just below the surface. Bonogo and Ogonob quickly jump into action and attempt to pull Thrin back from where he went under. They just manage to grab a hand before he slips through their grip and falls into a dark tunnel below, then vanishes as he is pulled along by something. Ogonob doesn't waste any time and jumps in after, using Misty Step to chase Thrin down, and creates a cube of magical darkness. Karsemine joins Bonogo as they decide to dash to catch up and help Thrin before he is truly gone. Seeing these three rushing after Thrin, Ephanna moves down off the wall and closer to the hole to see if they can help. Back in the tunnel Ogonob is getting closer to Thrin when they are suddenly hit with a horrible wave of psychic damage and is so frightened that he cannot move any further down the tunnel. Ogonob attacks the hybrid creature as it is pulling Thrin along.

In a quick decision Baergom, Stafl, Caelynn and Lysandra decide to check the area to the west along the wall. Baergom jumps over the wall and moves along the outside wall looking for danger. He sees only disturbed ground and the large crack in the wall so he prepares and waits for an ambush. After some careful analysis, Caelynn jumps the crack so she can work her way down the wall to continue to look for danger as well. She prepares a spell and then waits for any sign of an enemy. The sound of alarm bells gets louder and voices yelling as clearly there is a fight happening. Lysandra follows Caelynn and also keeps searching for any immediate danger. Hearing a commotion on the inside of the wall, Stafl runs to that side and looks over to find a swarm of creatures burrowing up from the ground just inside the wall where the crack formed. He quickly instructs the town guards on the wall to find some oil and then he pours out a cure meat potion onto the swarm. A large part of the swarm dies, but is still looking for a victim. Stafl yells over the other side of the wall to Baergrom that the monsters are using a tunnel under the crack in the wall, then fires his crossbow at the remaining swarm dealing massive damage.

What's left of the swarm moves to attack one of the townsfolk, but Caelynn casts Magic Missile, hitting the monster all three times. Despite the onslaught of attacks, the swarm continues on and grapples the woman and begins to pull her back into the hole at the wall. A second swarm appears from the hole and grabs a man and pulls him toward the hole. Finally, a third swarm appears and starts hammering on the crack in the wall, widening it and weakening it. Ephanna reaches the original hole and starts down the tunnel toward Thrin. They cast Eldritch Blast through the magical darkness at the hybrid monster holding Thrin. Ogonob moves back up the tunnel then uses another Misty Step to end up in the tunnel under the wall. Kasemine casts Ensaring Strike on the hybrid monster, dealing damage and holding the creature from moving down the tunnel. Her second arrow also hits for additional damage. Bonogo uses Steady Aim and fires his short bow dealing massive damage. The creature collapses and Thrin is released. Ephanna quickly uses Misty Step to appear next to Thrin and provides some temporary healing, just as they see another hybrid monster charging down the tunnel.

Back at the wall Baergom comes up with a plan to stop the monsters moving through the tunnels. With a swing of his hammer he breaks through into the tunnel below him, moves right to the edge of the wall then firmly plants his shield, blocking the tunnel. Up on top of the wall, Caelynn casts Sorcerer's Burst and rains down fire on the swarm holding the woman, dealing massive damage as the meat monsters have a weakness to fire. In the chaos one of the other townsfolk is able to break free from the monster and runs away, while another turns to fight and attacks the monster. Stafl sees the town guards approaching with a large barrel between them. He jumps down from the wall and helps up-end the barrel and spill oil along the ground and into the hole, covering the monster and the woman it is holding. He then lights a torch and the oil begins to burn. The monster attempts to go deeper into the tunnel, but is blocked by Ogonob. So it turns its attention to the woman and begins to take large chunks out of her flesh. They take the opportunity to attack the swarm, delivering a killing blow and releasing the woman. She appears to be in very bad shape. Ogonob is able to break free of being frightened, but takes some damage as they are hit by parts of the collapsing wall. They use Misty Step to clear the opening in the tunnel and fly up on the inside of the wall. The second swarm tries to flee, but is attacked by the town guards.

Seeing the terrible shape that Thrin is in, Kasemine casts Cure Wounds on him then he moves back up the tunnel while she moves closer to help Ephanna with the new hybrid monster. Thrin runs back up the tunnel, out of the hole and back to the wall. Ephanna casts Create Bonfire on the spot in front of the monster, lighting it on fire and dealing huge damage. But suddenly they feel the same terrible wave of psychic damage and become frightened, no longer able to move along the tunnel. The hybrid monster uses this opportunity to attack Kasemine, landing a hit with its tentacles and attempting to grapple her. She dodges out of the way and then casts Hellish Rebuke, engulfing it in flames and killing the monster. Meanwhile, Bonogo has been working his way back up the tunnel and ends behind Baergorm, preparing to attack anything that comes into range. Baergom activates his shield's Ember Aura ability, creating a temporary health boost for nearby allies. Caelynn again casts Sorcerer's Burst, sending fire down on the monsters. Lysandra recharges her Commanding Shout, but holds until she can direct her allies for an attack. Stafl casts Sleep on the swarm attacking the wall and the one in the tunnel. The swarm is able to break the spell right away and grabs the man nearby, pulling them into the tunnel, but finds the way blocked by Baergom. Bonogo uses his whip to attack right over Baergrom and hits the monster for big damage, killing off part of the creature, but it still yet lives.
