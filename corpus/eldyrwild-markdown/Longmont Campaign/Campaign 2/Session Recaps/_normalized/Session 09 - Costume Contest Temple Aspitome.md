# Session 9 Recap

After a long night's rest Karsemine, Ephanna and Bonogo head to Miss Thistlebottom’s Emporium to sign up for the costume contest. Wait in a long line to enter one at a time. In front of them is a Tiefling with an elaborate feather costume and comments that everyone has heard of them - they won last year’s competition. They suggest not trusting anyone and make sure to sabotage anyone they can. Behind them is a small gnome covered in glitter. They tell them not to trust the Tiefling. Bonogo and Karsemine tell others that their costume is a secret.

At the front of the line before they enter a door is an assistant - they open the velvet curtains to let them in one at a time. They are greeted by Miss Thistlebottom on a raised platform. She asks Bonogo what his costume is and he quickly dons his bag as a hat. He asks what the prize will be for winning. Even though Thistlebottom is upset about the question, she reveals the prize is a custom cloak. Because it is not money or gold Bonogo leaves without another word, leaving everyone in shocked disbelief. Karsemine, however, really wants to win the prize. She tells Thistlebottom that her costume is “Essence of Human” - including big hair and a “darkness” theme. She is approved to enter the contest. Caelynn is running some errands as well. She is headed to the Stormspire Academy. Ephanna and Karsemine also headed there to check on Dustwalker and Torbin. Just as they enter they meet an exhausted Winna. She has been up all night keeping watch over the Dustwalker, even though nothing eventful happened. Caelynn is looking for the sorcerer that she saved in the battle against the giant monster. Winna is able to direct her to find them in the Medical Wing near Torbin. Caelynn also leaves some mushrooms with Professor Tealeaf.

Karsemine and Ephanna go up and check on Torbin. He is very excited to see them and asks for some things - defensive magic spell book, toys, and of course, candy. He is not happy about being cooped up all day inside the medical wing waiting to be released to begin his schooling. Meanwhile, Stafl is interested in stocking up potions. He asks around the bar for the best alchemy shop and is directed to the goblin part of town. He and Baergrom head there and meet Grobnok who further directs them to the perfect store - Sputtering Flask, and ask for Blizzy. They get quite a few looks as they cross the goblin part of town, but find the shop. They see Bonogo enter right before they do. Inside a goblin, Blizzy, is running from pot to pot tasting each one before adding more ingredients and then running off to the next pot. Another goblin, Fizzlewart, tells them not to interrupt Blizzy and asks how he can help them.

Stalf comes up with a long list of potions that he deems beneficial in battle and even throws in a potion for Baergrom. Blizzy finally notices them and asks if they need any help. She is currently working on a potion using the tainted meat - wanting to figure out what it does. She hands a ladle to Baergrom to try some of her “Blizzy” potion. Just when Baergrom begins to feel sick from the potion, Stafl gives him some inspiration. Now he feels very energetic, but very thirsty. Stafl adds that to the list of potions needed and then has Fizzlewart bill it all to the Academy. Through the whole experience Bonogo pretends to not know Stafl and Baergrom. The entire group meets back up again and heads to the Cultural Ceremony at the Temple of Aspitome. The long history of the country and city are depicted here and in the center of the temple is the High Priest. There are magical lanterns spread throughout the large magical space. Everyone is giving a candle and then, after a banging of his staff, the High Priest conjures a complex illusion. Along the outside ring other mages begin to conjure as well. Together they create elaborate shadow figures depicting the history back to the old gods and monsters. After everything was consumed there is only one remaining place and outside the light is shadows filled with monsters. This was the Dawn of the Empire.

Just like every year before, everyone can come forward to the crystal and share their magic to help expand the known world. Stafl is first to approach and touch the crystal. The crystal flashes orange before going dark again. Ephanna approaches and the crystal flashes purple and a feeling like a reveal of a card trick. Karsemine sees the crystal flash silver-blue with a feeling of discomfort. She can feel the crystal feeding on her past rage. Caelynn sees a gray-purple flash of light and then a distant roll of thunder along with a gathering storm and a faceless form of her father. Baergrom is the last to go, Bonogo does not participate. At first nothing happens, possibly because of his lack of magic, but then he begins to feel a strong drumbeat - like dwarves working under the mountain. He sees a vision of the people and dwarves that were lost from his village, then a silver raven takes flight from the old well and leads him down a path. The rest of the people at the ceremony all come up one at a time and continue with the crystal while the group watches on.
