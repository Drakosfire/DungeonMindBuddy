# Session 7 Recap

Outside Karsemine attacks the Cultist, but misses. Instead hits with Horde Breaker and then again with Nick and finishes him off. Inside the barn Stafl escapes the grapple and flies back up again and then throws a potion at Caelynn, knocking her unconscious and then reviving her all in one action. Both Stafl and Caelynn are attacked by Cultists with daggers. Bonogo unchains the goblin children and then instructs them to use the chain to lock the doors. Finding the hole in the side of the barn he then uses his short bow, equipped with poison, and attacks the Dustwalker. He then lays prone below the hole, attempting to use it as cover. On the other end of the barn Ephanna summons their Fey and then they both attack and kill a Cultist. Caelynn is going to teleport using Thunder Step but has a very difficult decision to make: she can either take Stafl or Lysandra with her. She chooses Stalf and they teleport out of the hole by Bonogo and out into the field. The thunderous boom left behind damages all the Cultists and Fleshborn within a 10 foot range. Baergrom chooses to rescue the trapped Lysandra by cutting a hole right through the barn wall. He grabs her and pulls her through the hole and outside the barn.

The Fleshborn inside attacks with its tentacles right through the hole and attempts to grapple anyone in range, but misses all the targets. On the other end the priest moves to the other hole and uses Dark Whisper on Bonogo. The rest of the Fleshborn monsters all move closer to holes in the walls and the open door. Another monster attempts to grapple through the hole and this time is able to grapple Lysandra and Baergrom, Karsemine escapes. One other monster grapples through the opposite hole and grapples a goblin child and Bonogo, who is also prone. The Dustwalker finally finishes his spell, opening vertical rips in space near Karsemine, Baergrom, Caelynn and Stafl. Each portal spews out giant tentacles, all attempting to grab anyone within range. Karsemine and Caelynn are completely wrapped up, including their face and head. Stalf and Baergrom manage to dodge. The Dustwalker then opens the barn doors and, after kicking over one of the goblin children, makes a run for it with Misty Step. Lysandra unleashes multiple attacks on a monster then gives an inspiring shout to Baergrom. Bonogo attempts to get the goblin child to help free Caelynn, but they cannot get through the tentacles. Once again the fire begins to spread, both out into the field and further up the side of the barn. Karsemine tries to break the tentacles wrapped around her, but she succeeds, but takes psychic damage. Stafl is able to chase down the Dustwalker and hits him with Hideous Laughter, hoping to incapacitate and break the spells for the portals. The portals remain up. Back at the front of the barn the remaining Cultists attack Ephanna’s Fey, but all miss. Bonogo sneak attacks the tentacles holding Caelynn, but couldn’t free her, then attacks the monster holding him, but is unable to escape.

Ephanna’s Fey attacks and kills one of the Cultists before using Misty Step to attack a monster. Ephanna also uses Misty Step and then Eldritch Blast on the same monster to finish it off. Baergrom uses Bait and Switch then Commander’s Strike with Karsemine so she can attack the monsters surrounding her, then using Horde Breaker attack them again. Baergrom pulls out his great axe and uses cleave to hit multiple monsters as well. Caelynn uses Magic Missile on the portal holding her, and destroys it. A monster grappling Lysandra uses Devour the Weak, pulling her into the monster’s huge mouth and inflicting necrotic damage. The priest moves outside closer to Bonogo then casts Dark Wispers on him, dealing psychic damage. Then the monster holding Bonogo also uses Devour and deals a huge amount of necrotic damage. The portals lash out again and this time grapple Baergrom and one of the goblin children. The Dustwalker fails both the poison and laughter saves. The monsters attempt to attack Baergrom, but cannot break through his armor. So they attack Karsemine instead for a critical hit. Another monster attempts to grapple Lysandra, Karsemine and Baergrom again, but misses all three. Lysandra attacks it back. One of the goblin children is pulled into the portal and absorbed. Karsemine then goes for an all out attack on the portal holding Baergrom. She attacks, uses Horde Breaker, attacks again then uses Nick and destroys it, freeing Baergrom.

Stafl fires a shot from his crossbow at the portal then moves to the Dustwalker. Bonogo attacks the monster grappling him, attempting to escape, but cannot. Ephanna moves closer to the Dustwalker, then fires an Eldritch Blast at the priest. The first blast misses the target and instead knocks Caelynn unconscious. The second blast lands. Ephanna’s fey uses Misty Step to get in range of one of the monsters and lands a killing blow, while Baergrom uses his greataxe to attack multiple monsters. Caelynn makes her first death saving throw. The priest and the monster both attack Bonogo, who is now very close to being knocked unconscious as well. Another monster attacks Baergrom, this time dealing damage, while another monster misses an attack on Lysandra. The Dustwalker is still poisoned and prone due to hideous laughter. Lysandra misses both her attacks. Finally, the fire begins to spread up and out, now at an alarming pace.
