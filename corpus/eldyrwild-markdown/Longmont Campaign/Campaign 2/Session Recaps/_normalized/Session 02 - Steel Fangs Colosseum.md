# Session 2 Recap

Ephanna and Stafl took their seats in the stands, but not before Ephanna casts Summon Fey and creates a nice copy of Bonogo - affectionately called “Ogonob”. Bonogo, Baergrom and “Ogonob” enter the arena to face their first challenge: a one-on-one wrestling match. Across from the team enter the challengers - a feared and seasoned team known for their unorthodox tactics and fierce arena presence.

The Steel Fangs consist of 6 members: a massive and imposing Minotaur Brute known as Gorrun the Ironhide, a lithe Eleven Duelist named Seraphine "Bladewind" D’Lorien, a gruff and no-nonsense Dwarven Shieldmaster named Rurik "Stonebreaker" Thorne, the mysterious and striking Tiefling Spear-Dancer called Valera Stormstrike, the renowned Human Pit Fighter "Laughing" Yorrik Grent, and a formidable Half-Orc Monk called Morvian the Ashen.

The first match appears to be very lopsided - mountain dwarf against a massive Minotaur. Baergrom rushes forward intent on grabbing the Minotaur by the horns and throwing him off balance, however the attack has the opposite effect and the Minotaur flips Baergrom up and over his head towards the edge of the circle. Baergrom executed an aerial maneuver, grabbing the horns then using the momentum to throw the large Minotaur out of the ring. Stafl had used a little of his luck to help turn the momentum. The crowd erupts in thunderous applause - the battle is off to a great start.

Next, Bonogo moves into position across from the Elven Duelist “Bladewind”. Bonogo grabs some sand and throws it into the face of the Elf, but misses on his grapple. The Elf expertly twists her body and counters with her own grapple. Bonogo counters and spins out of her grasp. Just then the ground begins to shake. Between rounds the magically enhanced arena will randomly shift, throwing off the fighters and changing up the battle. Bonogo makes a dexterity save while the Elf is thrown off balance. As she is stumbling Bonogo uses a shove, then a punch, throwing the Elf out of the ring and out of the competition.

Next up is “Ogonob”, Ephanna’s fey. They are facing a very difficult challenger - the Dwarven Shieldmaster. The dwarf hunkers down, as if with a shield, then launches at Ogonob pushing them back to the edge of the fighting circle. Ogonob tries to grapple and carry the dwarf, but he is much sturdier than he appears. Just as they come to a stalemate, the ground heaves up and changes, throwing off both competitors as they are locked in battle. As one starts to fall, both fall, neither with an advantage. Both competitors land outside the ring, thus being eliminated.

With just Baergrom and Bonogo making it through it's now up to them to eliminate the rest of the challengers to move on as a team. Now Baergrom lines up across from Valera Stormstrike - an acrobatic looking Tiefling. Valera strikes first with a solid punch, but Baergrom shrugs it off and follows up with his own strike. Then a second, staggering the Tiefling. Valera then sprints forward ready to push Baergrom out of the ring. With another display of strength, Baergrom uses her momentum to lift and throw her over his head, pausing long enough to give the opposing dwarf a wink, and throws Valera clear out of the ring for another victory.

The excitement is building in the crowd - will Bonogo and Baergrom survive the last two challengers? Both bouts look very tough. First Bonogo vs the Human Pit Fighter. As soon as the match begins, with maniacal laughter, the pit fighter tries to throw sand at Bonogo and rush to tackle. With incredible dexterity Bonogo is able to dodge both attacks and then land a punch. However, all it seems to produce from Yorrik is a grin. Then the ground begins to change and throws both fighters off balance. Bonogo is able to catch his footing, but the pit fighter falls, grabbing onto Bonogo’s legs. Bonogo walks to the edge of the ring, carrying the pit fighter with him. Yorrik tries to pull Bonogo down from here, but gets his head slammed against the ground and then followed up with a well placed knee strike sending the pit fighter out of the tournament.

After the last two matches the crowd erupts in wild applause. The crowd is still going crazy, encouraged by Stafl, as the last match is about to begin - Baergrom vs the Half-Orc Monk Morvian the Ashen. Baergrom starts throwing punches then shoves the monk back. The monk attempts to retaliate, but misses. Again the ground shakes and morphs and sends both competitors sprawling, neither one able to keep their feet. Baergrom is first to react, jumping up and then with a mighty shove, pushes the monk out of the ring, ending the qualifying round.

All the while, Karsemine was on an important mission - helping Winna Mossglade get out of the trouble. Karsemine had promised to speak to someone at the Academy on her behalf as a thank you for getting Ephanna and Caelynn into the Mage’s Tournament. The first thing that she notices is how empty Stormspire Academy is. She discovers light coming from an office.

Inside she finds Zilla Cinderbranch, the Necromancer, with her face buried in some notes. Zilla finally notices Karsemine. To her surprise she discovers that Winna is not in any trouble at all. In fact the issue is that she is a strict rule follower and that she only thought she was in trouble. Kasemine cannot go back empty handed. Instead she asks Zilla for a note explaining the situation - she is not in trouble and should relax and enjoy the festival.

Just as Kasemine was leaving she ran into Caelynn who had come to help. Kasemine explained all that happened with Zilla as they made their way back to the Arcane Arena, determined to clear things up with Winna and maybe even convince her to join the group for the evening. And that's exactly what they do. After explaining all that happened with Zilla, Winna agrees to join the group for a drink and watch the Bardic Showcase at the glorious Glimmering Globe.

Situated at the edge of Lake Mirathorn, the Glimmering Globe is a masterpiece in Artificer craftsmanship. As Karsemine, Caelynn and Winna approach the beautiful, flower shaped building, they can already hear the steady murmur of voices. They find Ephanna, Bonogo and Baergrom already grabbing drinks from a familiar smiling face - Pippa! Winna squeals with delight when Caelynn takes her to Bubbles and then Karsemine tells her the tale of her daring rescue. Everyone gets their Ether Foam Ale from a very busy Pippa and they all head for a table together at the front near the stage.

Then Stafl appears on stage while seeming to direct people and reassure them - clearly he just jumped up on stage out of turn. Then, pulling Bonogo up on stage, begins a rousing original song about the Bugbear. The crowd goes wild, jumping to their feet and cheering for more. However, his turn is over and up next is a Human Bard - Dustwalker.

The Bard begins to play a melancholy song titled “The Shepard Eyes. Several other humans stand up in the crowd and begin to sing along to the slow and unsettling song. Finally, as the song ends, the crowd is completely silent, chilled by the lyrics. Stafl, seeing the murder in the eyes of the crowd, quickly jumps into action and ushers the bard off the stage. The last band quickly changes the mood as they play an upbeat local drinking song. With the competition over the group heads outside for the final event of the day - the Fireworks Display over Lake Mirathorn.

The fireworks display is a beautiful combination of magic and alchemy. The crowd was enjoying it until a sudden change in the wind brought in yet another terrible storm. Caelynn quickly checked to see if the storm was the same magical storm as before - explaining to Winna about how the group encountered storms just like this twice before. This time however, a trio of mages step in to cast complicated spells to dispel the storm in a circle around the city allowing the fireworks to resume and the crowd to stay dry. Zilla notices Willa sitting and having drinks with Karsemine and Caelynn - giving a smile and nod of approval.

As the day comes to an end the group discusses what to do next. The festival has been such a nice change of pace that they agree to continue with the festival the next day. But they all agree - they need to track down that bard, Dustwalker, and get some answers about the Shepard.
