# Session 18 Recap

Session 18 Recap After taking cold damage from Ephanna’s spell, the gigantic creature reorients itself in the darkness, then climbs up the side of the log and lunges out at Bonogo. It attacks with three fierce attacks all at once. Bonogo is able to avoid the bite and one of the claws, but is hit by a punishing blow from the second claw swipe. Bonogo is able to use his Uncanny Dodge and reduce the massive damage, then dodges out of the creature's grasp. He then steady’s his short bow and waits for a chance to take a shot. Karsemine fires two arrows into the dark sphere where she last saw the creature, but both miss. Stafl also takes a shot with his crossbow into the darkness, while Thrin fires off two arrows, but both miss as well. Caelynn casts Snowball into the same spot, hoping the spell’s area of effect will hit, and hits the creature, applying additional cold damage.

Ephanna, able to see into the magical sphere of darkness, uses Eldritch Blast, and lands another hit on the monster. Meanwhile, Baergrom swaps out his greataxe for his trusty sword and shield and moves up to the creature, preparing to protect his teammates. But again the Mother lunges out of the darkness and attacks Bonogo, this time crushing him in her massive jaws, before diving inside the log with Bonogo firmly held by her teeth. Baergrom slashes her as she rushes past, but does little to stop her. Just before Bonogo blacks out from all the damage, he hears Stafl use Cutting Words to reduce some of the damage and stave off death a little while longer. Stafl then casts Healing Word and Bonogo regains consciousness, but misses his Hideous Laughter spell.

Karsemine launches two more arrows, but misses both; frustration at not being able to help her friends beginning to set in. Seeing Bonogo in dire situation, Caelynn uses her Innate Sorcery ability and then uses Thunder Step to teleport Bonogo to safety and deal damage to the Mother as well as Thrin. He runs to the edge of the trees and takes two more shots, but misses both, however, Ephanna lands another hit with Eldritch Blast.

Baergrom leaps down inside the ancient tree trunk, following the Mother. He attacks with his sword, landing a hit, then uses Evasive Footwork to disengage and then dramatically boost his armor. But just then the Mother turns to the opening at the end of the tree and dashes toward the group of heroes standing on the platform. She rushes through the group and attacks Karsemine, taking half of her health is one blow of her mighty jaws. Karsemine retaliates with Hellish Rebuke, but it is not enough to slow down the Mother who then grabs Karsemine and glides off to the very far end of the clearing. Each of the other members of the group attempt attacks on the Mother as she escapes with Karesmine in her mouth. Bonogo opens a terrible wound with his dagger, Thrin adds a second wound with his short sword, and Caelynn lands three hits with Magic Missile. Still the Mother is able to glide off and lands on the trees at the edge of the clearing, 80 feet away, before climbing up into the canopy. Bonogo can just sense where she might be in the canopy and determines that she is just at the edge of the range for his short bow. He launches an arrow; it flies true and lands, leaving another terrible wound. Karsemine, realizing that she has to act fast or she will be carried back to the nest, begins stabbing at anything she can reach. After a few misses he plunges her sword into the giant creature’s eye, sending it into shock. The Mother begins to slide off the canopy then, with a terrible crash, falls to the ground below.

Karsemine is able to escape the dead creature’s grasp and then deals the final killing blow. A massive shiver runs through the forest just as the body begins to be absorbed by the forest itself. Thrin grabs at his shoulder, then tells the group that the forest has focused its attention on them. Bonogo steals a quick look at Thrin’s shoulder and can just make out tendrils moving under a bulge, but in a blink is gone. Stafl asks Thrin what will happen to the forest now. In his broken Common, Thrin tells the group that with the Mother dead there is now a void in the forest and that something will move to take its place. While Thrin is speaking Bonogo sneaks up in an attempt to steal his cloak and gets a better look at Thrin’s shoulder. It appears to be a transparent bubble with roots inside. When asked about it, Thrin reveals that it is a “witness see” and leaves it at that. With only one more area to cross the group hurries to exit the forest. They come across a shallow swirling pool of water and Thrin shows them a path up and across through the canopy. The group can see the trees thinning out and can see the reservoir and bridge over a river to Mossford. Much closer to the edge of the forest they can see an old farmhouse with a family of Dragonborn out front gawking at the moving forest. Now that the group has made it through, Ephanna uses the “rocky-talkie” to contact Lysandra and see where she is. Ephanna is connected with Frank and can overhear a muffled conversation he is having with Lysandra, and it's not sounding good; something about fighting, “meat” and that she is not where she is meant to be. The last few things Ephanna can make out are: “do not cross boundary”, “breached”, “evacuate” and “map not random”.

Meanwhile, the group has started to make their way across the field toward the farmhouse. Karesmine and Baergrom are watching their backs when Karsemine hears something coming from the forest. She can just make out a six limbed creature clinging to a tree, one of the Mother’s babies. She takes a quick shot with her bow, misses, but the baby remains in the trees. Stafl and Ephanna reach the family first and discover that they are moss farmers. Despite the danger from the forest, the matriarch refuses to leave so the father of the family escorts the group into town to find help from the mayor. At their current rate the forest is set to arrive at the town in 4-5 hours. They must put together a plan before the forest gets too close. Just like the Dragonborn family, all the townsfolk are out in front of the town looking up at the approaching forest, while Thrin is looking wide-eyed and feeling out of place. As they approach the mayor is speaking to the crowd, many of them are also Dragonborn, about the old town policy of digging trenches to stop the forest. The group discovers that they attempted to dig trenches, but the forest moved right over them. Ephanna tells the mayor about what the group learned using fire, salt or ice to direct the elder tree. The mayor is wary of the newcomers, but willing to take any help to save the town. With an impressive show, Stafl convinces the town to help the group and requests a map from the mayor. He quickly sketches a map of the town and the surrounding area. Now the heroes must come up with a proper plan to divert the town’s impending doom.
