# Session 27 Recap

Before heading back up and out of the tunnel, Ephanna looks back where the hybrid monsters are coming from and can see that the tunnel opens up into a chamber. Not wanting to stick around for another attack, Karsemine and Ephanna head back up out of the hole and back to the wall to rejoin the group. Again they can hear shouts and fighting coming from the west, but the immediate danger is the remaining swarms near the gate. Ogonob attacks the swarm that is attempting to break the wall inside the crack, missing the first attack, but killing a portion of the swarm with a second. Caelynn casts Sorcerer's Burst and launches fire at the remaining swarm, igniting it and killing it. Under the wall, inside the tunnel, Bonogo and Baergorm team up for an attack. Bonogo stabs with his short sword right past Baergrom's head, landing three brutal blows to the swarm, leaving very little alive. Baergrom follows this up with a hammer blow, finishing off the monster. The burned woman, now covered in mucus, is finally released. Suddenly, this part of the wall is quiet. All monsters have been put down. Ephanna takes the moment to finally check on Thrin. He is again rubbing his shoulder and continues to look ashen, despite being healed by Karsemine. He claims to have "heard it" and that something wants to get to him, and worse, wants to consume the walking forest. He is unsure what it is, only that it is speaking into his mind.

After a very brief discussion, the group decides to all head west along the wall and investigate the fighting they hear. They leave the guards with instructions to alert the group if anything else comes to the gate. Stafl and Baergrom walk along the inside of the wall, Bonogo along the outside, while the rest of the group continue along on top of the wall until they arrive at the lower wall surrounding Ironveil Warehouse. The group on the wall can hear screaming from inside the inner wall and Lysandro yelling about people inside dying. Lysandra immediately jumps into action, but Caelynn grabs her shoulder, stopping her, and reminding her to stick together as a team. Everyone, those on the wall and on the ground, can feel tremors in the ground. Much worse than anything they have felt so far. They can see dazed refugees wandering around, then suddenly split open and hybrid monsters burst out of their bodies. They can see Lysandro attempting to single-handedly brace the cracking wall with a beam, shouting to others to run and help, when the ground begins to bubble again as swarms of monsters start to emerge inside the wall. They can smell something large inside the city is burning.

Karsemine uses her Thaumaturgy ability to yell to the people inside the wall. She tells them to get away from the wall, watch the ground, and kill the monsters with fire. Some of the people run to gather barrels of oil. On the outside of the wall, Bonogo can see where tunnels are forming under the wall and then retreating into the distance. Baergrom and Stafl climb over the smaller inner wall just as the outer wall begins to shake violently and a portion of the wall collapses. Lysandra, Ephanna and Karsemine all fall prone, while the rest of the team is able to keep their footing. Seeing the wall collapsing, Stafl uses Suggestion on Lysandro to move away from the wall. Now the team can see the ground begin to bubble again right inside the wall. New grotesque creatures come pouring out. Karsemine is the first to jump up and slide down a beam, straight into the action. She casts Hunter's Mark on a hybrid monster then uses Fire Bolt, dealing massive damage. Bonogo climbs up and over the collapsed wall and attacks the creature still in the hole, missing his first attack but scores a huge hit, slicing up parts of the monster. Despite the large amount of damage, the creature remains and more continue to pour out of additional holes. He attacks again, still unable to kill the monster. He can now see a much larger monster below, and it turns its many eyes on him.

Ephanna is next to jump up and then casts Bonfire on the same hole where the creatures are coming from. The fire does extra damage, but creatures continue to climb out. Ogonob also attacks the same creature in the hole, but misses, then uses Misty Step to return to the wall. Several new monsters emerge from the holes including dog-like creatures covered in gore and lots of extra eyes. A swarm of organ-like creatures attack Caelynn, inflicting numerous bite wounds. Suddenly one of the refugees near the warehouse begins to split open before bursting open to reveal another hybrid creature. A latchling, the dog-like creature, runs up the broken wall, dodging attacks from Lysandra, and attacks Thrin. The monster rears up on its back legs, now looking almost human, and uses its other four arms to wrap up Thrin. A third swarm emerges from the ground and engulfs Lysandra, biting and inflicting damage. Another latchling emerges from a new hole and charges Ephanna, but takes a slash from Bonogo's short sword and Baergrom's hammer as it passes. Still, it bites down on Ephanna and begins to drag them back off the wall. Baergrom charges up the fallen wall to help out his teammates, swinging his hammer at the monster attacking Ephanna, but misses. Drawing on a well of inspiration, he attacks again, this time landing a crushing blow, knocking the creature prone, then follows up with a shield bash and sends the monster off the wall, taking damage from the fall.

Baergom then uses his Action Surge and attacks the monster holding Thrin, delivering two huge attacks from his hammer and then another shield bash to send this monster over the wall as well. Thrin immediately fires two arrows from his bow, killing the monster. But even as the monster dies, another latchling emerges from the hole under the wall and charges at Karsemine, missing its attack, slipping and falling prone. Lysandra jumps to her feet and uses Commanding Shout, commanding Karsemine, Baergrom and Thrin to attack. Karsemine attacks the prone monster at her feet, dealing massive damage, while Baergrom attacks one of swarms and Thrin fires an arrow at the other latchling below the wall. Even with all the damage the team is able to dish out, monsters keep emerging. Another latchling appears as well as a new swarm brood. The swarm attacks Bonogo, delivering multiple bites at once, and the latchling attacks Thrin, biting him and attempting to grapple again, but Thrin is able to dodge. In the distance they see another refugee split and another hybrid monster appears. It heads straight for Lysandro and several humans huddled under a tree. It grapples two of the humans, but Lysandro manages to dodge the attack. Caelynn, using Innate Sorcery, increases her spell abilities then casts Snowball on the area around the hole under the wall, hitting multiple swarms and will the hole with ice. Stafl then casts Sleep on the hybrid monster attacking Lysandro and gives Bardic Inspiration to Baergrom.
