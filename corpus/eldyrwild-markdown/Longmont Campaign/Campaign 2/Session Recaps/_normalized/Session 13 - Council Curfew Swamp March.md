# Session 13 Recap

As people are milling in the city center after escaping the fight in the Glimmering Globe, an announcement comes through the magical lights up and down each street. The Mirathorn Council has instituted a curfew and the festival has been postponed. The Questionable Company is instructed to meet with the Council immediately. In the distance they can hear a storm building again and sorcerers preparing to protect the city. Stafl, Baergrom, Caelynn and Ephanna head across the city square to the council building, hoping to catch up with Bonogo and Karesmine when they get there. Caelynn, looking for some extra help with the council, attempts to find Winna in the large crowd. Baergrom helps out by instructing his raven to fly up and search for her. They see the bird land off in the crowd so they start to make their way over to Winna. Concerned about the curfew, she is already headed back to the Academy. Knowing that she is a rule follower, Caelynn uses her badge to convince her that she should ignore the curfew and come with us to the council. The group finds Bonogo and Karsemine waiting in the shadows. They tell the rest of the group about the guard and how the other Dustwalker also turned to goo. Bonogo declares that the guard was corrupt, causing Winna to become upset and worried about the others at the Academy. She hurries back to the academy with Karsemine following to help her. Back inside the council building the group finds the entire council waiting. This time they are meeting in a much smaller room, though still lined with guards. Ephanna quickly checks each one, but everything seems fine. The council, Elara, Barin, Grobnok, Tinkerbright, Thalia, Torrin, Merril, and Rurik, are all waiting quietly as thunder rumbles outside.

The first question from Mayor Elara: why the group felt the need to kill the Bard during a competition. Was it because he was going to win? Ephanna offers a quick rebuttal, that with Stafl there is no need to kill him, he would have beaten the other Bard anyway. This earns a stern look from the mayor and laughter from Grobnok. She believes Ephanna, but that doesn't stop the rumors spreading throughout the city. She explains that, even after all the trouble the group has handled in the city already, she would have to institute martial law now. The Dustwalker being in two places at the same time hints at a much bigger threat. One that she believes should be handled by the proper authorities. Grobnok is ranting about the lost revenue if the festival is postponed, while Merril is concerned about any lingering tainted meat. The noise continues to grow in the room until Caelynn whistles to get everyone’s attention. She uses her ability to diffuse aggression and convinces the council to work together for a common goal. The mayor agrees and instructs the group to be added to the line of authority, reporting directly to Lysandra. Stalf uses Suggestion to get Lysandra a promotion and increased pay. Torrin, the dragonborn smith, suggests seeking help from an Artificer to work on the runes around the city. Bonogo, suddenly remembering his wanted poster, shows it to the council. Grobnok jumps up and agrees to help look for the hooded individual, and eventually the mayor agrees. However, the more immediate danger still lurks. Ephanna tells the council about the time they were locked up and about the Shepherd in a swamp. The council tells the group about a giant swamp to the north surrounded by human outposts. The mayor requests help in scouting the area and reporting back on the scale of the problem in the area.

Lysandra has now been assigned to lead the group. Rurik tells the group that there may have been trouble in the towns along the way and the best place to find details would be in Outtown. Many of the members of Questionable Company are then reminded of the trouble their own towns faced. Thalia, commander of the guard, is instructed to provide the group with all available resources. Just as the group is about to leave Lysandra finally breaks down. Everything that has happened recently has finally caught up to her. After a few minutes of freaking out, she regains her composure and fully takes charge. The group will gather provisions and head to the swamp. The journey will take about 2 weeks on horse and many towns await along the way.
