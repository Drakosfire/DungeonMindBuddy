# Session 19 Recap

The redscale Dragonborn Mayor and the dark skinned, graying human Sheriff are speaking with Stafl when Caelynn investigates the sky. She can see a very large storm building to the northeast and tracking southwest; the air is already starting to cool. The mayor is worried about another problem with the storm; telling the group that they had terrible storms in the last two weeks. The group comes up with a basic plan: they will ask Thrin about the Vanguard trees and how to divert them while the rest of the group gets the townsfolk to gather supplies. Just then Bonogo smells the tantalizing smell of cooking meat coming from the Inn nearby. He immediately heads off to get something to eat.

Stafl, not wanting Bonogo to wander off from the rest of the group, finds a child and gives her 5 gold to run ahead and get the meat for Bonogo. Instead, either misunderstanding or because she is cheeky, she just thanks Stafl and runs off with the gold. Undeterred by the antics, Stafl finds another child, this one shy and appears to be often picked on by the other children. This boy, a small halfling, runs ahead but stops when he catches up to Bonogo. He grabs Bonogo’s hand and tells him that Bonogo is supposed to buy him breakfast, an obvious lie. Bonogo can even hear the gold coins in his pocket. He shows the boy a “magic trick” and uses his long arms to reach all the way around and pickpocket the gold and then make it appear behind the boy’s ear. He then pockets the gold before continuing on to the Mossford Inn. Stafl begins to form a plan, while Ephanna, Karsemine and Caelynn decide to head out to the forest to test their magic. Before they leave they ask Thrin about the Vanguard trees; which he tells them are the trees slightly out front of the forest spread out every 50 trees. Stafl relays the plan to the mayor and requests supplies to be gathered and delivered across the river to the south of town. The mayor is happy to send farmers to the storage buildings to gather oil and salt, however the Sheriff is not quite as keen on this plan or the group running it. Ephanna, Karesmine, Caelynn and Thrin run back to the forest, and when they are close Karesmine is able to point out the tendrils feeling along the ground in front of the vanguard trees. Caelynn has a thought, and asks Thrin if he is able to speak to the trees. He looks very uncomfortable and replies “no”; clearly hiding something.

Back at the Mossford Inn, Bonogo is just walking up to the front with the halfling boy. He is greeted by several old people sitting on the front porch, unaffected by the approaching forest. They are the only people Bonogo encounters as he enters the Inn and is greeted by the proprietor: Old Maude. Despite the early hour, he orders meat and ale and takes a seat with the boy. Then, in an enlightening moment, the boy asks Grandma if he can eat too. She tells Stuart he can have whatever he wants as long as he can pay. That's when he discovers his coins are all gone. Bonogo generously offers to pay for him (with the Stafl’s money he stole from the boy). With no one else in the Inn and a traveler to finally talk to, Grandma also pours herself a drink. Through several more drinks each, Bonogo listens to Grandma tell the history of the town. She has seen the forest moving before and has seen enough “disasters” to be concerned at all by this one. Not wanting to pay for all these drinks, Bonogo waits until her back is turned to whisper to Stuart. He instructs him to run to find Stafl and get more gold. He also asks the boy about being bullied by the other kids. Stuart tells him it is mostly the girl from before, Stacey. Bonogo gives him a few of his darts to carry and sends him on his way.

Stafl heads across the river with the Sheriff to meet the workers bringing the supplies. He instructs them to start digging holes, but leave the oil in barrels along the ditch and the salt in the wagons. The Sheriff agrees to come along, but only because he doesn’t believe this plan will work and thinks he needs to supervise these travelers. Suddenly there is a tugging on Stafl’s pants. He looks down to see the halfling boy he sent out earlier. His eyes are huge and he is clearly in awe of the Bard. Stafl gives him two more gold when he asks for more and sends him back on his way. Ephanna, Karesmine, Caelynn and Thrin, now standing near the tree line, decide to test out their spells to create “dead ground”, places where the trees will feel the unnatural ground and should turn away. Caelynn starts with a Ray of Frost spell and the feelers move up and around the affected area and continue on. Ephanna casts Bonfire and the vanguard tree moves around the fire and continues on. Then Karesmine pours out some oil in a 50 foot line and lights it on fire with Fire Bolt. This creates the most extreme reaction from the trees, but they still manage to go around and move on. They decide they need a bigger spell. Finally, Caelynn uses Sorcerous Burst and casts a 30 foot area of cold damage. They can clearly see the tree and forest turn to go around this time.

Ephanna, wanting to go one step further, lines up at an angle and casts Eldritch Blast in an attempt to push the vanguard tree in a new direction. Again they can see that the trees begin to turn even further, and then a ripple through the forest as the other trees all begin to shift until the ones farthest back completely stop. Then, a massive swarm of red insects pour out of the trees, heading straight for the group of “attackers”. Thrin starts to panic and fires off an arrow, but misses the swarm. The angry swarm moves closer and the group can see they are red gnats. Ephanna launches an Eldritch Blast, which hits, but appears to do very little. They use Misty Step to move further away and also grant temporary health to Karesmine, who was still very low from the last battle with the Mother. Caelynn launches another Sorcerous Burst, but misses. Karesmine casts her Hunter’s Mark to help the group gain an advantage. She tells the others that the swarm is resistant to all types of physical damage. She then loads up two arrows and hits the swarm with both. It appears to do good damage as something falls out of the swarm and lands in the grass. Thrin is also able to land a hit with his bow. Caelynn tries to conjure lightning with her Sorcerous Burst, but it misses again. With a burst of purple energy, Ephanna hits the swarm with another Eldritch Blast, clearly doing damage as more fall to the ground. Then the group tries to turn and run, but the swarm continues to pursue them.

Back at the Inn, Bonogo takes Stuart’s hand again as they walk back outside, and tells Stuart that they are going to go pay Stacey a visit.
