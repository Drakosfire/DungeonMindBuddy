# Session 10 Recap

Let’s Go Crafting
 Originally set in the town square, the crafting sessions were moved due to the large hole still in the ground. Flyers all over the square advertised the new location and all the booths that would be available. Each booth would have a crafting station and for 2 silver each person could attempt to craft an item. The items are meant to be introductions to the craft, with maybe a little bit of magic. Bonogo is first to head to the goblin alchemy booth to find out about making and using poison. A middle aged goblin shows him how to craft a potion for endless bubbles. Bonogo ends up making something far more interesting: a green goo that has a mind of its own and is able to clean as it moves around. Ephanna sees a booth featuring runic wood carving. The booth has a very large crowd, mostly sailors looking to make charms to take on their next sea voyage. The individual running the booth, Splitbark, is surprised to see the group approaching. He excitedly explains the challenge is to carve a coaster that, with a matching rune, could make a mug hot or cold. He explains the danger in making runes and shows how challenging they can be. Ephanna, Karsemine and Caelynn all join in. With a little help from their Fey connection, Ephanna does extremely well and is rewarded with a Warding Rune. Caelynn also does well, while Karsemine has some trouble getting the rune just right.

The next booth looks very inviting: a Fey themed candy tent. Huge crowds are gathered around, lots of families with children. Here they have the challenge to create an Illusion Lollipop, which, with a tap, will change colors and flavors. Using a stick and an elaborate dance they can turn the wisps into a lollipop. Karsemine is determined to get one for Torbin and does so well she creates something else entirely. She creates everlasting taffy, which still changes flavor. Caelynn does a great job and makes all sour flavors. Ephanna also does really well. Finally, Bonogo tries his hand, only to end with lots of hair trapped in his, which then turns into a tiny fur monster that is able to escape into the crowd. So, with a quick sleight of hand, Bonogo steals one of the lollipops from a nearby child. Just then he feels like someone or something is standing behind him, but when he turns nothing is there. At the next booth the group finds a tired old lady creating a variety of teas. To everyone’s annoyance, she is very slowly showing how to create a sleepy tea. Caelynn, overjoyed to create another tea, does very well and impresses the old lady. She shows Caelynn how to create Stormbark Tea. In a major clash of ambiance, the next booth features a mini forge and a rowdy group of fans from the fighting arena. An old Dwarf is excited to see Baergrom and shows him the basics of making a metal feather. Not surprising, with a background in smithing, that Baergrom does an excellent job and the old dwarf asks if he is willing to help with a job. Baergrom then spends the rest of the crafting time helping make copper medallions.

The next booth features Druidic Carving. The group is immediately recognized again and told that the “Grove” appreciates all the help ending the abomination in Hempholm. Here they will attempt to make a dryad charm. Caelynn goes first and does an amazing job creating a heart shaped fairy light. Ephanna, not so well, and creates some sort of creature. Next, they head to the Illusion Painting booth. A dreamy, slightly aloof elf is teaching how to create illusion paintings of members of the Council. Ephanna uses their ability to create an exact copy, and earns high praise for their “gift” at painting. Again they are off to the next booth, this time jewelry crafting. A very, very, old goblin named Knobs is showing how to make jewelry from bones. Ephanna makes a nice tooth windchime for the manor, while Karsemine is able to craft something special: a charm that jingles when danger is nearby. Bonogo is able to make a special bone ring that whispers compliments. Finally, the group heads to the last booth: the Storm Magic booth. The very old human running the booth is in awe of Caelynn and tells her about a human professor the academy once had that felt they had reached the pinnacle of magic and then left, to protect everyone else. Their last known location was Bald Green Hill in the mountains, but warned he was very nomadic.

With crafting behind them, the group checks the schedule to find out it is time for the finale at the Coliseum. They are told there are 3 teams remaining and all will face the same challenge: win, but you must also earn points making the crowd happy. They will face off against 3 summoned Elementals and the arena will shift during the fight. The group can hear the other teams already fighting and the crowd cheering. The fighters, Bonogo, Ogonob and Baergrom, arrange themselves in the center of the arena. Then 3 Elementals are summoned: Lightning, Stone, and strangest of all, a huge foaming glass of ale. The Stone Elemental is large, the biggest the team has fought yet. Bonogo strikes first, attacking the glass of ale for huge damage and leaving behind a large crack. After being splashed with ale, he leaps back and attacks again with his whip, dealing large damage and being splashed again. The crowd, spurred by Karsemine, begin to cheer, and Bonogo gets one more attack in. The Storm Elemental splits into 2 forms and uses a storm burst attack that hits everyone in a 20 foot radius. Bonogo and Ogonob are damaged and knocked back, while Baergrom uses his shield to avoid any damage. Ogonob teleports to the ale and then attacks, shatters the glass, but fails to save on the splash and is blinded. Baergrom uses Commander’s Strike, but Bonogo is unable to land a hit. He then uses Evasive Footwork to move back from the Stone Elemental. The elemental creates a stone wall between Baergrom and his allies and then attacks him for huge damage and knocks him prone.

Suddenly, Bonogo gets the now familiar feeling of being watched. He quickly scans the crowd and lands on a dark hooded figure watching from the stands. (edited)
