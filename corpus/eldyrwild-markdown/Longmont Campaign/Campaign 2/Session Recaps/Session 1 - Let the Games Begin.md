Session 1: Let the Games Begin Despite all the merriment of a huge festival beginning, the group decided to head back to the Inn first and get some much needed rest before heading back out to partake in the plethora of events. As the group weaved their way through the crowd to the center of the city they could see all the paths leading off in every direction; over-filled with people and noise. The paths were packed with colorful tents. As they passed by groups they could overhear joy mixed with some unease. Some people had begun to notice fewer guards were patrolling and it seemed far fewer party-goers than expected. The adventurers took their long rest and awoke with excitement, ready to take in the remainder of the first day’s activities. While each member of the party looked over the list of things to see and do, Stafl began casting Identify on a scale he had been carrying around for a long time.
 The dragon scale turned out to be a real dragon scale, however it was not of a dragon that currently exists. It is possible the scale was from an ancient dragon that is long extinct. While holding the scale Stafl could feel vast and unseen power as well as a steady vibration, almost like breathing. Meanwhile, Ephanna and Caelynn decide to see if they can still make it into the Mage’s Duel. As they approach the Arcane Arena outside the Stormspire Academy they can see statues of Mages from famous duels and an overly enthusiastic apprentice yelling to every passerby. As they enter through the doors they can hear the boom of magic spells as they take in the beautiful surroundings. They also see a friendly face from their previous trip to the Academy: Head Clerk Mossglade. She is standing behind an enormous desk placing name cards on a wall forming a large tournament bracket as other apprentices come running in and out with results from the qualifying rounds.

After a few failed attempts to get her attention, Caelynn (with some Bardic Inspiration from Stafl) finally manages to pull her away from the wall long enough to ask about entering the duel. They are informed that the process to make the name tiles is very time consuming and that Mossglade is afraid of getting into further trouble from the night before. Stafl convinces her that Karsemine will take care of any past trouble if she can help get Ephanna and Caelynn into the Duel. Mossglade finally agrees and, after taking a drop of blood from each, creates the name tiles for them both. The Qualifiers consist of three rounds and test whether they have what it takes to duel. The test is to hit a floating target with a spell. The second test is to hold a special ice crystal and summon an Ice Elemental. The Elemental will attack and they attempt to hold their Concentration on the spell. And the final test is, when given a special stone, can they decipher the magic pattern within. First up: Ephanna. As a Warlock the spell choice was obvious: Eldritch Blast. The second test was not too difficult, even with an attack from the Ice Elemental. The third test turned out to be very simple for Ephanna. The mystical stone, when held, turned out to be a Fey toy, something that Ephanna was very familiar with and took no time at all to solve. The other wizards taking notes during the qualifiers became very excited at this new participant. Next up was Caeylnn. Feeling a little nervous about not passing the qualifying round she chose the easiest spell she could think of for the first test: Magic Missile. Again there are excited whispers from the other wizards. The second test went easily for her as well. The final test, however, was much more difficult. Having never seen this stone before it took a long time for her to decipher the pattern, but in the end she succeeded. While Ephanna and Caelynn were working hard in the qualifiers Stafl again decided to use Identify on another object. This time it was on an old fossil that Ephanna had been carrying around. What Stafl identifies immediately is that this fossil is a fake. It is in fact only made of porcelain. But, while shaking the fossil, he discovers something rattling inside, just like the seed from the slain meat monster. Ephanna cracks it open to discover a small pale silver key. After a close inspection they determine that this is not human, or dwarven, or elven. And with help from Caelynn they also discover it is not infernal or primordial. The key is cold to the touch, but always begins to warm up when held. Now the group (minus Karsemine) heads over to the Forge and Fire district in search of the Colosseum of the Storm. Bonogo and Baegrom are interested in testing their mettle in non-lethal combat. Again the outside is adorned with statues of past champions and the inside is lined with magic. All around the arena are seats for people to watch and bet on the action. This competition also has a qualifying round. Teams of up to 6 fighters must beat the other team 2 out of 3 times. At the end the winners will receive a prize. And soon even some mystery challengers will be announced.
