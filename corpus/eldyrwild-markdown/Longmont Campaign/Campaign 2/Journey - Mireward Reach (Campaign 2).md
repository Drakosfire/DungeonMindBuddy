## Terrain — current march

**Open plains** with **occasional low hills**. Many hilltops hold **ponds** — not natural in any simple sense; they read as **relics from before this era** (pre-layer geography or pre-whatever your table’s cosmology calls “now”). **They could be anything:** drowned works, old wards, collapsed fey mirrors, engineered cisterns, impact cups, ritual baths, sealed doors under silt — use when you want wonder, hazard, or a dungeon mouth without committing yet.
