# DOSSIER

## Lieutenant Lysandra Ironveil

*Mirathorn City Guard*

---

## CURRENT STATUS

**Rank:** Lieutenant, Mirathorn City Guard  
**Appointment Context:** Emergency promotion during civic instability, following a City Council session influenced by magical compulsion.  
**Personal Reaction to Promotion:** Negative, contained, deliberately deferred.  
**Public Posture:** Professional acceptance without celebration.  
**Private Assessment:** Politically expedient, procedurally tainted, strategically inconvenient.

**Operational Role:**

- Acting liaison between the City Council, Guard infrastructure, and the Questionable Company
- Field commander for off-city operations where Guard legitimacy is thin or compromised
- Buffer between institutional authority and irregular actors

**Immediate Priority:**

- Remove the Questionable Company from Mirathorn’s civic gravity
- Establish uncontested command authority in the field
- Initiate and maintain the northern march beyond the city

---

## TEMPERAMENT & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

**Baseline Disposition:**

- Disciplined, restrained, unsentimental
- Speaks plainly; avoids rhetorical or ceremonial language
- Does not soften necessary truths
- Measures people by reliability under pressure, not charm or rank

**Under Stress:**

- Narrows focus instead of escalating force
- Defers emotional processing until operational safety is secured
- Becomes quieter and more directive
- Delays confrontation until terrain and timing favor clarity

**Anger Expression:**

- Cold, deliberate, postponed
- Rarely explosive
- When expressed, it is precise and uncomfortable rather than loud

**Trust Model:**

- Does not trust institutions by default
- Trusts individuals who act decisively and accept consequences
- Once trust is extended, expects competence rather than loyalty displays

---

## AUTHORITY & COMMAND STYLE

**Command Philosophy:**

- Authority is a tool, not an identity
- Rank does not confer immunity, insight, or safety
- Orders exist to solve problems, not preserve hierarchy

**In the Field:**

- Establishes roles early
- Expects initiative within clearly stated boundaries
- Corrects mistakes privately when possible
- Will override consensus if time or safety demands it

**With the Questionable Company:**

- Treats them as capable operatives, not subordinates by default
- Expects professionalism once marching orders are issued
- Will not tolerate freelancing that compromises mission integrity
- Does not micromanage, but tracks patterns closely

---

## PSYCHOLOGICAL STATE (CURRENT)

**Condition:** Operationally functional, emotionally overloaded  
**Self-Assessment:** Aware of reduced resilience and compensating intentionally  
**Coping Strategy:** Movement, physical exertion, silence, delayed processing

**Known Regulatory Pattern:**

- Long walks outside Mirathorn
- Plains and mountain foothills preferred
- Morning or evening, liminal light
- Solitary or near-silent company

This is not nostalgia. It is a practiced method of regaining equilibrium.

**Unprocessed Factors (Active):**

- Public loss of agency during mass charm event
- Exposure of Guard vulnerability and infiltration
- Promotion under compromised circumstances
- Responsibility carried while exhausted and under observation

---

## RELATIONSHIP TO THE QUESTIONABLE COMPANY

**Why She Trusted Them Early (Observed, Not Yet Explained):**

- They identified threats before institutions did
- They acted decisively without waiting for permission
- They demonstrated resistance to magical influence
- They accepted risk without demanding authority

**Current Stance:**

- Sees them as necessary and effective
- Does not idealize them
- Fully aware they are unpredictable
- Intends to command them, not follow them

**Notable Individual Dynamic:**

- **Stafl:** Trust mixed with restrained anger. Appreciates effectiveness. Resents the method used in the Council chamber. Intends a private confrontation after regaining emotional footing.

---

## THE PROMOTION: INTERNAL MODEL

**What the Promotion Is (to her):**

- A load increase
- A visibility increase
- A narrowing of exit paths
- A political instrument, not a reward

**What It Is Not:**

- Validation
- Vindication
- Safety
- Control

**Resulting Behavior:**

- Increased caution in civic spaces
- Preference for off-city operations
- Heightened awareness of being observed
- Zero tolerance for ambiguity once in the field

---

## MORAL FRAME & RED LINES

**Core Beliefs:**

- Authority must be accountable to outcomes, not intent
- Magic invalidates assumptions of consent and control
- Silence in the face of rot is complicity

**Hard Red Lines:**

- Will not knowingly sacrifice civilians for expedience
- Will not ignore internal corruption once identified
- Will not allow charm or coercion to be normalized as "necessary"

**Gray Zones She Accepts:**

- Deception in service of containment
- Irregular forces acting outside Guard protocol
- Personal discomfort to preserve operational clarity
