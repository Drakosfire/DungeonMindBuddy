# Dustwalker — Sorin Haldrim at the Longmont table

**Dustwalker** is the cult **bard** tied to **Shepherd’s Flock** music, **charm**, and **fleshbarn** pressure in Mirathorn. **True name on sheet:** **Sorin Haldrim**. **Aliases:** The Dustwalker, The Spiral Voice, Maelthor’s Echo. For **AC, HP, CR, DCs, spells**, read `Elderwyld/Shephards Flock/NPCs/dustwalker/dustwalker_statblock.md` — this file is **continuity + voice summary**, not a second statblock.

## Disambiguators

- **Role:** Antagonist / captured cult lieutenant (post–barn fight) in C2.
- **Affiliation:** Shepherd’s Flock; oily-eyed choruses; **Maelthor** rhetoric.
- **Not:** Torbin (ward kid), generic “cultist” — he is the **named bard** with **sitar** and performance-centric menace.

## Voice (short)

Slow, **poetic**, turns answers into **performance**; obsessed with **music**, rhythm, hidden meaning; **smiles** at wrong moments; hums under stress; **hates mirrors**. He does not fear death — he fears **oblivion**.
