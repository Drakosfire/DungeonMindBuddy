# Thrin — of the migrating forest (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Thrin** is a **woodland elf–coded** guide met **inside the migrating forest** (Session 17). The table may introduce him in play as **Thrin of the Branchborn** — that **full epithet does not appear in current recap text**, but it fits the **branch / canopy** language around the forest (e.g. the **Mother of Broken Branches**).

## Disambiguators

- **Spelling:** Recaps consistently use **Thrin** (not “Thrinn”).
- **Not** the **Mother** / **Apex of the Canopy** — that is the **gargantuan wyvern** antagonist; Thrin **fights beside** the party against her.
- **Not** a city NPC — he is **out of place** in **Mossford** (Session 18); **Ephanna** keeps him on watch when the party moves (Session **20**; still a loose thread in Session **21** prep).

## Appearance & kit (from Session 17)

- **Look:** Woodland elf; **cloak of shimmering leaves**; **leaf-shaped short sword**; **bow** (introduced with a **short bow**; Mother fight recap also references a **long bow** — treat as **recap variance** or upgrade picked up in the forest).
- **Speech:** **Clicks** + **broken Common**; Stafl sorts name and intent through **gesture** and fragments of Common.
- **Motivation:** Wants to **leave the forest with the party** — says he broke **“some rules”** (details not yet given).

## Forest competence

- **Canopy paths:** Handholds, **natural bridges**, **mends** bridge stretches; knows **quiet** vs **dangerous** zones (e.g. insists on camp when **giant flying bugs** risk drawing worse predators).
- **Lore:** Forest felt **“gross”** and **had to move** after **“shiny rain”** — ties to Academy / Tealeaf’s **early migration** briefing (broader arc in Sessions **15–16**).
- **Vanguard trees (Session 19):** Explains **vanguard** spacing (~**every 50 trees**); points to **feelers** ahead of the line. When **Caelynn** asks if he can **speak to the trees**, he says **“no”** — **uncomfortable**, **hiding something**.

## Witness-see (shoulder)

After the **Mother** dies (Session 18): Thrin clutches his **shoulder**, says the forest’s **attention** has **locked onto** the party. **Bonogo** glimpses **tendrils** under the skin, then gone; a closer look shows a **transparent bubble** with **roots** inside. Thrin calls it a **“witness see”** and **refuses to elaborate**.

**GM knob:** This is your **Branchborn** magic, pact, surveillance, or exile-brand — not pinned down in corpus.
