# Torbin Jove — party ward (Hempholm → Mirathorn)

**Torbin Jove** is the **party-recruited** boy from **Hempholm** (Campaign 1): bright, impulsive, candy-obsessed, hero-struck. At the **Longmont** table he became the group’s **ward** with a negotiated contract (education, room and board, visits to his father). He was caught up in **Shepherd’s Flock** meat corruption, **Stormspire Academy** care, and (in Campaign 2) recovery alongside **Ethel** and ongoing **cult / dream** pressure. For **AC, HP, CR, attacks**, read the mechanical sheet in the Mirathorn hub — this file is **voice + continuity**, not a statblock.

## Identity and disambiguators

- **Full name:** Torbin Jove; father **Mark Jove** (Hempholm).
- **Role at table:** Child NPC / ally — not a combat centerpiece (CR **1/8** on the ingested kid sheet).
- **Where he lives in play (C2):** **Stormspire Academy** — medical wing recovery, then **schooling**; shares a floor with **Ethel** (Hempholm survivor) per session recaps.

## Voice and behavior

- **Tone:** Fast, eager, mischievous; fearless curiosity; blurts wants (candy, toys, “defensive magic spell book”).
- **Want:** To be a **hero** like the adventurers; immediate gratification (shiny things, sweets).
- **Complication:** Impulsive spending, needs **supervision** in danger, low **street smarts**; without food/rest/encouragement he flags — see `torbin_jove_care_guidelines.md` for roll prompts.
