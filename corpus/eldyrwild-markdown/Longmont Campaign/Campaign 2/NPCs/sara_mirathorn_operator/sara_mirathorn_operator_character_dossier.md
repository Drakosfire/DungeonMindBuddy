# Sara — Mirathorn switchboard (Longmont Campaign 2)

**Sara** is one of **two operators** who run Mirathorn’s **rockie-talkie** network from the city — the familiar voice **Caelynn** most often gets when opening a handset line. Her **fraternal twin** is **Frank** (same job, same switchboard family; **not** identical twins).

## Disambiguators

- **Not** a party member — **NPC** logistics / **comms** anchor.
- **Frank** is a **separate person** on the board; recap POV sometimes names **Sara**, sometimes **Frank**, depending who patches the call.
- Handset names in recaps vary: **rockie-talkie**, **rocky-talkie**, **“rocky-talky”** — one prop.

## Continuity spine (from recaps & prep)

- **Session 12:** Party reaches the operators through the guard; **Sara** and **Frank** named explicitly as the city’s **twin operators**; line used to pull **Lysandra** toward the **Globe** crisis (**Dustwalker** decoy). *Recap wording has Caelynn running to the guard, who connects to Sara and Frank.*
- **Session 18:** **Ephanna** opens the handset; **Frank** is on the line — she **overhears** muffled urgency between **Frank** and **Lysandra** (**meat**, **boundary**, **breached**, **evacuate**, **map not random**).
- **Session 20:** **Caelynn** gets **Sara**; Sara patches **Lysandra**, then absorbs **good news / bad news** (Lysandra found; **tainted jerky** implicating Mirathorn logistics). **Sara** is **very concerned about who she can trust** in the city; transfers Caelynn toward **Professor Tealeaf** (no pickup).
- **Session 21 prep:** **Sara** still the emotional temperature on the wire — **shaken**, trust eroding; **Tealeaf** handoff still hanging.

## Voice / portrayal (suggested)

- **Sara:** quick, tired courtesy; **relief** when friends check in; **fear** when the story turns inward (supply chain, insiders). She **does not** monologue — she **patches**, **confirms**, **holds the line**.
- **Frank:** same professional register; Session 18 beat sells **proximity to Lysandra’s crisis** — clipped, stressed, possibly **too close to the receiver** for comfort.
