## Single root cause — meat

Everything connects to **Shepherd’s Flock supply and conversion**:

- **Party trail rations** — tainted jerky sneaked into company stores (destroyed at table; audit trail points **inward** to Mirathorn logistics).
- **Mirathorn city** — same pipeline: quartermasters, contractors, guard-adjacent signatures (Sara’s **who can I trust**).
- **North swamp** — what was once a **community** is now a **fleshy pit**: meat **aggregates** there; frontier villages (Campaign 1 backstory) were **harvested** to feed construction and consumption.
- **S18 overhear** — **meat**, **boundary**, **breached**, **map not random** — guard net language for **pit ↔ city** coordination, not random panic.

**Player horror beat (north):** *Where did all this meat come from?* — answer ties to **enslaved frontier communities** and **Mirathorn crates**, not a local monster-of-the-week.

## Second front — subtle vs Globe

**First attempt (Sessions 12–13, loud):** Dustwalker at the Globe, **Word of the Shepherd**, curfew, **postponed** festival — **too visible**, failed to hold the city.

**Second attempt (now):** **Shepherd’s Flock** through **fresh guard corruption** — **subtle**, institutional:

- **No barn sermons on the wire.** Choral oath revision (ledger §8), **wrong meat** in legitimate channels, **static “Mael—”** near inner wall (§37).
- **Guard tells operators** the festival is **back** — morale script for **field liaisons** and **northbound heroes**.
- **Goal:** keep the company **committed to the swamp** while the city prepares **summoning** assuming the **pit front collapses** into their hands.

**Frank / Sara:** Not cartoon villains. They **relay what guard command authorizes**. Frank’s S21 register is **real table canon** — GM plays it as **scripted hope**, not Frank inventing a party. Sara’s **fear** (S20) may be **closer to truth** than Frank’s **hangover apology**.

## North arc — swamp flesh pit

**Sequence (long-term):**

1. **Travel pressure** — storm, deserted hamlets, comms that **discourage turnaround** (`session_22` runbook).
2. **Arrive swamp region** — community gone; **hole in the ground** reads wrong at every scale.
3. **Lysandra’s map pays off** — dungeon crawl into **pit architecture** ( chambers, meat logic, mind-bend descent — see brainstorming dump).
4. **Big bad at pit** — local apex of the **north conversion** (not the hierarchy summit).
5. **Pyrrhic timing** — party may win the pit and still learn **Mirathorn was lost in parallel** if they took too long (intended guilt: *if we had come sooner…*).

**Breadcrumbs en route:** rushed abandonment, slave-route hints, Mirathorn-stamped crates northbound, refugees who name **missing villages** (Campaign 1 village-destruction seed).

## South arc — city summoning (parallel)

While the company is north:

- **Cult leadership** (top of Shepherd hierarchy — **Maelthor** layer per ledger §1) treats the **swamp pit** as the **primary front**.
- **Mirathorn** hosts a **fresh summoning** — quieter than the Globe, **guard-corridor** access, festival **cover story** without festival **reality**.
- **Assumption:** pit falls → north meat **flows back** → city ritual **completes**.

**If party turns back early:** They interrupt **summoning prep** but face **entrenched** subtle corruption — not a repeat of Session 12 set-piece.

**If party finishes pit first:** Return to a city **transformed** or **mid-ritual** — endgame choice, not a wire-resolve.
