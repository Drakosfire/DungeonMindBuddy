CAPTAIN LYSANDRA IRONVEIL IS A FORMIDABLE AND IMPOSING FIGURE, STANDING AT SIX FEET TALL WITH A MUSCULAR BUILD HONED FROM YEARS OF RIGOROUS TRAINING AND COMBAT. HER SKIN IS A RICH TAN, MARKED BY A FEW BATTLE SCARS THAT SPEAK TO HER EXPERIENCE AND RESILIENCE. LYSANDRA'S PIERCING BLUE EYES ARE SHARP AND OBSERVANT, CONSTANTLY SCANNING HER SURROUNDINGS FOR ANY SIGN OF TROUBLE. HER HAIR IS A CASCADE OF DARK, WAVY LOCKS THAT SHE OFTEN TIES BACK IN A PRACTICAL BRAID DURING DUTY HOURS. SHE WEARS THE TRADITIONAL ARMOR OF THE MIRATHORN GUARD, BUT WITH ADDITIONAL ORNATE ENGRAVINGS AND A CLOAK THAT SIGNIFIES HER RANK AS CAPTAIN. THE ARMOR, POLISHED TO A GLEAMING FINISH, BEARS THE EMBLEM OF THE CITY—A MOUNTAIN PEAK ABOVE A TRANQUIL LAKE.


Armor Class : 13

## ACTIONS


Multiattack : 'Captain Lysandra makes two longsword attacks.


Longsword : 'Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.


Leadership Presence : 'As a bonus action, Lysandra can grant one ally within 30 feet an additional 1d6 to their next attack roll or saving throw.


Tactical Strike : 'When Lysandra hits a creature with a weapon attack, she can add an additional 1d6 damage once per turn.


Steadfast Defense : 'Lysandra has advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.
