### Basic Needs and Care Mechanics for Torbin

To keep both you and the players alert to Torbin's presence and needs, here are some basic mechanics and guidelines:

#### Daily Care Checklist

## Food and Water:

Ensure Torbin eats at least two full meals per day and drinks enough water.

Occasionally, he might complain of being hungry, especially after exertion.

## Safety:

Torbin needs to be watched in dangerous areas. If left unattended in a perilous situation, there's a chance he might get into trouble.

Roll a d20 for potential mishaps if he is left unsupervised: 1-5 minor trouble, 6-15 nothing significant, 16-20 he finds something interesting (good or bad).

## Rest:

Torbin needs a safe place to sleep each night. If he doesn't get proper rest, he may become cranky or less effective the next day.

Effects of lack of sleep: disadvantage on skill checks, lower morale.

## Emotional Support:

Torbin thrives on positive reinforcement from the players. Acknowledge his contributions to keep his spirits high.

Roll a d20 for morale each day: 1-5 feeling down, 6-15 normal mood, 16-20 particularly enthusiastic and helpful.

#### Spending Habits

## Impulsive Purchases:

Whenever Torbin receives money, there's a chance he'll spend it impulsively. Roll a d20: 1-10 he spends it on something frivolous, 11-20 he asks the players for advice on what to buy.

Players can influence his spending with guidance or by keeping his money safe.

#### Adventure Skills

## Climbing and Exploring:

Torbin can climb trees, buildings, and other structures to scout or retrieve items.

Roll a d20 for climbing: 1-5 he slips and needs help, 6-15 he climbs successfully, 16-20 he finds something interesting.

## Street Smarts:

Torbin has low ‘Street Smarts’ for navigating urban environments, finding shortcuts, and avoiding trouble.

Roll a d20 for street smarts: 1-11 he gets lost, 12-18 he navigates successfully, 19-20 he finds useful information or items.

## Foraging:

Torbin can forage for food and supplies when in a pinch.

Roll a d20 for foraging: 1-5 finds nothing, 6-15 finds basic supplies, 16-20 finds something valuable or useful.
