### Item: The Fickle Ring of Fey Echoes

“Magic in the Feywild is rarely as it seems, and never as it is twice.”

**Type**: Ring (Rare, requires attunement by a spellcaster)

### Appearance

The Fickle Ring of Fey Echoes appears as a twisting silver band with faint, iridescent flecks in its metal, as if holding bits of stardust or dew from enchanted forests. When worn, the ring shimmers subtly with changing colors, reflecting the emotions or intent of the spell stored within it. Occasionally, when it catches the light just right, tiny images—like the outline of a laughing fae or a spiraling forest path—appear on its surface, only to vanish just as quickly.

### Abilities

**Echoed Spell Storage**:

The Fickle Ring of Fey Echoes can hold up to three levels of spells (similar to a Ring of Spell Storing) cast into it by any spellcaster. Unlike a typical Ring of Spell Storing, however, the ring “remembers” these spells in an unpredictable, fey-like way.

When casting a stored spell from the ring, the spell’s effects might take on a fey twist: illusions of leaves and sparkles accompany the magic, or minor, harmless side effects occur, such as the sound of laughter, a floral scent, or colors briefly shifting.

**Fey Mirror Casting** (1/day):

Once per day, when Ephanna casts a spell from the ring, they can use Fey Mirror Casting, allowing the ring to echo the spell as if it were Twin Spelled (replicating the spell’s effect on a second target or area, if it makes sense for the spell). This allows Ephanna to target two creatures, or affect two areas, with the stored spell’s magic. However, there’s a catch: the second “mirror” spell is slightly unpredictable, potentially dealing slightly altered effects (such as adding an extra illusion or a minor fey effect on the target, like disadvantage on Insight checks until their next turn).

**Whim of the Feywild** (Optional Side Effect):

Each time Ephanna casts a stored spell from the ring, there is a 10% chance the ring will trigger a harmless, fey-inspired magical effect within a 30-foot radius. This effect could manifest as:

A sudden burst of strange, echoing laughter.

Illusions of dancing lights or flower petals for a few seconds.

A harmless, temporary polymorph on a small creature nearby (turning it into a butterfly or a tiny frog for one minute).

While harmless, these effects add a layer of unpredictability that can surprise foes—or entertain allies.

**Unpredictable Recharge**:

Unlike the typical Ring of Spell Storing, the Fickle Ring of Fey Echoes regains one spell level of storage each dawn, as it absorbs ambient magic from the Feywild. This recharge is random: it may restore only one level at a time, favoring level 1 spells over higher levels. However, if Ephanna uses the ring frequently in locations with strong fey magic or during certain celestial events (such as a full moon), it has a chance of fully recharging by the next dawn.

**Attunement Reward** (Unlockable):

As Ephanna continues to use and attune to the ring, the fey energy within may respond to their magical prowess. After extended use or a significant Feywild interaction, the ring might grant Ephanna a one-time use of a spell such as Misty Step or Detect Magic, drawing on its connection to the fae. This “attunement reward” resets each time Ephanna spends more than a day in the Feywild or performs a significant task for a fey patron.

### Roleplaying and Story Hooks

**Fey Communication**: The ring might occasionally “whisper” or even giggle, as though reacting to Ephanna’s actions. These echoes could sometimes hint at nearby dangers, opportunities, or the presence of fey creatures.

**Legacy of the Archfey**: If Ephanna delves deeper into the Feywild or encounters an Archfey, they may learn the ring was originally a gift from Oberon’s court to a favored emissary. This could open opportunities for Ephanna to forge their own legacy within the fae realms or unlock new powers by proving themselves to the fey.

**Unexpected Side Effects**: Fey magic can be capricious, and the ring’s Whim of the Feywild effect could bring laughter or confusion to tense moments. The ring’s sudden bursts of fey-inspired magic might be enough to distract foes or draw attention—something Ephanna may need to navigate carefully.
