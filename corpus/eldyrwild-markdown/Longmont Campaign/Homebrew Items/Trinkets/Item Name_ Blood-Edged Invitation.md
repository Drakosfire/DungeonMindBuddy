### Item Name: Blood-Edged Invitation

**Visual Description:
** A crisp, cream-colored invitation card with **embossed gold filigree**, now stained faintly with something **brown-red** at the corner. The handwriting is flawless, aristocratic. The party details have long since **faded**, except for a small symbol in the corner: a **dagger resting atop a wine glass**. Every so often, the ink seems to shift—**names appear and vanish** in the lower margin. It smells faintly of **lavender and rust**.

### Stats:

## Wondrous Item, Rare (Cursed, Plot-Tied)

**Passive Effect:** When carried, the invitation acts as a **magical calling card**—the murderer who gave it can **sense its bearer**, slowly narrowing their location.

**Curse Effect:** Once per week, the item causes a **sleepless night filled with dream-visions of the murder**—from both victim and killer's perspective.

**Obscure Marking:** A master spy or codebreaker may recognize the symbol as belonging to the secretive guild known as **The Pale Fork**, an aristocratic cult of murder-artists.

### Plot Hooks:

**The Killer is Hunting Bonogo**, but slowly—more curious than vengeful, almost like a cat playing with its food.

The invitation might **unlock hidden doors** at a noble estate—assuming Bonogo dares return.

Destroying it won’t help—the killer already remembers the scent. But delivering it to someone else could **transfer the killer’s gaze**.

There's a chance that a certain elite noble attending the Festival **recognizes the invitation’s style**… and its implications.

### Quote:

"The ink’s not magical. It’s just expensive. The curse is in the paper."
 —Thaddelus Quirk, Underground Stationer
