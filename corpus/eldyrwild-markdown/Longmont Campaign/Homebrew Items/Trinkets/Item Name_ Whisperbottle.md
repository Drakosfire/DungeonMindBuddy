### Item Name: Whisperbottle

**Visual Description:
** A small glass bottle sealed with a black wax stopper, no bigger than a palm. Inside floats a motionless, translucent **sprite-like creature**, limbs curled inwards, wings iridescent and folded tight. The sprite appears dead—until occasionally, when no one is looking directly, it seems to **shift positions slightly**. The air inside swirls faintly with glittering dust.

### Stats:

## Wondrous Item, Rare (Sentient, Dormant)

## Weightless

The "dead" sprite inside is **not dead**, but in **Fae stasis**, bound by an ancient pact.

Once per long rest, **holding the bottle and whispering to it** allows the user to:

**Gain insight** (ask one yes/no question and get a faint emotional response: warmth for yes, cold for no).

Or **cast Detect Magic** as a ritual through the sprite’s sight.

The sprite's **true name** is unknown. Calling it by name may awaken it.

### Personality (Dormant):

The sprite is **dormant**, bound by magic that is **part Fae, part arcane imprisonment**.

In dreams, Caelynn may hear faint laughter, feel watched, or experience **shared memories** of forests, dew, and forgotten songs.

It is **not hostile**, but it is **ancient** and has opinions.

### Possible Hooks:

The sprite may be a **scout**, a **messenger**, or even a **former Fae noble**—punished, hidden, or preserved.

**Awakening the sprite** could:

Grant Caelynn a familiar-like companion with its own goals.

Break an ancient taboo and draw Fae attention.

Reveal long-lost knowledge of the White Vault or the Shepherd's rise.

Stormspire Academy might want to **buy** or **lock it away**.

A **name whispered by a Dryad** in a grove may stir the bottle violently.

### Quote:

"She’s not dead. She’s listening. And she remembers."
 —Elara Greenleaf, after touching the glass.
