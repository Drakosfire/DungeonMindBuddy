### Stafl’s Mysterious Scale – The Scale of the Hollow Wyrm

(An enormous scale, perhaps from a dragon. Real, valuable, incredibly hard.)

### When Stafl Casts Identify…

The moment Stafl places his hands upon the **enormous scale**, magic ripples across its surface like **water over polished stone**. For the first time, the scale seems alive, thrumming faintly with energy. The spell reveals **the truth**, unraveling its origins like whispers from the past.

#### What Stafl Learns:

This is **no ordinary dragon scale**, nor does it belong to any dragon of legend.

It is a fragment of an ancient and forgotten being, something **not quite of this world**.

It is **real, valuable, and incredibly hard**, but it **does not** belong to any known species of dragon.

The scale is infused with **deep, latent energy**, but it is not magical in the way enchanted items are—it is **something older**.

The scale carries **memories**—when held, it gives **a flicker of something vast and unseen**, a sense of hunger, longing, and waiting.

### The Story Behind the Scale

This scale **belonged to a Hollow Wyrm**, a being of **unknown origin** that **should not exist** within the preserved reality of Lundeyll.

Unlike true dragons, **Hollow Wyrms do not grow, hoard treasure, or die of age**. They are displaced things, lost between **planes**, existing only in **fragments**.

The last known sighting of such a creature was **centuries ago**, when explorers claimed to have seen a **shadow in the sky**, with **no body, only a flickering outline**.

Some **scholars** believe the Hollow Wyrms are **remnants of an unfinished world**, creatures from **the edges of existence**, where the Aspitone’s reality was never fully formed.

### What Makes It Special?

#### (Without increasing power, just deepening the mystery and lore)

The scale is **incredibly durable**, stronger than even tempered steel, yet it **does not resonate as draconic**.

When held, **Stafl sometimes hears distant, low vibrations**, like **an echo of something enormous breathing in a long-forgotten place**.

It is **valuable**, though **no scholar or merchant can agree on its worth**—some say it is priceless, others say it is **worthless, because it does not belong**.

If struck or exposed to **dimensional magic (like teleportation, plane shifting, or pocket dimensions)**, the scale **reacts faintly**, darkening as if absorbing something unseen.

### Possible Future Hooks

**There are no others like it**—unless, perhaps, more exist **beyond the edges of the world**.

The **Stormspire Academy** may be very interested in studying it. Some wizards might even **fear it**.

If exposed to **certain ancient ruins**, the scale may **resonate**, reacting as if it remembers something.

A **strange merchant or old sage** may recognize it **not as treasure, but as a warning.**

### Final Summary

The **Scale of the Hollow Wyrm** is not just a **physical object**—it is a **piece of something unfinished, something lost between worlds**. It is **real**, it is **valuable**, and it is **incredibly hard**—but it is also a **reminder that not all things belong in this reality.**
