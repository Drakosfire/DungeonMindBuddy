### Item Name: Idol of the Sleepless Gaze

**Visual Description:
** A six-inch idol carved from an oily, gray-black stone that always feels slightly damp. It resembles a **twisting, abstract form**—something between a coiled beast and a weeping figure. Where its face should be, there is only a **smooth hollow**. At night, it sometimes feels **warm**, and faint scratching noises echo from inside the hollow when the room is silent.

### Stats:

## Wondrous Item, Rare (Cursed, Scrying Anchor)

While within 30 feet of a sleeping creature, causes **unsettling dreams and exhaustion checks** (DC 10 Con save each night).

Once identified, the idol becomes **active**:

**Every long rest**, the bearer must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or become the target of **Scrying** from an unknown entity.

On a failed save, the entity learns their location, emotional state, and immediate surroundings.

**Divination magic** around the idol detects it as faint until identified, then flares like a beacon.

**Destroying it** requires a **Remove Curse** and a strike from a **magic weapon within a Fae-glade or void-aligned location**.

### Plot Hooks:

The dreams become **increasingly vivid and shared**, potentially linking the party in hallucinated visions or false memories.

The entity watching is **not Abyssal or Fey**—it is from a **third, hidden influence** that predates both.

Karsemine may be the **only one who resists its full grasp**, due to her hunter’s will and connection to the land.

Wizards or scholars who see the idol **refuse to touch it**. One may whisper, “I saw that thing on the gate of the White Vault…”

### Quote:

"It doesn't want to kill you. It wants to remember what killing felt like."
 —Professor Lothwin, before sealing himself in silence
