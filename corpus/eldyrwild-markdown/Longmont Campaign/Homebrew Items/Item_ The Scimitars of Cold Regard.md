### Item: The Scimitars of Cold Regard

“Even steel can be cold-blooded when it’s on the hunt.”

**Type**: Two scimitars (or one special scimitar and one standard); Rare, requires attunement

### Appearance

The Scimitars of Cold Regard have sleek, curved blades made from a peculiar silver-blue metal that shimmers in low light. The hilts are wrapped in dark leather, with a faint, frost-like filigree etched into the cross-guards. The blades emit a soft, cool glow when held near humans, glowing brighter the closer they get—never enough to give away Karsemine’s position in the dark, but enough to let her feel their approach. When the blades cut flesh, they leave a faint blue aura on the wound, chilling the target briefly.

### Abilities

**Human Detection (Passive)**:

When humans are within 60 feet, the scimitars emit a faint, cold glow that only Karsemine can see. The glow intensifies if the humans are within 10 feet, giving her an almost instinctual awareness of their proximity, like a predator sensing prey. This effect does not work through walls or barriers thicker than a few inches.

**Cold-Blooded Strike** (1d6 Cold Damage, Once per Round):

When striking a human or a humanoid with similar physiology (such as a half-elf), each scimitar deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on the first hit in a round. The blade leaves a frostbitten mark, and the chill can slightly hinder the target’s reaction speed, giving Karsemine advantage on her next Dexterity (Stealth) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against them.

This additional cold damage is limited to once per round per scimitar (maximum 2d6 if she lands hits with both).

**Frostshield of Resentment** (Twice per Long Rest):

As a bonus action, Karsemine can channel the scimitars’ cold power to create a shimmering frost armor around herself, granting her a +1 bonus to AC and resistance to fire damage for 1 minute. The icy energy hovers over her skin, crackling and hissing if touched, giving her an almost intimidating aura. However, the frost armor dissipates if she is incapacitated or lets go of both scimitars.

**Chilling Retort** (Unlockable Ability):

When Karsemine reaches level 6 or performs a significant feat in the name of vengeance, the scimitars gain a reactive ability. If a human deals damage to her while she’s wielding both scimitars, she can use her reaction to make a single melee attack against them with one of the scimitars. This attack benefits from an extra 1d4 cold damage as a frosty energy lashes out, seeming to retaliate for her.

### Unique Flavor and Potential Backstory

The Scimitars of Cold Regard are rumored to have been forged by a half-elven smith with an old grudge against humans who betrayed her kin during a political upheaval. The blades are imbued with her resentment, and some say they still carry a sliver of her soul. While the scimitars aren’t fully sentient, they do seem to “wake up” around humans, humming slightly in Karsemine’s hands, as though whispering their own ancient grievances.

They might react slightly in response to Karsemine’s emotions—growing colder if she feels anger or disappointment, or dulling if her resolve wavers. These scimitars have a way of nudging her subtly when there are humans nearby, as if encouraging her to be ever-aware of old dangers.

### Roleplaying Hooks

**Connection to Ancestry**: If Karsemine explores the scimitars’ history, she may uncover an old half-elven clan who once wielded these blades in a conflict against human oppressors, revealing secrets or abilities connected to that heritage.

**Unforeseen Side Effects**: The scimitars might occasionally affect her interactions with humans, subtly chilling the air around her or giving her gaze a frosty intensity that unnerves others.

**Unlocking New Powers**: If she experiences betrayal or enacts revenge in a way that resonates with the scimitars, their powers might grow, particularly their ability to protect her against humans or even give her additional tracking abilities.
