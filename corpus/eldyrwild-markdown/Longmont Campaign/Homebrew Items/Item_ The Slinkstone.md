### Item: The Slinkstone

“In the shadows, vengeance finds its way.”

**Item Type**: Worn amulet, small talisman, or ring (Uncommon, requires attunement by a rogue)

### Appearance

The Slinkstone is a rugged, rough-edged black stone set in a tarnished silver clasp. When held, it’s unusually warm and seems to pulse faintly, as if resonating with Bonogo’s heartbeat. The stone itself looks nondescript, but anyone with knowledge of the arcane would detect faint traces of fey magic, an echo of the Shadowfell, or otherworldly energy. When attuned, it’s almost as if the shadows lean in slightly, becoming a shade darker around Bonogo.

### Lore

The Slinkstone is said to have been created in a time of strife, when outcasts and survivors from the fringes of society turned to the hidden powers of the Shadowfell. Rumor has it that Slinkstones were crafted to aid those who were hunted, allowing them to become the hunters instead. Passed down through black markets and thieves’ guilds, the stone’s origins have been lost, but its purpose remains: survival, escape, and retribution.

### Abilities

**Step Between Shadows** (Once per Short Rest):

As a bonus action, Bonogo can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see that’s in dim light or darkness. When he does, he leaves a fleeting, smoky image behind, which persists for a split second, often confusing those nearby.

This effect can be used for infiltration, evasion, or creating a diversion. If he uses it within 10 feet of an enemy, they must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 13) or be distracted, granting Bonogo advantage on his next attack against them.

**Veil of Shadows** (Passive):

While wearing the Slinkstone, Bonogo has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in dim light or darkness. This feature also allows him to “fade” momentarily, allowing for some protection while staying on the periphery.

Additionally, if Bonogo remains still for a full round in dim light or darkness, he gains partial cover (an illusory blur conceals him until he moves again). This effect is subtle, making him difficult to pinpoint in low-light environments.

**Retributive Bite** (Unlockable, requires attunement):

When Bonogo is struck by a critical hit, he can activate the Slinkstone’s latent power as a reaction. The stone releases a burst of shadowy energy, dealing 2d6 necrotic damage to the attacker and marking them with a faint shadow brand visible only to Bonogo, allowing him to track them even if they flee or hide.

This ability can be used once per long rest.

### Possible Development and Roleplaying Hooks

**Resonance with the Shadowfell**: The Slinkstone has a slight connection to shadow planes or energies, potentially drawing attention from those attuned to such forces. Bonogo might start noticing peculiar figures in his periphery, or his dreams could become oddly vivid, foreshadowing a deeper connection.

**Unlocking Powers**: The stone’s full potential might be unlocked by certain actions. For instance, it could evolve to amplify Bonogo’s abilities if he uses it to enact retribution for a past wrong—especially if he acts on his ideal of showing “the rich what life in the gutters is like.”

**Potential Drawbacks**: If Bonogo uses the Slinkstone excessively or in morally ambiguous situations, it may start to subtly influence his mind, instilling thoughts of paranoia or vengeance even when unwarranted, mirroring its shadowy nature.
