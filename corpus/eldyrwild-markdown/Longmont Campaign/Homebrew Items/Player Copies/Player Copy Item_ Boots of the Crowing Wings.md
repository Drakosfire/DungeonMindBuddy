### Item: Boots of the Crowing Wings

“They strut before they fly. Honestly, that's the warning.”

**Type**: Wondrous item, Boots (Uncommon, requires attunement)

### Appearance

A pair of soft, well-worn travelling boots in cracked tan leather. The seams and cuffs are stitched with **gold filigree shaped into small, silly chicken wings** — wings mid-flap, wings folded tight, one scratched in profile like a barnyard mark. Most of the gilt has flaked away, but a few stubborn glints remain. A tiny embroidered **comb** sits on the right tongue; three **claw-tally lines** on the left heel.

When activated, the surviving filigree **shimmers like sunlit feathers** and the boots emit a single, dignified-as-it-gets **chicken cluck** at takeoff.

### Abilities

**Crowing Flight** (Attunement):

While you wear these boots, you have a **flying speed equal to your walking speed**. You can fly for up to **4 hours**, all at once or split into shorter flights of at least **1 minute** each. If you're still flying when the time runs out, you descend at **30 feet per round** until you land.

The boots **regain 2 hours of flight for every 12 hours** they aren't worn (or every long rest in which Bonogo isn't using them).

### Quirks (flavour)

- **The Cluck**: One clear, audible chicken cluck on every takeoff. Heard by anyone within about **30 feet** who's listening. No combat penalty — just the cluck.
- **The Strut**: One unbidden chicken-strut step before lift-off. Roleplay it.
- **Roost**: Left near a fire overnight, the boots tend to end up sitting **toe-up, side by side**, like they're roosting. No one has seen them move on their own.

### Roleplaying and Utility

- **Looted from the drake nest** as part of Bonogo's lucky double haul. Beat-up gold filigree finally tipped someone off they were more than dirty boots.
- **Use them when**: you need to scout a slope, drop in on a hostile rooftop, dodge a swamp, or just **be the first eye** somewhere risky.
- **They're silly, and they work.** The cluck is a feature.
