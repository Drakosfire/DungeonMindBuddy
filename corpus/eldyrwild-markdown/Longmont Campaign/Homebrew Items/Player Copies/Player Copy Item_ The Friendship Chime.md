### Item: The Friendship Chime

“When you need a friend, sometimes all it takes is a little jingle.”

**Item Type**: Necklace, Charm Bracelet, or Anklet (Uncommon, requires attunement)

### Appearance

The Friendship Chime is a playful piece of jewelry decorated with small, dangling charms shaped like smiling moons, stars, and quirky faces. Each charm jingles softly when Caelynn moves, producing a soothing, cheerful sound that seems to lighten the mood. Occasionally, one of the charms will wink or makFriene a silly face, especially when Caelynn is actively trying to make friends or entertain.

### Abilities

**Friendly Aura** (Passive):

While wearing the Friendship Chime, Caelynn emits a mild aura of friendliness within a 10-foot radius. Creatures within this range find themselves oddly charmed by her, giving her advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks to establish friendships or defuse tense situations. Once per short rest, this aura also slightly calms hostile creatures, making them pause briefly or even reconsider their actions if they’re neutral.

**Summon a Backup Buddy** (Once per Long Rest):

As an action, Caelynn can jingle the chime to call forth a magical “Buddy Spirit” in the form of a small, friendly animal or a cute, squishy critter. This Buddy Spirit is harmless and can’t attack, but it distracts enemies, provides moral support, or simply cheers on Caelynn and her allies with small dances or friendly sounds.

The Buddy Spirit can use the Help action each turn to give an ally within 5 feet advantage on their next roll. It lasts up to one minute or until dismissed, and it has a 50% chance to puff out of existence in a sparkle of confetti and laughter if anyone tries to attack it.
