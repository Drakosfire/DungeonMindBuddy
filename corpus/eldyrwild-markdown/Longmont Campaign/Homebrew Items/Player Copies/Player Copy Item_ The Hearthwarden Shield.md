### Item: The Hearthwarden Shield

“Forge-warmed and unyielding as mountain stone.”

**Type**: Shield (Rare, requires attunement by a dwarf)

### Appearance

The Hearthwarden Shield is a solid, round shield crafted from an unusual metal alloy that seems to absorb light without dulling. Dwarven runes of protection are etched in a circular pattern around the edge, and a faint glow warms the shield’s center, like embers in a hearth. The center of the shield is adorned with a small, sturdy hammer emblem, symbolizing dwarven craftsmanship and resilience. When used in battle, the runes glow faintly in response to nearby magic, and the shield emits a low, steady hum.

### Abilities

**Ward of the Hearth** (Passive):

This shield provides Baergrom with +2 AC as usual, but additionally, it grants him advantage on saving throws against being frightened. Its warm, reassuring glow bolsters his courage, and the runes hum slightly whenever he faces supernatural fear.

**Ember Aura** (Once per Short Rest):

When Baergrom plants the shield firmly in the ground as an action, it creates a 10-foot radius of protective warmth around him for up to 1 minute. Allies within this area gain temporary hit points equal to Baergrom’s Constitution modifier at the start of each of their turns (this only works once per ally per turn).

This aura also creates dim light, dispelling natural darkness in the radius and providing allies a sense of security and focus. It’s ideal for keeping spirits high in dark, cold, or unsettling places.

### Roleplaying and Utility

**Symbol of Dwarven Honor**: The shield holds a ceremonial significance, symbolizing Baergrom’s role as a protector. If he’s around dwarves or other mountain folk, it might even be recognized as a symbol of respect, as shields like this were crafted only for the protectors of hearth and kin. Elders or dwarven leaders might treat him with extra regard if they see he wields such a shield.

**Inspiration in Dark Places**: Whether deep in caves or in hostile territories, Baergrom can use the shield’s warm glow and Ember Aura to comfort allies or even noncombatants he’s sworn to protect. This shield serves as a literal and figurative light in the dark, providing moments of peace even in dangerous areas.

**Heartspark Bond**: The shield’s fire-based abilities give Baergrom a connection to both the literal and symbolic idea of a hearth—the place of warmth, family, and protection. As he grows in renown, the shield might reveal other small abilities related to resilience or even light healing, especially if he continues to act in service of those he protects.
