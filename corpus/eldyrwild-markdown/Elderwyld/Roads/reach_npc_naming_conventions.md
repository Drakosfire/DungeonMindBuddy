# Mireward Reach — NPC naming conventions

## How to roll

- Roll **d100** on `reach_npc_first_names_d100.md` for the first name.
- Roll **d100** on `reach_npc_last_names_d100.md` for the surname.
- Read the row's **Region**, **Scaffold**, and **Notes** as a promotion seed, not a full biography.
- Optional region bias: if the party is within **two days** of a named anchor, reroll once if the row region mismatches the place. Use GM discretion when a mismatch is more interesting.
- Good mismatches explain movement: guard transfer, marriage, refugee flight, academy posting, fosterage, trade apprenticeship, or a road-name chosen after leaving home.
- Same first + last pair twice on one journey means the second NPC gets a distinguisher: nickname, limp, uniform patch, smell, "the younger," or visible trade tool.
- Names become table canon for a specific NPC only after play promotes them through the recap and, if recurring, an NPC hub.

## South→north map

| Region band | Rows | Phonology | Table use |
|-------------|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Mirathorn** | 01–25 | Cosmopolitan trade-city names; soft/classical given names; compound surnames built from virtue, trade, lake, guild, or academy imagery. | Clerks, guild factors, academy adjacency, city guard transfers, festival labor, operator-adjacent messengers. |
| **Mossford** | 26–50 | Shorter, practical river names; moss, reed, rush, creek, sluice, brewery, and moldyard surnames; less elven polish. | Dam crews, moldyards, brewery hands, ferrymen, field hands, watch auxiliaries, inn seconds. |
| **Mireward** | 51–75 | Charter-plain and garrison-short; clipped given names, one-syllable surnames, stink-trade compounds, tithe/ferry words. | Gate sergeants, retired spears, tithe clerks, ferry guards, tanners, patrol widows, apron inn folk. |
| **Edge-Fen** | 76–100 | Older and wary; reed, fen, verge, bell, hush, low-water, and last-ground morphemes; renamed refugees appear here. | Reed-cutters, hymn-hearers, hamlet evacuees, causeway tolls, night singers, crown riders from north. |
