# d100 Last Names — Mireward Reach

## How to use

- Roll **d100** here for a surname after rolling **T-NAME-F** in `reach_npc_first_names_d100.md`.
- The surname's **Region** can match or clash with the first name. A clash is useful: transfer, marriage, fosterage, adopted road-name, or refugee drift.
- If the party is within **two days** of a named anchor, you may reroll once if the region feels wrong.
- Use the **Scaffold** as a fast family trade, origin story, or social slot.
- Use **Notes** to decode compounds and decide whether the name sounds city-polished, river-practical, charter-harsh, or fen-liminal.
- Repeat rule: if the same first + last pair appears twice on one journey, add a distinguisher — nickname, limp, uniform patch, smell, "the younger," or a visible trade tool.
- Edge-Fen surnames may echo bells, reeds, silence, or thresholds, but never use cult titles or proper names.
- These are world reference names until a rolled stranger gets promoted into a real NPC hub.
