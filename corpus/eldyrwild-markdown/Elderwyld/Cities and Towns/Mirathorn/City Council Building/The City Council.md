**Mayor Elara Swiftwind** – **Charismatic Leader of the City Council**

**Race:** Gnome

**Role:** Mayor and Chair of the City Council

**Personality:** Elara Swiftwind is charismatic, clever, and has a knack for diplomacy. She has a sharp mind and is adept at navigating the city's complex political landscape. Her leadership is marked by her emphasis on unity and the prosperity of Mirathorn.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Deeply concerned about the cult's threat to the city's stability and well-being. Elara is determined to address the problem swiftly but is cautious about the potential fallout of causing public panic. She seeks to balance the need for action with the need for maintaining order during the Festival.

**Visual Description:** A petite and elegant gnome with bright, intelligent eyes that sparkle with curiosity. Her hair is a cascade of silver curls adorned with small, intricate jeweled pins. She wears finely tailored robes in shades of emerald and gold, embroidered with delicate patterns that reflect the city’s crest. Her demeanor is both approachable and commanding, and she carries a small gavel at her waist, a symbol of her role as mayor.

**Barin Coppergleam** – **Proprietor of the Copper and Quartz Inn**

**Race:** Dwarf

**Role:** Representative of Commerce

**Personality:** Barin is pragmatic and shrewd, understanding the importance of trade and business to the city. He is well-liked for his fairness but has a keen eye for profit.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Concerned about the economic impact of the cult’s actions and the potential disruption of the festival, which is crucial for the city’s economy.

**Visual Description:** A stout and broad-shouldered dwarf with a thick, graying beard and piercing blue eyes. His skin is weathered from years of hard work and managing the inn. He is well dressed in garish flaMBOYANT clothes of deep burgundy and forest green. His hands are calloused, and a small scar runs across his left eyebrow, hinting at a past of rougher times.

**Grobnok FuckFight** – **Owner of Grit and Grime**

**Race:** Goblin

**Role:** Representative of the Undercity and Cultural Sectors

**Personality:** Grobnok is lively and cunning, with a finger on the pulse of the city’s underbelly. He knows how to navigate the city's more illicit and hidden aspects.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Wary of the cult’s influence creeping into his territory. He sees this as a personal affront to the order he maintains in the city's nightlife and is eager to root them out.

**Visual Description:** A large, wiry goblin with green skin and sharp, pointed features. His eyes gleam with a mischievous glint, and he often wears a broad grin that reveals a set of slightly crooked numerous fangs. He dresses in flamboyant, patchwork clothing, adorned with jangling trinkets and baubles collected from his nightclub. His ears are pierced with multiple rings, and he carries a small wand tucked behind one ear, a symbol of his command over the nightlife. He always looks ready to dance, drink, fuck, fight, or maybe all four.

**Headmaster Tinkerbright** – **Head of the Wizard’s College**

**Race:** Gnome

**Role:** Representative of the Arcane

**Personality:** Headmaster Tinkerbright is a curious and inventive gnome, always tinkering with new magical contraptions and spells. He's known for his eccentric personality and brilliance in arcane studies. While he can be easily distracted by his experiments, he takes any threat to the city seriously.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Deeply troubled by the dark magic at play, Tinkerbright views the corrupted meat as a perversion of magic. He is eager to deploy the resources of the Wizard’s College to detect and counteract the cult’s influence.

**Visual Description:** A gnome with wild, white hair that sticks out in all directions, giving him a perpetually frazzled look. His eyes are magnified behind a pair of thick, round spectacles, making them appear almost comically large. He wears robes of deep violet, covered in burn marks and ink stains from countless experiments. Around his neck dangles a variety of small, arcane instruments and charms, and his fingers are stained with ink and chalk dust. When his eyes are focused, you can feel the ages and power, otherwise he comes across as hapless.

**Commander Thalia Ashenvale** – **Commander of the Guard**

**Race:** Human

**Role:** Representative of Law and Order

**Personality:** Thalia is disciplined and authoritative, with a strong sense of duty. She is fiercely protective of the city and its inhabitants. Unbeknownst to her, she has been ensorcelled by the Wolf to overlook the corruption within the guard, making her resistant to believing any accusations.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Defensive about the accusations against her guard, but will listen to solid evidence. Due to the magical manipulation, she may dismiss or be unaware of signs of internal corruption.

**Visual Description:** A tall, statuesque Human with sharp, chiseled features and a stern expression. Her silver hair dirty blonde hair is pulled back into a tight braid. She wears a polished breastplate bearing the crest of the city guard, over a dark blue tunic. Her piercing gray eyes survey her surroundings with a calm but commanding intensity, and her posture is always upright and alert.

**Torrin Flamescale** – **Master Smith and Representative of the Guilds**

**Race:** Dragonborn

**Role:** Representative of the Craft Guilds

**Personality:** Torrin is a stern and practical Dragonborn with a passion for his craft. He is respected for his craftsmanship and his ability to speak bluntly about issues affecting the city.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Concerned about the integrity of the city's defenses and the safety of its people. The idea of tainted meat being distributed in his city is an affront to the strength and honor he values.

**Visual Description:** A towering dragonborn with bronze scales that gleam in the light. His face is lined with the marks of age and experience, giving him a regal and intimidating presence. He is dressed in a sturdy, leather apron over a simple, blacksmith’s tunic, and his arms are muscular, bearing the signs of countless hours at the forge. His eyes are a piercing gold, filled with a mixture of wisdom and fiery determination.

**Merril Tealeaf** – **Leader of the Agricultural Union**

**Race:** Gnome

**Role:** Representative of Agriculture and Food Production

**Personality:** Merril is nurturing and practical, focusing on the well-being of the city's populace through food and agriculture. She is known for her green thumb and ability to cultivate rare and magical plants.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** The contamination of food is a direct threat to her domain. Merril is deeply troubled by the potential for widespread harm and is advocating for immediate measures to cleanse the food supplies.

**Visual Description:** A cheerful dark skinned gnome with a warm, round face and rosy cheeks. Her hair is a wild tangle of chestnut curls, often adorned with small sprigs of flowers or leaves. She wears a simple, earth-toned dress with a thick belt from which hang various pouches containing herbs and gardening tools. Her hands are small and nimble, stained with the rich brown of the soil, and she carries the scent of fresh herbs wherever she goes.

**Rurik Stonehammer** – **Chief Architect**

**Race:** Dwarf

**Role:** Representative of Infrastructure

**Personality:** Rurik is meticulous and detail-oriented, responsible for the city's structural integrity. He has a deep understanding of the city’s layout, including its hidden passages and sewers.

**Stance on Cult Issue:** Concerned about the cult using hidden passageways and tunnels for their schemes. He wants to assist in locating and sealing off any areas the cult might be using.

**Visual Description:** A stout, teak wood toned sturdy dwarf with a face that seems carved from stone, reflecting years of dedication to his craft. His hair is a dark auburn, streaked with gray, and his beard is meticulously braided and bound with silver clasps. He wears a set of heavy, earth-toned robes with intricate geometric patterns. His hands are large and calloused, with ink-stained fingers from the countless blueprints and plans he has drawn up for the city.
