### Session Outline: Battle with The Wolf and Aftermath

### 1. Battle with The Wolf in the Council Chamber

**Objective**: The players must confront **The Wolf** and corrupted guards while navigating an increasingly dangerous environment due to the **magical defenses** of the council chamber. The Wolf's goal is to **stall the players** and **escape** into the sewers, where he plans to complete a **kaiju-scale ritual**.

##### Initial Setup:

**Location**: The **Council Chamber**, a grand, circular stone room in the heart of the city, filled with ornate furniture and arcane symbols.

**Players' Position**: The players are likely split between reporting to the council and anticipating The Wolf's actions.

**Enemies**:

**The Wolf**: Corrupted second-in-command, uses illusion and stealth-based magic to confuse players.

**Corrupted Guards**: 8 regular guards (half corrupted), 2 elite guards (heavily corrupted), positioned along the walls.

**Thalia**: Innocent but ensorcelled Commander of the Guard (unaware of the corruption).

##### Magical Environmental Hazards:

As combat begins, the **council chamber's magical defenses** activate. These ancient wards, designed to protect the council from intruders, unintentionally affect the battle.

**Examples of Environmental Hazards**:

**Arcane Traps**: Bursts of arcane energy shoot from engraved runes on the floor, targeting **non-council members** and **non-guards**. These could deal **2d6 force damage** or cause **temporary paralysis** unless the players are wearing an **amulet of council** (only given to council members).

**DC 15 Dexterity Saving Throw** to avoid.

**Falling Debris**: As the battle rages, **chandeliers fall** or **shelves topple**, forcing players to dodge.

**DC 14 Dexterity Saving Throw** to avoid 1d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Magic Pulse Alarms**: A triggered arcane defense system begins emitting **sonic pulses** every few rounds, drawing **more guards** to the council chamber. The players may hear **footsteps echoing** as reinforcements are on their way.

**Illusory Walls**: Portions of the room flicker with **illusion spells**, disorienting the players as it becomes hard to distinguish real walls from false ones.

##### The Wolf’s Tactics:

The Wolf will take every opportunity to create confusion and **delay the players** while slowly working his way toward an **escape route**.

**Illusion Decoys**: The Wolf creates **illusory duplicates** of himself to throw off the players, forcing them to waste turns attacking illusions.

**Perception (DC 16)** to determine which Wolf is real.

**Charm and Enchantment**: The Wolf may cast **Charm Person** on Thalia or use **Hold Person** on key players to buy himself time.

Players can attempt to break the charm or **convince Thalia** of The Wolf's betrayal.

**Invisibility**: If his health drops below 50%, The Wolf casts **Invisibility** and makes his way toward the secret escape tunnel beneath the council chamber.

##### Reinforcements:

**Corrupted Guards**: As the fight continues, the **magical alarm system** will bring more **corrupted guards** from hidden passages. This creates escalating tension as more enemies arrive.

**Secret Doors**: Two hidden doors behind council banners will open to reveal **3-4 additional corrupted elite guards** and possibly a **cultist acolyte**. This puts extra pressure on the players to act quickly.

**Elite Guard Captain**: The Wolf calls in an elite guard captain, who charges in with **heavily armored guards**, providing more resistance and cover for The Wolf’s escape.

##### Hidden Council Room Defenses:

In addition to the traps, there are ancient **defensive wards** designed to protect the council, but these wards don’t distinguish between friend and foe.

**Amulets of Council**: Council members and trusted guards wear **amulets** that mark them as friendly to the chamber’s defenses. The players might not have these, causing traps to target them.

**Arcane Locks**: If the players attempt to **leave** the council chamber too early (before the Wolf escapes), the **doors lock magically**, requiring a **DC 17 Arcana** or **Thieves’ Tools** check to disable.

### 2. The Wolf’s Escape

Once The Wolf reaches a critical point (below 50% HP or with enough time stalled), he uses a **combination of Invisibility** and **Illusion** to disappear into the **secret passage** leading to the sewers.

**Illusory Distraction**: The Wolf creates a massive illusion, such as **an explosion or fire**, to cover his escape. The players will need to **dispel the illusion** (Arcana DC 16) or spend time working around it.

**Escape Tunnel**: The Wolf retreats through a **hidden trapdoor** in the council chamber that leads directly into the **city sewers**. The players can choose to pursue or stay to secure the council chamber.

### 3. Branching Paths After the Fight

After The Wolf escapes (or if the players are distracted by other matters), the session can split into **three potential branches**:

##### Branch 1: Chase the Wolf

**Narrative**: If the players follow The Wolf, they chase him through the **sewers** (detailed below), where they eventually confront him again during the **kaiju summoning ritual**.

**Challenges**:

The players will encounter **corrupted cultists** guarding the ritual and **sewer traps**.

The Wolf continues to stall, eventually disappearing into the ritual chamber.

##### Branch 2: Covert Ops Mission

**Narrative**: The players focus on the original plan to locate **corrupted meat** stored in **various guardhouses**.

**Mechanics**:

The players conduct **stealth checks** and **investigations** using magical runes provided by the wizards.

There’s a **timer** before the festival BBQ begins, increasing tension.

Failed searches can trigger **combat encounters** with **cultists**, uncover false leads, or lead the players into cult-controlled areas like the **sewers**.

##### Branch 3: Taking Care of Torbin

**Narrative**: The players return to help **Torbin**, who is suffering from **visions and mental manipulation** from The Shepherd. These visions may provide clues to the location of the **kaiju summoning ritual**.

**Objective**: Discover what is happening to Torbin and decide whether to leave him in the care of the Wizards’ College or keep him with the party.

##### Branch 2: Covert Ops Mission

**Narrative**: If the players decide not to chase The Wolf, they can move forward with the covert ops mission using **magical runes** to find the **corrupted meat**.

**Objective**: The party is tasked with searching **guardhouses** and **storehouses** for the corrupted meat hidden by The Shepherd’s Flock.

**Mechanics**:

Use the **Stealth, Investigation, and Timed Searches** mechanics.

The players may find clues about The Wolf’s escape (a hidden sewer entrance or a blood trail leading underground).

**Outcome**: Eventually, their investigation leads to the discovery of **an entrance to the sewers**, where The Wolf has fled.

##### Branch 3: Taking Care of Torbin

**Narrative**: If the players are concerned about **Torbin**, they can follow up with him. He is showing signs of corruption, hearing **nightmares** and being mentally manipulated by the Shepherd.

**Objective**: The players can either **protect Torbin** or **investigate his condition**. Arcana or Insight checks might reveal that his visions point to **underground movements** related to The Shepherd’s plan.

**Outcome**: The players can decide to leave Torbin in safe hands (e.g., the Wizards’ College) or take him along. Either way, they find **clues** that lead them to the sewers.

#### 3. Converging on the Sewers

No matter which path the players take, they will eventually discover that the **sewers** are central to the cult’s plan. They will find **hidden entrances** (either through the chase, covert ops, or Torbin’s visions) and be drawn underground.

### Sewer Intro and Encounter

#### Sewer Environment:

The sewers beneath Mirathorn are **ancient**, with a mix of **stonework and magical runes**. The air is thick with moisture and the faint stench of rot. Water runs in shallow channels, and the tunnel walls are slick with grime and moss.

**Narrative**: As the players descend into the sewers, they notice **strange glowing symbols** on the walls, faint chanting echoing from deeper below, and the overwhelming feeling that something **unnatural** is stirring.

#### First Sewer Encounter: Corrupted Patrol

**Description**: As the players progress, they come across a **patrol of 3-4 corrupted city guards**. They are guarding the entrance to the **ritual chamber** deeper in the sewers. The guards are visibly corrupted, with **oily, opalescent eyes** and distorted features.

**Objectives**: The players can choose to either **sneak past** the guards or engage in combat.

**Stealth Check (DC 14)**: The players can attempt to sneak by without detection. On failure, the guards will **ambush** them.

**Combat**: The guards use **standard guard stats** with **corrupted traits**, such as gaining temporary HP when near **corrupted meat** or using **dark magic** to delay the players.

**Clues from the Encounter**:

On the guards’ bodies, the players find **symbols of The Shepherd’s Flock** and possibly a **key** or **map fragment** leading to the next area of the sewers.

They also hear faint **chanting** and **drumming** from further below, hinting at the ritual.

#### Sewer Dungeon (Initial Layout):

**Section 1: Entry Hall** – The players enter the sewers and must navigate the slippery environment. The first challenge involves avoiding **hazardous terrain** (slippery floors, collapsing stones).

**Section 2: Patrol** – They come across the **corrupted guard patrol**. After dealing with them, they find a clue to the location of the **ritual site**.

**Section 3: Locked Grate** – The path forward is blocked by a **heavy iron grate** (requires a **DC 15 lockpick** or **DC 17 Strength** check to force open).

**Section 4: Hidden Meat Cache** – The players may find a **storage room** where some of the **corrupted meat** is stored. Destroying this delays the ritual but doesn’t stop it entirely.

### Next Steps: Into the Heart of the Sewer

The players must move deeper into the sewers, facing a series of **cultist traps**, magical wards, and corrupted minions as they work their way to the **ritual site**, where the cult is attempting to summon the **flesh kaiju**.

### Potential Surprises:

**Torbin’s Visions**: Torbin’s nightmares intensify, providing cryptic warnings or guidance to the players. His condition worsens as they near the ritual.

**The Kaiju Formation**: As the players move closer, the **flesh golem** begins to take form, pulling corrupted bodies into itself, creating a growing mass of writhing flesh.
