### Council Room Description:

Upon entering the council chamber, one is immediately struck by the **high, arched ceilings** that seem to stretch endlessly upwards. The architecture blends the ancient roots of Mirathorn with its gnomish engineering prowess—intricate stonework carved by dwarves, adorned with **gleaming metal inlays** that shimmer faintly with magical glyphs. The walls, thick with history, are lined with **tapestries** that depict the founding of Mirathorn, the exodus from the Lundayell Empire, and various pivotal moments in the city's rise to prominence.

A large, circular table dominates the center of the room, crafted from a single piece of **polished dark oak**, ringed with **copper rivets** and **quartz accents**. Around the table sit chairs carved from sturdy wood, each one subtly different, reflecting the personality of the council members they were built for—ranging from the simple, functional seat of Captain Thalia to the intricately detailed throne-like chair for Headmaster Tinkerbright.

Above the table, a magnificent **chandelier** hovers, not fixed to the ceiling, but **floating through subtle magical enchantments**. It illuminates the room with **soft, shifting lights**, occasionally dimming and brightening as the magic within the room fluctuates. The **light reflects off copper accents**, casting a warm glow around the space, and accentuating the slight hum of industrial gears embedded in the walls, a constant reminder of the city's technological pulse.

**Windows**, though high and narrow, allow slivers of moonlight to pierce the chamber, contrasting the warm interior with the cold night outside. **Heavy iron shutters** are locked in place—remnants of the time when the council chamber had to double as a fortress during the city's more tumultuous years. On the far side of the room, a small dais rises a few feet above the rest of the floor, where **Mayor Elara Swiftwind’s seat** sits, elevated to signify her role as the meeting's mediator and decision-maker.

The air in the room is thick with anticipation, carrying the scent of **aged parchment**, **polished stone**, and the faint aroma of a fireplace that burns low in the background, warding off the chill of the night. There are doors that lead off to **side chambers** for private deliberations, but the council room itself is open, designed for **transparency and collaboration**. The floor is **cold flagstone**, worn smooth by years of pacing, debating, and tension-filled meetings.

In each corner of the room stand **large statues** of Mirathorn’s founding heroes, gnomes, and dragonborn who first broke ground on the city. Their stony visages seem to watch over the proceedings, offering silent testimony to the weight of history that permeates every council meeting held in this room.

### Details to Consider for Play:

**Acoustics**: The room is designed so that voices carry clearly, allowing every word spoken to reach every corner. This means private conversations are difficult unless hushed or taken to side rooms.

**Lighting**: The soft lighting from the floating chandelier could flicker or dim at crucial moments, adding tension or mystery to the session.

**Historical Gravitas**: The room is rich in history, making every council member acutely aware of their responsibility. The tapestries and statues could be referenced for political or emotional weight.

**Gears in the Walls**: The slight hum from the walls could be a subtle reminder of the city’s technological heart or serve as a motif for impending action or conflict. The players might even notice an unusual change in the hum when something significant happens.
