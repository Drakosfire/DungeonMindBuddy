# The Loot Room: The Terrifying Meat Room

## General Description

This chamber is located off a branching path from the main sewer tunnels, marked by faintly glowing runes on the walls and ceiling. The air is humid and carries a metallic tang, mingling with the unmistakable scent of decay.

The room is circular, with a domed ceiling supported by corroded iron pillars. The floor is covered in moss and grime, but beneath the muck, ancient tiles of intricate geometric patterns can be seen.

A broken stone door lies ajar, partially consumed by the flesh-like corruption spreading through the sewers, indicating the cult has recently found and ransacked this space.

## Key Features

### Central Treasure Pedestal

At the center of the room is a raised stone pedestal surrounded by faintly glowing wards. The wards appear to be designed to protect the pedestal, but they have been partially corrupted, causing them to flicker and spark.

**Arcana Check (DC 14):** Reveals that the wards could still activate if tampered with recklessly, releasing an arcane explosion (4d6 force damage, DC 15 Dexterity Save to halve).

Loot: The pedestal holds a gilded chest containing rare magical items (see Loot section).

### Hidden Vault

A concealed panel on the far wall is revealed by the faint outline of a rune. The panel can be opened with a successful **Investigation Check (DC 15)** or by using **Detect Magic.**

Inside the hidden vault are several scrolls and potions, carefully preserved from the room’s original purpose.

Loot: Scrolls of Fireball, Greater Healing Potions, and a mysterious sealed letter addressed to the founding council.

### Flesh-Tainted Cache

Near the corrupted section of the room, a pile of discarded goods lies half-absorbed by the spreading flesh. Many items are ruined, but a few still gleam with promise.

**Medicine Check (DC 12):** Reveals that any item removed from the corruption should be cleaned immediately to avoid lingering contamination.

Loot: A Flesh-Bound Dagger (grants +1 to attack and damage but whispers unsettling thoughts to the wielder, requiring a DC 10 Wisdom Save after prolonged use).

## Encounters

### Flesh-Tainted Guardians

As the players interact with the loot, the corruption stirs, forming two Flesh Sentinels from the walls. These creatures are twisted amalgamations of bone and sinew, animated by the cult’s dark magic.

Abilities:

**Tentacle Lash:** 2d6 bludgeoning damage, with a chance to grapple.

**Regeneration:** Heals 5 HP at the start of its turn unless hit with fire or radiant damage.

**Flesh Meld:** Can temporarily merge into the fleshy walls, becoming untargetable for one round.

Tactics: The sentinels focus on protecting the loot and will prioritize attacking anyone tampering with the central pedestal.

## Loot

### Central Pedestal Chest

## Enchanted Cloak of Shadows:

Grants +1 to AC and Stealth checks. Once per day, the wearer can cast Invisibility for up to 10 minutes.

## Runic Blade:

+1 magical sword with the ability to cast Detect Magic once per day when drawn.

## Talisman of Resilience:

Grants the wearer resistance to poison damage.

### Hidden Vault

**Scroll of Fireball:** Deals 8d6 fire damage in a 20-foot radius (DC 15 Dexterity Save to halve).

**Scroll of Counterspell:** Nullifies a spell being cast within 60 feet (3rd level or lower automatically, higher levels require a spellcasting check).

**Greater Healing Potions (x2):** Restores 4d4 + 4 HP.

### Flesh-Tainted Cache

## Flesh-Bound Dagger:

Grants +1 to attack and damage. Prolonged use requires a Wisdom Save (DC 10) to avoid unsettling whispers that impose disadvantage on Insight checks for 1 hour.

## Twisted Shield:

Grants +2 to AC but requires a Constitution Save (DC 12) each long rest to avoid temporary exhaustion due to its corrupting influence.

## Decision Points

## Loot or Leave:

Players must decide how much time to spend gathering items, knowing the ritual continues while they explore.

## Risk of Corruption:

Items from the Flesh-Tainted Cache carry a risk of spreading corruption if not handled carefully.

## Hidden Vault Letter:

The letter hints at the founding council’s use of forbidden magic to stabilize Mirathorn’s initial planar bubble. Sharing or withholding this information could impact political dynamics with the city council.

This loot room provides both valuable rewards and high-stakes decision-making, encouraging players to balance risk and reward while delving deeper into the corrupted sewers.
