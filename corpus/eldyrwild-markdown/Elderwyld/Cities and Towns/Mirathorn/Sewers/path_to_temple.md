# Path Exploration to the Temple

## General Atmosphere

The air grows damp and heavy, with an acrid, fleshy stench that sticks to the back of the throat. The dim light flickers as arcane pulses run through the meat-encrusted walls.

The tunnels are increasingly claustrophobic. Organic growths push against the stone, creating uneven surfaces, pulsating with faintly glowing veins that seem to feed off the corruption.

Faint chanting fills the air, not from a single source but reverberating throughout the walls themselves. It is maddeningly repetitive, laced with otherworldly harmonics that unsettle even the strongest minds.

## Key Features

### Chanting Cultist Procession

A steady stream of robed cultists, heads bowed and chanting, walks toward the ritual chamber. They carry nothing, hands folded in a gesture of reverence.

Occasionally, a stray cultist is ensnared by fleshy tendrils that emerge from the walls. The tendrils wrap around the unfortunate individual, dragging them screaming into the pulsating structure. The others do not react, continuing their procession as if nothing happened.

**Stealth Check (DC 13):** Failure could result in cultists stopping to investigate or calling for reinforcements.

### Growing Organic Corruption

The walls of the tunnel begin to shift unnaturally, veins pulsing beneath the surface. Players might notice:

**Perception Check (DC 14):** The walls seem alive, breathing faintly.

**Arcana Check (DC 15):** The growths are tied to necrotic planar energy, drawing power from some anchor deeper within.

Tentacles become more frequent, randomly lashing out.

**Dexterity Save (DC 12):** Failure results in being restrained until freed or succeeding on an Athletics check (DC 13).

### Clues to the Temple

Players encounter glyphs and symbols carved into the walls, etched in a language of planar sigils and celestial script.

**Religion Check (DC 13):** Recognizes these as ancient symbols of the nameless, the Nameless Goddess.

**History Check (DC 15):** Learns that these glyphs predate Mirathorn, likely from the settlers' first airship. They mark the way to a central focus of planar energy.

The glyphs glow faintly when approached, leading to a side passage branching off from the main path.

### The Red Flag: The Cultist’s Reluctance

The cultists actively avoid a branching tunnel marked with glowing glyphs. Despite their trance-like chanting, they give this passage wide berth, their gazes flicking toward it with clear unease.

Observing the cultists’ behavior:

**Insight Check (DC 12):** Realize that whatever lies down that passage terrifies even the corrupted cultists.

**Perception Check (DC 14):** Hear faint whispers from the passage, distinct from the chanting.

## Encounters

### Tentacle Ambush

At a chokepoint, players navigate a narrow tunnel encased in pulsating organic growth.

Random tentacles lash out:

**Strength Check (DC 14):** To break free if grabbed.

Failure results in 1d8 necrotic damage as the tentacle drains vitality.

### Cultist Patrol

A small group of cultists (4 regular, 1 leader) walks the path carrying an ornate lantern that glows with corrupted energy.

If noticed, the cultists will attempt to block the players' path, calling out for reinforcements if not silenced quickly.

Lootable Lantern:

**Arcana Check (DC 14):** Reveals it can disrupt corruption temporarily (1-time use, 10-foot radius, lasts 1 round).

## Decision Point

At the branching tunnel, players must decide:

**Follow the Glyphs:** Explore the side passage toward the hidden temple of Erathis.

**Continue Down the Main Path:** Move closer to the ritual chamber and encounter heavier corruption and resistance.

This section uses environmental hazards, cultist behavior, and red flags to provide tension and a clear invitation to explore the hidden temple without forcing the choice.
