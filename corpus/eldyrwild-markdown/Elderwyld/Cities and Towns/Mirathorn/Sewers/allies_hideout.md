# The Allies' Hideout: Refuge of the Resilient

## General Description

Nestled within a less-corrupted section of the sewer system, this room is a haphazardly fortified hideout created by remnants of the covert ops teams sent ahead of the players. The room smells of sweat, blood, and desperation, contrasting with the musty and acrid stench of the surrounding sewers.

Crates, barrels, and debris have been used to create makeshift barricades, and glowing runes etched onto walls offer minimal protection from the encroaching corruption. A single magical lantern casts a flickering, pale blue light over the occupants.

## Key Features

### Improvised Barricades

The entrance to the hideout is heavily fortified with stacked crates, overturned carts, and rusty metal debris. It appears hastily constructed but effective enough to slow down any pursuers.

**Strength Check (DC 15):** Required to breach the barricade forcibly.

**Persuasion Check (DC 14):** A successful plea will get the occupants to cautiously open the barricade.

### Occupants

## Captain Idris Flamewind (Human):

A grizzled veteran mercenary who took command after the mission fell apart. Idris is injured, her left arm in a makeshift sling, but her resolve is unshaken.

**Personality:** Stern but fair. Distrustful of strangers but quick to ally with those who prove their competence.

**Dialogue Example:** "If you're here to loot corpses, turn around. If you're here to fight those bastards, take a seat—we’ve got work to do."

## Apprentice Mage Zoran Emberwell (Half-Elf):

A nervous but determined young mage from the Stormspire Academy. Zoran has been using their limited spells to fend off the corruption and tend to the wounded.

**Personality:** Timid but resourceful. Eager to prove themselves.

**Dialogue Example:** "I... I can cast some minor wards, but I don’t know how long they’ll hold. Please tell me you have a plan."

## Three Wounded Mercenaries:

Two human fighters and a dwarven ranger, all too injured to continue fighting but capable of providing intelligence or basic assistance.

Dialogue Example: "We’ve seen those cultists dragging crates of that meat into the deeper tunnels. They’re preparing for something big."

### Supply Cache

Despite their dire situation, the occupants have a small stockpile of supplies, including:

**Healing Potions (x3):** Restores 2d4 + 2 HP.

**Basic Ammunition:** Arrows and bolts.

**Alchemical Salves:** Removes poison or disease from a single target.

**Spell Components:** Common materials for spells up to Level 2.

## Encounters and Interactions

### Earning Trust

The players must convince Captain Flamewind and her group that they are not cultists or corrupted infiltrators.

**Persuasion (DC 14):** Successfully assures the group of the players' intentions.

**Deception (DC 16):** If players fabricate a story, it may succeed but risks being uncovered later.

**Proof of Integrity:** Offering healing, information, or sharing a piece of corrupted meat for examination immediately earns trust.

### Intel from the Hideout

Once trust is earned, the group can provide critical information:

**Cultist Numbers:** They estimate at least two dozen cultists and several sewer meat creatures remain in the tunnels ahead.

**The Ritual Location:** They overheard talk of a large chamber directly beneath the city, with a cistern playing a pivotal role.

**Weak Points:** Zoran detected a planar instability around the ritual area, suggesting it could be exploited to disrupt the summoning.

### Optional Ally Recruitment

If the players provide healing or encouragement, Captain Flamewind and Zoran may decide to join them:

**Captain Idris Flamewind:** A skilled melee fighter (CR 2) who can deal significant damage with a longsword and rally allies with Commander’s Strike.

**Zoran Emberwell:** A novice mage (CR 1) with spells such as Magic Missile and Detect Magic.

## Loot

### From the Hideout

**Runed Dagger:** A +1 magical weapon that glows faintly in the presence of corruption.

**Stormspire Pendant:** Grants the wearer a +1 bonus to saving throws against charm and fear effects.

**Scroll of Shield:** A single-use scroll that provides +5 to AC until the start of the caster’s next turn.

### From the Mercenaries

**Maps of the Sewers:** Hand-drawn but functional, showing some of the unexplored paths and areas likely to contain more cultist activity.

**Alchemical Torch:** A small, ever-burning flame in a lantern that can be used to ignite flammable material or provide light.

## Decision Points

**To Fight or Ally:** Players can choose to rest and strategize with the remnants or use the opportunity to interrogate and gain intel by force.

**Aid the Injured or Move On:** Healing the injured mercenaries may delay progress but can yield additional rewards and goodwill.

**Investigate the Planar Instability:** Zoran’s observations provide a lead that could make the upcoming ritual battle easier if acted upon.

The hideout provides a temporary safe haven, a moment for reflection, and a chance to gather critical resources and allies. It also emphasizes the dire stakes of the players’ mission as they push deeper into the corrupted tunnels.
