### Framework for Engaging Dungeon Design: The Sewers Beneath Mirathorn

## Core Goals for the Dungeon

**Engagement Through Variety**: Combine exploration, combat, puzzles, and roleplay opportunities.

**Pacing**: Keep encounters and obstacles tense but mindful of the players' time constraints.

**Environmental Storytelling**: Reflect Mirathorn’s history and the cult's corruption throughout.

### Initial Passage: The Stormspire Basement to the Sewers

#### Description

The passage is ancient, built during Mirathorn’s founding. It was originally a concealed escape route or smuggling path that fell into disuse and obscurity. Now, the cult has exploited this forgotten path to move meat and resources undetected. The stone walls are damp, lined with faintly glowing moss, and carved with faded runes of protection—most of which are now broken or flickering weakly.

#### Key Features

**Locked Gate**:

A rusted iron gate, adorned with faded Stormspire Academy symbols, bars the way forward.

**Obstacle**: The gate is arcane-locked. Players must use thieves' tools (DC 20) or solve a puzzle to bypass it.

**Puzzle Option**: Runes engraved on the gate glow faintly. Matching three glyph pairs in sequence unlocks it. If the sequence is failed, the glyphs trigger a magical shock trap (2d6 lightning damage).

7

**Trap: Pressure Plate**:

A narrow section of the passage has concealed pressure plates beneath the flagstones.

**Trigger**: Weight activates a collapsing ceiling stone, blocking the way back and causing 1d8 bludgeoning damage.

**Clues**: Slight scratches on the walls above the plate and uneven stones on the floor.

**Avoidance**: Investigation (DC 14) to notice; Dexterity save (DC 12) to avoid.

**Environmental Detail**:

Half-rotted wooden crates and barrels, remnants of old supplies, sit abandoned. Inside one is a dead rat, swollen and discolored—a hint of the cult's influence.

### Connecting Passage to the Larger Sewer

#### Description

The passage opens into a massive cylindrical sewer chamber with rushing water cascading into side drains. The air is thick with the stench of decay and a faint, rhythmic pounding noise echoes from deeper within—a drumbeat tied to the summoning ritual.  There are two bridges across a 10 meter river of sewage and river water, and 5 exits. Once exit is with the water upstream, one is downstream, then three locked doors across the bridges.

#### Key Features

**Branching Paths**:

**The Main Bridge: **Is made of stone, and has a bomb trap set to detonate the bridge in the middle. DC 15 to detect the trip wire, the bomb is underneath the bridge and requires acrobatics DC 20 to access.

**Secondary Bridge: **Is made of planks and wood and is at the far end of the room.

**Left Path**: A narrow walkway leads to a dead end, where a section of the sewer wall has collapsed into a dark sinkhole. Investigation here reveals minor treasure: a satchel containing 50 gold and an Arcane Scroll of Identify.

**Right Path**: Leads to a side chamber used as a cult storage area. It contains a pile of corrupted meat, pulsating faintly and surrounded by meatworms.

Combat Encounter: 4 **Meatworms** (homebrew CR 1/2 creatures; deal acid damage and latch onto enemies).

**Upstream: **Leads to a giant 10m grate covering the path to the river and bay.

**Downstream: **Leads through a giant spinning fan blade that chops up detritus and leads to a holding tank for fermenting and clarifying the sewage. This is below the central spire that the ritual is happening on.

**Primary Path**:

A grated walkway follows the flow of the sewer water. The area grows increasingly slick and treacherous, requiring players to succeed on Dexterity checks (DC 10) to avoid slipping into the muck below.

## Encounter: Remnants of the Vanguard

The first wave of wizards and mercenaries lies dead or dying here. Their battle was clearly brutal—scorch marks and acid burns line the walls. Among the wreckage are:

**1 critically injured mercenary**: Desperate for healing, but willing to trade information. He reveals details about the cult's stronger forces ahead, including a description of the **Sewer Meat Blob**.

**Loot**: A wand of Magic Missiles (5 charges remaining), a Potion of Greater Healing, and a partially used spell scroll of Fireball. Slate etched with runes, glowing brightly in the presence of corrupted meat.

**Combat Option**: A few corrupted sewer creatures emerge to scavenge, including two **Flesh Rats** (twisted giant rats dripping with caustic ichor).

### NPC: The Injured Mercenary

**Name**: Gorvin Blackhand

**Rank**: Freelance Mercenary (Vanguard Scout for Stormspire Academy’s contracted operations)

**Species**: Half-Orc

**Appearance**: Gorvin is a broad-shouldered half-orc with dark green skin, a shaved head, and piercing amber eyes that seem dulled from pain and exhaustion. His left arm is burned and limp, hanging uselessly at his side, while his leather armor is torn and covered in scorch marks. A large, bloody gash runs along his thigh, making him unable to walk unaided.

### Personality

**Gritty Realist**: Gorvin is practical and direct. He’ll help the players but only if they show they’re not wasting his limited time.

**Wounded but Proud**: Despite his critical injuries, he exudes pride and determination. He doesn’t beg for help but will grudgingly accept aid when offered.

**Insightful**: Gorvin has keen observational skills, having scouted the area before the ambush. He’s willing to share what he knows, though his information is limited to what he saw before chaos erupted.

### Dialogue Prompts

**Introduction**:

“You…you’re not cultists, are you? If you are, you’ll have to finish me, I wont eat the damn meat.”

“Mercenaries always draw the short stick in these contracts. Should’ve charged more for sewer work.”

**Desperation**:

“I don’t have much time. Heal me, and I’ll tell you everything I saw.”

“There’s…a monster ahead. A blob. A giant thing made of flesh and…something worse. It killed most of us.”

**Insights About the Cult**:

“They didn’t fight like normal thugs. They had magic—dark magic—and something was pulling them…calling them.”

“I heard one of the cultists say something about a ‘Shepherd’s calling’…whatever that means.”

**On the Sewer Meat Blob**:

“It’s not just meat. It thinks. I swear it knew where we were going before we did.”

“If you’re smart, you’ll burn it. Burn everything. That thing’s a mistake that should never leave these tunnels.”

## On the Sewer:

“We snuck pretty deep in, got pretty close to wherever that drumming sound is coming from, where they are taking meat.”

“Except for the guards, the rest of them don’t even notice you, suppose they think you are following the call too.”

### Condition

Gorvin will die without immediate aid. A successful **Medicine (DC 12)** or a healing spell/potion stabilizes him.

If healed, Gorvin can move but will not fight. His injuries make him a liability in combat, but he offers to guide the players back to the last safe location he scouted or sketch a crude map to the next section of the tunnels.

### Loot

If the players search Gorvin or assist him, he offers:

**Gorvin’s Contract Papers**: Signed by a representative of Stormspire Academy, it outlines the mission objectives and payment agreement.

**A Broken Sword**: A silvered longsword shattered in the fight. Can be reforged into a weapon with a unique property (e.g., extra damage against undead or corrupted creatures).

**Map Fragment**: A hastily drawn scrap showing part of the sewers, including dead ends, traps, and possible cultist positions.

### Tying It Together

**Tension from Time**: The rhythmic thumping grows louder as the players progress, urging them to move quickly. Visual clues, such as deeper red hues in the sewer water or glowing meat fragments stuck in grates, increase the pressure.

**Narrative Hooks**:

The injured mercenary drops hints about the sewer kaiju ritual and mentions seeing cloaked figures deeper in the tunnels.

The side chamber with the meat storage gives insight into the cult’s operations and provides evidence to connect the ritual with the corrupted meat.

### Battle Scene: Initial Sewer Encounter

#### Map Description

The sewer chamber is a 40-ft wide Cylindrical room with slippery grates on each side, two bridges across a 10m gap, 5m down to fast-moving water.

Platforms along the edges hold cultist paraphernalia, including lanterns glowing with sickly green light.

One side features a broken walkway that players must leap across to follow the main path.

#### Creatures

2 **Corrupted Guards** (standard melee fighters with moderate resistance to non-magical damage).

2 **Flesh Blob Spawn**: A smaller, detached fragment of the kaiju entity, it moves sluggishly but hits hard, spraying caustic ichor when damaged.

4 **Flesh Rats:** Minions, one hit, add pressure to protect the Mercenary.

#### Combat Mechanics

The Flesh Blob Spawn lobs acid balls with a range of 60ft harassing the players from the other side of the water (Dex save DC 13, 2d6 acid damage).

Environmental Hazard: Any player who slips into the water must succeed on a Athletics/Acrobatics to catch themself (DC 15) on fail a Str/Dex save (DC 15) once in the water, to avoid being swept toward spinning blades in the sewer drain, taking 1d10 slashing damage.

### Next Steps: Deeper Sewer Layers

The players’ choices—whether to press on or return to regroup—set the stage for the next phase. Uncovering cultist staging grounds and clues about the summoning ritual are key milestones ahead.

### Active Staging Ground: Cultist Operations Hub

#### Purpose of the Staging Ground

This room is where corrupted meat is gathered, sorted, and prepared for delivery to the ritual site. Cultists are actively organizing supplies, including enchanted tools for meat preparation and maps marked with routes to the ritual site. The area also serves as a waypoint for cultists being assigned to various tasks, providing an opportunity for interrogation and stealthy infiltration.

### Room Description

The chamber is cavernous, with sewer water flowing through the center in a sluggish current. Rough wooden planks and crates form makeshift bridges across the water, and cultists move purposefully between piles of corrupted meat, crates of supplies, and a central stone table covered in papers and glowing glyphs.

Lanterns emitting a faint green light hang from iron hooks, casting eerie shadows on the walls. A rhythmic thumping sound reverberates through the room, growing louder and more oppressive with every step toward the ritual site.

**Environmental Features**:

**Central Table**: Covered in ritual glyphs and marked maps showing paths to the ritual site.

**Meat Piles**: Pulsating, corrupted flesh emitting faint wisps of dark energy.

**Sewer Channels**: Wide channels of murky water divide the room, with rickety wooden planks acting as bridges. Falling in requires a Strength save (DC 12) to avoid being swept away (leading to another part of the sewers).

### Inhabitants

**4 Cultist Elite Guards**: Human leaders dressed in dark robes adorned with abyssal symbols, barking orders to the workers.

**Behavior**: Assertive and focused, but not expecting combat. One carries a pouch containing vials of corrupted meat ichor used for marking the ritual site.

**Knowledge**: They know the ritual's location and timeline but are unaware of how close the players are to disrupting it.

**8 Sleeper Guards**: Guards disguised as workers, carrying crates of meat. They will attack if violence breaks out but try to blend in until the fight begins.

**2 Sewer Meat Monster**: A low-CR meat construct shaped like a humanoid figure, sluggishly hauling corrupted supplies. It is mindless and will continue working unless directly attacked.

### Roleplay and Interaction Opportunities

**Interrogation Incentives**:

Players can overhear the Overseers discussing their progress, with comments like:

"The Shepherd's calling grows stronger—we must hurry to complete the preparations!"

"The Ikon needs more mass; tell the others to bring what’s left from the guard barracks!"

The Overseers carry detailed maps leading to the ritual site and coded messages about their operations.

**Stealth Mechanics**:

Players can sneak around the edges of the room to eavesdrop or pilfer maps and documents from the central table.

**Stealth Challenge**: DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) checks to move unnoticed past cultists. A failed check alerts the Sleeper Cultists, who subtly signal the Overseers.

**Persuasion/Deception**:

Players disguised as cultists (or with high Deception) can approach the Overseers with a fabricated story about being sent as reinforcements or messengers.

**Skill Check Options**:

Persuasion (DC 15): Convince the Overseers to reveal information.

Insight (DC 12): Spot weaknesses in their bluff to extract critical details.

### Combat as a Last Resort

If the players fail to maintain stealth or diplomacy, combat ensues.

#### Combat Setup

The Overseers use **Command** and **Hold Person** to incapacitate key players.

Sleeper Cultists focus on ranged attacks with crossbows, using the wooden planks as cover.

The Abyssal Worker Creature charges recklessly, attempting to grab and slam players into the corrupted meat piles, dealing bludgeoning and necrotic damage.

#### Dynamic Environment

The meat piles explode when struck, releasing necrotic spores in a 10-foot radius (DC 13 Constitution save or take 2d8 necrotic damage).

Cultists may attempt to knock players into the sewer channels, forcing them to contend with the rushing water and regain footing.

### Loot and Clues

**From the Overseers**:

Detailed ritual map leading to the summoning site.

Coded instructions from higher-ranking cult members (requires an Intelligence check DC 12 to decipher).

A vial of corrupted ichor that pulsates faintly in proximity to other tainted meat.

**From the Table**:

Ritual notes explaining the final stages of the kaiju summoning.

Enchanted tools (e.g., a sacrificial dagger with minor necrotic damage, or a branded ritual amulet).

### Branching Outcomes

**If the Players Interrogate**: They learn the exact location of the ritual site, as well as critical timing details (“The Shepherd will rise before dawn!”).

**If the Players Defeat the Cultists**: They obtain maps and ritual notes but might lose time deciphering them.

**If the Players are Discovered and Escape**: The cultists may alert the ritual site, forcing the players to face tougher defenses ahead.

This setup rewards creative problem-solving while allowing combat to remain a natural consequence of failure or aggression. It also ties directly into the larger narrative, ensuring the players feel the weight of their decisions.

As the players delve deeper, the environment shifts from grimy sewers to a horrific biomechanical nightmare, reflecting the growing influence of the dark entity behind the cult. The encounters and spaces should tempt exploration while building tension toward the final ritual room.

### 1. Abandoned Temple of Nameless God

**Purpose:** A thematic side-distraction and lore-building opportunity, with potential for unique loot or inspiration.

## Description:

The walls shift from stone to faintly glowing veins of meat-like material intertwined with cracked marble. The air hums faintly, as if a distant hymn to the Nameless God still lingers.

A ruined altar lies at the center, depicting a faint, faceless feminine figure. Dusty offerings and tarnished golden plates surround the base.

The murals on the walls are partially obscured by pulsating flesh, showing scenes of a past golden age before chaos.

Behind a secret wall is an room, inside is the loot.

## Encounter Options:

**Puzzle**: A glowing sigil on the altar can be deciphered (Arcana or Religion check DC 14) to reveal a lost blessing of the Nameless God, granting temporary protection (+1 AC for 1 hour) or a vision of the city's founding.

**Trap**: A corrupted glyph triggers if the players desecrate the altar or fail to solve the puzzle, summoning a swarm of fleshy, winged creatures (CR 1).

**Loot**:

**Golden Reliquary**: A magical container that purifies 1 cubic foot of liquid into a party worth of potion of long rest once per year.

**Amulet of Divine Recall**: Once per day, allows the wearer to reroll a failed saving throw against charm or fear.

### 2. Room of Elites and Loot

**Purpose:** A tough encounter with significant rewards, showcasing the cult’s powerful enforcers.

## Description:

A cavernous chamber, its walls entirely replaced with pulsating, sinewy flesh. A macabre chandelier made of bones and glowing viscera hangs from the ceiling.

Four **Elite Cult Guards** (CR 3) and a **Cult Enforcer** (CR 4) stand guard over crates of stolen supplies and tainted meat.

## Encounter Details:

The guards are actively sorting supplies and discussing plans. If the players approach stealthily, they can overhear critical information about the ritual’s timing and mechanics.

If combat breaks out, the chandelier shatters when hit, dealing **3d8 bludgeoning damage** and scattering glowing viscera, which creates difficult terrain in a 15-foot radius.

## Loot:

**Sword of Lingering Wounds**: A +1 longsword; when it hits, the target cannot heal for 1 minute unless they succeed on a DC 14 Constitution save.

**Fleshbound Tome**: A book containing dark rituals; if studied for 1 hour, provides advantage on Arcana checks against the cult’s magic for the next 24 hours.

Potions of Greater Healing and Elixirs of Resistance (Necrotic).

### 3. Wizard and Mercenary Hideout

**Purpose:** An opportunity for alliances, moral dilemmas, and reinforcement.

## Description:

A small, partially hidden chamber off the main path, barricaded with scavenged wood and debris.

Inside, two wounded **mercenaries** and a terrified **Stormspire wizard** (Apprentice Wizard, CR 1/4) cower. Their torches cast long, flickering shadows on the walls, now slick with oozing liquid.

## Interaction Options:

Players can persuade the survivors to join their cause (Persuasion DC 13) or interrogate them for information about the ritual room layout.

The survivors may offer aid:

A scroll of **Fireball** for the party’s strongest spellcaster.

Directions to a bypass that allows the players to avoid a heavily trapped corridor.

If the players decide to rest here, the survivors will warn them that the flesh around the walls reacts violently to prolonged presence (triggering an encounter).

## Complication:

One of the mercenaries is unknowingly tainted by the corrupted meat and begins transforming mid-dialogue, creating a tense moral dilemma or surprise combat.

### Transition to the Ritual Room: Geiger-esque Meat Hell

## Atmospheric Details:

The walls are now entirely sinewy, with veins pulsing rhythmically and faint screams emanating from unseen depths.

Fleshy growths have overtaken the stone, forming pseudo-limbs and grotesque sculptures. The air reeks of decay and iron.

Occasional bursts of steam from swollen pustules line the hallways, obscuring vision and forcing Constitution saves against nausea (DC 12).

Tentacle-like appendages periodically lash out from the walls, requiring Dexterity saves (DC 13) to avoid **1d6 bludgeoning damage**.

### Final Encounter Before Ritual Room

## Setup:

At the threshold of the ritual room, a final defensive line blocks the players. This includes:

**2 Elite Corrupted Guards** (CR 3 each).

**1 Cult Priestess** (CR 4), a devoted servant directing the defenses.

The Priestess wields spells like **Hold Person**, **Inflict Wounds**, and **Shield of Faith**, prolonging the fight and calling reinforcements.

## Combat Features:

The floor is unstable, with sections of exposed flesh that can collapse under weight (Dexterity saves DC 14 or fall into a pit for **2d10 acid damage**).

A fleshy growth at the far end emits bursts of necrotic energy every round, dealing **1d6 necrotic damage** to anyone within 20 feet unless destroyed (AC 15, HP 30).

## Post-Combat Discovery:

The players find a blood-stained map of the ritual room layout and notes hinting at the kaiju summoning ritual. This offers clues for tactical planning in the final encounter.
