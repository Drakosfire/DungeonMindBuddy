## The Ritual and The Kaiju

"The Shepherd seeks to rise, formed of flesh and bone, devouring the corrupted to fuel its birth. The city will tremble under its weight."

"The ritual needs corrupted flesh, and it will begin at dawn. It is hidden deep in the sewers, beneath the square where the festival will gather."

The players learn that a **flesh kaiju** is the ultimate plan, using the corrupted guards and cultists as sacrifices. If they don't act before dawn, it will be too late.

## Corrupted Meat Distribution and Storage

"We hid the meat within your very walls, in the cellars of the guards, in warehouses that your council trusts, beneath your nose."

"You think you've found it all, but there are more stores, protected, guarded by those too lost to be saved. Even now, they guard their prize."

This can confirm that there are **hidden caches of corrupted meat** the players haven’t found yet, and **some of the city’s trusted locations are complicit.**

## The Cult's Origins and Leaders

"We are The Shepherd’s Flock, guided by the Shepherd who speaks to us from beyond the veil. We are the true saviors, the heralds of a new dawn. You will not stop what has already begun."

"Lyra still breathes. She knows the city better than any, and she guides the flock, preparing the feast."

They learn that **Lyra is still active**, and she’s coordinating things. The players might have thought they had dealt with the leadership by killing The Wolf, but this reveals that they’re still in serious danger.

## The Otherworldly Patron

"You see only the surface. There is a voice, a call, that speaks through the flesh. It desires, it hungers, and it feeds upon your fears. It is older than your gods."

"The Shepherd feeds it, and it will grow until nothing is left but the consuming dark."

This provides a hint that the **Shepherd is more than just a cult leader—it’s a conduit for something far more ancient and malevolent**, which is why the corrupted meat has such an effect.

## Torbin’s Connection

"The boy is marked. The Shepherd calls to him, through the beat of the drum, through the whispers in his sleep. He will be there when the Shepherd rises, whether you wish it or not."

This clarifies that **Torbin is somehow tied to the ritual** or even meant to be a part of it. It’s a hint that the players need to protect him carefully or investigate why he’s so important.
