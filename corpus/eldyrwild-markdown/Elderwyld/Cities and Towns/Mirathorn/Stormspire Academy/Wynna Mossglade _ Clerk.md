### Reception Clerk: Wynna Mossglade

**Race:** Half-Elf
**Age:** Mid-30s
**Class/Role:** Administrative Clerk of the Stormspire Academy

**Appearance:
**Wynna Mossglade has a **delicate, almost ethereal beauty** typical of her elven heritage, but it’s offset by the **dark circles** under her eyes, hinting at **long nights** of paperwork and organizational chaos. Her **auburn hair** is pulled back into a **tight bun**, and a pair of **spectacles** rests on the tip of her nose, constantly slipping down. She wears the **official robes** of the Academy, but they’re slightly **crumpled**, suggesting she hasn’t had time to change or rest in quite a while. A few **ink stains** mar her fingers, and a **quill pen** is tucked behind one ear.

**Personality:
**Wynna is **efficient and meticulous**, but the stress of recent events has left her a bit **frayed around the edges**. Normally calm and unflappable, the current crisis has her on high alert, and she has no patience for interruptions or people who can’t follow instructions. She’s the kind of person who **loves order**, and the chaotic state of the city right now has her feeling like she’s **losing control**. Still, she’s **sharp-witted and observant**, with a **dry sense of humor** that slips out when she’s stressed. She’s been at the Academy long enough to know **everyone’s business**, and she’s a reliable source of gossip—if you can get her to relax for five minutes.

## Quirks:

Constantly **adjusts her spectacles**, pushing them back up her nose.

Mutters to herself when she’s **trying to organize her thoughts** or **stressed.**

Has a habit of **making lists**, even when she’s speaking, as if she’s mentally cataloging everything around her.

**Backstory & Role:
**Wynna grew up in the shadow of the Academy, the child of a **human bookbinder** and an **elven scholar**. She has been working at Stormspire for over a decade, starting as a **library assistant** before moving into a more administrative role. She’s **seen the Academy through its ups and downs** and has a **deep respect** for its traditions. While not a powerful mage herself, she has a **strong understanding of magical theory** and knows how to navigate the labyrinth of bureaucracy that keeps the Academy running. Right now, she’s been tasked with coordinating the **covert ops teams**, a job that’s testing her patience and organization skills to their limits.

## Dialogue Examples:

"Yes, I see you’ve brought a... dead body. That’s fantastic. I have exactly two minutes to find out why you’re dropping this on my desk before the next group comes in for their assignment."

"You’re going to need to fill out a requisition form if you want healing potions. No, I don’t care that it’s an emergency. **Everything’s** an emergency right now."

"Honestly, if this place falls apart, it’ll be because no one followed protocol. Not because of some... tentacled monstrosity or whatever is lurking down in the sewers."

## Secrets:

She’s got a **soft spot for underdogs**, and if the players manage to show a bit of **vulnerability or humor**, she might be more helpful than she initially appears.

She’s **overheard more than she should have**, and might let slip **small pieces of information** about the covert ops efforts, the state of the city guard, or even rumors about **who might be corrupted**.

## Goals in the Scene:

**Maintain order** amidst the chaos. She’s trying to keep everything running smoothly, and the players showing up with a corpse isn’t helping.

**Direct the players where they need to go** but will make them jump through bureaucratic hoops unless they **convince** her otherwise.

**Provide information** that might guide the players if they can gain her trust. She’s more perceptive than she lets on, and she might have noticed **something odd** about recent Academy activities or staff behavior.
