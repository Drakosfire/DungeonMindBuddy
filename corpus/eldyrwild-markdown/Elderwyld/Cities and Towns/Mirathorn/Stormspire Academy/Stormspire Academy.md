**The Stormspire Academy** is a venerable institution, over 150 years old, known for its arcane prowess and magical innovation. Located on the edge of reality, it’s one of the most powerful establishments in the region, housing a wealth of knowledge, artifacts, and spellcasters skilled in a variety of disciplines. However, while the Academy has immense resources, it must balance aiding the players with maintaining its defenses and secrecy. Here’s how it can equip and support the adventurers without overshadowing their level.

**1. Traversing from the Council Building to the Academy**

## City Circle Atmosphere:

Describe the **unsettling quiet** of the city at this hour, with the **bright glow of lanterns** outlining the streets. There’s a sense of urgency in the air, as you spot **groups of covert ops teams** moving discreetly between buildings, hushed whispers and the glint of weapons catching in the dim light. The **streets** themselves are **sparsely populated**, but those who are out move quickly, looking over their shoulders.

You can paint a picture of the **half-assembled BBQ pits** and **stalls**, hastily prepared for the morning but now standing eerily still. **Banners flap in the night breeze**, and a few workers are still trying to get things set up, unaware of the looming threat. This sets a stark contrast between the city's planned celebration and the dark undertones of the ongoing crisis.

## Possible Roleplay:

The players might encounter a **member of a covert ops team** who doesn’t recognize them. They could be suspicious, confused, or relieved to see more people helping. This character can ask for a **quick update** on what the players are doing, forcing them to either bluff their way through or briefly explain.

A **drunk reveler** could stumble into the street, lost and eager to join the festivities. This could lead to a brief and awkward roleplay moment where the players have to handle this out-of-place NPC who has no idea of the danger the city is in.

### 2. Describing the Stormspire Academy (Exterior and Interior)

## Exterior Description:

The **Academy looms against the backdrop of the Stormspire Mountains**, its spire reaching up like a dark needle piercing the night sky. The two domes to each side glow faintly, with magical wards pulsing in soft colors, creating a gentle hum that resonates through the air. A few **robed figures** patrol the grounds, their faces partially hidden, but their presence feels tense, purposeful.

## Interior Description:

Upon entering, the players are greeted by **a grand, vaulted hallway** with **polished stone floors** and **walls lined with arcane sigils** that shimmer faintly. The air smells of **aged paper, incense, and alchemical potions**.

In the main atrium, there’s a **buzz of activity**, with **students and wizards** moving hurriedly, carrying scrolls, potion vials, and equipment. Conversations are quick and urgent, snippets of phrases like "**locate the meat**" and "**strengthen the wards**" can be overheard.

A **small reception desk** is manned by a **young, flustered clerk** who appears to be the **organizer of the covert operations** from the Academy’s side. She has a **clipboard and ink-stained fingers**, and she barely looks up as the players approach, still noting down names and assignments. When the players burst in with the dead body, she’s momentarily stunned, fumbling over her words as she tries to process what she’s seeing.

### 3. Merril Tealeaf and Checking on Torbin

**Location:** The infirmary room in **Stormspire Academy** is dimly lit, with flickering **candlelight** casting soft, warm glows across the stone walls. The air is heavy with the scent of **lavender and chamomile**, herbs that Merril has spread around the room to create a calming atmosphere. There’s a faint **hum of magic** that seems to pulse through the walls, almost like a heartbeat, reinforcing the protection spells around the Academy.

**Torbin’s State:
**As the players enter, they see **Torbin**, a young, frail figure lying on a cot, his face pale and glistening with a sheen of sweat. Even in his **magically induced sleep**, his body is **restless**, twitching as if caught in a nightmare. Occasionally, he murmurs softly, his lips forming unintelligible words. His eyes, though closed, flutter rapidly, and there’s a sense that he’s **battling something** even in his unconscious state. The room feels **tense and fragile**, as if something unseen is trying to break through the calm that Merril has tried to create.

**Merril Tealeaf’s State:** Merril Tealeaf, the leader of the Agricultural Union, stands beside Torbin's bed, looking **tired and worried**. Her usually bright, round face is shadowed with concern, and there are **deep lines** around her eyes that speak of **sleepless nights**. She’s a **halfling woman** with **auburn curls** tucked under a **green headscarf**, and her hands are stained with **soil** and **herbs**, a testament to her work. In her hand, she clutches a **small vial of herbal tincture**, and she occasionally leans over to gently dab Torbin’s forehead with a **cool, damp cloth**.

**Merril’s Personality & State of Mind:** Normally **cheerful and practical**, Merril is known for her **down-to-earth demeanor** and **warmth**. But right now, there’s a flicker of **uncertainty** in her eyes. She’s torn between her **duty** to help **purify the city’s food supplies** before the festival and her **concern** for Torbin, who seems to be struggling with something beyond her understanding. Merril’s hands are **steady** as she cares for him, but there’s an underlying **nervous energy**—like a spring wound too tight, waiting to snap.

She speaks in a soft, **soothing tone**, almost as if her words alone can keep the darkness at bay, but her voice carries the weight of someone who’s **overwhelmed** by the responsibilities suddenly thrust upon her.

## Dialogue Examples:

"I’ve tried every calming draught I know, but nothing seems to reach him… Poor lad. It’s like he’s caught in a storm he can’t escape from. Have you ever seen anything like this before? Please, if you have any ideas… I’m willing to try anything."

"I need to be preparing the purifiers for the festival, but I can’t bear to leave him like this. It’s like… he’s being called somewhere, and I can’t let him go alone. This is going to get worse, I’m not sure he’ll wake up."

"I’ve dealt with all kinds of stubborn plants and animals in my time, but whatever’s got its claws in Torbin… it’s something darker, something I can’t see. It makes me feel… powerless."

## Quirks:

Merril **adjusts her headscarf** when she’s nervous, pulling at the edges as if to tighten it.

She keeps a **small pouch of herbs** at her belt and will instinctively reach for it, crushing the leaves between her fingers for comfort.

When she’s deep in thought, she **mutters recipes and herbal mixes** under her breath, as if trying to find a solution through the familiar routine.

## Goals in the Scene:

**Reassurance and Convincing:** Merril needs to decide whether to stay by Torbin's side or continue with her preparations for the festival. The players can **convince her** to prioritize one over the other, reassuring her or offering solutions that help her manage both.

**Gathering Information:** Merril will ask the players if they’ve seen or heard anything that might explain Torbin’s condition, probing for details without knowing exactly what to ask. She’s looking for **clues**, and she’ll share anything she’s heard about the cult, the corrupted meat, or strange occurrences around the city in return.

**Caring for Torbin:** She might request the players’ help to **cast a protective spell** over Torbin, **track down a specific herb** to ease his symptoms, or even **stay with him** while she goes to check on her duties.

### 4. Zyla Cinderbranch’s Mindset and Roleplay

## Zyla’s State of Mind:

Zyla is a **practical, no-nonsense gnome**, and the crisis has her in a **hyper-focused state**. She’s been pulling all-nighters trying to coordinate the magical resources of the Academy to help the city. While she’s **tense**, she’s also **sharp and quick-witted**, able to process information fast.

When the players arrive with the dead body, she’ll be **annoyed at the disruption**, but once they explain, she’ll **switch gears** and focus on the Speak with Dead ritual. She has no time for niceties, so she’ll want to get straight to the point. She may also be **skeptical about how useful the dead Wolf will be**, but she’ll let the players push her into performing the ritual.

### 5. Roleplay Opportunities and Dialogue:

## Reception Clerk:

“If you’re here for the meat hunt, take a number and a rune. Otherwise, I’m going to need some names and some credentials, because we’re a bit... overloaded at the moment. Wait—what is that? Why do you have a dead body?”

This character could be played with a bit of **nervousness** but also **strict adherence to procedure**, so if the players push past her, she’ll get flustered and call for backup, making things a bit awkward and tense.

## Torbin and Merril Conversation:

Merril: “He’s been like this since they brought him in. I’ve tried everything I know, but it’s like he’s hearing something we can’t. You said they’re connected to this... cult, right? Do you think he’s... still in danger, even in here?”

This can prompt players to **reveal what they know** or **discuss what they plan to do next**. Merril might offer a potion or an herb to **help keep Torbin calm**, but she’ll also be listening carefully for **any hints that she should be aware of**.

## Zyla Cinderbranch:

“I hope you have a very, very good reason for barging in here like this. I’ve got half the city’s magical resources pointed at finding this meat, and I don’t have time for distractions... unless this is important. So, what is it?”

When the players explain, she’ll nod curtly. “Fine. We’ll do this, but it’s going to be quick. And if you’re wrong about this being important, I’m docking you the next batch of Greater Healing potions. So, lead the way.”

### 6. Mechanics for Roleplay and Investigation

## Skill Checks:

Players can use **Persuasion, Insight, and Intimidation** when interacting with Zyla, Merril, or the clerk. This can help get them quicker access to information, resources, or help.

Players might make **Arcana or Medicine checks** when looking at Torbin, giving them insight into what he’s experiencing and what might be causing it.

## Consequences:

If the players **don’t convince Zyla** to prioritize the Speak with Dead ritual, they might have to wait, giving Draven and other cultists more time to act.

**Failing to interact diplomatically** with the clerk might mean additional checks to get what they need, or **drawing unwanted attention** that could complicate things later.

## Resources

### 1. Healing and Recovery

## Greater Healing Potions:

Restores 4d4 + 4 HP, and each player can receive up to two. These are brewed by the Academy’s **Alchemical Society**, a group that specializes in creating potent brews and elixirs.

Appearance: These potions glow softly, with a faint shimmer of gold that dances across the liquid’s surface, giving a sense of warmth and vitality.

## Restoration and Detoxification:

Cleansing Draught: A special potion that can remove poison, curses, or lingering effects from the corrupted meat. The **Head Healer**, an ancient elf named **Aeryndel**, will oversee a thorough check of each player to ensure they are free from any taint.

Ritual of Purification: For deeper, magical afflictions, the Academy can perform a **30-minute ritual** that cleanses more severe curses or magical influences. This ritual involves an intricate dance of sigils and runes, glowing blue and green as the magic swirls around the participants.

### 2. Arcane Resources

## Utility Scrolls and Infusions:

Scroll of Detect Magic and Dispel Magic: These allow players to investigate corruption and magical disturbances. Available as **single-use scrolls** or **infusions** that can be applied to an item, granting it the ability to perform the spell once.

Scroll of Locate Object: These scrolls are keyed to track down specific materials, including corrupted meat. They have a range of up to **1 mile** but must be used with caution, as prolonged use can alert nearby cultists to their presence.

Arcane Eye Elixirs: Drinking this potion grants the player **Arcane Sight** for an hour, allowing them to see invisible entities or hidden magical effects. Useful for detecting illusions or invisible foes.

## Protective Wards:

Warding Amulets: Elegant silver amulets inscribed with **protection runes**. These grant an extra saving throw when resisting charm effects or magical corruption. Each amulet lasts for **24 hours** before needing recharging at the Academy.

Defensive Runes: The Academy can inscribe small runes onto the players' armor or weapons, giving them **temporary resistance** to certain effects, such as poison or necrotic damage. These runes last **12 hours** and can absorb one instance of a chosen effect before fading.

### 3. Magical Equipment and Enhancements

## Temporary Buffs:

Weapon Oils: A vial of **enchanted oil** that grants a weapon +1 to hit and damage for an hour. Available for melee, ranged, or even magical foci. The oil has a subtle blue glow, and once applied, it allows the weapon to strike with greater precision.

Elixirs of Speed and Dexterity: These elixirs enhance the drinker’s agility, providing benefits akin to the **Haste spell** for a short period. However, they come with a caveat: once the effect wears off, the user must succeed on a **Constitution save** or suffer a point of exhaustion.

Arcane Sigil Stones: Small, smooth stones etched with runes that can be pressed against an item to **temporarily enhance its abilities**. For example, a stone might grant a shield **+1 AC** or a staff **+1 spell attack** for an hour.

## Rune Detection Devices:

Scrying Compasses: These are specially designed **hand-held devices** that point toward the presence of corrupted meat or other planar disturbances. When they detect a target, the needle begins to spin erratically, and the compass face glows a faint red. Players can use these to navigate hidden paths or track the cult’s hidden caches.

Wardstones: Small pebbles that can be placed in locations to **create a radius** of protection against minor curses or corruption. Useful for setting up safe areas or defending a camp from infiltration.

### 4. Specialized Tools and Training

## Customized Covert Ops Kits:

The Academy can offer **customized kits** for covert operations, such as lockpicking sets enchanted to be **whisper-quiet** or **smoke bombs** that release a cloud of harmless illusionary smoke to provide a distraction.

Silent Step Boots: Soft leather boots enchanted to **muffle footfalls**, granting advantage on stealth checks for an hour.

## Rune-Crafted Keys:

The Academy provides **rune-crafted keys** that can unlock arcane locks, hidden doors, or bypass magical wards. Each key is tuned to a specific lock, and once used, it **crumbles to dust** to prevent reverse engineering.

### 5. Rest and Recuperation Services

## Enhanced Rest Chambers:

The Academy has specialized rooms where the players can **rest and recuperate** more effectively. These chambers are enchanted to allow **short rests to count as long rests**, but can only be used once per day per person.

Sanctuary of Dreams: A small chapel within the Academy where players can **meditate and gain insight**, effectively gaining the benefits of a **Divination** spell after a long rest, with a chance of guidance from the Academy’s spirit guardians.

### Additional Options

## Guarded Escorts:

The Academy can offer **guarded escorts** for the players as they move about the city. These escorts are Academy-trained combatants who are skilled in **detecting and countering corrupted magic.**

These guards might not fight alongside the players in battles, but they can assist in **investigations, provide distractions, or intercept cultists trying to interfere.**

## Localized Planar Seals:

Small devices that **seal off planar connections** within a **10-foot radius**, preventing teleportation or interplanar communication. Useful for blocking cultists’ means of escape or preventing the manifestation of eldritch horrors during rituals.

### Example Flavor Descriptions

**Potion of Greater Healing**: A crystal vial containing a shimmering red liquid that emits a faint warmth. As you drink, it spreads warmth through your veins, knitting wounds and restoring vitality.

**Elixir of Speed**: A slender, dark glass bottle filled with a silvery fluid that seems to ripple and pulse like liquid mercury. When uncorked, it emits a faint hum, resonating with latent energy.

**Wardstone**: A smooth, blue-gray stone etched with glowing white runes. It feels cool to the touch, and a subtle hum can be felt, like the distant toll of a bell.

### Balancing These Resources

To ensure the players don’t feel overpowered:

**Limit the quantity**: Provide **one-time use items** and restrict the more powerful aids, like warding amulets and enchanted oils.

**Require conditions**: Make certain items only usable under specific conditions (e.g., a rune detection device only works within certain areas).

**Balance with exhaustion**: While the Academy can heal, some items may come with trade-offs, such as temporary exhaustion after use.

## Ambush after Speak with Dead

### Corrupted Professor’s Assistant: Draven Willowcrest

**Role**: Assistant to a senior necromancy professor at Stormspire Academy.

**Race**: Human

**Appearance**: Draven is a lanky, pale-skinned man in his late twenties, with dark circles under his eyes from countless nights spent studying forbidden texts. His hair is slicked back, and he wears round, silver-rimmed spectacles. He dresses in a dark, slightly disheveled robe, with ink stains on his cuffs and a faint, almost undetectable patchwork of magical runes stitched into the fabric.

**Backstory**: Once a promising student of necromancy, Draven was curious about pushing the boundaries of life and death. This curiosity led him to secret, forbidden experiments—his fascination making him an easy target for the cult. He was corrupted by the cultists through the use of the tainted meat, unknowingly ingesting it at a dark, underground ritual disguised as an "experimental study group" where they discussed necromantic theory. Now, he acts as a sleeper agent within the Academy.

**How He Influences the Fight**: Draven has subtly tampered with the Academy's internal defenses, weakening the wards and magical alarms around the basement morgue. He’s set up an "experiment" that will create a temporary disturbance when activated, making it easier for the corrupted guards to slip through without triggering the full force of the Academy’s protective measures. During the fight, he might act as if he’s defending the Academy but will subtly miscast spells, fail to seal entry points, and strategically allow enemy reinforcements to slip through. He could also cast spells that create chaos, such as darkness or illusions, making it harder for the players to focus on protecting The Wolf’s body.

### 2. Gnomish Necromancer: Professor Zyla Cinderbranch

**Role**: Expert in necromancy and death rituals at Stormspire Academy. She will be leading the ritual to speak with the dead.

**Race**: Gnome

**Appearance**: Zyla is a short, spry gnome with wild, frizzy silver hair that sticks out in all directions, often tucked under a black headscarf adorned with tiny skull charms. Her skin is weathered and covered in faint, faded tattoos of arcane symbols. Despite her grim specialty, her eyes are sharp and curious, and she has a playful smirk that offsets the eerie nature of her work. She dresses in layered robes of dark purples and greens, with numerous pockets filled with small tools, bones, and vials of mysterious liquids.

**Personality**: Zyla is eccentric, lively, and endlessly fascinated by the mysteries of death. She’s known to be a bit macabre but also deeply knowledgeable and surprisingly empathetic to those who’ve lost loved ones. She sees necromancy as a tool for understanding life, not just death.

**Role in the Fight**: Zyla is committed to the ritual and will fiercely protect The Wolf’s body. If the corrupted guards break into the morgue, she will use her necromantic spells to raise skeletal defenders from the bodies stored there, creating a temporary but effective barrier. Her experience with spirits allows her to channel protective wards that momentarily repel corrupted forces, buying the players time. She’s not a fighter, but she’s strategic, using her spells to disrupt enemy formations and assist the players.

### 3. The Corrupted Guard Invasion

## Entrance Tactics:

The corrupted guards have secretly tunneled through a forgotten part of the Academy’s basement, leading from the sewers into the morgue area. Draven ensured that this passage wasn’t discovered by subtly misdirecting Academy staff and weakening certain wards. When the invasion begins, the guards will force their way in, shoving open sewer grates and bursting out of hidden passageways.

They bring with them a mixture of corrupted creatures—like sewer-dwelling monsters and semi-undead constructs, grotesquely altered by dark rituals.

**Mechanics**: The players might need to roll Perception or Investigation checks to spot hidden entry points or detect the sounds of approaching enemies. Draven might make a deception roll to feign surprise or act clueless when asked why the defenses are failing.

The dim, flickering glow of the **Stormspire Academy’s basement** lanterns casts long, eerie shadows across the cold, stone floor. The room is heavy with the scent of musty tomes and the faint, lingering tang of old, dried herbs. The **Speak with Dead** ritual just finished, and as the players exchange tense glances, they can feel the air grow colder. Zyla receives a sudden, urgent message—a small, mechanical bird flutters to her shoulder, whispering in her ear. With a frown, she excuses herself, saying she must attend to an urgent matter elsewhere in the academy, leaving the players alone with the still, unsettling silence.

The moment Zyla disappears from view, **Draven**, the corrupt assistant, shifts his stance, a sinister smile playing on his lips. In a fluid motion, he signals with a subtle gesture, and almost immediately, the far wall of the basement shudders. With a sickening, wet squelch, the stonework splits, and cracks of dark, oily substance seep through. Suddenly, the **grates leading to the sewers burst open**, and a wave of corrupted guards spill into the room, their eyes glazed and oily, faces twisted with an unnatural sneer.

From the sewers, the grotesque, malformed shapes of **sewer meat creatures** slither out, dragging themselves across the floor, leaving a trail of viscous sludge in their wake. The room, once still, is now filled with the sounds of snarls, shuffling feet, and the wet, rhythmic thud of meat hitting stone.

The players stand between Draven, the approaching corrupted guards, and the **oozing monstrosities** from the sewers, their backs against the arcane ritual circle that still hums with faint energy. It’s a fight on multiple fronts, and the smell of rot and corrupted flesh fills the air, mingling with the hiss of steel being drawn and spells being prepared.

## Battle Scene Map Description:

The **basement** is a large, rectangular room, lined with tall, wooden shelves and heavy, dusty books. To one side, there’s a wide, arcane circle etched into the floor, still glowing faintly from the recent ritual. Several smaller tables are scattered around, cluttered with scrolls, glass jars, and alchemical tools. At the far end of the room is a massive, **ancient stone door** that leads to the sewers, currently ajar and seeping dark sludge. The **sewer grates** are located on either side of the room, providing multiple entry points for enemies.

## Key Features:

**Ritual Circle:** Players near the circle can use it for limited, brief magical advantages (e.g., protection against necrotic damage or extra healing once per encounter).

**Shelves and Tables:** These can be knocked over or used for cover, potentially blocking some enemy advances but also cluttering the space.

**Dim Lighting:** The room is poorly lit, with only a few magical lanterns casting dim, flickering light, providing ample opportunity for stealth tactics or ambushes from the enemy.

**Sewer Tunnels:** Dark, narrow, and slimy, with eerie, echoing sounds. New creatures can emerge from these tunnels if the battle drags on.

## Sewer Meat Creatures Description:

The **Sewer Meat Things** are a horrific amalgamation of rotting flesh and corrupted magic. They are roughly humanoid but are twisted and grotesque, with limbs that seem to have been crudely stitched together from various creatures. Patches of their skin are stretched taut, while other areas sag loosely, leaking dark, oily fluids. Their eyes are sunken, glowing faintly with an unnatural, sickly yellow light, and their mouths stretch too wide, filled with rows of jagged, broken teeth.

## Tactical Abilities:

**Creeping Viscera:** The creatures can emit a thick, sticky sludge that hinders movement. Characters caught in this sludge must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** or have their speed reduced by half until the end of their next turn.

**Necrotic Bite:** A melee attack that deals **2d6 necrotic damage**, with a chance to poison the target. If the creature scores a critical hit, it latches on, continuing to deal necrotic damage each turn until it is dislodged (requires an opposed Strength check).

**Flesh Meld:** The creatures can temporarily merge into the environment, making them harder to detect. If they haven't been attacked for a turn, they can use their action to blend into the dark, gaining advantage on stealth checks.

**Reeking Burst:** When killed, these creatures explode in a cloud of necrotic gas, forcing all nearby characters to make a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw** or take **1d6 poison damage** and become poisoned for 1 minute.
