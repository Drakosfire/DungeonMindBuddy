# Roderic Marr — character seed (Mossford)

Pre–deep-dive framing for **Sheriff Roderic Marr**: the human lawman who carries **crown charter** in a town that answers to **water, labor, and fear** before it answers to ink.

## At the table (one glance)

**Role:** Sheriff of Mossford; **Mayor Sareth Mosscale’s** tempering weight in rooms where policy can spark a riot.  
**Look:** Human man in his early fifties; **dark brown skin**; **close-cut hair and beard** both **salt-and-pepper**; jaw **crooked** from an old riot break (healed wrong on purpose, he jokes — “harder to buy”). **Brown eyes** that stay on **hands** before faces.  
**Wear:** **Dull green** cloak when wet weather threatens; **badge** is plain iron stamped with **reach-road knot** — not pretty, not negotiable.

## Backstory (what makes him *this* sheriff)

**River years.** Marr started as a **crown river-warden** two days’ ride down the **Mireward Reach**, breaking up **smuggling runs** that timed their oar-strokes to **hymns** sung on the bank — not cult doctrine, just **human signal**, but it taught him early that **music and coordination** can move crime as cleanly as swords.

**The posting that stuck.** Eight years ago the **circuit office** assigned him to Mossford **temporarily** after the prior sheriff took a bribe that drowned two ferry families in paperwork instead of rescue boats. Temporary became permanent: the replacement **never shipped**, and Marr stopped asking.

**The deputy who didn’t come home.** **Hen Clow**, youngest of his watch, died on a **warehouse raid** that should have been **grain theft**. Official story: **bandits**. Marr scraped **chalk** from under an overturned cart — a **crook-and-circle** mark he has seen once before on a **Mirathorn prisoner transfer** docket footnote. He could never **prove** linkage; he stopped trying to sound sane about it in public. He **did** start treating **crowd euphoria** and **“free” performances** like **weapons checks**.

**Family.** His sister **Veta Marr** keeps the **watch ledgers** and the **evidence chest keys** — not because he doesn’t trust his people, but because he **does** trust her to say **no** when he is tired.

## Relationships

- **Mayor Sareth Mosscale:** Mutual, wary respect. Sareth builds **consensus**; Marr holds the line when consensus wants to **lynch** or **surrender**. They argue **after** doors close.
- **Constable Meret Rusk:** His **right hand**. He gives her **public room** to be the calm face; he takes the **crown messages** and the **ugly** warrants.
- **Marla Brambleback:** He would **not** want to arm-wrestle her. He **does** want her **on the ditches** when the town is scared — he stepped near her **once** when she went for Bonogo and decided **Caelynn’s bracelet** was cheaper than **his jaw**.
- **Questionable Company:** **Gratitude** for the forest turning east; **suspicion** of anyone who can **move** a crowd or **cut off** justice with a sentence. He remembers **Ephanna** telling the town the company was **leaving** — file that under *unfinished*.

## Voice and tells

- Sentences short. **Never** repeats an order.
- When **angry**, he **whispers**; when **afraid** for the town, he **counts** people in a room.
- Asks: **“Who profits if we panic?”** more often than **“Who did it?”**

## Hooks

- **Mirathorn travelers** get **courtesy** and **extra questions** — especially if they **sing** well.
- **Saltfen-style rumors** (rolling chapel, hymn without choir) route straight to his **night roster**.
- If the party returns **disguised**, he may **not** clock faces — he **will** clock **shoelaces**, **sword habits**, and **who stands** like they’ve **broken a line** before.

## Canon tension

Table recaps sometimes call him simply **“the sheriff.”** This seed **names** him without erasing **Session 19–20** beats: he’s the **graying human** authority beside the **mayor** when the **storm** and the **forest** are the story.
