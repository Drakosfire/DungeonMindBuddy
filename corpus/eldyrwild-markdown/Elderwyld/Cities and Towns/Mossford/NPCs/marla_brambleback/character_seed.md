# Marla Brambleback — character seed (Mossford)

Pre–player-contact framing for **Marla Brambleback**: Stacey’s mother, a load-bearing adult in Mossford—strong, competent, visible in town, and charismatic because she is not trying to be. She is the kind of person who enters a scene already belonging there.

**Work and station.** Late 30s to early 40s, bugbear. In Mossford she reads as heavy labor with authority: cooper, cartwright, river-hauler’s quartermaster, or similar—always a reason to be around barrels, wagons, tools, and supply movement. The town likely has stories about her (wagon wheels from ditches, barrels carried alone, floods fixed, nonsense shut down with one sentence).

**Appearance.** Large, broad-shouldered; beautifully kept dark chestnut fur with reed-thread and copper trinkets braided in. Smells like cedar oil, leather, river water, and woodsmoke. Rolled sleeves, strong forearms, practical clothes that fit because they must. Usually carrying something heavy like it offended her personally.

**Vibe at the table.** Low laugh; steady eye contact; gives orders like suggestions that get obeyed anyway—not flashy, undeniable. Children go still when she uses their full name. Dogs like her.

**What she wants.** Stacey alive; the town functioning; fewer idiots improvising with fire near essential supplies; children accounted for if evacuation begins.

**Stacey.** Marla knows exactly what kind of terror she is raising. She is trying to turn Stacey from “future menace” into “future leader.” She does not excuse Stacey’s behavior, but she understands it—and is likely harsher with Stacey in private than anyone realizes.

**Stuart.** Marla sees Stuart clearly: Stacey picks on him because he is reactive, sweet, and available, and Marla disapproves. She has likely fed him before, patched a sleeve once, and barked at Stacey for going too far when it crossed a line.
