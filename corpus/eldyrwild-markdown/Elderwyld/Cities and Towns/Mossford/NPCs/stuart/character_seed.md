# Stuart — character seed (Mossford)

Pre–player-contact framing for **Stuart**: shy halfling boy, emotional contrast, easy to recruit. He is small even for a halfling child—nervous posture, huge expressive eyes, reactive face (his feelings show immediately), slightly rumpled clothes, often holding something like he was sent on an errand.

**Core read.** Eager to please and very easy to fluster. He hero-worships bold people quickly and wants badly to belong. At a traveling-party table, the coolest person in his world can flip in a day or two when someone treats him with attention and agency.

**Vibe at the table.** Eager; truthful until he is trying desperately to impress someone; follows stronger personalities; wants approval badly.

**What he wants.** To be seen as brave; to stop being embarrassed by Stacey; to stay near interesting people; to be included.

**Stacey (relationship).** From Stuart’s perspective: unfair, terrifying, embarrassing—and always where interesting things happen. A subtle truth: he may secretly admire her courage and competence even while resenting her.

**Family texture (intentionally loose).** Raised mostly by his gran; parents absent, gone, dead, or away for seasonal work; spends time around the inn and adults rather than with a stable family unit. That makes him emotionally vulnerable and susceptible to orbiting stronger kids like Stacey.
