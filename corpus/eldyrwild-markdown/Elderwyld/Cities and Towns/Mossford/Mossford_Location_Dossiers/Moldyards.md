## Moldyards

Shaping yards for ironmoss lumber

# At a Glance

The Moldyards are the industrial soul of Mossford: vast sheds, curing racks, steam pits, shaping tables, and stone molds where cultivated moss is turned into planks, ribs, beams, shingles, and structural forms. To outsiders the place looks impossible. To locals it is simply work.

# Quick Reference

# Sensory Profile

The Moldyards smell like hot tannin, damp fiber, mineral salts, boiled bark, and wet stone heated too long. Steam ghosts in and out of open sheds. Workers shout through vapor clouds while saws hiss, presses knock, and curing chains rattle overhead. The place tastes faintly metallic in the back of the throat.

# Physical Description

It is not one building but a district of process. Long sheds hold shaping tables and lattice frames. Open yards carry rows of stone molds, some shallow as trays and some ribbed for beams. Salt vats and hardening baths sit under vented roofs, while elevated drying racks hold finished stock in neat, dark ranks. Every path is slick with some combination of water, slurry, and condensed steam. Chalk numbers and gang marks cover beams in every state of becoming.

# People Here

Use the profiles below as quick-play references. Each is written so you can step into them at the table with minimal prep.

## Helka Mouldhand — Foremaster

**Appearance: **A stocky dwarf woman with thick wrists, steam-curled hair cropped short, and old burn scars across one forearm she never bothers explaining.

**Smell: **Hot tannin, stone dust, mineral baths, and the sharp clean scent of cold iron tools.

**Voice: **Solid, blunt, and workmanlike. She speaks the way she sets a mold: firmly, once, and expecting it to hold.

**Attitude: **Practical, unsentimental, and proudly local. She hates condescension from out-of-town builders almost as much as she hates imprecise language.

**Mannerisms: **Corrects ‘wood’ to ‘mosswood’ without fail; folds arms when judging quality; gestures with chalk like a commander with a baton.

**What they want right now: **To find proof that fine stock is leaving the yards through someone with more authority than she enjoys challenging.

**Play them as: **A master craftsperson who treats terminology as respect. She warms slowly but permanently.

**Useful private note: **She suspects a civic hand is skimming premium stock before it reaches public inventory.

**Sample dialogue: **“It is not wood. Stop calling it wood if you want serious answers.”

• “The wall remembers every shortcut you take before it falls.”

• “If Mirathorn wants miracles, they can pay industrial rates for them.”

## Siv Marr — Curing chemist

**Appearance: **A soft-spoken human with ringed stains on every apron, tired blue eyes, and a careful mouth that looks permanently on the verge of saying ‘almost.’

**Smell: **Salt, bark tincture, hot mineral water, and boiled plant fiber.

**Voice: **Quiet, thoughtful, and unexpectedly dry when someone pushes him into humor.

**Attitude: **Gentle, precise, and more patient with materials than with people who rush them.

**Mannerisms: **Tests moisture by touch without seeming to touch; stares into vats as though reading weather in tea leaves; rewrites recipes in cleaner script after each batch.

**What they want right now: **To determine whether recent brittleness came from bad slurry, bad water, or something introduced upstream.

**Play them as: **Introverted intensity. He seems absent until the subject turns technical, then becomes very hard to derail.

**Useful private note: **He has begun keeping sample shavings from suspect batches in labeled jars under lock.

**Sample dialogue: **“Strength is a recipe. If you rush it, the wall remembers.”

• “No, that one isn’t ruined. It’s simply honest about its limits.”

• “Bad water leaves a taste in the beam long before it leaves a crack.”

## Drevan Coil — Contract hauler

**Appearance: **A scarred dragonborn wagon-master with smoke-dark scales, heavy shoulders, and a permanent squint from judging loads against bad light and worse promises.

**Smell: **Harness leather, wet rope, horse sweat, and axle grease.

**Voice: **Gruff, sardonic, and loud enough for yard work without being theatrical.

**Attitude: **Transactional, blunt, and suspicious of anyone else’s measurements.

**Mannerisms: **Taps loads with a hook before trusting them; mutters counts under his breath; laughs once, sharply, when hearing an unrealistic schedule.

**What they want right now: **To protect his crews from being blamed when someone else’s ledger fraud turns into a missing shipment.

**Play them as: **Road-tough and unimpressed. He likes honest work and honest complaints equally well.

**Useful private note: **He has already hauled one load cut to peculiar Mirathorn measurements and hated the feel of the contract.

**Sample dialogue: **“They always order light and ask why it costs heavy.”

• “If your numbers came from a clerk instead of a cart, start over.”

• “I move weight, not excuses. Tie them down separately.”

# What This Place Offers

Custom molded structural pieces, bulk lumber contracts, repair stock, consultation on mosswood use, and loadout for regional builders. Not retail-friendly, but determined customers can still buy small shaped pieces if they accept contempt with the transaction.

# Goods & Services

# Town News & Rumor

• A recent batch cured too brittle and cracked in test stress. Helka says it was bad slurry. Siv says the water was wrong before it reached him.

• Mirathorn buyers have started asking for curved ribs instead of straight stock, which means somebody there is building ambitiously.

• A labor crew walked off a night shift after hearing knocking from inside an empty hardening shed.

# Hidden Tension

A hidden side contract routes unusually fine stock out of town before it reaches the public yard, and Helka suspects a civic hand in it. She has not said so aloud because proof matters more than righteous volume here.

# Exploration Hooks

▸ A finished beam flexes after curing as if something inside it has not died yet.

▸ A builder from Mirathorn pays triple for stock cut to measurements that match no common structure.

▸ The foremaster wants neutral eyes on the ledgers before she accuses someone important of theft.

# Running the Location

This district should feel ingenious, grimy, and deeply local. It is the place that proves Mossford solved a regional problem by refusing to think like anywhere else.
