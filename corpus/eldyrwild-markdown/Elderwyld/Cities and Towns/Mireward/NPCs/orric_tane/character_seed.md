# Mayor Orric Tane — character seed

**Role:** Mireward **civilian mayor** — elected-ish local face, not crown command. Owns the town's **counter-festival** tone: punkier, anarchic, pro-free-spirit programming as deliberate distance from Mirathorn's Expansion festival.

**Soap in one breath:** Genuinely loves what Mireward **is** — loud bands, road performers, strange instruments, a town that thrives far from spires. Fears that naming **siege** will break the crowd before the enemy does. Not a fool; a man betting on morale as infrastructure.

**Voice:** Fast, charming, festival-organizer cadence. Turns fear into schedule changes. Uses "we" and "our town" constantly. Gets brittle when someone calls him cowardly in front of bards.

**Look:** Colorful sash over practical coat; ink on his palms from poster runs; hoarse from announcing sets. Smells faintly of smoke and fried dough.

**S23 stance:** Heard Hester's packet through **Salla Vey** and **Nera Coalstep**; believes **something** is wrong north, but wants **phased** response — quiet ferry checks, no public bell panic, performers kept on stage so the town doesn't hollow out. Wrong slowly until the north alarm proves him wrong fast.

**Table lines:**

- "We are not Mirathorn. We do not collapse because a clerk says sing louder."
- "If you scare the festival, you lose the people who would have held the wall willingly."
- "Bad news after the second song, not before the first."

**Peers:** **Salla Vey** (lists vs his crowd); **Nera Coalstep** (bells now vs his pacing); **Lysandro Ironveil** (likes him; thinks he waits too long); bard NPCs TBD (his allies and his blind spot).
