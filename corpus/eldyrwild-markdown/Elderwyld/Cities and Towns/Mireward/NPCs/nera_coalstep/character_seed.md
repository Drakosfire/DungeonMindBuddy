# Nera Coalstep — character seed

**Role:** Mireward **sergeant-emeritus** — bought out with mustering coin, never stopped reading the wall walk. **Acting defense lead** tonight: festival recalled the quick ones, long patrol still out, crown youth gone south.

**Soap in one breath:** Old spear who **does not romanticize peace**. Believed Hester's packet the moment the wax broke. Wants bells, barricades, north-gate watchers, and honest muster counts — not speeches.

**Voice:** Short sentences. Reach patrol jargon. Praise is scarce and therefore real. Doesn't argue philosophy; assigns ground.

**Look:** Grey braid, old Mirathorn issue tabard cut down for Reach work, knee that predicts rain. Carries a whistle and a chalk map of the north apron.

**S23 stance:** Treats Edge as **already here**. Pushed **Salla Vey** for stores and **Lysandro Ironveil** for neighbor watchers because formal garrison headcount is a lie tonight. Frustrated with **Orric Tane**'s festival pacing; respects **Delwen Rast**'s fen read.

**Table lines:**

- "The packet isn't asking for worry. It's asking for walls."
- "You want a miracle, pray. You want dawn, assign a lantern."
- "I didn't retire to watch a town forget how to count heads on a walk."

**Peers:** **Lysandro Ironveil** (orders the wall; he makes the town help); **Salla Vey** (paper vs her pace); **Calyx Ironveil** (green kid on walk — may appear under Nera's direction).
