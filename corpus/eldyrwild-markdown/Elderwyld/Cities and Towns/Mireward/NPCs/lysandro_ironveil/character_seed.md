# Lysandro Ironveil — character seed

**Role:** Mireward **Reach Gate** emeritus; **Lysandra Ironveil’s father**. Walks the wall because someone competent must, and the festival took the quick ones.

**Soap in one breath:** Sincerely anti-authoritarian in the **embarrassing** way — pamphlet energy, optional salutes, **mutual aid as gate policy** — while still doing the job better than the crown clerk who quit at sundown.

**Voice:** Warm, **too loud** for tenth bell; drops to murmur only for Lysandra. Calls her **Lysa**. Will tell the party **childhood stories** she forbids.

**Look:** Rain-grey beard (grey early); one Mirathorn pauldron, one Mireward patch; **no helmet** (“so I can hear when the town lies”).

**Family chorus (names only):** wife **Merrow** (fuller, mock reeve); son **Calyx** (green gate kid); father **Torr** (retired tithe clerk, quiet); sister **Bib** (shrine widow, husband **Hest Voss** on long patrol).
