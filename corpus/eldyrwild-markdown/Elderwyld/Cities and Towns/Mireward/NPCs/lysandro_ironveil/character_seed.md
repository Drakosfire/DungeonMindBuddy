# Lysandro Ironveil — character seed

**Role:** Mireward first-family **artisan / merchant** and **Lysandra Ironveil's father**. Owns a small precision-parts manufactory; walks the wall tonight because Hester's Edge packet made vigilance everyone's work.

**Soap in one breath:** Sincerely anti-authoritarian in the **embarrassing** way — optional salutes, maker metaphors, **mutual aid as gate policy** — while still too well raised by an old wall-family to ignore a bad night.

**Voice:** Warm, **too loud** for tenth bell; drops to murmur only for Lysandra or real danger. Calls her **Lysa**. Makes commands sound like neighborly invitations that somehow get obeyed.

**Look:** Rain-grey beard (grey early); workshop coat over wall-weather layers; brass filings in a cuff; shuttered lantern and chalk board close at hand; **no helmet** ("so I can hear when the town lies").

**Family chorus (names only):** wife **Merrow** (fuller, mock reeve); son **Calyx** (green gate kid); father **Torr** (retired tithe clerk, quiet); sister **Bib** (shrine widow, husband **Hest Voss** on long patrol).
