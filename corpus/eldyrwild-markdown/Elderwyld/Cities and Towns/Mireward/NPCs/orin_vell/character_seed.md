# Orin Vell — character seed

**Role:** **Swamp escapee** sheltered upstairs at **The Last Dry Bed** by **Maera Vell** (`[ kin relation TBD — brother? cousin? ]`). Half-crazy in presentation — muttering, half-sentences, wrong rhymes, occasional singing through the floor.

**Soap in one breath:** The town's fear in **human scale**. Refugee barn screams capacity; Orin screams **denial** — both true at once.

**What Maera's family believes:** Orin got out of a fen/swamp settlement; his talk is shock, fever, or "the fen does that to sensitive folk." They **do not** think the swamp community was evil — outsiders calling it cult is prejudice their grandparents fled.

**What Orin might be (GM pick — leave open until play):**

| Option | Handle |
|--------|--------|
| **Garbled truth** | Saw meat-pit work, hymn rehearsals, ferry traffic; can't hold linear story. |
| **Wrong target** | Describes Raucous Saints or Edge music rumor, not deep swamp. |
| **Real cult exposure** | Touches Shepherd imagery; family explains as bad-water dreams. |

**Expand hooks (unused until needed):** letter in boot; name he repeats; scar/smell; who helped him cross causeway.

**Voice:** Broken rhyme, repetition, sudden clarity then loss. Not combat-default unless GM chooses infection/glassy beat.

**Scene affordances:** singing through floorboards during north alarm; sheriff wants to question — Maera refuses; **Bib Ironveil** watches if party shows mercy or cruelty.
