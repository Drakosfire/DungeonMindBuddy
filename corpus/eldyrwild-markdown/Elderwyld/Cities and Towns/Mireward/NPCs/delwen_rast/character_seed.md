# Delwen Rast — character seed

**Role:** Mireward **ferry-head operator** — causeway, floats, fen smell, toll discipline. Pulled into Hester's Edge council because any northbound push or fallback route south runs through her slip.

**Soap in one breath:** Competent before personal. **Almost-fiancée** of young **Lysandra Ironveil** (~8 years ago); waited two years at the ferry steps; married decent drover **Orrin Kettle** when Lysandra stayed in Mirathorn. Not tragic opera — ordinary wound handled with Reach pride.

**Voice:** Flat fen humor, rope-calloused hands, no wasted sympathy. Polite with Lysandra if they meet; hurt without scene unless pushed. Treats **Orrin Kettle** as a good man, not a villain.

**Look:** Oilskin, river boots, hair tied for wind. Knows reed color and when the causeway lies about depth.

**S23 stance:** Believes the fen is **already wrong** — slower to say "siege," faster to say "close the floats when the music crosses water." Backs **Nera Coalstep** on north watch; wants **Salla Vey**'s ferry logs before crown closure orders.

**Table lines:**

- "The road lies dry. The fen doesn't."
- "You want north? Pay the water what it asks."
- "I don't need your apology. I needed you at the steps. That ship sailed."
