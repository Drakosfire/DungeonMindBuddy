# Maera Vell — character seed

**Role:** Innkeeper of **The Last Dry Bed** — Reach overnight, "last hot meal before the smell." Runs bar/floor; family lives on-site. Full rooms; rumor hub; human-scale knob for stacked town fear.

**Soap in one breath:** Tired, protective, **not a villain** — keeps upstairs kin **Orin Vell** hidden because she believes care and tolerance are the town's best virtue. That virtue may be **dangerously wrong** tonight.

**Voice:** Warm floor-voice for guests; steel when someone threatens Orin. Uses Lundayell-flavored lines: *we don't turn folk away for being strange.* Not Shepherd doctrine — Mirathorn founding myth filtered through Reach pride.

**Look:** Sleeves rolled, keys on a chain, flour or stew on an apron. Knows every drover by drink order; knew young Lysandra as **Lysa, off the counter.**

**Inn beat:** Party may hear **singing through the floorboards** — she says Orin "always had a voice." Offers floor space if party vouches; asks them not to upset him. Town split: sanctuary vs suspicion when north alarm rises.

**Table lines:**

- "He's been through enough. You want a bed, you leave him be."
- "Strange isn't evil. My grandmother taught me that before your crown did."
- "Full house. Floor or nothing. That's not cruelty — that's Tuesday."
