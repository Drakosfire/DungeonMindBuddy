# Reeve Salla Vey — character seed

**Role:** Mireward **crown reeve** — tithe clerk elevated to local logistics authority. Physically holds **Private Hester's Edge field packet** after delivery. Runs lists, scales, granary access, and requisition paperwork from the crown compound / tithe barn.

**Soap in one breath:** Believes the town survives through **order you can weigh** — grain by the bushel, beds by the head, runners by the route. Not cruel; not soft. Thinks panic is a inventory problem until it isn't.

**Voice:** Dry, precise, tired. Counts while she talks. Uses legal Reach phrasing when scared people need boundaries. Softens one degree for anyone who brings numbers instead of noise.

**Look:** Ink-stained cuffs, crown seal on a chain, boots that have seen the tithe scale in rain. Keeps the Edge packet in a waxed folio, not loose in a pocket.

**S23 stance:** Read Hester's packet as **siege preparation**, not rumor. Wants ration control, refugee intake rules, bell codes written down, ferry closure on her authority, and a muster roll that matches beds actually available. Frustrated that **Mayor Orric Tane** treats naming the crisis like breaking the counter-festival.

**Table lines:**

- "I don't need poetry. I need head-counts and who signed for the grain."
- "Edge asked for beds. We have hay in the chapel and families in my barn. That is not a metaphor."
- "You may dislike the crown. You may not dislike arithmetic."

**Peers:** **Orric Tane** (civilian calm vs her lists); **Nera Coalstep** (wall now vs her paperwork pace); **Lysandro Ironveil** (respects his neighbors; distrusts his lack of signatures); **Delwen Rast** (ferry logs must match requisition).
