Context, While resting at the Grit and Grime the players will witness Grobnok getting annoyed at something buzzing around his head. It’s an enchanted paper bird flying around.

In the city of Mirathorn, the Wizards’ College employs a unique and whimsical method to summon council members and important individuals to an emergency meeting, especially during the night. They use enchanted paper birds, known as Arcane Couriers, to deliver messages.

### Arcane Couriers: Enchanted Paper Birds

Appearance: These are delicate origami birds made from enchanted parchment, each one glowing faintly with a silvery-blue light. They are meticulously folded into the shapes of birds like sparrows, owls, or even phoenixes, depending on the urgency of the message.

Activation: When a wizard from the college needs to send an urgent message, they whisper the recipient's name and the message into the bird. The bird then comes to life, flapping its paper wings with a soft rustling sound and taking flight.

Flight and Delivery: The Arcane Courier swiftly flies through the city, navigating rooftops, windows, and doorways with ease. It has an uncanny ability to find its intended recipient, no matter where they are, and will land gracefully on their shoulder or in front of them.

Message Delivery: Upon landing, the bird unfolds itself into a small scroll, revealing the message inscribed on its inner surface. The message is written in a glowing, ethereal script that is readable even in complete darkness. After the recipient reads the message, the paper crumbles into harmless sparkling dust, ensuring confidentiality.

### The Summoning Process

When the call for an emergency meeting is made:

Preparation: A wizard at the college takes out several pieces of enchanted parchment and begins folding them into birds, each tailored to the individual council member.

Enchanting: The wizard then whispers the message, which typically reads something like: "Urgent meeting at the Wizards' College. The hour grows dire. Your presence is required."

Sending: The birds are released from the college tower, their glow visible against the dark sky as they scatter to deliver their messages across the city.

Arrival: The birds arrive at the council members’ locations, whether they are sleeping in their chambers or working late into the night. The glow of the bird and the soft rustling sound serve as a gentle, yet unmistakable, summons.

### Narrative Flow and Connective Tissue:

## City Council Meeting as a Distraction:

The city council meeting is called in the dead of night, pulling the players into tense discussions and decisions regarding the cult, the meat, and the guard corruption. This will keep their focus on politics and problem-solving, potentially making them overlook or delay investigating other oddities.

During the meeting, certain council members (notably Commander Thalia, who is under the Wolf’s influence) will downplay the guard's disappearance and any strange occurrences, reassuring the council that everything is under control.

Mayor Elara Swiftwind will try to keep the council calm and focused, while Headmaster Tinkerbright may be the first to express unease, sensing disturbances in the arcane flow of the city.

## Torbin’s Nightmares:

While the players are occupied at the council, **Torbin begins having intense nightmares**, hearing the rhythmic and unholy drumbeats calling the corrupted guards to the underground chamber. These nightmares will grow worse, with him tossing and turning in his sleep, murmuring eerie phrases, or even waking up with strange, glowing symbols briefly appearing on his skin (a mark of the eldritch being's influence).

Torbin might sleepwalk, unconsciously following the drumming sounds, almost leading the players to the hidden chamber in the sewers if they notice and follow him.

## Disappearing Guards:

As the council meeting proceeds, the players may hear reports from Captain Lysandra Ironveil about the sudden **disappearance or strange behavior of half the city’s guards**. The Wolf, being a high-ranking guard, will be subtly trying to delay any response to this, suggesting it’s just a case of late shifts or “something manageable.”

The **guard's disappearance** can also be hinted at by common folk or NPCs in OutTown or the merchants' districts, who notice a lack of patrols or guards acting strangely, walking in a trance.

## The Flesh Horror's Summoning:

**The Wolf and the corrupted guards** are gathering in an underground chamber deep beneath the city, **a vast cavern beneath the sewers**, where they are ordered by the abyssal being to **sacrifice themselves**. The flesh kaiju is forming from their corrupted bodies, merging into a writhing mass of flesh, tendons, and bones, growing larger and more monstrous with each sacrifice.

The **flesh kaiju** is a nightmarish creature, resembling a mass of melted bodies, corrupted faces, and limbs constantly emerging and writhing from its form. It pulsates with a faint, sickly glow from the tainted meat inside it, growing stronger with each added body.

## Triggering the Battle:

As the **city council meeting reaches its climax**, a messenger (or perhaps Torbin in a possessed state) will rush in, shouting about **something massive moving underground** or strange noises from below the city. This could be accompanied by the sound of **cracks in the earth or distant rumblings** as the fleshy horror begins to push its way up toward the surface.

**The eruption** could happen during the middle of the council meeting, with the creature **bursting out into the town square or central market**, sending debris and bodies flying. This creates immediate chaos as the players are thrust into an epic battle.

## Flesh Kaiju Combat:

The kaiju is a massive, grotesque, **otherworldly horror**. It’s partly made of the corrupted guards and tainted meat, with tendrils of flesh reaching out to grab people, and **faces and limbs fused into its body**, moaning and screaming.

The fight will be a city-wide event. Players will need to battle not only the kaiju but also any **remaining corrupted guards** that didn’t sacrifice themselves. These guards could act as distractions or protectors, trying to prevent the players from getting too close to the kaiju.

Perhaps the **Wizard’s College, Grobnok**, and the **city's defenders** join in the fray. Wizards might be throwing fireballs and barriers, while Grobnok sends out some of his goblin clubbers to create chaos and support the players.

## Role of Torbin:

**Torbin’s connection** to the eldritch entity grows stronger during the battle. He may be hearing the kaiju's thoughts or commands and might need to be **protected from possession**. Alternatively, Torbin’s visions and link could be the key to defeating or weakening the monster.

Perhaps Torbin has the ability to locate a **core or weak point** within the kaiju that the players need to target. He might also know the location of a **magical weapon or device** in the city that can harm the creature.

### Opportunities for Player Action:

**Lock-picking and Exploration:** The hidden chamber where the corrupted guards are gathering could be **guarded by traps**, magical locks, or secret passages. This gives the players opportunities for lock-picking and investigation to reach the heart of the kaiju’s creation.

**Puzzle Elements:** The players might need to **translate eldritch runes** or decipher strange maps found on cultists to understand where the kaiju is being formed or what its weaknesses are.

**Tactical Combat:** The battle with the flesh kaiju should be a dynamic, multi-stage event where the players have to **coordinate with the city’s forces**, defend civilians, and use the environment to their advantage. They may need to collapse parts of buildings or lure the creature to certain locations.

### Pacing and Roleplay:

**First Phase:** The session starts with roleplay-heavy interactions during the city council meeting, giving the players the chance to report, negotiate, and gather information. As the council debates the next steps, strange occurrences start happening (Torbin’s nightmares, disappearing guards).

**Second Phase:** The players are pulled into the **action when the flesh kaiju emerges**, leading to a desperate battle in the city streets.

**Third Phase:** The climax of the session is a **massive showdown** with the kaiju, which could involve rallying city forces, exploiting weaknesses, and uncovering hidden elements (like Torbin’s link or the cult’s secret plot).

This combination of high-stakes politics, terrifying otherworldly horror, and a city-wide battle will make for an unforgettable session!

A dungeon crawl into the sewers offers a perfect opportunity for a grittier, more investigative approach to discovering and dealing with the corrupted cultist guards before the flesh kaiju emerges. Here’s how to structure it:

### Sewer Dungeon Crawl

#### Entrance to the Sewers

The players can choose to investigate the **disappearing guards** by heading into the **sewers beneath the city**. Perhaps they find a **hidden hatch in the Guardhouse’s storage room**, as you mentioned earlier, or they follow **rumors of strange noises** or **Torbin’s sleepwalking** leads them to a sewer grate in the street.

**Environment:** The sewers are ancient, dark, and damp, with **twisting tunnels** that smell of rot and stagnant water. The walls are slick with moss, and the air is thick with humidity. Small trickles of foul water run through channels on either side of the tunnels.

**Sensory Detail:** Faint sounds of **footsteps and murmuring chants** can occasionally be heard echoing through the tunnels, accompanied by a rhythmic, unnatural **thumping** that seems to vibrate through the stone, calling the guards to the abyssal gathering.

#### Encounters in the Sewers

As the players descend deeper into the sewers, they encounter a variety of traps, creatures, and clues hinting at the cult’s growing presence.

## Corrupted Guard Patrols

Groups of **corrupted guards** roam the sewers, either in a daze or actively patrolling to prevent intruders from discovering the gathering.

**Clue:** Players may notice these guards have a strange, opalescent gleam in their eyes (indicating corruption). Defeating them could yield **small pieces of corrupted meat** or **scrolls** with cryptic orders written in Infernal or Abyssal, referencing "the flesh rising."

## Environmental Traps

The cultists have rigged parts of the sewer to collapse or **release flooding water** if someone not part of the cult follows them.

## Trap Ideas:

**Pressure-plate Flooding:** Step on a loose stone, and a section of the sewer floods, forcing players to make **Dexterity saving throws** or **Athletics checks** to avoid being swept away.

**Gaseous Traps:** The cult has enchanted some areas to release **noxious fumes** that cause hallucinations or disorientation. Players might start seeing **illusory enemies** or become separated.

## Corrupted Vermin

**Giant Rats**, **Sewer Oozes**, or **slithering corrupted snakes** can serve as minor but disgusting enemies. These creatures have also consumed small bits of tainted meat and now show signs of minor corruption (glowing veins, extra limbs).

These encounters will be low-stakes combat to build tension.

#### Clues and Hints in the Sewers

Throughout the dungeon crawl, the players should find several key hints and pieces of information that build up to the final confrontation.

**Symbols of the Cult:** Markings carved into the stone, written in **Abyssal runes**, or painted in a **black, oily substance**, depict grotesque symbols of the abyssal being the cultists worship.

If someone reads Abyssal, the symbols say things like “**The Flesh Calls**” or “**The Body Rises**.”

**Hidden Hatch:** Deeper in the sewers, the players may find an old, **hidden hatch** leading into a **large underground chamber**, where the corrupted guards are gathering for their final sacrifice. This hatch will likely require **lockpicking** or solving a simple **puzzle** based on the cult’s markings to unlock.

**Torbin’s Nightmares:** If Torbin is with the party, he may start to **mutter in his sleep** or become visibly agitated the closer the party gets to the central chamber. His connection to the eldritch being grows stronger, and he might even **sleepwalk** directly toward the hatch.

#### Final Sewer Chamber: The Cult's Gathering

The players eventually come upon the **central chamber** deep below the city. This chamber is a massive, underground cavern with an eerie, fleshy glow.

**Environment:** The chamber is covered in **pulsing flesh-like growths** that extend from the walls and ceiling, almost as if the cavern is alive. **Corrupted guards** stand around the edges, chanting in a low, rhythmic drone. In the center of the room, a **pulsing mass of flesh**—still incomplete—slowly grows larger as more corrupted guards throw themselves onto it.

**Trigger:** As soon as the players arrive or make a move to stop the ritual, the **flesh kaiju** begins to take form, assembling from the writhing bodies of the corrupted guards.

#### Combat: Flesh Kaiju Formation

**Initial Combat:** At the start of combat, the players must fight through the **corrupted guards** in the room to reach the fleshy mass.

**Kaiju Emergence:** After a few rounds of combat or when enough corrupted guards are defeated, the **flesh kaiju erupts** from the center of the room, breaking through the cavern ceiling and beginning its ascent toward the surface.

Players can **choose to chase it to the surface** for a massive boss battle in the town square, or they may have the option to try and **collapse the tunnels**, cutting off its escape route (though this would involve high-level strength and planning).

#### Final Boss: Flesh Kaiju Battle

Whether the players face the flesh kaiju underground or in the streets, this will be a massive battle. The kaiju's body is a mass of melted, corrupted flesh, constantly shifting and changing form, with faces and limbs screaming in agony from its body.

## Abilities:

**Flesh Tendrils:** It can lash out with long, sinewy tendrils to grab players or drag them toward its body.

**Devour Guards:** It consumes any nearby cultists to heal itself or grow stronger.

**Corrupted Aura:** The kaiju radiates an aura of corruption, dealing **psychic damage** or inducing **fear** in those too close to it.

#### Outcomes:

If the players **stop the flesh kaiju in the sewers**, they prevent it from wreaking havoc on the city, though its remains will still be dangerous and need to be disposed of.

If the kaiju **escapes to the surface**, the players will need to coordinate with the city guard, the Wizard’s College, or even Grobnok's underground forces to take it down in a dramatic, city-wide battle.

### Alternate Encounter Paths:

If the players get too distracted with the **city council meeting** or other events and don’t investigate the disappearing guards, they may only face the **flesh kaiju** after it has reached full form and burst into the town square.

This dungeon crawl option gives the players an opportunity to deal with the corrupted cultists earlier and prevent the larger catastrophe, but with plenty of risks and combat along the way. If they manage to root out the cultists, they might save the city from an even greater disaster!
