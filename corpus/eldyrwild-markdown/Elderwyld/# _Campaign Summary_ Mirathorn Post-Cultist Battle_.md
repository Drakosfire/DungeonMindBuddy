# **Campaign Summary: Mirathorn Post-Cultist Battle**

## **State of the World**

The **city of Mirathorn** has survived a catastrophic cultist plot. The **Shepherd’s Flock** attempted to summon an **Abyssal Flesh Kaiju** beneath the city by feeding corrupted human cultists into a ritual pit. The party **disrupted the summoning**, defeated the cult leaders, and destroyed the pre-Kaiju entity. However, the **Shepherd** still exists, and remnants of the cult remain.

Meanwhile, **the Festival of Expansion is still proceeding as planned.** The city leadership has decided to suppress knowledge of the crisis to maintain order. The party’s role in recent events is well known to certain factions but remains a mystery to the general populace.

## **The Player Characters (PCs)**

A **level 4 adventuring party** who have cemented their reputation as major players in Mirathorn’s fate.

- **Stafl** – A mysterious **Aasimar Bard** wielding the **Peacemaker Fiddle**, an artifact of immense power.

- **Bonogo** – A resourceful **Goblin Rogue**, known for daring acrobatics and precise strikes.

- **Karsemine** – A **Tiefling Ranger**, dual-wielding magical scimitars that glow in the presence of humanoids.

- **Ephanna** – A **Warlock** with ties to **fey magic**, skilled in devastating Eldritch Blasts and area control spells.

- **Baergrom** – A **Dwarven Fighter**, wielding a powerful **enchanted shield** that provides temporary healing and defenses.

- **Caelynn** – A **Sorcerer with Storm Magic**, who recognizes **the storm above Mirathorn as unnatural and connected to past events**.

The party is recovering after a brutal underground battle. Some may have sustained injuries, and all are dealing with the ramifications of their victory.

## **Torbin Jove: The Party’s Adopted Ward**

- **Background**: A **human child from a peaceful farming village**, originally introduced as the kid who **bought a potato for 5 gold**, which then **grew into a magical tree that destroyed part of his village**.

- **Personality**: Curious, **fearless in the way children can be**, and **easily inspired by the party’s grandeur**. Has a habit of **spending money carelessly**.

- **Current State**: Was **affected by the Shepherd’s influence**, drawn into nightmares and compulsions. He is currently recovering at **Stormspire Academy**.

- **Future Possibilities**: Could be targeted again due to his connection to the players. His fate may be **tied to the remnants of the cult** or the **forces manipulating Mirathorn from the shadows**.

## **Key NPCs and City Leadership**

The **Mirathorn City Council**, once fractured by cultist influence, is now united against the remaining threats.

### **City Council Members:**

- **Elara Swiftwind (Gnome, Mayor)** – A charismatic and diplomatic leader. Wants to maintain stability during the festival and beyond.

- **Barin Coppergleam (Dwarf, Copper & Quartz Innkeeper)** – A shrewd businessman and political player, supportive of the party.

- **Grobnok FuckFight (Goblin, Grit & Grime Owner)** – Street-savvy council member, tied to the underground scene.

- **Headmaster Tinkerbright (Gnome, Wizard’s College Leader)** – Oversees Stormspire Academy, now investigating the lingering magical effects of the corruption.

- **Commander Thalia Ashenvale (Elf, Leader of the Guard)** – Recently freed from The Wolf’s manipulation, now refortifying Mirathorn’s defenses.

- **Torrin Flamescale (Dragonborn, Master Smith)** – Representative of the Guilds, interested in city defense and industrial projects.

- **Merril Tealeaf (Halfling, Agricultural Union Leader)** – Oversees food production, deeply involved in purifying any remaining tainted meat.

- **Rurik Stonehammer (Dwarf, Chief Architect)** – Concerned with the **structural stability of Mirathorn**, particularly after the underground battle.

### **Notable Allies & Figures of Interest:**

- **Lesandra Ironveil (Captain of the Guard at the Gates)** – The party’s strongest ally in the guard, now cracking down on lingering cultist sympathizers.

- **Zyla Cinderbranch (Necromancy Professor, Stormspire Academy)** – Assisted with Speak with Dead on The Wolf, now investigating deeper secrets.

- **Idris (Human Captain of a Mercenary Group)** – A hardened warrior who fought alongside the players in the sewer battle.

- **Zora (Stormspire Mage)** – One of the surviving wizards from the underground vanguard.

## **Ongoing Tensions & Plot Hooks**

## 1. **The Shepherd’s Next Move**

- The **true Shepherd** is still at large, manipulating events from afar.

- Cultist sleeper cells may still be active in Mirathorn.

- **The corrupted meat crisis is not entirely over.** Some stores of it may remain undiscovered.

## 2. **The Festival of Expansion Continues**

- The **public is unaware of the crisis**—should the party reveal the truth?

- **Games, competitions, and citywide events are ongoing**. Time for celebration or further investigation?

## 3. **The Storm Over Mirathorn**

- Caelynn identified it as unnatural and **identical to the storm that nearly killed the party in StoneBridge**.

- **Is the Shepherd linked to this storm? Or is another force at play?**

## 4. **The Airship-Temple Ruins Beneath Mirathorn**

- The **remains of the settlers' original airship** lie buried beneath the city.

- The temple, dedicated to **Erathis (The Nameless Goddess)**, may hold **ancient knowledge or lost artifacts**.

- **Does the cult seek to corrupt it further?**

## 5. **The Corrupted Seed**

- Stafl holds **a dangerous abyssal seed**, possibly linked to the Shepherd’s power.

- **Destroy it? Study it? Use it against the cult?**

## 6. **The City Guard is Weak**

- Half of Mirathorn’s **Guard were cultists**, leaving the city vulnerable.

- The **Council may ask the players to assist in rebuilding security**.

- Is there **a new threat waiting to exploit the city’s weakness?**

## 7. **The Fate of Torbin**

- He is **recovering, but still vulnerable** to supernatural influences.

- What **long-term effects** will his connection to the cult have?

- Could he be **a future target**?

## **Next Steps**

- **Rest & Recovery**: The party may want to regroup and rest after the brutal underground battle.

- **Investigate Leads**: Should they track down **the Shepherd, the corrupted seed, or explore the ruined temple**?

- **Celebrate or Work?**: The **Festival of Expansion continues**—they can **participate, investigate, or warn the populace**.

- **City Politics**: The **Council will likely call a follow-up meeting** to discuss the city’s future.

This summary serves as a launch point for the next stage of the campaign. Mirathorn has been saved—for now. But the scars of this battle remain, and the forces lurking in the shadows are not yet done with the city.
