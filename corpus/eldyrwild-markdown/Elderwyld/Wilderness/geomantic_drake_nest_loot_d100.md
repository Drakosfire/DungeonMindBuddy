# Geomantic drake nest — context and stealth

## Your table canon (GM)

- **Brood:** Roughly forty to fifty young drakes; most have fledged.
- **Present:** Nine juveniles and two elders.
- **Behavior:** Corvid instincts—rival piles, theft, sorting by glint and sound—but the **cache** skews toward **things that matter**: coins, craft, magic, and secrets people dropped on the road.
- **Tone:** No gag trash. **Worst** rolls are **dull but valuable**; **best** rolls are **memorable power or plot**.

---

## Sneaking in — Stealth DC

**Strict 5e:** Compare Stealth to each observer’s Passive Perception (or active Perception rolls).

| Observer | Passive Perception |
|----------|-------------------|
| Juvenile (×9) | 14 |
| Elder (×2) | 18 |

**Fast DC:** **17** Stealth for one scout, or **group check** with at least **half** the party **≥ 17**.

| Circumstance | DC |
|--------------|-----|
| Dusk squabble / feeding | 15 |
| Midday torpor | 14 |
| Night storm or fresh spellfire nearby | 19 |
| Nest already disturbed | 20 |
| Open slope, clanking armor | +2 or disadvantage |

**Fail by 1–4:** Alarm calls; juveniles harass; elders seal the richest mound. **Fail by 5+:** Both elders fight; treat as serious encounter.

## Using the table

- **One** roll for a quick lift; **three** if they sift a whole mound; **five** if elders allow it or the party wins a clean fight.
- **Bold play bonus:** once per nest, add **+10** to the d100 result (cap at 100) if they traded a shinier bait, used midday torpor, or split distraction and thief roles well.
- **Elder negotiation:** on **91–100**, elders may demand a **favor** or **future shiny** instead of blood—treat that as part of the prize, not a penalty.
