# d100 Universal Forest Encounter Table — The Migrating Forest

Use this table for **any unkeyed hex**, or when the party lingers, rests, backtracks, causes damage, or draws attention.

**Rule of Escalation:** If the same result or category appears again, do not repeat it literally. Increase intensity, speed, scale, or consequence. The forest remembers pressure.

Each entry is written to be usable raw, expandable in play, and resolved quickly.

## 1–10 · Forest Attention

The canopy goes quiet all at once, as if listening. Movement elsewhere in the hex subtly slows. Someone or something is deciding whether the party matters.

Roots surface near the party’s path, not blocking it, just close enough to force careful steps. The forest is adjusting around them. Continued travel here will be noticed.

A breeze moves against the party’s direction of travel only. Leaves brush faces and gear with gentle insistence. The forest is suggesting another way.

Bark splits on a nearby tree, exposing fresh, pale wood that quickly darkens. The forest is venting stress. Staying nearby invites a stronger reaction.

A low vibration hums through boots and bone for several seconds, then stops. The ground is re‑settling. Something large has shifted course.

The smell of sap thickens suddenly, sweet and cloying. Tools and packs stick slightly when set down. The forest is marking this place as occupied.

Moss creeps a few inches over exposed stone or metal while the party watches. Permanence is being challenged. Leaving traces here will not last.

Shadows between trees feel deeper than they should. Light bends subtly, hiding distance. The forest is obscuring scale.

A familiar landmark looks wrong from this angle, as if rotated. Orientation becomes uncertain. The forest is nudging navigation errors.

Several trees creak in unison, like a slow exhale. The sound carries farther than expected. Attention has been drawn, but not yet focused.

## 11–20 · Dinosaurs (Presence Before Violence)

Massive tracks cross the area, fresh and deep, then vanish into dense growth. The ground is still warm where they pressed. Something large passed recently.

A distant, resonant call rolls through the canopy and is answered far away. The exchange lasts only moments. The party is between territories.

Heavy movement parallels the party’s travel, always just out of sight. Branches sway in time with unseen steps. The party is being evaluated.

The remains of a recent feeding site lie ahead, quiet but tense. Scavengers wait at the edges. The hunters may return.

A wounded dinosaur crashes through brush and freezes when it sees the party. Pain has made it unpredictable. It will fight or flee based on the next move.

Trees shake violently somewhere nearby, followed by a thunderous roar. A territorial display is underway. Staying risks involvement.

The ground trembles in short bursts as a group migrates past the hex. Their path will reshape the terrain. Delay makes travel harder.

A sudden charge explodes through undergrowth nearby, not aimed at the party but terrifyingly close. The warning is clear. Next time may not miss.

A predator watches from above, perfectly still. If noticed, it withdraws silently. If ignored, it follows.

Two dinosaurs clash violently just beyond clear sightlines. The fight is brief but devastating. The aftermath will draw others.

## 21–30 · Storm Residue & Magical Weather

Static prickles across skin and hair despite clear skies. Magic feels tight and brittle. Storm energy lingers.

Burnt leaves crunch underfoot though no fire is visible. The damage is old but unfinished. The forest hasn’t healed.

A shallow pool reflects a storm that is not happening. Lightning flashes silently across its surface. Touching it risks unpredictable effects.

A tree splits open with a sound like breaking glass, scattering shards of hardened sap. Storm scars are reactivating.

A sudden, localized downpour soaks only this hex. The rain smells metallic. Equipment and tempers are tested.

Wind surges through the canopy in sharp, directional bursts. Travel becomes exhausting. The storm wants something to move.

Thunder rolls with no clouds. The timing feels wrong. Animals flee upward.

Faint glowing veins appear in leaves and bark as dusk approaches. Magic bleeds close to the surface. Staying here invites complications.

A brief hail of translucent pellets falls, melting on contact. Each impact hums faintly. The storm is probing boundaries.

The sky darkens rapidly for a few minutes, then clears. Time feels compressed. Someone lost track of how long they stood still.

## 31–40 · Branchbound Influence

A rope bridge or lashed platform appears where none existed before. It is crude but safe. By morning, it will be gone.

The party’s camp is subtly rearranged while they rest. Nothing is stolen. One item is missing, taken for a reason.

A single whispered warning drifts down from above, naming a danger the party hasn’t seen yet. No explanation follows.

Predators that should be active nearby are conspicuously absent. The area has been quietly redirected.

A bundle of supplies wrapped in leaves hangs just out of easy reach. It was meant for someone else. Taking it has consequences.

The party notices signs of movement overhead that vanish when watched directly. They are being assessed, not followed.

A safe-looking path appears that avoids a hazard but costs time. Efficiency is being tested against caution.

A Branchbound figure watches briefly, then leaves a simple mark before disappearing. It is either guidance or judgment.

Help arrives only after a party member acts generously toward the forest or its creatures. The response is swift and silent.

A Branchbound voice asks one quiet question from the canopy, then withdraws regardless of the answer. The answer will be remembered.

## 41–50 · Resource Stress

Food spoils overnight, mold spreading unnaturally fast. The forest dislikes storage. Hunger approaches.

Water sources here taste bitter or metallic. They are safe, but unpleasant. Morale suffers.

A critical piece of gear is damaged by shifting roots or falling debris. Repair will take time or ingenuity.

Packs feel heavier than they should. Straps creak. Something wants to be put down.

A pack animal panics suddenly, sensing something unseen. Calm is possible, but delay draws attention.

Fire struggles to catch, smoke clinging low. Warmth is possible, but stealth is lost.

Ammunition or small tools go missing without tracks. The loss feels deliberate.

A cache of usable materials is found, but harvesting it leaves obvious marks. Take now or remain unseen.

Rest here feels shallow and unsatisfying. Fatigue lingers. The forest discourages comfort.

An essential resource is contaminated briefly, then clears. The message is restraint, not punishment.

## 51–60 · Navigation & Perception

Familiar landmarks no longer align. The party is subtly off course. Correction costs time.

The canopy closes in, forcing a detour through rough terrain. The forest tightens its grip.

The party circles back to a place they are certain they left. The loop is gentle but undeniable.

A shortcut presents itself that feels wrong but efficient. Taking it has consequences later.

Compasses, magical or mundane, fail entirely for several minutes. Direction becomes communal judgment.

The sun’s position feels incorrect. Time perception wavers. Arguments about pacing begin.

Sounds carry inconsistently, making distance hard to judge. Danger feels closer than it is.

A clear route suddenly becomes choked with growth. The forest has reconsidered.

Tracks appear beside the party’s own, then fade. Someone walked with them briefly.

A sense of being gently herded settles in. Resistance is possible, but tiring.

## 61–70 · Social & Psychological Pressure

An argument escalates unusually fast over a small decision. The tension feels external.

One party member feels compelled to act alone for a moment. Separation tempts disaster.

Old grudges surface without clear cause. Resolution or restraint matters here.

Shared dreams trouble the party during a rest, blending memories. Emotional boundaries thin.

Someone becomes convinced they are being followed, even if they are not. Confidence wavers.

A comforting scent or sound from the past drifts through the hex. Stopping feels safe. It isn’t.

Trust becomes harder than it should. Leadership is tested.

A party member feels watched, but not threatened. The feeling lingers long after leaving.

Humor falls flat. The forest does not appreciate levity here.

A moment of clarity strikes one character, followed by doubt. Insight has a cost.

## 71–80 · Signs of Outside Influence

A crude symbol carved into bark is already being overgrown. Someone else passed through recently.

Animal remains are arranged deliberately, not for feeding. The message is unclear.

A small patch of corrupted growth spreads slowly. The forest is resisting it.

A whisper promises safety through control. It fades when challenged.

A survivor begs to follow the party out, hiding something dangerous or transformative.

A lone figure offers directions confidently and incorrectly. They mean no harm.

The air smells wrong, like rot and incense mixed. Something alien brushed this place.

A temporary structure collapses moments after being noticed. Someone left in a hurry.

A wound heals too quickly or not at all. Reality slips slightly.

The forest feels younger here, growth aggressive and bright. Time is uneven.

## 81–100 · Escalation & Major Disruption

Distant thunder rolls despite clear skies. Escalation is imminent.

Dinosaurs migrate suddenly through the hex, reshaping terrain as they pass.

Trees uproot themselves nearby and begin to move. Paths close behind the party.

A storm scar reactivates violently, warping magic and movement.

Branchbound abandon a nearby perch abruptly. Something scared them.

A massive creature passes through, altering the hex permanently.

The ground collapses, revealing depth and roots below. Travel halts.

A localized storm front forms unnaturally fast. Shelter becomes urgent.

The forest closes multiple routes at once. Choice becomes limited.

A familiar hex changes character entirely. The board is not stable.

A roar or crash echoes from far closer than expected. Something is coming.

Resources spoil, paths shift, and predators stir simultaneously. Pressure spikes.

The forest reacts strongly to a past action. Consequences arrive late.

A safe area becomes hostile without warning. Complacency is punished.

The party is forced into another hex unexpectedly. Control is lost.

A storm, migration, and predator event overlap. Survival, not victory, matters.

Branchbound intervene openly for the first time, or withdraw entirely.

The forest marks the party unmistakably. Future encounters escalate faster.

A choice made earlier becomes unavoidable. The forest closes the loop.

The forest pauses, utterly still, then resumes with renewed intensity. Whatever was decided, it was not in the party’s favor.
