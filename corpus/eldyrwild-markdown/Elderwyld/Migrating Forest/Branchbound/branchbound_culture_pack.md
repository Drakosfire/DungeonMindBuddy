# Branchbound Culture Pack

A playable, frictional faction for the Migrating Forest mini-campaign

## Purpose

The Branchbound exist to answer a question Mirathorn is failing to ask correctly.

They are not guardians of the forest. They are not rebels waiting to return. They are not guides, leaders, or sages.

They are people who survived by rejecting permanence.

Use this culture pack to keep them coherent, opinionated, and difficult to instrumentalize.

## Core Identity (Non-Negotiable)

The Branchbound are:

Former Mirathorn citizens and rebels (gnomes, elves, humans, others)

Canopy-dwelling, mobile, and intentionally temporary

Survivors who rejected walls, councils, and fixed infrastructure

Skilled, observant, and tired of explaining themselves

They do **not** want to be:

Leaders of others

Guardians of the forest

Representatives of a cause

They are here because this way of living works.

## Cultural Absolutes — What They Will Never Do

These rules are absolute. Breaking one causes internal crisis, not debate.

The Branchbound will never:

## Build permanent structures

No stone

No foundations

No straight lines meant to last

## Claim territory

They do not say “ours” about land

They abandon places without sentiment

## Use fire carelessly

Fire scars are remembered by the forest

Fire is for moments, not comfort

## Lead outsiders

They may guide indirectly

They will not escort, command, or plan for others

## Preserve records

No maps

No written histories

Memory lives in people, not objects

If players attempt to force any of the above, Branchbound cooperation degrades quietly and immediately.

## How the Branchbound Test Outsiders

They do not test with riddles or formal trials. They test through **observation under pressure**.

Common tests include:

Do outsiders leave things behind?

Do they repair damage they cause without being asked?

Do they accept indirect help without demanding explanation?

Do they move vertically when possible?

Do they abandon advantages once those advantages become burdens?

Passing a test is rarely acknowledged.

Failing one is remembered.

## Festival Behavior in the Canopy

Branchbound festivals are:

Short (hours, not days)

Mobile

Triggered by conditions, not dates

Common reasons for a festival:

A successful migration redirect

Survival through a major storm

The safe abandonment of a long-used perch

Festival characteristics:

Music made from breath, bark, and tensioned vines

Food shared freely but never stored

Dancing that emphasizes balance, release, and letting go

Storytelling that avoids names, dates, and fixed sequences

Outsiders may be invited to witness.

Participation is rare and meaningful.

If anyone attempts to schedule, commemorate, or formalize a festival, it ends immediately without explanation.

## Social Frictions They Cannot Ignore

The following always trigger response:

Fire damage left unaddressed

Stone or metal structures introduced into canopy space

Outsiders attempting to stay too long in one place

Being asked to choose sides between outside factions

Attempts to appoint a Branchbound “leader”

When these occur, the Branchbound do not argue.

They withdraw, redirect, or vanish.

## Internal Pressure (Unresolved by Design)

The Branchbound quietly struggle with **aging**.

Older members move more slowly through vertical space

Accepting help risks permanence

Slowing down endangers everyone

This pressure is never discussed openly.

It shapes decisions, timing, and departures.

## Design Mandate for Play

When portraying the Branchbound:

Be calm, not cryptic

Be helpful without being available

Never explain more than necessary

Treat permanence as a warning sign

They are not a quest hub.

They are a different answer to the same problem Mirathorn is failing to solve.
