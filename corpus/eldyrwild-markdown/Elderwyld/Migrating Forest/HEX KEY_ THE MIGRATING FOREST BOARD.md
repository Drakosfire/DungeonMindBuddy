# HEX KEY: THE MIGRATING FOREST BOARD

## 1. The Root Argument (Central Triple Spiral)

**Adventure Tag:** Environmental / Omen / Choice

**Narrative:
** Three vast root spirals dominate this hex, coiled inward like clenched fists. The ground subtly shifts underfoot, not enough to throw balance, but enough to be noticed. Water pools at the base of each spiral, slowly rotating in opposite directions. Sound behaves strangely here. Voices feel swallowed if raised, but whispers carry farther than they should.

This is a place where the forest is deciding something. The spirals are not ancient ruins; they are current events. Branchbound elders come here briefly to read migration pressure and storm cadence, then leave before the forest notices them noticing it.

**Escalation:
** If the party camps here or casts divination magic, the spirals tighten overnight. Paths leading out subtly change. Dinosaurs avoid this hex entirely for a full day afterward.

## 2. The Shallow Spiral Pool

**Adventure Tag:** Hazard / Discovery

**Narrative:
** A single spiral dominates the forest floor here, filled with dark green water that rotates slowly, pulling leaves and debris into its center before releasing them again. The surrounding trees bend inward, their roots partially exposed as if being gently but constantly tugged.

The water is not deep, but it is not entirely here. Reflections do not match the canopy above, showing older growth, different skies, or flashes of storms long past.

**Escalation:
** A creature or object submerged fully may emerge hours later… elsewhere on the board. The Branchbound consider this hex “useful but untrustworthy.”

## 3. The Canopy Paths

**Adventure Tag:** Navigation / Social

**Narrative:
** This hex shows the clearest forest paths on the board, winding naturally between thick trunks and under high branches. The paths feel intentional without being straight. Fallen limbs lie exactly where one might need a step up or a handhold.

This is not coincidence. This hex is quietly maintained. No one greets the party, but movement here feels subtly guided, as if mistakes are being gently corrected before they happen.

**Escalation:
** If the party follows the paths respectfully, they avoid one random encounter later. If they damage the terrain, the paths vanish within hours.

## 4. The Fallen Spine

**Adventure Tag:** Tactical / Ambush

**Narrative:
** A colossal fallen tree dominates this hex, its trunk split and partially hollowed, roots torn free and clawing the air. Smaller trees have grown around it, but the spine-like trunk still defines movement and sightlines.

This is a natural chokepoint and a favored hunting ground. Sound carries oddly through the hollow sections, making distance hard to judge.

**Escalation:
** Predators use this hex aggressively. The Branchbound avoid it unless desperate.

## 5. The White-Leaved Tree

**Adventure Tag:** Sacred / Test

**Narrative:
** One tree here bears pale, almost white foliage, stark against the surrounding green. Its bark is smooth, unmarred by age or storm. No undergrowth grows at its base. The air feels still, even when wind moves the surrounding canopy.

The Branchbound do not climb it. They do not cut it. They will not explain why.

**Escalation:
** Magic cast within its shadow behaves cleanly but exactly. No embellishment, no surge, no error. The tree is watching, not judging.

## 6. The Moss Memory Ring

**Adventure Tag:** Lore / Psychological

**Narrative:
** Trees here grow in a rough circle around an open center carpeted in thick moss. The moss is springy and warm, almost pleasant to lie on. Old boot prints appear and vanish within minutes.

Those who rest here dream vividly of places they once felt safe.

**Escalation:
** A long rest here restores morale but leaves the party emotionally raw. Branchbound use this hex only in emergencies.

## 7. The Storm-Scar Canopy

**Adventure Tag:** Hazard / Wild Magic

**Narrative:
** Several trees here are shattered at the crown, their upper branches fused into glassy shapes. The leaves carry a faint blue-gray tint. Shallow pools glow faintly at night.

Storm residue lingers. The forest hasn’t healed yet.

**Escalation:
** Spells risk unintended side effects. Dinosaurs are erratic and aggressive here.

## 8. The Wet Root Lattice

**Adventure Tag:** Traversal / Resource

**Narrative:
** Roots crisscross shallow water here, forming natural bridges over slow-moving pools. The water is clear enough to see movement beneath but deep enough to hide danger.

This hex is a natural crossing point and a place where supplies can be gathered… if one is careful.

**Escalation:
** Lingering attracts attention from below. The Branchbound harvest here quickly and quietly.

## 9. The Deadfall Cluster

**Adventure Tag:** Survival / Combat Aftermath

**Narrative:
** Multiple dead trees lie tangled together, snapped rather than rotted. Bark is stripped in places. Something large passed through recently, or something large died badly.

The area smells faintly metallic.

**Escalation:
** A dinosaur kill site, abandoned but not forgotten. Predators return eventually.

## 10. The Quiet Canopy

**Adventure Tag:** Refuge / Unease

**Narrative:
** This hex feels calm in a way that is almost suspicious. Birds avoid it. Insects are scarce. The canopy is intact, but the forest feels… attentive.

The Branchbound sometimes shelter here briefly, though they never stay overnight.

**Escalation:
** If the party rests too comfortably, the forest reminds them they are not invisible.

## 11. The Broken Crossing

**Adventure Tag:** Choice / Consequence

**Narrative:
** A natural crossing once existed here, roots and fallen trunks aligned just well enough to pass. It has been deliberately broken. The remains suggest intelligent dismantling, not decay.

Someone didn’t want pursuit.

**Escalation:
** Repairing it draws attention. Leaving it broken preserves anonymity.

## 12. The Edgeward Drift

**Adventure Tag:** Transition / Foreshadowing

**Narrative:
** Here the forest subtly thins. Water becomes more common. Roots float instead of anchoring. The air smells faintly sour.

This hex faces the swampward future of the forest. Everything here feels temporary.

**Escalation:
** Staying too long invites creatures and forces from beyond the forest’s control.

# THE BRANCHBOUND

## Culture Summary

## Non-territorial

## Vertical

## Memory-avoidant

## Festival-based, not calendar-based

They believe permanence is a trap and that anything remembered by the forest will eventually be corrected.

They value:

Balance

Quiet competence

Letting go

They distrust:

Walls

Maps

Leaders

Fire

## Their “Town”

They do not have one.

Instead, they maintain **three concurrent canopy clusters**, only one of which exists at any time. The other two are memories and plans.

If pressed, they call their gathering places **“Perches.”**

A perch lasts:

Weeks to months

Never a season

# d100 WILDERNESS ENCOUNTERS — THE MIGRATING FOREST

### 1–5 · Environmental Omens

A sudden hush rolls through the canopy as birds flee upward. Something large has changed direction nearby.

The ground subtly tilts over several minutes, just enough to disorient. The forest is re-settling.

A low vibration travels through roots and trunks like distant thunder, but the sky remains clear.

Leaves fall upward for a moment before drifting back down. Magic is thin here.

A path the party used earlier is partially gone, overgrown or collapsed in a way that feels recent.

### 6–10 · Branchbound Influence

A rope bridge appears overnight, crude but functional, then is gone by morning.

The party’s camp is subtly rearranged while they sleep. Nothing is stolen, but something is missing.

A distant voice calls out a warning just once, from above, then silence.

Predators that should be hunting nearby are absent, driven elsewhere.

The party finds a bundle of supplies wrapped in leaves, clearly meant for someone else.

### 11–15 · Dinosaurs (Presence, Not Combat)

Fresh, massive tracks cross the hex and vanish into dense undergrowth.

A low, resonant call echoes through the trees, answered from far away.

The party hears heavy movement parallel to them, unseen but close.

A nesting site lies ahead, quiet but tense, eggs hidden beneath foliage.

A wounded dinosaur limps through the area, aggressive but desperate.

### 016–20 · Dinosaurs (Escalation)

A territorial display erupts nearby, shaking branches and roots.

A hunting pack shadows the party, testing reactions rather than attacking.

A sudden charge crashes through undergrowth, not aimed at the party but terrifyingly close.

A predator stalks silently from above, retreating if noticed.

Two dinosaurs clash violently, forcing the party to choose sides or flee.

### 21–25 · Storm Residue

The air crackles faintly, hair lifting as static builds and fades.

Burnt leaves crunch underfoot despite recent rain.

A shallow pool glows faintly, reflecting a storm that isn’t happening.

A tree splits open with a sound like breaking glass.

A spell cast here behaves slightly off, stronger or weaker than expected.

### 26–30 · Forest Memory

The party hears sounds from a place they once rested, long ago.

A familiar scent drifts through the air, impossible but comforting.

Moss briefly forms shapes resembling faces before smoothing over.

Footprints appear beside the party’s own, then fade.

A tree bears markings that match a party member’s past.

### 31–35 · Lost Folk

A feral survivor watches from cover, fleeing if approached.

A small camp shows signs of recent use, abandoned in haste.

The party encounters someone who insists they left Mirathorn years ago… recently.

A lone figure offers misleading directions, not maliciously, just wrong.

Someone begs to follow the party out, hiding something dangerous.

### 36–40 · Resource Pressure

Food spoils unexpectedly overnight.

Water sources here taste metallic or bitter.

A critical piece of gear is damaged by shifting terrain.

A pack animal panics, sensing something unseen.

Supplies go missing without tracks or noise.

### 41–45 · Navigation Problems

The canopy closes in, forcing a detour.

Familiar landmarks no longer align.

The party circles back to a place they are certain they left.

The ground becomes treacherous, slowing travel dramatically.

A shortcut appears that feels wrong.

### 46–50 · Predator Ecology

Smaller predators flee en masse.

Scavengers gather at an unseen kill site.

The party stumbles upon a feeding ground, quiet but tense.

A territorial marker blocks easy passage.

The forest goes unnaturally still ahead.

### 51–55 · Spiral Influence

Roots twist into unfamiliar patterns overnight.

Water nearby begins to rotate slowly.

Dreams that night are shared between party members.

Compasses, magical or mundane, fail completely.

The party feels watched, but not threatened.

### 56–60 · Social Friction

An argument escalates unusually quickly.

A party member feels compelled to act alone.

Old grudges resurface unprompted.

Someone is convinced they are being followed.

Trust feels harder than it should.

### 61–65 · Subtle Threat

Poisonous spores drift through the air unnoticed.

A harmless-looking vine tightens unexpectedly.

Insects swarm one party member specifically.

A tree creaks as if about to fall.

Something brushes past a leg in the undergrowth.

### 66–70 · Branchbound Tests

A path appears that avoids danger… at a cost.

The party is silently observed and judged.

Help arrives only if the party acts kindly first.

An obvious ambush is intentionally left open.

A Branchbound voice asks a single question, then vanishes.

### 71–75 · Signs of the Shepherd

A crude symbol carved into bark, quickly overgrown.

Animal remains arranged deliberately.

Corrupted growth spreads in a small patch.

A whisper promises safety through control.

A survivor speaks of “being chosen” before fleeing.

### 76–80 · Temporal Slippage

The sun’s position feels wrong.

A short rest takes far longer than expected.

The party remembers events differently.

A wound heals too fast or not at all.

The forest seems younger here.

### 81–85 · Escalation Warnings

Distant thunder rolls despite clear skies.

Dinosaurs migrate suddenly through the area.

Trees uproot themselves nearby.

A storm scar begins to reactivate.

Branchbound abandon a perch abruptly.

### 86–90 · Major Disruption

A massive creature passes through, reshaping the hex.

A storm front forms unnaturally fast.

The ground collapses, revealing depth below.

A spiral tightens visibly.

A predator attacks without warning.

### 91–95 · Consequences Catch Up

Something the party did earlier returns to matter.

An NPC reappears, changed.

The forest retaliates subtly.

Dinosaurs remember the party.

The Branchbound withdraw protection.

### 96–100 · Rare Events

The forest briefly parts, revealing a safe passage.

A dinosaur hatchling cries nearby.

The canopy opens to show something impossible.

A Branchbound elder intervenes directly.

The forest pauses. Everything waits.
