# Tripod Null-Calf

*Large aberration (flesh-horror), unaligned*

The **Tripod Null-Calf** is a grotesque amalgamation of limbs and sensory organs that seems more an affront to geometry than a creature. It serves as a scout for the approaching siege, testing and probing defenses with a chillingly calculated indifference to life.

## Table role

- **Use in C2S23:** On-screen Tripod reveal during the Mireward north-gate pressure sequence.
- **Battlefield job:** Siege scout, geometry-breaker, cart / gate / rope-line pinner.
- **Challenge expectation:** Serious pressure for six level-5 PCs with NPC allies, but not a full boss. Its danger is positional control, gate marking, and cure-line interruption more than raw damage.
- **Best first appearance:** The north gate bell gives one dull note without being touched; reflective puddles show the creature before it rounds the road bend.

## Traits

**Three-Limb Bracing.** As long as at least two limbs are functional, the Tripod Null-Calf has advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone or shoved. It can also choose to anchor itself to a cart, gate, wall, or the ground as a bonus action.

**Rotating Facing.** Once per round, if the Tripod Null-Calf is attacked by a creature it can see, it can rotate its body, denying flanking bonuses or similar positional advantages for that attack.

**Multi-Point Pin.** The Tripod Null-Calf can grapple up to two targets, or one target and one object such as a cart, gate brace, rope line, or barricade. Grappled creatures have disadvantage on attempts to escape while the Null-Calf is anchored.

## Actions

**Threefold Slam.** The Tripod Null-Calf makes two melee attacks: one with its Hooked Knuckle and one with its Spear Joint, and then makes one grapple attempt using its Finger-bone Pad. Melee Weapon Attack: reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning or piercing damage, as appropriate to the limb.

**Hook the Line.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 15 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d10+3) damage, and the target is pulled 10 feet toward the Tripod Null-Calf.

**Wrong Angle (Recharge 5-6).** The Tripod Null-Calf rotates through impossible geometry. Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or suffer one effect of the Tripod Null-Calf's choice: knocked prone, frightened until the end of its next turn, or loses its reaction until the end of its next turn.

**Mark the Gate.** If the Tripod Null-Calf spends one turn touching a gate or barricade, that structure is marked. Siege monsters have advantage on attacks against the marked structure until the mark is removed.

## Reactions

**Brace Against the World.** When the Tripod Null-Calf would be moved against its will or knocked prone, it can instead anchor itself to a nearby object or surface, reducing the forced movement by 10 feet or transferring damage to the object it is anchoring.

**Reflection Omen (Recharge 6).** When attacked, the Tripod Null-Calf can appear in nearby reflective surfaces before moving, granting it a +3 bonus to AC against the triggering attack or allowing it to teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

## C2S23 handling notes

- The Null-Calf should test the north gate and cure line instead of only attacking PCs.
- Its **Mark the Gate** trait directly sets up the Latch-Harrow's **Siege Adaptation** trait.
- It should retreat, vanish into reflection, or leave a mark if reduced below a threshold the GM chooses, so it feels like a scout rather than a sack of HP.
- Good player counterplay: break line of sight with reflections, brace the gate, disable limbs, pull civilians out of its geometry, or force it away from marked structures.
