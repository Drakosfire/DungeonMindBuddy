# Corrupted Meat Golem

The corrupted meat golem is a grotesque and nightmarish abomination composed of chunks of corrupted flesh, sinew, and bone. Its flesh is an unnatural mix of sickly greens and bruised purples, with patches of glistening, oily opalescence that make it seem both rotten and bizarrely beautiful. Strips of muscle and tendon are stitched together by an unknown force, forming a hulking, misshapen body that stands over seven feet tall.

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The Corrupted Meat Golem can make two Slam attacks.

**Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6+5) bludgeoning damage.

**Noxious Burst.** When the Corrupted Meat Golem is hit by an attack, it emits a burst of noxious gas. All creatures within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of their next turn.
