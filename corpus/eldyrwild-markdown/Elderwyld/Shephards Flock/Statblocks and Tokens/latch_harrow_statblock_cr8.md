# Latch-Harrow

*Huge aberration (corrupted siege horror), chaotic neutral*

The **Latch-Harrow** is a later-wave siege breaker, a massive construct of corded meat, timber, and iron, designed to compromise defenses and instill fear. Its presence heralds the undoing of safety, as it targets structures and civilians with terrifying precision and intent.

## Table role

- **Use in C2S23:** Later-wave siege breaker after the Tripod Null-Calf has tested Mireward's north gate.
- **Battlefield job:** Breach-clock monster, gate-breaker, defender-choice engine.
- **Challenge expectation:** Major crisis for six level-5 PCs with NPC allies. It should be hard to simply delete with focus fire because its real threat is advancing breach pressure while forcing decisions around civilians, curing, and gate defense.
- **Default target:** North gate. If players are not challenged, redirect toward the Bell / Shrine, tithe barn shelter, or ferry chain.

## Traits

**Siege Adaptation.** If the Tripod Null-Calf previously marked the north gate, the Latch-Harrow deals an additional 10 damage to structures.

**Breach Conversion.** The Latch-Harrow's attacks against structures add progress to a Breach Clock. At 4 progress, the north gate is compromised; at 8 progress, it fails. If the Breach Clock reaches 8, the north gate is destroyed.

**Latch Sense.** The Latch-Harrow has advantage on attack rolls against restrained, braced, locked, or barricaded objects.

## Actions

**Ram the Memory of the Gate.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d8+8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a structure, it advances the Breach Clock by 1. Creatures within 10 feet of the structure must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Latch-Hook Sweep.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d10+6) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled 15 feet toward the Latch-Harrow. This attack can also be used to tear away a brace or rope line.

**The Door Is In You.** The Latch-Harrow targets one cured or glassy civilian it can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be compelled to move toward the nearest gate, door, or opening on its next turn unless protected.

**Inward Flex (Recharge 5-6).** The Latch-Harrow causes gates, carts, or barricades within 30 feet to bend inward, creating difficult terrain for 1 minute. Creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone and restrained until the end of their next turn.

## Reactions

**Crush the Helpful.** When a creature within 10 feet of the Latch-Harrow repairs, braces, heals, or cures, it can use its reaction to make a **Ram the Memory of the Gate** attack against that creature.

## C2S23 handling notes

- The Latch-Harrow is a **clock monster**. Track the north gate Breach Clock visibly.
- It should target structures and helpers, not only front-line PCs.
- Its best dramatic job is forcing the party to choose between killing it, keeping the gate standing, protecting cure work, and preserving civic morale.
- If the Tripod Null-Calf marked the gate first, call that out at the table: the Latch-Harrow arrives already knowing where the gate is weak.
- Good player counterplay: brace structures, lure it away, break hooks, use mud/ice/chains, protect curing civilians, or use bardic bagpipes to disrupt its rhythm.
