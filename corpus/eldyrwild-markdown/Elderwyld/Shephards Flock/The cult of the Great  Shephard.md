### The Shepherds’ Flock Cult

The **Shepherds’ Flock** is a secretive and growing force within the city of Mirathorn, though its roots run deep in the continent's swamps. What began as a group of disillusioned human refugees has now transformed into a fanatical cult, whose members carry out violent acts in the shadows, aiming to overthrow the diverse populace of Mirathorn and establish a human-only dominion.

#### Origins:

The Shepherds’ Flock was born from the same refugee wave that saw the founding of Mirathorn. However, while the majority of refugees embraced the city's vision of integration and cooperation with non-humans, a faction of humans felt disgusted by what they saw as a betrayal of their identity. They came from the oppressive **Lundayell Empire**, where forced multiculturalism under an authoritarian regime had left them feeling like outsiders in their own homeland. These humans longed for a return to their perceived “purity” and distanced themselves from Mirathorn’s inclusive ideals.

Around five years ago, this group left Mirathorn in a barely noticed schism, along with the other settlers. They ventured into the swamps of the north, founding their own **isolated human city**, far from the eyes of Mirathorn’s leadership. They presented this town as just another frontier settlement, part of Mirathorn’s expanding influence, while in truth, it was a breeding ground for their radical ideas of human supremacy.

#### Transformation into a Cult:

For years, they existed as an insular society, keeping their xenophobic tendencies hidden. However, their fortunes changed when they discovered an **ancient overgrown temple** deep within the swamp, hidden beneath the roots and muck of the land. This temple led them to a long-forgotten **sepulcher of a dead god**, an **eldritch being of madness and corruption**. Within the dark recesses of the sepulcher, the group encountered visions, promising power and domination over the world if they would feed the god's flesh to their followers. From that moment, the Shepherds’ Flock was born—a cult dedicated to this dead god’s resurrection, now known simply as the **Great Shepherd**.

The discovery warped them, not just ideologically, but **physically and mentally**. The Shepherds began to harvest **tainted flesh** from the temple, using it to corrupt others and bind them to their cause. The human city became a **thrall to the Great Shepherd**, and their raids on the surrounding lands turned from simple banditry into purposeful **campaigns of terror**, designed to eliminate all non-human life. They captured humans from the hinterlands, brought them back to their swamp city, and converted them into the fold of the cult, feeding them the **corrupted meat** of the dead god. They burned down villages, killed non-humans, and created chaos, making these attacks seem like the random misfortunes of frontier life. In this way, their evil actions went unnoticed for years.

#### Hierarchy of the Shepherds’ Flock:

**The Great Shepherd (Dead God)**:
The source of the cult’s power, the Great Shepherd is a forgotten, ancient god whose decayed flesh now corrupts the minds and bodies of its followers. Though dead, its influence lingers, and its flesh is fed to cultists to bind them to its will. The cult's ultimate goal is to **resurrect** this being and unleash its apocalyptic power on the world.

**The Shepherd** (**Seren Darkroot**):
The charismatic and dangerous leader of the Shepherds’ Flock, Seren Darkroot, found the ancient temple and was the first to be consumed by the Great Shepherd’s influence. She now leads the cult with an iron fist, spreading its doctrine of **human purity** and conducting **rituals** to strengthen their connection to the dead god. Lyra’s zeal is matched only by her ruthlessness.

**Eirik "The Wolf" Draven (Second in Command of the Mirathorn City Guard)**:
The Wolf is Seren’s trusted lieutenant and the highest-ranking infiltrator within Mirathorn itself. Once a loyal soldier of Mirathorn, they were captured by the Shepherds, fed the corrupted meat, and turned into one of the cult’s most dangerous assets. Their position within the **city guard** allows them to subtly influence the city’s security and sabotage investigations. While outwardly appearing loyal to Mirathorn, they are secretly orchestrating the cult’s operations from within, controlling a network of **sleeper agents** and planting false information to protect the cult.

**The Shepherd’s Council**:
The core leadership of the Shepherds’ Flock, the Council consists of human elders from the swamp city who each oversee different aspects of the cult’s operations. These include:

**The Butcher**: Oversees the harvesting and distribution of the dead god’s corrupted flesh, ensuring it’s secretly spread through both the cult and the unsuspecting population of Mirathorn.

**The Prophet**: Responsible for interpreting the visions and messages from the dead god, guiding the cult’s long-term strategies.

**The Flamebearer**: Commands the raiding parties, ensuring that non-human settlements in the frontier are burned and their human survivors brought back to be indoctrinated.

**The Flock (Cultists)**:
The rank-and-file members of the Shepherds’ Flock. They consist of human refugees and frontier settlers who have been **corrupted by the dead god’s flesh**. These cultists are completely loyal to Lyra and the dead god, willing to die for their cause. Some cultists have infiltrated Mirathorn under the guise of refugees or traveling merchants, slowly spreading the dead god’s influence.

#### Influence in Mirathorn:

At first glance, the Shepherds’ Flock appears to be just another town of Mirathorn’s growing hinterlands—a group of hardy humans trying to make their way in the dangerous swampland. In reality, they have **deeply infiltrated** the city. Through **sleeper agents** within the city guard and political manipulation by The Wolf, the Shepherds have gained significant influence over **security operations**. Their raids on frontier towns, which primarily targeted non-humans, were designed to look like random bandit attacks or town failures, masking the true scale of their atrocities.

**City Guard Corruption**: Many city guards have been **secretly corrupted**, fed the **tainted meat** of the dead god. These guards now serve as the Shepherds’ eyes and ears within Mirathorn, ensuring that any suspicion of cult activity is quickly silenced.

**Sewer Operations**: The cult also uses the **underground tunnels and sewers** beneath Mirathorn to transport their corrupted flesh into the city, hiding it within the guardhouse cellars and other secret storage areas. They have been slowly poisoning Mirathorn’s food supply in preparation for a larger attack during the upcoming Festival of Expansion.

**Sleeper Cells**: Throughout the city, the Shepherds have planted **sleeper agents**, humans who live ordinary lives but are secretly loyal to Lyra and the cult’s cause. These agents gather intelligence, sabotage critical infrastructure, and await the signal to launch coordinated attacks.

#### Current Agenda:

The Shepherds’ Flock is now preparing for their **most significant attack** yet: the spread of **tainted meat** at the city's grand Festival of Expansion, which will corrupt hundreds of citizens in one fell swoop. Meanwhile, **The Wolf** has been subtly leading key figures of the city guard into the cult’s fold, ensuring that when the time comes, the city will be too divided and confused to defend itself.

Their ultimate goal is to **summon an avatar of Great Shepherd**, a Lovecraftian flesh kaiju, and wipe out all non-human life in Mirathorn, reclaiming the city as the pure human refuge they believe it should have been from the start.
