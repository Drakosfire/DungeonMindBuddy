## The Hearthbound Bake-Off

### Setup:

**Setting and Event**: The HearthBound Bake-Off is a new initiative by the city council to rally the people during the winter months. The several prior generations struggled and fought the land to stake a claim here in the Eldyrwyld. It has been about a decade since any major catastrophes wandered out of the mist to cause major setbacks. As a sign of confidence the council is sponsoring this festival of winter and the crowning facet is the Bake-Off.

**Context**: Rival Mirathorn’s newspapers “The Mirathron Gazette” and the “Mirathorn Observer” are sponsoring the event. So there are Tilly TuffCrust from the Gazetter and Harland Broadquill providing commentary and opportunities and the framing of an entertaining spectacle. They have magic foamy stone enchanted to project their voices to the crowd. They also have gnomish cameras, floating and creating images that are projected onto enchanted sheets strung up around the stage.

**Mood**: The Emberlight Festival transforms the City Circle into a bustling wonderland of sights, sounds, and smells: Stalls Overflowing with Treats: Vendors sell candied fruits, roasted nuts, and fresh-baked breads. The scent of cinnamon, nutmeg, and warm sugar fills the air. Street Performers: Musicians, acrobats, and illusionists entertain the crowds, their talents enhanced by the city’s mix of arcane magic and steampunk technology. Magical Decorations: Floating lanterns, enchanted fireworks, and glowing streamers add a sense of wonder. The Bake-Off itself is the crown jewel of the festival: The Competition: Teams of bakers present their finest creations in a series of challenges, judged on flavor, presentation, and innovation. The Judges: Three esteemed locals with discerning tastes will determine the winner, offering praise, critique, and plenty of drama. The Stakes: Besides the enchanted prize, the Bake-Off offers fame, fortune, and the respect of Mirathorn’s growing community.

**Player Hook**: The Hearthbound Bake-Off Welcome to Mirathorn, a fledgling beacon of freedom, cooperation, and innovation nestled at the base of the Stormspire Mountains. Decades after the first settlers fled the authoritarian grasp of the Lundayell Empire, the city has grown into a thriving hub of trade, culture, and magic. Though its grand walls and towering spires hint at its future greatness, Mirathorn is still young, brimming with untamed energy, rough edges, and the dreams of its diverse founders. At the heart of the city lies the City Circle, a vibrant district home to the Council Hall, the Stormspire Academy, and an eclectic market square. This is where the annual Emberlight Festival takes place, celebrating the resilience of Mirathorn’s early settlers as well as the bountiful harvests of the surrounding lowlands. The festival is a time for joy, feasting, and friendly competition, and it culminates in the much-anticipated Hearthbound Bake-Off.

Character's Level 6, former adventurers turned bakers who have come together as a team to compete for the Bake-Off prize of a magic Cake Stand. Anything on the stand never goes bad. Think about what have you entered the Bake-off? Prestige? The prize? To impress someone in particular?

### Problem:

**Main Issue**: Each of the teams will be visited by Lieth Hollowood princling of the fae, to be offered a deal of an ingredient that is sure to make their offering of the highest quality they are capable of baking. As long as you stir it in with milk from a float goat and NOT the milk of a cow, stir it 100 times to the right and 50 times to the left, do NOT stir it more or less and it will totally be fine. Have I tried it! No, I am a connoisseur but alas lack the skills to bake myself.

**Cause of the Problem**: Each team will accept the ingredient, players can make their own choice.

**Effects of the Problem**: The baked goods will manifest into baked good monsters, and put everyone they can into a magical slumber for one year.
