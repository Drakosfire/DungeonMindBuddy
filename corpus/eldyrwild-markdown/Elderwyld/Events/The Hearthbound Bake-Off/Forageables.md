### Unique Ingredients and Flavors

**Glowberries** – Luminescent berries found in the shadowy forests near the Veiled Fen, known for their tangy-sweet taste and faint glow.

**Dragonfire Cinnamon** – A rare spice with a warm, smoky heat, harvested from the fiery dragon lily blossoms.

**Ember Ash Flour** – Flour infused with magical ash from the Stormspire Pines, imparting a smoky flavor and a golden hue.

**Stormfruit** – Electric blue citrus with a sharp, zesty kick; its juice tingles on the tongue like a crackle of lightning.

**Frostmint Essence** – A cooling extract from frostmint leaves, perfect for adding a refreshing contrast to warm dishes.

**Stardew Honey** – Honey produced by enchanted bees that gather nectar from starlit blossoms; has a faint shimmer and a deep, floral sweetness.

**Moonpetal Zest** – Shavings of the petals of moonpetal flowers, which bloom only under moonlight, giving a delicate, floral bitterness.

**Riftberries** – Juicy berries with a fizzy, effervescent quality; they pop gently when bitten.

**Amber Sap Syrup** – Syrup from the amber trees of the Stormspire foothills, prized for its caramel-like richness.

**Edible Starlight Crystals** – Tiny, crystallized sugar formations infused with light magic, adding sparkle and a subtle crunch.

### Wild and Foraged Herbs

**Hearthsage** – A savory herb with a warm, peppery flavor, often used in spiced breads and savory bakes.

**Silversprig Thyme** – An aromatic herb that gives a cool, herbal undertone, popular in meringues and frostings.

**Snowcap Mint** – A pale mint that grows on the edges of glaciers, with a sharper bite than regular mint.

**Goldenroot** – A tuber with a mild, earthy flavor and hints of saffron, often ground into a golden powder for baking.

**Candied Frostbark** – Thin strips of bark from frost-touched trees, simmered in sugar syrup for a sweet, woody flavor.

### Exotic Seasonings and Additions

**Cinderpear Compote** – A rich, spiced fruit compote made from fire-resistant cinderpears, known for their smoky, caramel-like taste.

**Shadowbean Nibs** – Dark, bitter cocoa shards from enchanted cocoa plants, perfect for balancing overly sweet dishes.

**Dawnlight Rosewater** – Distilled from dawnlight roses, this fragrant essence adds a hint of citrusy florality to desserts.

**Fae Sugar Threads** – Fine strands of spun sugar that seem to dance in the air, used as delicate garnishes.

**Charred Apple Chutney** – A smoky, sweet-and-sour spread made from blackened winter apples, perfect for complementing buttery pastries.
