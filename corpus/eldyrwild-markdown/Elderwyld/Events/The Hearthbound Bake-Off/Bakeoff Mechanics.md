### Bakeoff Structure:

The bakeoff is broken into stages w/ points awarded by the judges (ie, roleplay the judges, but the points are all imaginary, creating criteria for pretend baked goods is more work) and audience applause taken as it’s own vote at the end.

Each player can take two actions during a baking challenge. With four players this means two actions will have to be individual players.

When players act together they each get to roll, not advantage, individual rolls in order to beat a combined DC. However by acting together they have a ‘too many cooks in the kitchen’ penalty, which is a 1d6 - their CHA modifier.

The players can roll history to remember more about recipes.

### General Rules for Phases

## Actions Per Phase:

Each player gets **2 actions** to spend during each phase, but too many actions on the same task could trigger a **Too Many Cooks Penalty**.

The penalty is rolled as **1d6 - CHA modifier** and subtracted from the cumulative success of the task. This encourages teamwork without overloading single tasks.

## Skill Rolls:

Each phase has **recommended skill checks**, but players may propose alternative skill applications if they justify them narratively.

Example: Using **Arcana** instead of **Intelligence** during the Bake phase if they’re magically controlling the oven’s heat.

## Success Thresholds:

**DC 10:** Passable, but unimpressive results.

**DC 15:** Solid success, earns favor.

**DC 20:** Exceptional work, likely to impress the judges.

### Phases

**The introduction** :

The Newspaper characters will introduce themselves, introduce the judges, each team, and then explain the rules. This is an opportunity to have a CHA check/performance check

## The First Bake [Ember Crust Pies]:

Roll for Practice

They can roll for practice in the past, since Ember Crust Pie and The Cornucopia of Delights is known ahead of time. Anything above 40 (ie 10 avg roll from each player) give them a plus to their skill checks at each phase. 40-45 +1, 46- 51 +2, 53 - 60 +3, 61- 60 = +4, 61-71 = +5, 71- 80 + 6

Forage : During the forage phase the players can decide how many forage rolls they want to make and what they want to forage for.

If players have ingredients they want to describe and have foraged, they can homebrew on the spot. Then roll a d20 for the quality of what they foraged or sourced.

Otherwise players can roll a once, and an additional time for their preparation bonus to have sourced ingredients. The type is a d20 from the forageables list. Then a d20 for quality.

Quality of forageables table:

**Prepare Ingredients** :

During this phase players Survival checks to chop and otherwise prepare ingredients.

If magical ingredients are being used an arcana check is needed to ensure they are prepared correctly.

Sleight of Hand for any fine work.

**Dough** :

**Athletics (kneading dough):** Ensures the dough is worked thoroughly for proper elasticity.

**Insight (judging rise time):** Helps the baker anticipate when the dough has proofed sufficiently.

**Arcana (if magically proofing):** Speeds up the proofing process but risks uneven results if done poorly.

**Shmooze** : Interact with the judges when they come around, potentially positively influence their judgement of you.

**Persuasion or Deception (talking up your work):** Sways the judges with confidence or exaggeration.

**Performance (entertaining):** Provides levity and a distraction from minor flaws in your bake.

**Insight (reading the judges):** Gains information on what the judges are looking for and adjusts your approach.

## Bake :

**Intelligence (timing):** Determines when to adjust the temperature or pull the pie from the oven.

**Perception (observing bake cues):** Tracks subtle signs like color or bubbling.

**Arcana (magic-assisted baking):** Controls magical ovens or enchanted heat sources.

## Plating :

**Sleight of Hand (arranging decorations):** Ensures an elegant, cohesive look.

**Performance (theming):** Creates a story or aesthetic around the pie to captivate the judges.

**Dexterity (transportation):** Carries the pie safely to the judges without spilling or damaging it.

For the **Technical Challenge**, we can adapt the mechanics to focus on interpreting the vague recipe and overcoming the uncertainty it introduces. Players will have to rely on intuition, teamwork, and their baking skills while tackling the phases. Here's the refined breakdown:

### Phases and Mechanics for the Technical Challenge

#### 1. Understand the Recipe

**Objective:** Decipher the cryptic instructions and identify key steps. The players must determine what the recipe means by phrases like "bake until the dough whispers" or "let the air sing to cool it."

## Mechanics:

## Skill Checks:

**Investigation (analyzing the recipe):** Break down the instructions logically to determine what each step requires.

**Insight (reading intent):** Understand the recipe’s tone and interpret phrases that may be metaphorical or descriptive.

**Arcana (if magical ingredients or methods are mentioned):** Clarify any enchanted steps in the process.

## Outcome:

A successful roll grants advantage on later phases or negates penalties from misinterpretation.

Failing this phase adds a **-2 penalty** to one roll in the "Prepare Ingredients" or "Bake" phase, as the players are working with flawed assumptions.
