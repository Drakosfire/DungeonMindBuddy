### Tilly Tuffcrust

**Affiliation:** The Mirathorn Gazette
**Role:** Food Critic and Columnist for Tilly Tuffcrust’s Tastings
**Catchphrase:** “Keep your pies warm and your critics warmer!”

**Biography:
**Tilly Tuffcrust was born to a family of gnomish bakers in the bustling Docklands of Mirathorn. Her parents ran the beloved **Tuffcrust Bakery**, famed for its hearty loaves and cheerful atmosphere. From a young age, Tilly showed a sharp tongue and a sharper wit, delighting her family with her ability to charm (or scold) customers into buying more bread.

Though she trained in baking herself, Tilly’s real passion lay in storytelling, and her natural curiosity led her to the world of journalism. At first, her editor at the Gazette hesitated to let her focus on culinary writing—“Who wants to read about pies, Tilly?”—but her first column’s success silenced the doubters. Her mix of vibrant descriptions, humor, and heartfelt appreciation for food turned Tilly Tuffcrust’s Tastings into a beloved staple of Mirathorn’s cultural life.

Tilly has a knack for connecting with people, from bakers to market stall vendors, making her a cherished presence in the City Circle. She’s known to wield her pen like a double-edged rolling pin—her glowing praise can make a career, but her cutting critiques are legendary. Yet, despite her sharp words, Tilly writes with a genuine love for food and the stories it tells about those who make it.

## Personality:

**Cheerful and Witty:** Tilly has an infectious energy and a biting humor that keeps her columns lively and her readers engaged.

**Empathetic:** While she enjoys a bit of sass, Tilly genuinely cares about Mirathorn’s bakers and uses her platform to uplift deserving talent.

**Chaotic Creative:** Tilly loves flair and creativity in baking, often prioritizing a dish’s story or presentation over technical perfection.

**Signature Look:
**Tilly dresses in colorful, mismatched outfits adorned with quirky accessories, often resembling a confection herself. Her oversized spectacles and flour-dusted notebook are her trademarks.

### Harland Broadquill

**Affiliation:** The Mirathorn Observer
**Role:** Senior Culinary Critic
**Catchphrase:** “The craft of baking is too important for frivolity.”

**Biography:
**Harland Broadquill grew up in the austere halls of the **Eldyrwyld Culinary Conservatory**, an institution known for its exacting standards. As the youngest son of a prominent human merchant family, Harland was expected to pursue a “respectable” trade, but his fascination with the science of baking led him to a career in culinary critique.

Harland’s first major piece—a scathing takedown of a celebrated baker’s inconsistent soufflés—earned him a position at the Mirathorn Observer. Over the years, he built a reputation for his unflinching standards and no-nonsense style, becoming one of the city’s most feared critics. Harland approaches food as a craft and a science, dismissing “frivolous” trends like enchanted pastries or overly artistic presentations as distractions from true quality.

While his columns are respected for their insight, Harland is notorious for his uncompromising tone. He believes the role of a critic is to educate the public, even if it means sparing no one’s feelings. His rivalry with Tilly Tuffcrust is the stuff of legend, with their columns often serving as dueling manifestos of culinary philosophy.

## Personality:

**Rigid and Serious:** Harland has little patience for whimsy or charm, valuing substance and precision above all else.

**Perfectionist:** He has an encyclopedic knowledge of baking techniques and judges each dish against an impossibly high standard.

**Prideful:** Harland firmly believes he is the voice of reason in Mirathorn’s culinary world and often dismisses dissenting opinions (especially Tilly’s) as amateurish.

**Signature Look:
**Harland is always impeccably dressed in crisp, dark suits with a silver quill pin on his lapel. His sharp, angular features and perpetually furrowed brow make him look as though he’s constantly critiquing the world around him.

### Dynamic Between Tilly and Harland:

**Professional Rivalry:** Their opposing philosophies—Tilly’s love of flair and creativity versus Harland’s obsession with substance and precision—make them natural foils.

**Public Spats:** Their rivalry often spills into their columns, delighting readers who see their feud as part of the entertainment.

**Undercurrent of Respect:** While they publicly spar, both secretly admire the other’s dedication and passion. Harland may scoff at Tilly’s whimsy, but he knows she connects with her audience in ways he can’t. Tilly, meanwhile, respects Harland’s technical expertise, even if she finds him insufferably pompous.
