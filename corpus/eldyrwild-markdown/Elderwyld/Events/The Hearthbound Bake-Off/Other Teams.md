### The Kobold Bakers – The Cinderbite Collective

**Bakery Name:** **The Cinderbite Collective
**Nestled in the heart of Mirathorn’s industrial district, The Cinderbite Collective is a small but fiercely popular bakery run by kobold artisans. Known for their innovative use of heat and fire (with a touch of chaos), their pastries are bold, smoky, and unforgettable. Their unique style has drawn customers from all over the city, proving that great flavor knows no bounds of culture or race.

### The Kobold Team

#### 1. Grizzle Cindersnap

**Role:** The Leader

**Personality:** Grizzle is the brains behind The Cinderbite Collective. Calculating and precise, he’s obsessed with innovation and sees baking as an extension of the forge—a craft to be mastered through science and discipline. Though serious and prone to over-planning, he has a soft spot for his team and their shared love of fire-roasted treats.

**Specialty:** **Flame-Seared Pies** – Grizzle’s pies are famous for their caramelized crusts and perfectly smoky fillings.

#### 2. Zivvy Sparksnout

**Role:** The Firestarter

**Personality:** Zivvy is the team’s wildcard, an enthusiastic and slightly chaotic kobold who can’t resist playing with fire. She often throws in unexpected ingredients or experiments with flamboyant baking techniques, leading to either genius creations or spectacular failures. Her boundless energy keeps the team motivated, even if she occasionally singes an eyebrow.

**Specialty:** **Spicy Pastries** – Zivvy’s signature treats include fiery turnovers and spicy pepper-laced biscuits that pack a delightful punch.

#### 3. Vakkle Emberwhisk

**Role:** The Tactician

**Personality:** Quiet and methodical, Vakkle is the team’s logistics expert and cooling presence. He has an eye for detail, an impeccable sense of timing, and an encyclopedic knowledge of ingredients. He’s the one who reins in Zivvy’s chaos and keeps Grizzle from overcomplicating their recipes.

**Specialty:** **Smoky Soufflés** – Vakkle is known for creating impossibly light soufflés with a distinct, wood-fired flavor.

#### 4. Pikka Ashglaze

**Role:** The Finisher

**Personality:** Pikka is the artist of the group, with a delicate touch and an eye for presentation. Friendly and optimistic, she takes great pride in creating desserts that look as good as they taste. She often talks to her creations as she decorates them, claiming it helps “bring out their personality.”

**Specialty:** **Charcoal-Glazed Cookies** – Pikka’s cookies are famous for their glossy black glaze and intricate patterns.

### Team Dynamics

**Strengths:** Their mastery of heat-based baking gives them an edge in creating bold, flavorful dishes with a unique twist. They also work like clockwork, combining Grizzle’s leadership, Vakkle’s precision, Zivvy’s creative spark, and Pikka’s artistry.

**Weaknesses:** Their reliance on fire can backfire (literally), and their bold flavors sometimes overpower more delicate palettes. Zivvy’s chaotic tendencies occasionally derail their plans.

### The Hearth and Horizon Bakers

**Team Name:** **The Hearth and Horizon Bakers
**This eclectic team came together through shared ambition and an unshakable passion for culinary artistry. Each member brings their unique background and style to the table, creating a blend of tradition, innovation, and raw talent. They hail from different corners of Mirathorn and represent the city's melting pot spirit.

### The Hearth and Horizon Team

#### 1. Torrik Emberwrought

**Race:** Dragonborn (Bronze)
**Role:** The Foundation

**Personality:** Torrik is a stoic and dependable presence, often serving as the team’s anchor when tensions rise. A former blacksmith who turned to baking after an injury ended his career, he treats dough like molten metal, working it with precision and care. He takes pride in perfecting the basics and believes in baking as both art and therapy.

**Specialty:** **Hearthbreads and Rustic Loaves** – Torrik’s bread is hearty, with golden crusts and the perfect chew, often using ingredients that pay homage to his smithing roots (like smoky spices or metallic accents).

#### 2. Wrenna Nibbletop

**Race:** Gnome
**Role:** The Innovator

**Personality:** Wrenna is the team’s whimsical genius, brimming with curiosity and a knack for tinkering with recipes. Always carrying a pouch of unusual ingredients and miniature tools, she constantly experiments with magical infusions and new flavor combinations. Her enthusiasm is infectious, though her inventions occasionally veer into the bizarre.

**Specialty:** **Enchanted Mini-Treats** – Wrenna creates small, intricate desserts like glowing macarons or animated marzipan figurines.

#### 3. Elivien “Eli” Thornwillow

**Race:** Half-Elf
**Role:** The Artist

**Personality:** Elivien is soft-spoken but deeply passionate about creating food that tells a story. Trained as an illustrator before becoming a baker, they approach each dish like a canvas, crafting visuals that captivate the eye and evoke emotion. They are the team’s mediator, keeping the others focused and harmonious.

**Specialty:** **Floral Desserts** – Eli’s creations often feature edible flowers, delicate sugar sculptures, and pastel colors, making their bakes as beautiful as they are delicious.

#### 4. Corwin Bellfield

**Race:** Human
**Role:** The Showman

**Personality:** Corwin is the face of the team—charismatic, dramatic, and always ready with a quip or a smile. A former traveling actor, Corwin has a flair for presentation and knows how to sell their team’s vision to the judges and crowd. While not the most skilled baker, he often pulls through in moments of crisis with quick thinking and unorthodox solutions.

**Specialty:** **Theatrics and Plating** – Corwin excels in creating grand, over-the-top displays that wow audiences, even if the flavors sometimes play second fiddle.

### Team Dynamics

## Strengths:

A perfect mix of talents: Torrik’s fundamentals, Wrenna’s inventiveness, Eli’s artistry, and Corwin’s flair.

They thrive in **Presentation** and **Shmooze** phases, often dazzling judges with their creativity and charm.

## Weaknesses:

They can struggle with execution when Wrenna’s experiments go awry or when Corwin overcomplicates things for dramatic effect.

Their teamwork sometimes suffers from miscommunication, as Torrik and Eli prefer quiet focus while Corwin and Wrenna thrive in chaos.

### Team Signature Dynamic:

Their bakes reflect their mixed heritage and approach, often combining rustic, artisanal foundations with whimsical or artistic flourishes. For example, a classic fruit tart might feature edible glowing sugar birds or cascading caramel ribbons shaped like vines.
