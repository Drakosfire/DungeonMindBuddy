# 🎲 Festival Crafting Chaos Table (d20)

### Mechanics Framework

**Entry Fee**: 2 sp (covers materials).

**Participation**: Choose a skill (Arcana, Sleight of Hand, Performance, etc.) that makes sense for your approach.

**DC**: 12–15 (easy booths DC 12, more volatile ones DC 15).

**Outcomes**:

**Success**: Crafting works, draw attention, earn a quirky trinket.

**Failure**: Mishap! Something funny, chaotic, or briefly inconvenient (never lethal).

**Critical Success/Failure**: Double the prize / extra chaos.

## Quick Use Notes

Drop **party-impact references** organically via quips (barn rescue, rooftop charm, Wolf’s Manor deed, Unity Dance, deputization).

For **Bonogo’s stalker thread**, seed clues at #7, #8, #18 (ink, paper, lantern dedications), and any crowded booth—then cut to a subtle chase among stalls.

For **Caelynn’s father**, let #5 Thalos Veyren invite a private follow-up (“Bring tea. Bring storms.”).

## 1) Potion Brewing Booth — Belroth “Fizzskin” Marren (Goblin, 41)

**Booth**: Copper alembics puffing pastel steam; a chalkboard of wild flavors (“Lavender Lightning,” “Marmalade Moon”).
 **Crowd**: Thick crowd of teens daring each other; city watch off to the side “just in case.” Popular because it’s messy and photogenic.
 **Fail**: Sticky foam blast; bubbly coat for 1 hour.
 **Prizes**: Bottle of Endless Bubbles • Potion of Minor Hiccups • Vial of Living Slime
 **Quip**: “Questionable Company! If you can brew a flood-saving ice raft from thin air, surely you can keep bubbles in a bottle.”

## 2) Glassblower’s Flame — Liora Cindervain (Fire Genasi, 28)

**Booth**: A roaring kiln under a jeweler’s awning; rods of molten glass like liquid sunsets.
 **Crowd**: Quiet, respectful onlookers—mesmerized but cautious. Rumor says last year a phoenix figurine actually flew.
 **Fail**: Shatter—1 hp nicked fingers.
 **Prizes**: Glass Cat Figurine • Prism Bauble • Glass Bat
 **Quip**: “You shaped storms into art on a rooftop. Let’s see if fire behaves any better for you.”
