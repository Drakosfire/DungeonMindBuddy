## 🥧 9:00 AM – The Great Bake-Off: Preliminary Rounds

**Location:** Market Plaza – three enormous enchanted baking tents, each one a riot of color, smells, and magical mishaps.

### 💫 Magical Flavor:

Ovens whisper helpful hints… or snide insults.

Enchanted measuring spoons that try to "help" by correcting recipes.

Weather-like enchantments in each tent: Wind Tent, Fire Tent, Fog Tent

### 🧑‍🍳 Format:

**3-Round Challenge**:

**Pies of Precision** (Dex-based)
 – Roll for Sleight of Hand to decorate during minor wind spells.

**Pastry Peril** (Concentration)
 – Ingredients animate: sugar sprites, jam slimes.

**Mystery Ingredient Round
** – Incorporate a glowing fruit (from the Veiled Fen). Tastes change per baker!

### 🎭 NPC Highlights:

**Merrin Puddinfoot** – Halfling head judge, cheerful but intense, wears a crown made of whisks.

**“Chef Ash”** – Tiefling elemental cook who uses fire spells to flambé mid-air.

**Rilfa & Grolg** – Goblin couple using old family fungus recipes, very competitive.

### 🧩 Hook:

One contestant vanishes between rounds—only a frosting smear and a faint whisper left behind. The trail leads toward a shadowy stall.

## 🔨 11:00 AM – Weapon Forging Demonstration

**Location:** Blacksmith’s Corner in the Foundry Quarter. Open-air forge surrounded by admiring onlookers.

### 💫 Magical Flavor:

Forgehammers hum with elemental resonance (air, earth, fire).

Magical anvil occasionally lets out a "gong" when impressed.

Spectral ancestors of blacksmiths watch silently from the edges of the forge fire.

### ⚒️ Activity:

**Public Demonstration:** Master smiths forge ceremonial weapons.

## PCs can participate:

1-hour forge time

Roll with **Smith’s Tools (or Arcana)** DC 15 to create a +1 weapon hilt or enchanted pendant.

### 🧑‍🏭 NPC Highlights:

**Torrin Flamescale** – Dragonborn councilmember, runs the forge. Gruff, deeply proud.

**Sahra Flintaxe** – Half-orc artisan, mentors any PC willing to try.

**Milo Wrenchfast** – Gnome who’s made a “fully mechanized” dagger. It wiggles.

### 🧩 Hook:

One weapon bears **ancient Abyssal runes** from before the Empire’s founding. Stormspire mages nearby take notice—and start whispering about forbidden crafts.

## 🤸 1:00 PM – Acrobatic Extravaganza

**Location:** Glimmering Globe – outdoor stage and high-wired performance space beside the lake.

### 💫 Magical Flavor:

Trapezes float without ropes.

Springboots enchanted for **double-height leaps**.

Illusion trapeze artists blink in and out of visibility.

Music synched to movement via magical symphony nodes.

### 🎟️ Format:

Open-call performances, duets and solo stunts.

Contestants judged by motion-synced illusions: colors swirl based on crowd reaction.

### 🧑‍🎤 NPC Highlights:

**Niri Nimblecloak** – Half-elf ringmaster, covered in bells and sashes, very encouraging.

**The Five Feets** – Goblin quintuplets on a single tightrope. Acrobatics prodigies.

**Spectro the Swirling** – Illusionist who paints rainbows midair.

### 🧩 Bonogo Hook:

**Bubbles the Float Goat** shows up! A kid rides her, doing flips.

Niri tries to recruit Bonogo or Ogonob to join the act for the finale—“The Flying Gritspike!”

Backstage illusion booth might be hiding someone tailing the party.

## 💃 6:00 PM – Unity Dance Rehearsal at the Community Hall

### 🌟 Location:

A round, acoustically-tuned wooden structure near the heart of Mirathorn, strung with colored lanterns and woven silk banners representing every major heritage in the city. Windows overlook the lake, casting the rehearsal space in golden evening light.

### 🎶 Purpose:

A symbolic **multi-racial, multi-tradition dance** meant to open the final night’s grand celebration.

The dance combines **elvish flow**, **dwarven stomp patterns**, **draconic war cadence**, and even **bugbear tribal spins**, taught by rotating instructors.

Outsiders are **encouraged** to participate. It’s meant to look chaotic, but feel unified.

### 🧑‍🏫 NPCs Involved:

**Master Miraen Tallsway** (Half-Elf choreographer): Overwhelmed but passionate. She believes dance builds peace.

**“Cud”** (a stoic bugbear monk): Leads the rhythm section using drums and body percussion. Terrifyingly gentle.

**Twilla Softbloom** (halfling movement mage): Uses minor levitation and illusion to help dancers visualize their steps.

### 🌈 Magical Elements:

Floor runes **glow** when a step is hit in perfect time, creating pulses of light and sound.

Missteps cause small **phantom echoes**—harmless but dramatic misfires.

Caelynn’s panflute, if played, begins to harmonize with the ambient rhythm—a sign of **old magic recognizing shared purpose.
**

### 🧩 Hooks:

The pattern of the dance hints at an **ancient fey circle**—subtle geomancy in motion.

A dancer goes into **a minor trance mid-step**, muttering something about “the mirror turning inward.”

One of the instructors might be **a disguised Shepherd’s Flock spy**, gauging the crowd.

## 🪄 8:00 PM – Magicians’ Midnight Show at Stormspire Academy

### 🏛 Location:

The **Grand Lecture Spire** of the Stormspire Academy—normally reserved for state-level magic ceremonies—transformed into a **circular auditorium** with shifting platforms, illusions flowing like curtains, and multi-planar sound systems.

### 🎭 Vibe:

Think magical science fair meets theater festival.

Audience sits in rotating arcane chairs that float gently as the show changes.

Invitations were required… or clever infiltration.

### 🎩 Performers:

A mix of prodigies, theorists, and attention-seekers.

**Professor Quen Vellum** – Summons holograms of lost cities using temporal echo.

**Ixy the Impossible** – Goblin illusionist who turns herself inside out in a comedic clockwork trick.

**Rivoan Taleth** – Elven planar scholar; presents a portal to a blank realm he swears was once home to a **lost moon**.

### 🌌 The Showstopper:

Near midnight, a **new instructor** named **Magistra Nyaleen**, unknown to most, unveils a spell involving “sympathetic resonance” between mirrors, memory, and spell echoes.

It **goes wrong.
**

A **rift opens** briefly—just a slice of nonspace—but visible within is **Maelthor’s realm**: flickers of flesh runes, violet-black flame, and reversed music.

Glyphs surge into the auditorium before mages **collaborate to “skyweave”** a magical seal.

### 🧩 Fallout & Hooks:

Attuned PCs can roll **Arcana or Insight** to sense this wasn’t an accident—it was **sabotaged**.

**Elara Swiftwind** and **Professor Zilla Cinderbranch** will be called in to “clean” the anomaly.

A **mirror** near the exit remains **slightly warped**. It reflects things… a few seconds too late.
