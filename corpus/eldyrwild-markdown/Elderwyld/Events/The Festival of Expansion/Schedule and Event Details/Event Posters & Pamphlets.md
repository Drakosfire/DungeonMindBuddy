### Festival of Expansion - Event Posters & Pamphlets

Each event has an accompanying **vibrant pamphlet, poster, or banner** scattered around the city. These are posted in **shop windows, market stalls, tavern walls, and notice boards**, with some even **handed out by street criers or magically projected onto fabric banners by minor illusion charms**.

## Day 1: The Grand Opening

### 📜 “The Great BBQ – A Feast for All!” (All Day, Various Locations)

**A massive, illustrated wooden sign** outside butcher shops, taverns, and the city square. The words **"SAVOR THE FLAME-KISSED MEATS OF MIRATHORN!"** are emblazoned in golden lettering over a stylized **roaring firepit with a boar turning on a spit**. A **list of competing pitmasters** is posted below, and the scent of sizzling meat fills the streets.

**📍 Locations:** The Copper & Quartz Inn, The Docklands, Market Plaza, The Out-Town Food Stalls

### 📜 “The Founders’ Parade – March Through History!” (Morning, Citywide)

**A massive, painted cloth banner** draped across the **Frontier Gate** proclaims:
**“BEHOLD THE HEROES WHO BUILT MIRATHORN!”
**A vivid illustration of past explorers and city leaders on horseback, airships, and marching troops. Smaller **silk flyers** depict detailed **float designs** and lists of honored citizens participating in the procession.

**📍 Route:** Frontier Gate → Market Plaza → City Circle

### 📜 “The Grand Melee! Step Into the Colosseum!” (Afternoon, The Colosseum of the Storm)

**A bold red poster** featuring two armored warriors clashing mid-air, swords sparking with magic. The words **"TEST YOUR STRENGTH – PROVE YOUR METTLE!"** are inscribed in **steely, embossed script**, with **"NON-LETHAL – VICTORY BRINGS HONOR AND PRIZES!"** in smaller gold ink. There is a **list of matchups**, but **mystery challengers remain unlisted**…

**📍 Location:** The Colosseum of the Storm, near the Forge & Fire District

### 📜 “Mage Duel Qualifiers – Witness the Arcane Elite!” (Afternoon, Arcane Arena)

A **floating, shimmering illusion** hovers over the **Stormspire Academy**, showing **previous duels frozen in time**—bolts of lightning colliding, a mage throwing up a brilliant barrier of force. The pamphlet handed out by apprentices reads:
**"Magic Unleashed! Will You Be Crowned Champion?"
Rules and Entry Fee: Free for Mirathorn Citizens
**Spectators Welcome!

**📍 Location:** Arcane Arena, near Stormspire Academy

### 📜 “Fireworks Over Lake Mirathorn – A Night of Wonder!” (Evening, Waterfront)

**Hanging lanterns** near the **docks and central square** display small **animated illusions** of colorful **explosions in the night sky**. The poster’s elegant script reads:
**"LOOK TO THE SKY—THE NIGHT BURNS BRIGHT!"
**Best views from the Docks, Mirathorn’s Walls, and the Copper & Quartz Balcony!

**📍 Locations:** Docklands, Lake Mirathorn

## Day 2: Feasts & Forges

### 📜 “The Great Bake-Off – Who is Mirathorn’s Sweetest Genius?” (Morning, Market Plaza)

**Delightful, hand-painted wooden signs** outside bakeries, each with **a different pie or cake enchanted to release a delicious aroma**. A parchment flyer lists:
**"Three Rounds! Pies, Pastries, & The Grand Secret Challenge!"**

**📍 Location:** Market Plaza

### 📜 “Trials of the Shield – Do You Have What It Takes?” (Afternoon, The Colosseum of the Storm)

A **bronze-plated signpost** featuring an **engraved shield cracked down the center, reforged with golden filigree**. Below, **bold text reads**:
**"A test of ENDURANCE, TACTICS, and LOYALTY!"
**Open to all! Teams of 3-5 encouraged. A proving ground for Mirathorn’s future defenders!

**📍 Location:** The Colosseum of the Storm

### 📜 “The Magicians’ Midnight Show – Mysteries & Marvels Await!” (Evening, Stormspire Academy)

**A dark, velvet poster** depicting a **robed figure summoning glowing runes**, their eyes shining with unearthly light. The words seem to **shimmer and rearrange when read**, revealing:
**"A Night of Wonders – Entry by Invitation or Bravery!"**

**📍 Location:** Stormspire Academy

## Day 3: Legends & Lights

### 📜 “Skyship Races – Speed, Strategy, and Skyborne Glory!” (Morning & Afternoon, The Aerial Course)

Large **wooden placards** with **miniature wind-powered skyship models** bobbing along their surface. Below, a bold proclamation:
**"The Fastest Ships in Mirathorn Battle for the Sky!"
**Betting booths open in the Docklands and Market Plaza!

**📍 Locations:** The Aerial Course, visible from the city walls

### 📜 “The Grand Coliseum Championship – Who Will Stand Victorious?” (Afternoon, The Colosseum of the Storm)

**An enormous tournament board** hung outside the **Colosseum gates**, displaying combatants’ names and their **imposing, enchanted portraits**.
A **mysterious section of the bracket is covered in black cloth**, with the words:
**"A CHALLENGER UNKNOWN—WHO WILL FACE THEM?"**

**📍 Location:** The Colosseum of the Storm

## Day 4: Champions’ Day

### 📜 “The Enchanted Lights Display – A Dance of Magic and Color” (Evening, Central Park)

**A translucent silk banner**, enchanted so that **twinkling lights ripple across it**, forming different animal and elemental shapes. Below, an elegant script reads:
**"Behold Mirathorn’s Brightest Night!"**

**📍 Location:** Central Park

## Day 5: The Grand Conclusion

### 📜 “The Ritual of the Nameless Goddess – A Sacred Calling” (Afternoon, Temple of Aspitome)

**A solemn, hand-stitched tapestry** draped outside the **Temple of Aspitome**, woven with **silver thread forming a faceless figure holding outstretched hands**. The bottom reads:
**"For those lost, for those remembered. Step forward if you dare."**

**📍 Location:** Temple of Aspitome

### 📜 “The Grand Finale Fireworks – Let the Festival End in Splendor!” (Evening, Citywide)

A **folded parchment program** details the **finale’s grandest displays**, with a **hand-drawn map marking ideal viewing spots**. A bold note at the bottom reads:
**"A LASTING MEMORY, A NEW TOMORROW!"**

**📍 Locations:** City Walls, Lake Mirathorn, Copper & Quartz Balcony
