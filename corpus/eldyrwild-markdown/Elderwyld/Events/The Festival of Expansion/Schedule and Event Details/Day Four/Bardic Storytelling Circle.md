# 🎤 Bardic Storytelling Circle at the Glimmering Globe

### The Setting

**Visuals:** The Globe’s crystal dome scatters prismatic starlight overhead. Every clap, sigh, or chuckle refracts into the air like auroras.

**Sounds:** Whispering anticipation before each story; laughter echoing too long, like bells; gasps rippling with faint chimes.

**Smells:** Sweet etherfoam ale from Pippa’s kegs, spiced honey-nuts, and faint incense of starflower resin.

**Texture:** Cushions strewn in concentric rings; benches creak; the oak “Seat of Echoes” at the center hums faintly, vibrating like a tuning fork when touched.

The circle feels alive. Failures leave “echo-stains”—ghostly snippets of the performer’s flub that play back faintly for minutes. Success resonates as literal starlight weaving through the dome.

## Mechanics Framework

### 🎲 Core Bard Rolls

**Performance (Primary):** Delivery of the tale—voice, music, drama.

**Persuasion:** Convince the crowd of truth, stir hearts.

**Deception:** Sell exaggerations or lies as myth.

**Insight:** Mid-performance adjustment—spotting whether children are bored, or the warriors want bloodier glory.

**History/Arcana/Religion:** For weaving lore, divine allusions, or clever allegories. Adds situational bonuses if rolled well.

## DCs (scalable by table mood):

Easy crowd: DC 12 (kids, ale-happy commoners).

Mixed audience: DC 15.

Harsh judges: DC 18 (rival bards, scholars, Dustwalker cult plants).

### 🎨 Mood Resonance (The Globe Reacts)

**Laughter:** Silver ripples chase across the dome.

**Tears:** Deep violet swells like twilight.

**Triumph:** Gold bursts overhead, raining harmless sparks.

**Tension/Fear:** Lights dim, whispers echo.

**Failure:** Echo-stains repeat the botched line with mocking distortion.

Players can lean into or subvert these reactions for dramatic effect.
