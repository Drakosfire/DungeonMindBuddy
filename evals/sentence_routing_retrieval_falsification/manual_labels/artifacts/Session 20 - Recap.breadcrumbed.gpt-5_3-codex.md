# Session 20 Recap

Back out near [Location][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/edge_of_the_forest/] the forest [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/], [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/], [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] and [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/thrin/] continue to battle the swarm of red gnats. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/thrin/], desperate to see something go in their favor, takes two more shots with his bow. The first misses, but the second does enough damage to knock a group of insects out of the swarm.Then the swarm turns and envelopes [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/], the rest of the team can only see her dim shadow under all the flying insects. Thanks to her high constitution, she is able to withstand the stinging and only take minor damage. 

[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/] unleashes two Eldritch Blasts, the first missing and the second one dealing enough damage to knock another cluster out of the swarm. They Misty Step away and add some temporary health to themselves. Now that the swarm is around [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/], [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] is able to use her scimitar and short sword for a series of attacks, landing 4 hits on the swarm. She also uses Zephyr Strike to increase her movement and dash away. Finally [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] is able to duck out of the swarm, then facing them, casts a powerful Thunderwave spell, splitting the swarm in two and pushing it back 10 feet. As [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/thrin/] and [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] move back the swarm finally gives up and heads back into the forest. [Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/] The team decides they have enough “testing” and should head back to tell the others what they discovered.

Back in [Location][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/town/], [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] is being guided by [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] down an alley to a [NewHubCandidate][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/half_burned_warehouse/] half burned building. According to [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] this is where they will find [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stacey/]. As soon as they step inside the large warehouse they find a group of children formed into two groups with what appear to be the leaders arguing in the middle. One of them is [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stacey/], the bugbear girl they are looking for. She is in a heated argument with the other children who are accusing her of continuing to be too bossy. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] whispers into [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/]'s ear, “you know what to do” then sends him over. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stacey/] immediately gets upset at seeing [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] and tells him that he can’t play with them, but [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] is undeterred. He sticks his hand out and demands his gold back, convinced that she is the one that stole it. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] takes a quick glance at [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] before he pulls out the dart and threatens her with it. Alarmed, she yells at [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] and then throws her gold pouch at him before storming out the door. 

[NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] is so happy with the outcome that he runs out the door, racing off to tell his mom the good news. The other kids now ask [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] to play with them instead. He agrees to play hide and seek as long as the kids hide first. He counts to 20 then leaves the building without making a sound. He is intent on following [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stacey/] and catches up to her in the next alley. After seeing no one else around, he quickly grabs her and puts a knife to her throat. He tells her to stop bullying [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] or he will come back, then dashes into the shadows. Clearly shaken, [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stacey/] runs home. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] makes his way back to the group out in the field.

As the group approaches the preparations happening in the field, they find [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/stafl/] singing and directing the workers from a makeshift throne of barrels on the back of a wagon. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/], [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] and [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] relay their findings from their tests: do not attack the trees directly, but the forest should respond to changes on the ground. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] takes up a supervisor role and is observing the work when he hears footsteps quickly approaching behind him. He is able to dodge a slap from a furious Bugbear woman. Even with the missed slap, she gets up into [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/]’s face and begins to berate him for encouraging [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/stuart/] to harass her daughter, causing many of the workers to stop and watch. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] tries to shift the situation and asks why she is not helping with the ditches and should get to work instead. This, of course, only makes her more furious. 

She stands up to her full Bugbear height and begins to scream at him. She finds just the right words to cut deep: he smells like a circus animal. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/stafl/] takes a look around at the crowd to gauge their reactions. A farmer whispers to him that [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/stafl/] better step in and help because [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/marla/] is not someone to mess with. They reveal that she is in charge of the workers in town and she means business. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] continues to escalate the situation, telling [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/marla/] he sees where her daughter gets her attitude from. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/marla/] then grapples [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/] and is about to do much worse when [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] comes to the rescue. Using her bracelet she is able to diffuse the aggression and get everyone back to work, united against the approaching forest. [Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/] The rest of the Questionable Company decide to take a short rest as their final preparation.

[Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/] Questionable Company, along with the townsfolk, watch as the forest comes within range of their plan. As the forest finally reaches the fortifications, the fires are lit in the ditches [NewHubCandidate][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/fortified_ditches/]. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/marla/] approaches [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] and asks her how she should deal with [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/bonogo/], but [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/] quickly intervenes, letting her, and the town, know that the [Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/] Questionable Company is leaving town to continue their journey. And now that the danger has passed [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] asks the [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/mayor/] mayor about [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/], but finds out that they have never heard of her.

After a brief pause she is connected with the familiar voice of [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/sara/], one half of the operators in [Location][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/mirathorn/]. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] relays her request and [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/sara/] calls [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/]. She tells [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] that all she could hear was mumbling about the forest leaving and that something strange happened to the time. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/sara/] then connects [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] directly to [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/] who is relieved and overjoyed that the group is ok and that they took care of the forest. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] wants to know if she is ok and where she is. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/] tells her that she can’t remember much after the group left, only that she decided to go around the forest and she could smell meat while trying to sleep. She is exhausted and disoriented. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] tells her to stop where she is and make a camp and rest, [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] will lead the team to her.

Using her extensive tracking skills, [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] is able to estimate the distance and direction that [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/] may have traveled. [Party][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Parties/questionable_company/] The group sets off from the town, [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/] keeping a close eye on [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/thrin/]. Thirty minutes later they come across an unusual sight: a wagon partly unloaded and horses wandering around a stack of crates. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] approaches the makeshift shelter and hears mumbling from inside. She finds [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/] drawing in the dirt. She says it is a tower where the voices are coming from and she knows where it is [NewHubCandidate][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/mysterious_tower/]. She quickly begins to make the antidote from the tea in her bag. While the tea is being prepared, [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] takes a closer look at the drawing. It appears to be a top-down blueprint of a tower and is very well done. Finally, after drinking the tea, [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/] comes out of the spell. She is very confused, wondering where she is and how she got there. All that she is able to remember is voices in the dark after the group left into the forest. 

Back outside, [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] is rounding up the horses and making sure they are properly taken care of. She can see that the storm is still building in the distance, but will soon be upon the camp. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/stafl/] starts sorting through the provisions, convinced that someone snuck in the tainted meat before leaving [Location][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Locations/mirathorn/]. With a huge sigh of relief he finds the bacon untouched, however the other crates of jerky did not fare as well. Mixed in with the meat is cleverly disguised tainted meat. Not being able to separate the good from the bad, the whole thing will have to be burned. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] calls [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/sara/] to tell her the good news about [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/lysandra/], and bad news about the tainted meat. [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/sara/] is very concerned about who she can now trust in the city. She transfers [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] to [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/professor_tealeaf/], but she doesn't pick up.

[PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/caelynn/] continues to wait on the line for an answer from [NPC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/NPCs/professor_tealeaf/] as the rest of the group set up a proper camp for a rest. They need to set up a shelter for the animals and themselves as it is clear the storm is approaching and [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/karesmine/] can see it is bringing the magical shimmering rain. [PC][Longmont Campaign/Campaign 2/Player Characters/ephanna/] plans to create a disguise and go back to town for new supplies, but for the moment the group is settling in for a rest around the camp.
