Shepherd's Flock meat threats and Mireward siege monsters for the S23 north-gate pressure stack. These are reference tools, not proof of what has appeared in play.

Recommended S23 flank mix Start with skirmish pressure at the road bend, then commit the main body if the gate/road choice stalls: 2x Sewer Meat Creature (CR 3), 1x Corrupted Meat Golem (CR 3), with Meat Worms or an Aberrant Meatwing for movement pressure. If the gate / cure line stabilizes too cleanly, reveal the Tripod Null-Calf (CR 5). Hold the Latch-Harrow (CR 8) for later-wave breach pressure or a hard escalation.

Quick Picks

Main body

Use when the horde reaches the apron.

2x Sewer Meat Creature + 1x Corrupted Meat Golem. Fleshborn Hybrid is a shock-troop swap if you want grapples over gas.

Skirmish pressure

Use before the main body rounds the bend.

Meat Worms pull attention to civilians and cart wheels; Aberrant Meatwing pressures clustered helpers with charm and flight.

Alien scout reveal

Use when the party thinks it understands the fight.

Tripod Null-Calf pins carts / rope lines, rotates facing, and can mark the north gate for later siege adaptation.

Later-wave breach monster

Use as a north-gate clock crisis.

Latch-Harrow converts structure attacks into Breach Clock progress; redirect to Bell/Shrine, tithe barn, or ferry chain only if needed.
