# C2S23 North Gate Callout Runbook

This is table-facing prep for the Mireward north-gate opening. It is not a post-session recap and should not be cited as past play until narrated at the table.

## Opening beat

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> The southern road gives way to the dark wall of Mireward. Lanterns move along the parapet. Before anyone on the wall finishes deciding what to do with you, a second alarm rises from the north end of town — not a bell, not yet. Voices. Names. A count shouted badly, then shouted again.

> [!GM-NOTE]
> Start with the party outside town at the south approach. Lysandro should be the human accelerant: he recognizes the danger faster than the authorities can reach consensus and pushes the town into motion.

## North-gate pressure

> [!WARNING]
> The refugee column is already at the north apron. The meat flank is 3–8 minutes behind them. Do not let the table treat this as a calm intake scene.

> [!RULES]
> Track the opening crisis as three visible pressures: **Gate**, **Civilians**, and **Cure Line**. If the party delays, advance the road/flank pressure before adding more exposition.

## Glassy civilians

> [!GM-NOTE]
> The glassy civilians are infected/dreaming/signal-receiving, not default enemies. Their behavior should be frightening but curable.

> [!RULES]
> Use the Cure Line as a shared 0–6 clock. The question is whether the party protects the cure process while the gate and road pressure worsen.

## Escalation

> [!WARNING]
> If the party stabilizes too easily, bring the Tripod Null-Calf on-screen as geometry-breaking pressure. Hold the Latch-Harrow as later-wave breach pressure unless the session needs a hard cliffhanger.

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> For a breath, the north road noise falls into a wet, rhythmic dragging. Then a shape steps where no shape should fit — three limbs, wrong angles, its weight making the mud remember it too slowly.
