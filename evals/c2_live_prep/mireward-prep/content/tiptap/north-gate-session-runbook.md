# C2S23 Mireward Reach North Gate Runbook

## At-table intent

This runbook is now live from the ingested Session 23 context. The table-facing settlement name is **Mireward Reach**; the stable corpus route still uses `Mireward/`. Keep the table on immediate choices: finish the first wave, let the town breathe just long enough to matter, then reveal that the second wave is built to tear down walls.

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> The north road is a wound full of bodies, wagons, and wet gray fog. Bells hammer behind the gate while the refugee line surges toward any hand that looks like authority.

## Opening frame

> [!GM-NOTE]
> Keep this scene about triage: gate safety, civilian crush, and whether Lysandro weaponizes panic. Do not solve the whole siege here.

If the party turns toward the argument, foreground [Lysandro Ironveil](#dmb-ref:npc:lysandro-ironveil) and let him demand that the north gate close before the infected can reach the line. Use the ingested Session 23 memory as the active source: the current fight is already underway and Caelynn's lightning bolt is the table's next flash point.

## Table start checklist

- Point to the gate lever, the refugee crush, and the cure line before anyone debates policy.
- Ask who takes command, who watches Lysandro, and who looks for infected civilians.
- Put one named civilian or family in the line so the choice has a face.
- Keep [Gate Dilemma d12](#dmb-ref:roll-table:gate-dilemma-d12), [Sewer Meat Creature](#dmb-ref:statblock:sewer-meat-creature), and [North Gate Combat](#dmb-action:combat:north-gate-combat) one click away.

## First player prompt

"The gate captain looks past rank and panic and fixes on you. Who do you let through first, and who do you order to wait?"

Follow immediately with: "Which pressure are you watching: the gate, the civilians, or the cure line?"

## First five minutes

- Ask which PC takes the gate, the crowd, and the cure line.
- Show one infected refugee as a person before the threat becomes a mass.
- If the line breaks, roll or choose from [Gate Dilemma d12](#dmb-ref:roll-table:gate-dilemma-d12).
- If Lysandro gains the crowd, make his demand concrete: close the gate, separate the marked, or hand authority to Ironveil soldiers.

## Pressure states

> [!RULES]
> Use visible pressures instead of hidden timers: Gate, Civilians, Cure Line. Advance one whenever the table stalls, chooses a costly success, ignores a pressure, or lets Lysandro define the scene.

> [!WARNING]
> The meat flank is minutes behind the refugees. Do not let the opening become a static council debate.

### Gate clock

- 0: Gate holds, guards are listening.
- 1: Bar chains strain; one wagon jams the approach.
- 2: Guards choose sides unless a PC gives a clear order.
- 3: Gate line breaks; pivot toward [North Gate Combat](#dmb-action:combat:north-gate-combat) or a desperate closure scene.

### Civilian crush clock

- 0: Crowd is frightened but responsive.
- 1: A child or elder falls near the wheels.
- 2: The crowd surges against any visible authority.
- 3: Panic injury or trampling forces triage before tactics.

### Cure line clock

- 0: The cure line can still screen and separate.
- 1: Marked refugees hide symptoms to stay with family.
- 2: A false accusation or Ironveil shove turns the line hostile.
- 3: Infected reach the press of bodies; bring the meat threat forward.

## Decision fork

- If they talk, make Lysandro name a specific cost for keeping the gate open.
- If they fight, bring in the [Sewer Meat Creature](#dmb-ref:statblock:sewer-meat-creature) and pivot toward [North Gate Combat](#dmb-action:combat:north-gate-combat).
- If they flee or split, cut between the gate lever, the crush, and the first scream behind the wagons.

## If the party chooses closure

Let closure buy safety at a visible human cost. Ask who is sealed outside, who witnesses it, and what Lysandro claims the party just proved.

## If the party chooses triage

Make triage active: screen, split the line, assign guards, and spend social capital. Reward clear roles by holding one pressure steady while another advances.

## If the party chooses violence

Do not treat violence as failure. Let it answer one problem and worsen another: the crowd scatters, the gate loses order, or Lysandro names the party as the new threat.

## What to say when they stall

"While you weigh it, the line moves without permission. A wagon wheel cracks. Someone screams that the gray is already inside the gate. Which pressure do you answer first?"

## Exit ramps into combat / council / chase

- Combat: the meat flank hits the last wagon or the gate line breaks; open [North Gate Combat](#dmb-action:combat:north-gate-combat).
- Council: the party stabilizes all three pressures and forces Lysandro to negotiate in public.
- Chase: a marked refugee, Ironveil courier, or meat-touched civilian bolts through the wrong opening.

### First-wave end triggers

Pick one as soon as the fight has peaked:

| Trigger | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| **Lightning breaks the line** | Meatwings scatter, one tripod staggers, and the surviving flank starts dragging itself backward toward the dark. |
| **Wall discipline holds** | Nera's watchers and Lysandro's neighbors keep the gate from becoming a second battlefield. |
| **A civilian is saved publicly** | The party's triage choice becomes town rumor before the smoke clears. |
| **A monster dies wrong** | A corpse collapses into worm-meat or starts humming, proving the battle is over but the contamination is not. |

### Do not let the end feel safe

- Leave one visible wound in the wall, gate, or road.
- Leave one infected or glassy civilian unresolved.
- Let Brin Holloway count the survivors out loud and get a different number twice.
- Let the party see the north road go quiet in a way that feels deliberate.

## Respite beat

> [!GM-NOTE]
> The respite is a pressure chamber, not a rest stop. Give the players room to choose priorities, spend resources, and attach to NPCs. Then end it with the second wave warning sign.

The town has enough time for buckets, bandages, arguments, and one or two meaningful asks. It does **not** have enough time to solve the siege, evacuate everyone, or make the walls safe.

## Second wave: wall-tearing horror

> [!WARNING]
> The second wave is not another skirmish. It is the answer to the first wave failing: breach the wall, sour the safe places, and make Mireward Reach choose what it cannot protect.

### Second wave warning signs d6

| d6 | Sign |
|----|------|
| 1 | The ground under the north apron exhales warm, rotten air. |
| 2 | Caltrops, ball bearings, or broken arrows begin sinking point-first into mud. |
| 3 | Every hinge in the north gate screams once, though nothing touches it. |
| 4 | A glassy civilian walks calmly to the nearest doorway and waits. |
| 5 | A line of cressets along the wall goes out from bottom to top. |
| 6 | Something below knocks three times against the palisade posts. |

### Wave composition

Use the exact mix only if the table wants a full tactical escalation. Otherwise, run this as a visible clock with monster appearances.

| Role | Creature | Source / note |
|------|----------|---------------|
| **Siege breaker** | **Latch-Harrow** | CR 8 clock monster; attacks gates, braces, helpers, and "doors" in people. |
| **Foundation threat** | **Palisade Gnawer** or similar burrower | Burrow 30 ft.; damages wood/stone, splinter spray after biting structures. |
| **Infiltration / hinge panic** | **Gatekisser** | Rot metal, kiss hinges, wear a missing civilian face. |
| **Swarm pressure** | **Meat Worm / Corrupted Heap** | Low-tier ground horror; good for foundations, drains, and panic spaces. |
| **Remaining air threat** | **Aberrant Meatwings** | Keep one or two if the wall defenders need split attention. |

### Breach Clock

Start at **0 / 8**. At **4**, the north gate is compromised. At **8**, a section fails or an alternate target is lost.

Advance the clock when:

- Latch-Harrow hits the gate, brace, wall, or a repair crew.
- A burrower spends a round under the palisade without being stopped.
- Gatekisser destroys a hinge, chain, lock, or metal brace.
- PCs ignore wall work to chase only kills.
- A glassy civilian reaches a marked doorway, gate, cellar, or culvert.

Reduce or pause the clock when:

- PCs assign NPC crews with clear commands.
- Nera gets lanterns and braces placed.
- Salla commits stores or tools instead of arguing jurisdiction.
- Lysandro mobilizes neighbor crews under PC protection.
- Delwen closes or controls the causeway/ferry chain.
- A PC spots and blocks the underground route.

### Second-wave opening read-aloud

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> For one breath, Mireward Reach sounds like a town after a battle: coughing, crying, orders shouted by people pretending orders are enough. Then the north gate answers from below. Not a crash. A knock. Three slow blows from inside the earth, and every hinge in the wall screams like it remembers being a door.
