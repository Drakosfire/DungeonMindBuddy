# C2S23 North Gate Session Runbook

## At-table intent

This runbook opens at [North Reach Gate](#dmb-ref:location:north-reach-gate). Keep the table on immediate choices: who gets through, who is left outside, and whether panic becomes violence.

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> The southern road buckles into a wall of bodies, wagons, and wet gray fog. Bells hammer behind the gate while the refugee line surges toward any hand that looks like authority.

## Opening frame

> [!GM-NOTE]
> Keep this scene about triage: gate safety, civilian crush, and whether Lysandro weaponizes panic. Do not solve the whole siege here.

If the party turns toward the argument, foreground [Lysandro Ironveil](#dmb-ref:npc:lysandro-ironveil) and let him demand that [North Reach Gate](#dmb-ref:location:north-reach-gate) close before the infected can reach the line.

## First five minutes

- Ask which PC takes the gate, the crowd, and the cure line.
- Show one infected refugee as a person before the threat becomes a mass.
- If the line breaks, roll or choose from [Gate Dilemma d12](#dmb-ref:roll-table:gate-dilemma-d12).

## Pressure clocks

> [!RULES]
> Use visible pressures instead of hidden timers: Gate, Civilians, Cure Line. Advance one whenever the table stalls or chooses a costly success.

> [!WARNING]
> The meat flank is minutes behind the refugees. Do not let the opening become a static council debate.

## Decision fork

- If they talk, make Lysandro name a specific cost for keeping the gate open.
- If they fight, bring in the [Sewer Meat Creature](#dmb-ref:statblock:sewer-meat-creature) and pivot toward [North Gate Combat](#dmb-action:combat:north-gate-combat).
- If they flee or split, cut between the gate lever, the crush, and the first scream behind the wagons.
