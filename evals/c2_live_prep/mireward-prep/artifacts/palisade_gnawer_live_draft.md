# Palisade Gnawer
*Large monstrosity, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 16
**Hit Points** 140 (16d10+48)
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|16 (+3)|2 (-4)|10 (+0)|6 (-2)|

**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened
**Damage Vulnerabilities** fire
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands no languages
**Challenge** 5.0 (1,800 XP)

## Traits
**Brutal Durability.** The Palisade Gnawer ignores difficult terrain caused by rubble.

## Actions
**Multiattack.** The Palisade Gnawer makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Skull Ram.
**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage. The bite deals double damage to wood and stone structures.
**Skull Ram.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.
**Foundation Gnaw.** The Palisade Gnawer deals double damage to wood and stone structures when using its bite attack.
**Splinter Spray (Recharge 5-6).** After damaging a wooden structure, the Palisade Gnawer can unleash a 15-foot cone of splinters. Each creature in that area must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Description
The Palisade Gnawer is a massive, rat-like creature with a beaver's gnawing instincts. Its skull is heavily plated, allowing it to batter through defenses, while its teeth are sharp and stone-like, perfect for tearing apart fortifications. Though not intelligent, it is a formidable siege beast that thrives in destruction, capable of turning wooden barricades into splinters with ease. Its presence is often heralded by the sound of cracking wood and the sight of collapsing structures.
