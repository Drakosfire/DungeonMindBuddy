# Of Conks & Cons — GM card Gold

These are target cards for Plan and Play. Their grouping is a Gold example.
Score whether an equivalent arrangement lets the GM run the adventure and
handle a detour. The [source checklist](conks-source-to-plan-gold.md) owns
source coverage and page references. Keep both Gold documents out of model
inputs. The cues below are paraphrases and proposed GM presentation.

## How the deck works

In **Plan**, the GM can group and reorder cards, preview their Play view,
attach map pins and mechanics, and inspect dependencies. In **Play**, the GM
can inspect any card, bring one forward, open a side situation, and return.
Provide a quick view of what the GM needs now, with expandable source,
mechanics, and related cards. A card's display order can change without
changing its triggers. Inspecting a card records no fictional outcome.
Record what happened on the run, linked to the card and relevant World nodes.

## H1 · Arrive in Hempholm

**At a glance.** A village has an immediately visible danger: a grotesque
tree beside the Jove home attacks creatures that approach.

- **Open with** the party seeing its aggression, or let a villager draw them
  into the situation. Give the GM enough to begin at the tree or in town.
- **If they investigate**, open J1. **If they head to town**, open H2.
  **If they wait while the threat remains**, make J2 available as escalation.
- **Keep at hand:** village map and clickable pins for the Shacks, Morwin's
  store, Saladin's wagon, the Jove home, and the tree. Pin only supported
  positions on an excerpt. Mark generated geography as authored prep.

Source: C01 and C18–C20. Printed pp. 5–10.

## H2 · Hempholm people and places

**At a glance.** Open these in the order the players choose. Keep shop,
merchant, and tavern details within reach without forcing a town tour.

| Open | What the GM needs | Jump to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Shacks and Nar | Lodging and gathering place. Nar can become an ally through goodwill, persuasion, or an appeal to her faith. | J2 for wounded villagers, V1 for celebration, M2 for care of the child. |
| Morwin Blackwell | Runs the village store, sells basic gear and hemp, and is sour about Saladin's arrival. | Rough stones or **Improvise NPC** if the party escalates. |
| Saladin's wagon | Trading and unusual wares, including the statue. | M2 if the party asks him to care for the child. |
| Village diversions | Broken distillery repair, inspection of Morwin's rough stones, or the Shacks competition. | Return to the party's actual scene when finished. |

**Improvise NPC.** From Plan or Play, provide only a name and short description
for a shopkeeper with no supplied statblock. Buddy should propose table-ready
mechanics the GM can review, edit, and explicitly save, then let Combat load
that exact revision. Treat invented mechanics as authored prep. In Play,
return to the interrupted card and keep the encounter outcome in the run.

Source: C06, C18–C23, C25. Printed pp. 7–11 and 12–13.
The improvisation is a benchmark action rather than module content.

## J1 · Jove home and the surface tree

**At a glance.** The Jove home is endangered. Mark and Torbin can explain
what they know about the seed. Approaching the tree exposes its aggression.

- **If they talk**, make the Joves' account and seed origin available.
- **If they test or fight it**, open the exact Grotesque Tree mechanics.
- **If they wait**, offer J2. **If they use fire**, keep the applicable
  spread condition available. **When they destroy the surface tree**,
  record that fact and start the raid's fictional delay. Choose V1 or V2
  according to what happened.

Source: C02–C04, C24. Printed pp. 10–11, 13, 17.

## J2 · The village runs out of patience

**At a glance.** If the party stalls, the tree can grow and damage nearby
homes. Villagers may try to fell it and get hurt.

- Show the new danger to the Jove home and neighbors. At this stage,
  using fire risks igniting surrounding homes.
- If the villagers attack, offer aid to the wounded and Nar's help.
- Return to J1 when the party intervenes. Keep elapsed fictional time
  distinct from time spent browsing the deck.

Source: C04–C06. Printed p. 13.

## V1 · Celebration at the Shacks

**Bring forward when** the surface tree is destroyed and the village
was spared fire. Villagers celebrate and treat the party as heroes.

- Optional prompts: children want stories and may ask to borrow weapons,
  drinking can impair PCs, and PCs may end up apart.
- Keep R1 available for the later alarm. Record only the prompts that
  actually became played events.

Source: C07, C09–C10. Printed p. 14.

## V2 · Hempholm is on fire

**Bring forward when** the party has caused a village fire. The immediate
choice is to help or leave. Keep the firefighting exertion check and possible
home losses available, with displaced families using the Shacks.

- If the party stays, R1 can interrupt firefighting once its trigger is met.
- If the party leaves, record departure and the unresolved threat. The raid
  does not require the party to witness it.

Source: C08, C10. Printed p. 14.

## R1 · Caretaker raid

**Bring forward when** hours have passed after destruction of the surface tree.
It can interrupt celebration or firefighting.

- **Run it:** caretakers attack people and structures in groups, avoid fire,
  and withdraw after enough time or losses. Open twig blight mechanics.
- **Ask the GM to record:** whom the party protected, what was damaged, and
  whether they pursued the caretakers. Their openings give access to D1.

Source: C10–C12. Printed pp. 14–15.

## D1 · Down into the roots

**At a glance.** The raid openings connect into the hollow root network.
Caretakers watch from narrower tunnels and signal ahead. If attacked from
the main corridors, they retreat deeper into inaccessible passages.

- Offer a pause, investigation, or continued descent. Bring up M1 when
  the party reaches the hub.

Source: C12. Printed p. 15.

## M1 · The Marrow encounter

**At a glance.** Converging corridors lead to a chamber with a protected,
glowing cocoon. A guardian and two caretakers attack.

- Open Guardian and caretaker mechanics. Keep the cocoon and helix in the
  encounter description so the GM can handle players interacting with them.
- Once the party can investigate, open M2. Record the actual encounter result.

Source: C13–C14, C24. Printed pp. 15–17.

## M2 · The child and the helix

**At a glance.** Two independent decisions share this space. The party may
free the child and may also choose how much metal resin to harvest.

- **Child:** leave it, free it, or decide its care. Nar or Saladin can help.
  A freed child needs metal and learns from those who raise it.
- **Resin:** keep the safe amount and the warning about further damage
  visible together. Destroying the helix for additional resin can collapse
  the root network and destroy Hempholm. Open the escape check if triggered.
- **Wrap:** record the surviving people, damage, the child's disposition,
  and the village's reaction. Offer E1 as a possible continuation.

Source: C14–C17. Printed pp. 15–16.

## E1 · The guild follows up

**Optional continuation.** Paelias Sian investigates, pays for silence,
and seeks to eliminate evidence of the experiment, including the offspring.
Use the actual state of the village and the child's custodianship to decide
how this enters a follow-up. Leave it as prep until it happens in Play.

Source: C17. Printed p. 16.

## Acceptance rehearsal

1. In Plan, move the town card earlier and preview it. Save and reopen.
   Verify that map labels, source references, and raid dependencies survive.
2. In Play, visit Morwin before the Joves. Open the shop from the map or
   deck. Rehearse an unexpected confrontation with an incidental NPC,
   review and save the improvised statblock, run one round, and resume
   the interrupted scene. Inspecting the tree card early must not mark
   it fought or completed.
3. Return to the tree and take the fire branch. Bring up the raid during
   firefighting after the qualifying fictional delay. Verify celebration
   does not become played history. Reopen the run and check scene notes,
   actual choices, and exact mechanics revision.
4. Check both Marrow decisions can be opened independently. Record only
   the selected outcomes. Publish the reviewed recap and use a fresh
   session to verify a cited departure from the Plan.

Score coverage using C01–C25. Score table use separately by whether the GM
can find usable cues for the detour, preserve causal triggers, open exact
references, and resume after interruption. An unavailable action is a
capability gap. Record any manual bridge.
