Here’s a tight refresher on **Mirathorn** based on current CANON:

**Overview**
- **Mirathorn** is a major city-state and **strategic trade hub**, founded over 200 years ago by settlers fleeing the **Lundayell Empire**.
- It’s currently in a **high-tension state**: a festival is underway, but cult activity, political conflict, and public unrest are all converging.

---

### Key Attributes

**History**
- Founded by settlers fleeing the **Lundayell Empire**.
- Early settlers endured peril to carve out a safe haven; this is reflected in the cultural idea of **“Reaches”** symbolizing bravery and progress.
- Growth is commemorated by the **Festival of Expansion**, tied to the sacrifice of the **Nameless Goddess**.

**Geography**
- Located **by Lake Mirathorn** and at the base of the **Stormspire Peaks**.
- Approached through **expansive farmlands and workshops**, then into a crowded perimeter settlement (**Out Town**) that encircles the city.
- The city itself includes **hidden passages, sewers, and underground infrastructure**—currently exploited by the cult.

**Demographics**
- Population ~**7000 residents**, plus a surge of visitors for the festival.
- Highly **diverse**: humans, dwarves, elves, gnomes, halflings, dragonborn, plus some goblins and bugbears.
- Currently swollen with **frontier folk, merchants, and travelers**.

**Economy**
- A **key trade hub** with industries like brewing, craftsmanship, fishing, agriculture, and magical goods.
- The **Festival of Expansion is crucial** to the city’s economy.
- Trade flows in from frontier towns; congestion at the gates is impacting commerce.

**Defenses**
- Surrounded by **ancient stone walls** with guard towers (now mostly symbolic/lookout).
- **Main gates** are reinforced wood and iron—currently a bottleneck.
- Defense integrity is a **major concern**, with proposals like **arcane lockdowns** and ward activation.
- The city has **less fortified areas**—a vulnerability the cult may exploit.

---

### Current Situation (Important for Play)
- **Crowded gates + high toll** → unrest and protests (led by **Shepherd’s Flock cultists**).
- **Cult threat**:
  - Operating **beneath the city via sewers and hidden routes**.
  - Distributing **corrupted meat** causing illness (notably among non-humans).
  - Attempting a **kaiju summoning ritual**.
- **Political tension**:
  - City Council is divided on how to respond (military strike, purification, covert ops, arcane solutions).
  - Possible **cult influence within the council**.
- **City stability at risk**:
  - “Will fall if immediate action is not taken.”
  - Debates and delays worsen the situation.

- Outside: **festive chaos**—tents, performers, traders, protests.
- At the gates: **bottleneck + agitation**.
- Inside: **political intrigue + looming crisis**.
- Underneath: **cult activity and ritual buildup**.
