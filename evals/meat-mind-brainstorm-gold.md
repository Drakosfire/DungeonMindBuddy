## 5 · Draft playable mechanics on request

> Draft the full statblock in Markdown here. Keep the original combat profile where it fits. Give the truce and the fight for control mechanics I can run at the table. I want to review it before saving.

**Gold reply shape**

- A complete standalone GM-readable statblock uses the pinned Meat Mind
  revision. Preserve its unchanged statistics and mechanics verbatim where

> **Divided Control.** The Dust Walker controls Edge when the encounter
> opens. Once per round, a creature that can communicate with the Meat
> Mind can use an action to propose a ceasefire and make a DC 15 Charisma
> (Persuasion) check. A character who invokes the node’s wish for autonomy
> has advantage. On a success, the node takes control until the end of
> its next turn. It orders the residents to stand down and withholds
> hostile actions while the party observes the ceasefire. If both sides
> agree before that turn ends, the stand-down continues while both honor
> it. On a failed check, the Dust Walker remains in control and the
> party can try a different appeal on a later round.
>
> **Rain Breaks the Truce.** If a party member resumes hostilities against
> the town or the Meat Mind, the ceasefire ends, the Dust Walker takes
> control, and rain signals that the townspeople are mobilizing. Use the
> base encounter’s creature statistics. The truce gives the party time to
> talk or withdraw. It settles nothing about the residents’ original
> identities or condition.

**Pass.** The draft is usable at the table and distinguishes source-backed

players commit. Specify action cost, trigger, effect, duration, failure,

candidate votes. On these Build retrieval requests, the host selects reads
whenever any applicable non-keyword candidate gets an include vote. The
finish fallback matters only after the include set is empty. Neither trace
had a post-read plan question.
