# Gold exemplar — Master Tindlewix at the Aspitome ceremony (GM prep)

Benchmark gold: a **Tindlewix-first** prep sheet that still respects the same ceremony document. Matching this in embeddings **and** keyword coverage without the corpus is intentionally hard.

## Who Tindlewix is at the table

**Master Tindlewix** is the **head illusionist**: an **ancient gnome** whose authority is theatrical but gentle. They are not a combat boss here—they are the **person who makes the room consent to magic**. Their **staff glimmering** is a stage prop and a focus; their real tool is timing.

## Opening beat (canonical)

They step forward after the hall has settled with candles lit. They deliver the opening lines essentially verbatim from the text:

- *“Tonight, we let the **past speak through light and shadow**.”*
- *“If you wish, add your story—**every shadow shapes our world**.”*

Then they **gesture the shadowplay to begin**—do not let them monologue; the ceremony is **participatory**.

## What they want from the crowd (layered)

**Surface:** participation, wonder, emotional investment—people should feel they co-authored the myth.

**Underneath:** they are quietly auditing for **wrong shadows**—anything that moves **out of sync**, any whisper that does not match the agreed story. They care about **authenticity** of memory, not polish.

## How they use magic on-screen

They coordinate **Minor Illusion**, **Prestidigitation**, and **Mage Hand** as ensemble tools—Tindlewix is conductor, not soloist. The space already sells the scene: **illusion-lanterns**, **hanging silks**, the **silk screen** drinking color from the **bubble**.

## PC touchpoints (run these early)

1. **Guest shadowcasters:** invite a PC with **Arcana or Performance** to step behind the screen early—low stakes, high buy-in.
2. **Improvised canon:** offer **Charisma or Deception/Performance** to voice a **forgotten god** or **shadowy hero**—reward bold improvisation.
3. **Investigation:** **Arcana or Insight** on the moment the wrong icon appears; land the **“old power”** beat as ambiguous signal.
4. **The child hook:** a child says the shadow is not from the story and appears in dreams—Tindlewix should treat that as **signal**, not noise.

## If something goes wrong mid-ritual

Tindlewix stays **composed** but **accelerates corrections**—tighter illusions, sharper cues. They should seek competent PCs after with the **strange disturbance** invitation. They will not stop the ceremony publicly unless collapse is imminent; their default is **contain and continue**.

## Sensory tells for roleplay

- Voice: soft, precise, a little amused—someone who has said these words a hundred times and still means them.
- Physicality: small precise gestures—**mage-wands** tapping rhythm, staff used as baton.
- Smell cue from the hall: **melted wax**, **cinnamon**, **faint burnt sugar**—when the **shimmer** touches silk, add the **strange “otherness”** note.

## Anti-patterns (gold says “don’t”)

- Do not reduce Tindlewix to comic relief gnome.
- Do not resolve the boundary mystery in this scene—**seed** only.
- Do not skip the participatory invitation; the whole ceremony fails if the crowd is treated as audience only.
