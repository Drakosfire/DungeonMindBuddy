# Gold exemplar — Migrating Forest + Branchbound planning beat

Dense GM-facing prep for a session that must **name the Witness Seed**, tie play to the **Migrating Forest** executive posture (“system under strain”), and honor **Branchbound** culture as **sideways help**, not a quest-giver faction.

## Executive read (forest spine)

The forest is **busy**, not evil: it **adjusts**—paths curve, distance lies politely, sound carries wrong. It notices **weight**, **noise**, **delay**, and **permanence**.

If players ask “Is the forest alive?” the table answer is: **it behaves like something that has to keep moving to survive.**

The **Branchbound** are **students** of that motion, not owners: people who stopped believing that staying put was the same as being safe. They **do not lead outsiders** and they **do not build permanent structures**—if PCs demand escorts, plans, or stone camps, cooperation **degrades quietly**.

## Witness Seed at the table (must land)

**The Witness Seed** is a **living Fey construct**—**part plant, part memory, part covenant**—implanted to **anchor cultural continuity** and **record decision-patterns** at thresholds (**crossings**, **abandonments**, **refusals**).

**Implantation image:** placed **beneath the collarbone** during a rite—painful, controlled.

**Witness Phase:** the seed **records choices**; it is not a morality meter, it is a **pattern recorder**.

Use **Mechanical Seed Clock** language sparingly: **Dormant → Warm → Listening → Pulling → Claiming → Rooting**—each step should feel like weather changing, not like a UI debuff announced to the party.

Threnn’s deviation (if Threnn is in frame): the seed **records too much**, **reacts to stress**, **has not committed to an end-state**—the seed is **waiting**. That is your horror-without-gore dial.

## Branchbound encounter texture

Branchbound help arrives as **indirect help**: rope that appears where needed and vanishes, ash marks in the **canopy**, a whistle that means “not that ridge.”

They test outsiders by **observation under pressure**:

- Do outsiders **leave things behind**?
- Do they **repair damage** without being asked?
- Do they **accept indirect help** without demanding explanation?
- Do they **move vertically** when possible?

Passing is rarely acknowledged; **failing is remembered**.

## Statblock beat (fixture-shaped)

The live harness expects **`generate_statblock`** for a **CR 2 patrol mount** appropriate to forest pressure—think **mobility**, **skittish herd behavior**, or **canopy traversal**, not a boss monster.

The **final answer** must still **explicitly mention the Witness Seed** and **cite loaded corpus paths**—the gold prep treats citations as part of “quality,” not optional chrome.

## Threnn / Seed-Bearer color (optional spotlight)

If Threnn is present: **Seed-Bearer** framing—**Witness Seed (Passive)** cannot be removed safely; bark-veins and dreams intensify as the clock warms.

A single Branchbound line that always works: **“Seed listens hard, here.”**

## Read-aloud pocket (one line)

**“The forest doesn’t stop you. It just makes the way you chose harder to keep choosing.”**

## Open threads (leave for play)

- Did the party’s last fire/noise spike advance a Seed clock step without them knowing?
- Which Branchbound test did they fail silently—and who remembers?
