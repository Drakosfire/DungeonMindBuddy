# Gold exemplar — Cultural Ceremonies at the Temple of the Aspitome (GM prep)

This document is **benchmark gold**: a deliberately dense, scene-faithful prep brief. A model answer that matches it in **embedding space** and **critical keyword coverage** without copying is unlikely unless it actually internalized the same source material.

## Situation at the table

The PCs arrive for **Festival of Expansion — Day Three** at the **Temple of the Aspitome**. The main worship hall is **high-arched and dimmed**; **drifting illusion-lanterns** and **hanging silks** make the space feel like half temple, half theatre. The crowd is already carrying tension: some treat this as sacred civic duty, others treat it as spectacle.

## I. Stories in Shadow (first movement)

### Sensory spine

- **Sight:** A long **silk screen** across the altar shimmers with colors borrowed from the **bubble** outside—sometimes **motes of blue and gold**, sometimes red. **Shadowcasters** move behind it; their silhouettes should feel alive even when still.
- **Sound:** Hushed voices, a low drumbeat, the soft tap of **mage-wands** on floor or staff.
- **Smell:** **Melted wax**, **cinnamon**, **faint burnt sugar**, and that trace of **strange “otherness”** whenever the shimmer from the wall touches the silk.

### Ceremony beats you can run

1. **Gathering:** Attendees file in; each receives a small **beeswax candle**. Let the table feel the social sorting—children cluster, elders nod, cynics whisper.
2. **Opening ritual:** **Master Tindlewix**—the **head illusionist**, an **ancient gnome**—steps forward, **staff glimmering**, and delivers the canonical invitation: *“Tonight, we let the past speak through light and shadow. If you wish, add your story—**every shadow shapes our world**.”* Keep it short; the power is invitation, not lecture.
3. **Shadowplay begins:** Narrate the scripted shadow history in broad strokes: the fall of the gods; a **shadowy figure (Erathis)** gathering heroes; the building of the **Aspitome**; the **exodus through the shimmering bubble**. On the table, emphasize that **mages and actors** lean on **Minor Illusion**, **Prestidigitation**, and **Mage Hand**—this is participatory illusionism, not a cutscene.

### Player-facing hooks (Stories in Shadow)

- Invite PCs as **guest shadowcasters** (**Arcana or Performance**).
- Allow improvised “voices” for **forgotten gods** or a **shadowy hero** (**Charisma or Deception/Performance**).
- For investigators: **Perception** or **Insight** may catch **one shadow moving out of sync**, or a rumor that a secret will surface tonight.
- **Climax image:** the screen flares as the outside bubble aligns; for a beat something wrong intrudes—a **wolf-headed form**, a spiral, or runes **not part of the script**. A success on **Arcana or Insight** should land the line about a surge of **“old power,”** ambiguous enough to be warning or plea from the boundary.

### Post-scene follow-through

If PCs helped shape the story, Tindlewix may seek them afterward with the cult-adjacent hook: *“I saw how you shaped that shadow—would you help us with a **strange disturbance**?”* A child may insist **“That shadow isn’t from our story. I see it in my dreams sometimes.”** Leave a physical clue: a **hidden symbol** on the screen matching a cult sign or a PC mystery.

## II. The Expansion Ritual (second movement)

### Space and spectacle

Move the table to the inner sanctum: a pillar of crystal called **“Aspitome’s Heart”** on a circular dais; **smaller crystals** sit in **wrought-iron holders**, glowing in patterns that echo the **shimmer** seen earlier. **Pulsing blue-white light**, crackling sparks, runes in **every language known to Mirathorn**.

### Ceremony beats

- **The Summoning:** The **high cleric** in **iridescent robes** calls silence and speaks the charge: *“As the Aspitome’s light grows, so does our world…”*  
- **Collective Spell:** Row by row, attendees touch a smaller **charge crystal** and “will” memory, hope, or magic into it. Describe the crystals brightening with each touch.
- **Twist:** If a PC is fate-marked, their touch may **surge**—pulse, flicker, or **crack**. When a crystal fails, something falls out: **ancient rune**, fragment of a **goddess’s name**, a **forgotten relic**. A priest or wizard may murmur the investigation line: *“The **Aspitome is never supposed to react like that**—unless…something is wrong.”*

### Vision / long-arc seed

Allow a brief vision—**Nameless God**, original exodus, boundary trembling—enough to spook without resolving.

## GM pacing notes

Keep both ceremonies **flexible**: spotlight lore-heavy PCs without forcing wallflowers through every beat. Reward curiosity with extra sensory detail, Inspiration, or a minor boon. Plant **at least one durable hook** (cracked crystal, cult sigil in shadow, child’s recurring dream, cryptic vision).
