# Session 1 Recap

After traveling together for some time together as merchant guards, our mish mash of travelers;  [Karsemine](dmb-node:pc_karsemine) the Tiefling Ranger, [Stafl](dmb-node:pc_stafl) the 'Human' Bard, [Caelynn](dmb-node:pc_caelynn) the Half Elf Sorcerer, [Ephanna](dmb-node:pc_ephanna) the Kenku Warlock, [Bonogo](dmb-node:pc_bonogo) the Bugbear Rogue, and [Baergrom](dmb-node:pc_baergrom) the Dwarf Fighter, found themselves outside the town of [Stone Bridge](dmb-node:item_stone_bridge).

The town of [Stone Bridge](dmb-node:item_stone_bridge) is known for very few things, in fact [Stone Bridge](dmb-node:item_stone_bridge) is hardly known. It has the [Stone Bridge over the river](dmb-node:location_stone_bridge_over_the_river) [River name], it's tavern  [The River's Edge Pub](dmb-node:item_the_river_s_edge_pub) run by [Grishna](dmb-node:character_grishna) the Half-orc, and that's about it. It did have a [job board](dmb-node:item_job_board), and most importantly the local brewer [Glowkindle](dmb-node:character_glowkindle) had posted a help request on the jobs board and spread word all around town of his need of a band of mercenaries to help clean up some rats.

While doing some drinking at the Riv'ers Edge Pub to wash away the road, [Grishna](dmb-node:character_grishna) was quick to share that [Glowkindle](dmb-node:character_glowkindle) had been through, where the The [Wizard's Tower Brewing Co](dmb-node:location_wizard_s_tower_brewing_co) was located. Up river, west at the big rock, walk till you see it. [Bonogo](dmb-node:pc_bonogo), having very little awareness or care for the cost of things, and greatly enjoying the beer, bought a [Firkin of ale](dmb-node:item_firkin_of_ale) for two gold. He quite enjoyed the hike to the brewery.

[Grishna](dmb-node:character_grishna) was true to her word, the directions were sound. There was a clear trail along the river to an [enormous boulder](dmb-node:item_enormous_boulder).  As the group approached it resolved into what must have been the [foot of a once enormous statue](dmb-node:item_foot_of_a_once_enormous_statue). Or a mad sculptures dedication to someone's foot, probably the  former but who really knows with art anyway.

Another few hours walk away from the river along the trail led the group to [the Wizard's Tower Brewing Company](dmb-node:item_the_wizard_s_tower_brewing_company).  Bustling with activity, and smelling of brewing, the fine tap room lit by [magical crystals](dmb-node:item_magical_crystals), was empty except for [the troupe of gnomes](dmb-node:character_the_troupe_of_gnomes) busily brewing.

Within they met [Glowkindle](dmb-node:character_glowkindle) who told them, a bit about the issue at hand. [Giant rats](dmb-node:item_giant_rats) had assaulted his [excavation crew](dmb-node:item_excavation_crew) after they broke through a wall expanding the [fermentation cellar](dmb-node:item_fermentation_cellar). For a healthy prize of 25 gold pieces each, the team agreed to clear out the rats.  Which was significantly harder than expected, led to multiple folk going down, a mysterious [cat owl](dmb-node:creature_cat_owl) being tossed into the room to help, a lot of blood from rats and teammates, and many, many, many [health potions](dmb-node:item_health_potions) being downed.

A fine first combat to bring the team together!

Finally, free to explore the team found a [beautifully tiled hallway](dmb-node:item_beautifully_tiled_hallway), a [trapped mosaic](dmb-node:item_trapped_mosaic) on the ground, a room full of [broken alchemical tools](dmb-node:item_broken_alchemical_tools). As [Karsemine](dmb-node:pc_karsemine) wisely searched the room, eventually looking up, she made eye contact with another resident of the shatter mages tower. Some kind of [flaming magma infused spider monstrosity](dmb-node:item_flaming_magma_infused_spider_monstrosity).
