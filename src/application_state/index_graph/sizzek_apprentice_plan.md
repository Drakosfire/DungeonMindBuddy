# Sizzek's Apprentice

For four level-five characters at Sizzek's Carnival. Source: Sizzek's Apprentice.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=beat id=beat:opening-day beat_kind=spine -->
## Opening day

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:contract -->
### The contract
Sizzek hires the party to supervise the opening-day crowds for 100 gp per day, with another 100 gp for work beyond crowd management. She asks them to assist patrons and be kind before throwing troublemakers out. Her apprentice is unveiling a funhouse.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:missing-patrons -->
### Missing patrons
Forty-five minutes after the gates open, Sizzek returns in a panic: patrons have disappeared into the funhouse. Find them and the apprentice; ask him to shut down the funhouse and speak to her. She must lead the opening ceremony.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=beat id=beat:funhouse beat_kind=spine -->
## The funhouse

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:entrance -->
### Entrance and spinning hall
The funhouse entrance is a ten-foot demon face; its mouth crashes shut after groups enter. A thirty-foot hall is filled with an illusion of spinning stars. A DC 15 Dexterity save keeps a character standing; a fall slides them to the mirror hallway. On the spiral stairs, a click turns the steps into a slide; a DC 15 Dexterity save keeps a character standing.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:mirrors -->
### Mirror portals
Touching a mirror sends a character to one of six portal rooms. A midway trap, noticed with DC 20 Perception, drops a portal to split the party. The rooms contain rainbow bubbles, grasping skeletal hands, entrancing sand, an underwater siren illusion, reversed gravity, and an illusory crumbling path over lava. See the source document for each save and portal destination.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:fall-and-rotation -->
### The fall and rotating room
In darkness, the party seems to fall forever. A hidden door is found by feel or a DC 17 Perception or Wisdom check; light reveals the illusion. The next room rotates every thirty seconds: DC 15 Dexterity to stay standing. A hatch in the ceiling drops into the trap room when it rotates to the floor.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=beat id=beat:apprentice beat_kind=spine -->
## The apprentice's trap

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:trap-room -->
### Delphinius and the closing walls
An illusory Delphinius sits on a throne of bones; DC 20 Perception or Arcana reveals it. The real apprentice hides behind a DC 13 hidden wall in the north control room. If detected, he fights unless the party attempts dialogue. DC 20 Charisma or effective roleplay convinces him to surrender and speak to Sizzek. Otherwise six skeletons attack and the east and west walls move inward five feet each round.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=choice id=choice:confrontation -->
### How does the party confront the apprentice?

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=option id=option:talk -->
- Attempt to reason with Delphinius.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=option id=option:fight -->
- Fight while the walls close in.

<!-- dmb-playable-element:v2 kind=scene id=scene:return -->
### Return to Sizzek
If the party brings Delphinius to Sizzek, she paralyzes him, says she will deal with him later, hugs the party, and gives them 100 gp before asking them to finish their day's work.
